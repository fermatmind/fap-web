# Administrative Services Managers vs. Purchasing Managers on an Office Furniture Order

## Which work do you want to own?

An administrative services manager works out what employees need and how a delivery fits the office. A purchasing manager organizes the buying process and compares what suppliers can commit to. Both may track costs, but they hand the decision-maker different information.

## Compare the outputs

| Work product | Administrative Services Managers | Purchasing Managers |
|---|---|---|
| Example | Quantities, setup dates, and receiving needs | Comparable quotes, delivery terms, and an award recommendation |

## How the work intersects

A new team arrives in two weeks. The administrative manager counts workstations, checks what can be reassigned, and finds an installation window. The purchasing manager compares suppliers using the same specifications and takes note of the cheapest supplier's longer lead time. The approving manager gets both the timing impact and the supplier tradeoff.

## Build entry experience

BLS describes a bachelor’s degree and related experience as typical for administrative services managers. [BLS occupational preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/management/administrative-services-managers.htm#tab-4) O*NET classifies purchasing managers as work that generally requires considerable preparation. [O*NET Job Zone](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/11-3061.00#JobZone) Job Zones describe a broad level of preparation. Ask the employer which education, training and experience count for the specific job.

To enter office services, show a project where you coordinated inventory, users, and deadlines. To enter purchasing, prepare a fictional supplier comparison and explain why you would reject an option. Ask employers what experience they require and which decisions the buyer can make.

## What to ask at an interview

An office move can put the administrative manager on site checking space and delivery. Purchasing work may spend more time comparing terms and documenting changes before an award can move forward. Ask the office team how often moves disrupt ordinary services. Ask the purchasing team how it handles supplier delays, contract changes, and disputed deliveries.

## Moving between the roles

Move toward purchasing by converting an office request into measurable bid criteria. Move toward office services by adding a receiving, installation, and staff readiness plan to a buying schedule.

## Try the work in 30 minutes

In a fictional exercise a team needs twelve desks on day 14 and can borrow eight. Quote A is 1,200 credits and includes installation on day 21. Quote B is 1,400 credits and includes installation on day 12. The invented budget is 1,450 credits. Spend ten minutes describing the temporary shortfall, fifteen comparing the quotes and five writing a recommendation and naming who must approve. These are exercise figures.
