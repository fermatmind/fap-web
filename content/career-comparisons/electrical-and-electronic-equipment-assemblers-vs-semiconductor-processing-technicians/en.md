# Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers vs. Semiconductor Processing Technicians: Matching the Lot to the Module

## What each role delivers

Electrical and electronic equipment assemblers install and inspect components. Semiconductor processing technicians follow process orders, inspect wafers or devices and maintain lot records.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers | Semiconductor Processing Technicians |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Module assembly and wiring inspection | Wafer process and lot inspection |
| Basis | Component and assembly IDs | Lot IDs, order revisions and inspection |

[O*NET 51-2022.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/51-2022.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 51-9141.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/51-9141.00#Tasks)

The grouped data do not yield a separate median for the role. [O*NET 51-2022.00 wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/51-2022.00)

## How the work meets

At a fictional plant, module M7 fails a wiring check and an incoming part report notes a surface defect. The assembler records the module and part IDs. The technician supplies the process lot record. The lead first checks whether the records refer to the same lot.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for semiconductor processing technicians. For assembly, ask about drawings and inspection responsibilities. For processing, check shift and cleanroom expectations with the employer. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Assembly may involve repeated component and connection checks. Processing work may follow production steps, inspection points and shifts. Ask how each station records exceptions.

## Moving between roles

Moving to processing, build on inspections and add process and lot controls. Moving to assembly, bring traceability skills and learn component positioning and assembly drawings.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a mock record: module M7 fails a wiring check and uses lot L12. The surface defect report is for L09. There is no L12 inspection report in the packet. Spend ten minutes each documenting assembly, sorting lot records and identifying a link you cannot yet establish.
