# Recycling Coordinators vs. Transportation, Storage and Distribution Managers on a Collection Day

## Start with the work

Recycling coordinators organize collection and keep material and participant records. Distribution managers plan where materials go, what space is available, and who moves them. When volumes change, the teams exchange different constraints.

## Compare the handoffs

| Work product | Recycling Coordinators | Transportation, Storage and Distribution Managers |
|---|---|---|
| Example | Material acceptance and collection log | Vehicle, labor, and room for staged material |

## One operating scenario

A fictional neighborhood event brings paper and plastic to one drop-off site. The coordinator checks the program's acceptance list and records quantities separately. The distribution manager schedules transfers around space and vehicle availability. Material outside the confirmed program list is recorded and referred to the designated person.

## How to prepare

O*NET assigns recycling coordination to Job Zone 1–2 and distribution management to Job Zone 4. Program support and collection records can build recycling experience. Receiving, storage and shift coordination can prepare you for distribution. Confirm the material scope and applicable training or authorization. [Recycling Coordinators preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/53-1042.01#JobZone) · [Transportation, Storage and Distribution Managers preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/11-3071.00#JobZone)

## What shapes the workday

Recycling work can follow event timetables and questions from participants. Distribution follows arrival volumes, space and site staff. Ask how each team covers changes on the day.

## Moving between roles

Move into distribution by adding capacity and vehicle planning to your material records. Move into recycling by learning program acceptance, participant communication, and coordination.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use fictional counts of twelve bags of paper and eight of plastic. Space remains for ten paper bags and nine plastic bags. A vehicle can remove six bags already in the paper area at 4, before the new material arrives at 4:15. Spend ten minutes writing participant and sorting notes, fifteen balancing both spaces, and five identifying who confirms the plan.
