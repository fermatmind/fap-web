# Remote Sensing Technicians vs. Surveying and Mapping Technicians: An Image and a Field Point Disagree

## What each role delivers

Remote sensing technicians process and verify imagery. Surveying and mapping technicians measure and record locations and organize spatial data. The two roles may compare results on the same map.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Remote Sensing Technicians | Surveying and Mapping Technicians |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Processed imagery and discrepancy log | Field coordinates and location record |
| Basis | Layers, acquisition date and alignment | Coordinate reference, point IDs and measurement conditions |

## How the work meets

A fictional street project finds that cover J7 appears in different positions in the imagery and the survey notes. Two stable reference features agree. The imagery technician preserves the original layer and checks its date. The survey technician checks the point ID, photos and coordinates before requesting a new field check.

## How to enter

A bachelor’s degree was the most common O*NET education response for remote sensing technicians at 62%. BLS lists a high school diploma or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for surveying and mapping technicians. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [O*NET education data 19-4099.03](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-4099.03#Education)

## Work conditions

Imagery work may follow processing batches. Survey and mapping tasks may alternate among fieldwork, data entry and layer review. Ask how the teams track a shared discrepancy.

## Moving between roles

To move into survey and mapping work, add instrument practice and field records to coordinate and layer skills. To move into imagery, build metadata, alignment and batch tracking skills.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records show agreement at two reference points and a three-meter difference at J7. The image is five months older than the field record, and the street was repaired in between. Spend ten minutes each on an image check, a field check and when to update the map.
