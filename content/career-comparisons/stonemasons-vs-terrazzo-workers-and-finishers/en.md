# Stonemasons vs. Terrazzo Workers and Finishers: Aligning a Stone Base and Floor Divider

## What each role delivers

Stonemasons position stone and finish its joints. Terrazzo workers and finishers form floor panels and check divider placement and the finished surface.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Stonemasons | Terrazzo Workers and Finishers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Stone base and joint record | Floor panel and finish record |
| Basis | Base dimensions, stone joints and exposed face | Panel lines, divider strips and finish |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $57,390 | $76,170 |

## How the work meets

A fictional lobby has a stone display base. The stonemason hands over the joint location. The terrazzo crew finds that a floor divider misses it during layout and sends both records to the lead.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and apprenticeship for both. Ask how the training covers stone setting, surface finishing and work at adjoining trades. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Stone setting can follow piece delivery and work by section. Terrazzo work may follow divider, placement and finishing stages. Ask who coordinates access and sequence.

## Moving between roles

Moving to terrazzo, use layout and stone familiarity and add panel and finish work. Moving to stone, bring finish inspection and add setting and joints.
