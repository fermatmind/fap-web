# Chemist vs. Materials Scientist: Do Similar Composition Results Predict the Same Performance?

## What each role delivers

Chemists analyze the composition and properties of substances and may develop ways to measure them. Materials scientists study how structure and processing relate to performance in use. Both may work on formulations and test materials.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Chemists | Materials Scientists |
|---|---|---|
| Main question | What does this method establish about the sample? | What explains its behavior in the intended application? |
| Output | Composition or property report and method scope | Performance evidence, failure analysis or material recommendation |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $91,240 | $117,790 |

## How the work meets

A fictional stationery manufacturer tests plastic pieces for a folder. One lot cracks during a flex test. The chemist sends the materials scientist the composition report and method scope. The tests found no difference in the measured properties. The materials scientist compares the production histories, crack locations and test conditions. Their report identifies which results can be compared and what evidence is missing before making a durability recommendation.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor's degree as typical entry education for both occupations. A chemistry applicant can show an analytical report with its scope. A materials applicant can show a property test and explain what it supports about a material choice. Check the degree and project experience required for the role. [BLS Table 1.2](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) Some chemistry and materials research jobs may require a master’s degree or Ph.D. [BLS research education](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/chemists-and-materials-scientists.htm)

## Work conditions

Chemistry assignments may follow sample arrivals and instrument schedules. Materials research may also wait for prepared specimens, processing records and performance tests. Ask who preserves specimens and raw records.

## Moving between roles

Chemists can bring analytical methods and learn more about structure, processing and mechanical performance. Materials scientists can bring experiment design and failure records and build skills in analytical controls and method verification.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use three mock records: no composition difference within the measured scope, cracks in lot B and a missing production entry for that lot. Spend ten minutes writing what the analysis establishes, ten listing what the materials review needs and ten writing a short status for the selection team. Use the paperwork only.
