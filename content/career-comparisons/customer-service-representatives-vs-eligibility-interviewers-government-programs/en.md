# Customer Service Representative vs. Government Program Eligibility Interviewer: Who Determines Eligibility?

## What each role delivers

A customer service representative answers questions, records requests and routes unresolved cases. A government program eligibility interviewer assesses applicant information and handles eligibility decisions or review within program authority.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Customer Service Representatives | Eligibility Interviewers, Government Programs |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Service case, document status and handoff | Interview notes, eligibility evidence and decision status |
| Basis | Receipt status, process stage and disclosure limits | Program rules, verified records and delegated authority |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $44,770 | $54,210 |

## How the work meets

A fictional assistance program receives income documents. The representative checks which records they are authorized to view, records the question and routes the case. The interviewer checks the months covered against the program rules and requests one missing month. Customer service then communicates the confirmed request and current status.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent as typical for both. It lists short-term on-the-job training for customer service representatives and moderate-term training for government program eligibility interviewers. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Customer service may follow queues and response deadlines. Eligibility work also follows document requests, interviews and decision deadlines. Ask which case statuses each role can update.

## Moving between roles

Representatives can bring intake skills to eligibility work and add evidence verification, rules and decision authority. Eligibility interviewers can bring rule explanation to customer service and add multichannel communication and queue management.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock requirement: the program requires proof of income for the last three months. Two months are on file and the applicant says the third is being obtained. Spend ten minutes each on a status reply, the evidence gap and a handoff for the next review.
