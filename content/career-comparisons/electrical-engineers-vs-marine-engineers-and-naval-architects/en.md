# Electrical Engineers vs. Marine Engineers and Naval Architects: Adding Equipment to a Vessel

## What each role delivers

Electrical engineers design and verify electrical systems. Marine engineers and naval architects cover ship machinery, power and propulsion as well as hull design, layout, weight and stability. Shipboard power and equipment interfaces can involve both specialties. [BLS marine engineering duties](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/marine-engineers-and-naval-architects.htm)

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electrical Engineers | Marine Engineers and Naval Architects |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Electrical distribution design and load record | Vessel arrangement or machinery design and review |
| Basis | Supply, loads and distribution equipment | Hull, machinery interfaces, weight and vessel constraints |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $120,630 | $112,230 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 17-2071 and 17-2121](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional workboat will receive new equipment. Electrical engineering reviews supply and loads with the ship machinery team. The naval architecture side checks the arrangement and finds the new unit missing from the weight sheet before joint review.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for both. Compare electrical system design with vessel layout and analysis work. Ask the employer which designs and approvals the assignment owns. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Electrical work may follow supply interfaces and installation review. Vessel design may coordinate hull, spaces and equipment. Ask whether work follows concept design, shipyard support or testing.

## Moving between roles

Moving to marine work, bring distribution and load skills and add vessel arrangement and stability knowledge. Moving to electrical work, bring integration and learn distribution design and verification.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock card: an equipment load is recorded and the proposed position is compartment A. There is a space record but no entry for the unit in the weight sheet. Spend ten minutes each on the electrical handoff, vessel design question and missing evidence before placement approval.
