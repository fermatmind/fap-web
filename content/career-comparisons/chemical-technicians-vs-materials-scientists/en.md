# Chemical Technician vs. Materials Scientist: When Can Two Test Results Guide Material Selection?

## What each role delivers

A chemical technician tests samples and records how each result was obtained. A materials scientist studies how structure and properties affect performance and evaluates a material for a given use. Both can participate in the same tests.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Chemical Technicians | Materials Scientists |
|---|---|---|
| Main checks | Sample ID, method and instrument status | Use conditions, required properties and comparison scope |
| Output | Traceable test result and exception record | Interpretation of performance and a material recommendation |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $60,390 | $117,790 |

## How the work meets

A fictional packaging team compares two films for a humid warehouse. The technician sends the readings and notes that the lab changed instruments between the two sets without verifying their agreement. The materials scientist sees that both sets also reflect dry conditions. The scientist asks the lab to review comparability, then records the evidence still needed for the intended storage conditions before recommending either film.

## How to enter

BLS lists an associate's degree and moderate-term on-the-job training as typical for chemical technicians, and a bachelor's degree for materials scientists. A lab applicant can show how a result was recorded and checked. A materials applicant can show a project linking use requirements to a test. [BLS Table 1.2](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) Some chemistry and materials research jobs may require a master’s degree or Ph.D. [BLS research education](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/chemists-and-materials-scientists.htm)

## Work conditions

Chemical testing can follow sample arrivals and instrument schedules. Materials research may also depend on prepared specimens and several rounds of performance testing. Ask how method updates and original records are tracked.

## Moving between roles

Technicians can bring traceable data to materials research and add structure, properties and selection criteria. Materials scientists can bring experiment design to technician work and practice running approved methods and documenting routine results.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use mock reports for two films tested dry on different instruments and intended for humid storage. Spend ten minutes drafting the lab handoff, ten marking comparisons that need verification and ten writing what the selection team still needs. Work from the reports alone.
