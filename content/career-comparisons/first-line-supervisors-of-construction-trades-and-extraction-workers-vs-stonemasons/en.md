# First-Line Construction Supervisors vs. Stonemasons: A Sample Mismatch at the Entrance

## What each role delivers

First-line construction supervisors plan work and coordinate site access. Stonemasons lay out and set stone to the selected pattern, with attention to position and joints.

## Compare the work

| Work item | First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers | Stonemasons |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Area schedule and decision follow-up | Stone layout and finish record |
| Checks | Crew, delivery and access windows | Stone size, alignment and joints |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $79,920 | $57,390 |

## How the work meets

At a fictional entrance restoration, the mason finds a replacement stone that differs from the approved sample. The supervisor also learns the entrance must stay open in the morning. The mason logs a location and sample reference for the approver, and the supervisor revises the work sequence once the decision is available.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and apprenticeship as typical for stonemasons. For first-line supervisors of construction and extraction workers, BLS lists high school or equivalent and five or more years of related work experience as typical. [BLS rows 47-1011 and 47-2022](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Masons spend more time fitting and checking a specific surface. Supervisors switch among deliveries, access and adjacent crews. Ask who signs the sample record and when the area can close.

## Moving between roles

Move into supervision by turning sample and setting records into area planning and crew coordination. Move into stonemasonry by bringing plan-reading and material tracking, then learning stone selection, sample checks and setting under supervision.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use two fictional entrance locations. A falls outside the project’s approved sample range, B matches, and the entrance must remain open in the morning. In 30 minutes write a sample discrepancy, an access plan, and the conditions and decision owner for revising the assignment. Work from paper only.
