# Driver/Sales Workers vs. Stockers and Order Fillers on a Late Add-On

## Start with what you deliver

Driver/sales workers deliver goods and may inform customers about products and record sales. Stockers and order fillers check locations, available quantities and what is ready to leave. An add-on order needs evidence from both teams.

## Compare the handoffs

| Work product | Driver/Sales Workers | Stockers and Order Fillers |
|---|---|---|
| Example | Customer request, confirmed quantity and delivery record | Pick count, reserved stock and shortage note |

## Follow one order

A fictional store asks for two extra cartons before the truck leaves. The driver confirms what the store wants and when. The order filler checks the shelf and goods reserved for other orders. The driver responds using the confirmed amount and records whatever the customer accepts.

## How to prepare

BLS describes high school or equivalent as typical for delivery truck drivers and driver/sales workers, plus short on-the-job training and a state driver’s license and clean record. Check the class for the vehicle and assignment. O*NET groups stockers and order fillers in Job Zone 1–2. Receiving and order support can build counting and exception-reporting experience. [O*NET stock preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/53-7065.00#JobZone) [BLS driver/sales preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/transportation-and-material-moving/delivery-truck-drivers-and-driver-sales-workers.htm#tab-4)

## Work conditions

Route work follows travel and customer windows, with handling at delivery sites. Order filling can follow picking batches and truck deadlines. Ask about loads, shift timing and who handles short orders.

## Move between the roles

From stock work, add customer communication, delivery records and the applicable driving qualification. From route work, learn location, scanning and reservation checks.

## Try it in 30 minutes

A fictional six-carton order is already ready. At 9:10 the customer asks for two extra. Three are on the shelf, two reserved for another order, and the truck leaves at 9:20. Spend ten minutes finding the available amount, ten drafting a response, and ten listing what must be approved before changing the order.
