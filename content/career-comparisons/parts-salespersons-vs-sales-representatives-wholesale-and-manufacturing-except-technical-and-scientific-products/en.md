# Parts Salespersons vs. Wholesale Sales Representatives for Nontechnical Products: Quoting a Split Delivery

## What each role delivers

Parts salespersons check part numbers and stock and place orders. Wholesale and manufacturing sales representatives for nontechnical products explain pricing, availability and terms to business customers.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Parts Salespersons | Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Except Technical and Scientific Products |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Part number and stock record | Volume quote and delivery terms |
| Basis | Model, stock and back orders | Quantity, price and split delivery |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $38,630 | $72,080 |

## How the work meets

A fictional repair chain wants 30 standard casters. Parts sales verifies the model and finds 12 in stock, then shares the part number and quantity. The wholesale representative checks when the rest can ship and issues an authorized quote that specifies the quantity in the first shipment.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential for parts sales and a high school diploma or equivalent for nontechnical wholesale sales. It lists moderate-term on-the-job training for both. Ask who can approve volume pricing and promise delivery. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Parts work may track stock and back orders. Wholesale may involve quotes and account follow-up. Ask how stock changes reach the quote owner.

## Moving between roles

Moving into wholesale adds pricing and account management to parts knowledge. Moving into parts adds catalog and inventory work to order experience.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: demand is 30, stock for the correct part is 12 and the rest has no confirmed date. Spend ten minutes each on a stock note, conditional quote and checks before the customer accepts split delivery.
