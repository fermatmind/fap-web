# Biologist vs. Zoologist and Wildlife Biologist

## Broad biological research and research focused on animals

Biologists can study living systems and design ways to investigate biological processes. Zoologists and wildlife biologists focus on animals and their habitats. Depending on the job, they can use observations to guide monitoring or wildlife management.

## Compare the work products

| Work | Biologist | Zoologist or wildlife biologist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First check | Whether two observations can be compared | Which animal activity, site, and habitat information is available |
| Deliverable | An explanation of what the evidence can support | A monitoring record and recommendation for follow-up observations |

## Sightings differ at two wetland sites

At a fictional refuge, observers report more bird sightings at Site A than Site B. The biologist checks observation time, weather, and how observers counted sightings. The wildlife biologist checks site and habitat records and proposes which conditions to record consistently next time. Both send their findings to the person coordinating monitoring.

## U.S. education and first projects

O*NET classifies biologists in Job Zone Five, a category that commonly involves graduate preparation. A supervised project can provide experience designing comparisons and documenting observations. BLS says a bachelor’s degree is typical for entry-level zoologist and wildlife biologist jobs, and a doctorate is typical for independent research. Wildlife coursework and supervised field or data projects can help you build experience. [O*NET biologist preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-1029.04#JobZone) · [BLS zoology and wildlife biology preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/zoologists-and-wildlife-biologists.htm#tab-4)

## Compare the workplaces

Biologists may work indoors or outdoors depending on the project. BLS describes office, lab, and outdoor work for zoologists and wildlife biologists, with irregular hours possible during fieldwork. Ask when field observations happen, what travel they involve, and who checks the data afterward. [O*NET biologist work context](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-1029.04#WorkContext) · [BLS wildlife work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/zoologists-and-wildlife-biologists.htm#tab-3)

## Moving between the roles

A biologist can bring study design and analysis into wildlife work, then develop skills in species identification and habitat monitoring. A wildlife biologist can bring long-term observation experience into broader biology research, then practice designing comparisons across different systems.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use two fictional entries. Site A has eight sightings in 60 minutes on a clear day. Site B has three in 30 minutes in rain, and no water-level entry. Spend ten minutes listing the differences in observation conditions, ten writing what you can and cannot conclude, and ten making a checklist of time, weather, and water level for the next observation.
