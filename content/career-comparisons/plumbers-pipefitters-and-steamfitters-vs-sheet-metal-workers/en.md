# Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters vs. Sheet Metal Workers: A Ceiling Route Conflict

## What each role delivers

Plumbers, pipefitters, and steamfitters locate and join piping. Sheet metal workers fabricate, join and install metal components, including ducts in this example.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters | Sheet Metal Workers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Pipe route and connection record | Duct fabrication and assembly record |
| Basis | Route, fittings and opening locations | Duct envelope, seams and installation space |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $63,800 | $61,800 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 47-2152 and 47-2211](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

At a fictional clinic, a water pipe opening overlaps the duct envelope. The piping crew records the fitting and centerline. The sheet metal crew records the duct edge and joint. Both send the conflict to the coordinator using the same drawing revision.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and apprenticeship for both. Most states and some localities require plumbers to be licensed. Check the authority for the specific jurisdiction, piping work and level of responsibility. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [BLS licensing overview](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/plumbers-pipefitters-and-steamfitters.htm)

## Work conditions

Piping crews may work by system or floor. Sheet metal work can span shop fabrication and field assembly. Ask who coordinates shared space and field changes.

## Moving between roles

Moving to sheet metal, bring drawing and coordination skills and learn fabrication and seams. Moving to piping, use fit checks and learn piping systems, joints and supervised practice.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a mock drawing: the pipe opening is centered on A7, the trial duct edge overlaps it by 35 mm and both crews have revision C. Spend ten minutes each on a pipe record, a duct record and the question for coordination.
