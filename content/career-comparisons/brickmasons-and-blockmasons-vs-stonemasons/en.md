# Brickmasons and Blockmasons vs. Stonemasons: Where Brick Meets a Stone Door Surround

## What each role delivers

Brickmasons and blockmasons lay out masonry walls and check courses and openings. Stonemasons lay out and set stone and finish joints. These are overlapping masonry trades.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Brickmasons and Blockmasons | Stonemasons |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Brick masonry and layout record | Set stone and finished joints |
| Basis | Wall line, courses and openings | Piece dimensions, pattern and joints |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $62,120 | $57,390 |

## How the work meets

A fictional lobby has a brick wall with an opening for a stone surround. The brick crew hands over wall and opening measurements. The stonemason checks the set-out and finds that the top joint will miss the expected brick course. The site lead receives both records.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and apprenticeship as typical for both. Ask whether training covers brick, block, stone or several materials. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Brick work may follow wall sections. Stone work may depend on piece delivery, trial layout and pattern review. Ask who accepts interface dimensions and sample work.

## Moving between roles

Moving to stone, bring layout and joint skills and learn stone selection, fit and setting. Moving to brick masonry, bring alignment skills and learn course and opening control.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a mock drawing and field notes: the opening and stone widths comply, but the top stone joint is 12 mm off the required sixth-course alignment. Spend ten minutes each on the masonry record, stone joint sketch and the change question for the site lead.
