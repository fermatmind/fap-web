# Craft Artist vs. Manufacturing Potter: Taking an Approved Cup Into Production

## What each role delivers

A craft artist develops an object’s shape, materials and finish. Manufacturing potters form, dry, glaze and fire wares. They may also develop or sell their own pieces. A commission can assign sample approval and batch production separately.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Craft artists | Potters, Manufacturing |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Approved cup sample, appearance and dimensions | Pilot pieces and dimensional and appearance checks |
| Basis | Shape, glaze appearance and intended use | Forming consistency, fired dimensions and process timing |

[O*NET 27-1012.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/27-1012.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 51-9195.05](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/51-9195.05#Tasks)

## How the work meets

A fictional studio receives an order for ceramic cups. The craft artist hands over an approved sample and identifies the rim profile and finish. The potter makes a pilot batch and checks dimensions and appearance after firing. The potter reports undersized rims, and the craft artist and client review the acceptable range before production continues.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and long-term on-the-job training as typical for craft artists. It includes manufacturing potters in the broader molders, shapers, and casters group (except metal and plastic), for which high school completion or equivalent and long-term on-the-job training are typical. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Craft development can take several sampling rounds. Pottery production follows drying and firing schedules and needs time for final inspection. Ask who authorizes a new pilot when measurements vary.

## Moving between roles

Craft artists can bring form and material knowledge to ceramic production and add process handoffs, batch records and measurement. Potters can bring clay and finish experience into craft and add object development and client revisions.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock record: the approved rim diameter is 80 millimeters, within two millimeters either way. Three pilot cups measure 77, 79 and 81. Spend ten minutes each on the sample specification, the pass or fail check and the next checks before producing the rest.
