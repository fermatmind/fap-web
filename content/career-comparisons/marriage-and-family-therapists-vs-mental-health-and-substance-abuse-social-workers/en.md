# Marriage and Family Therapist vs. Mental Health and Substance Abuse Social Worker

## Relationship treatment and access to services

Marriage and family therapists treat concerns in the context of relationships. Mental health and substance abuse social workers may connect clients with services and follow up on referrals. Licensed clinical social workers can also provide therapy, including family therapy, within their authorized scope.

## Compare the work products

| Work | Marriage and family therapist | Mental health and substance abuse social worker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First check | The interactions the family wants to change | Which services are available and what makes them hard to use |
| Deliverable | A treatment goal and plan for review | A referral and follow-up plan; clinical roles may also deliver treatment |

U.S. median annual wages in May 2025 were $66,940 for marriage and family therapists and $60,280 for mental health and substance abuse social workers. These national OEWS medians cover wage and salary jobs and exclude self-employment. ## A family faces arguments and missed appointments

In a fictional service program, a family reports arguments about caregiving. A client also misses appointments because of late shifts and a bus transfer. The therapist discusses a relationship goal with the family. The social worker checks appointment and transportation options. With consent and under the program’s confidentiality procedures, the roles share the scheduling details needed to coordinate follow-up. In this example, the social worker is assigned to service coordination and the authorized therapist provides relationship treatment. If a clinical social worker takes the treatment role in another program, confirm their scope and supervision before assigning the case.

## U.S. education and credentials

BLS describes a master’s degree and state licensure for marriage and family therapists. Clinical social work also requires a master’s degree, supervised clinical experience, and state licensure. For social work openings, check whether the role includes clinical practice, what supervision applies, and what credential the state requires. New York, for example, publishes separate family therapy requirements and distinguishes LMSW and LCSW scope. An LMSW may provide clinical social work under qualified supervision, while an LCSW may practice independently within that scope. [BLS family therapy entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/marriage-and-family-therapists.htm#tab-4) · [BLS social work entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/social-workers.htm#tab-4) · [New York family therapy requirements](https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/marriage-and-family-therapists/license-requirements) · [New York social work scopes](https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/social-work/faqs/what-difference-between-lmsw-and-lcsw)

## Compare workplaces and schedules

BLS lists mental health centers and private practice among family therapists’ workplaces. For social workers as a broad group, BLS describes office work, visits, and meetings with other service providers. Ask how the specific role divides time among appointments, visits, case records, and supervision. [BLS family therapy work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/marriage-and-family-therapists.htm#tab-3) · [BLS social work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/social-workers.htm#tab-3)

## Moving between the roles

A therapist can bring interviewing experience to social work training and build skills in case coordination and community services. A social worker can bring case documentation and collaboration into family therapy training, then build relationship-specific practice and meet the applicable credential requirements.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use two fictional notes. The family reports arguments about caregiving responsibilities. The client missed Tuesday’s appointment because of a late shift and a bus transfer. Spend ten minutes separating the stated issues, ten writing a question for each role, and ten drafting a scheduling note with only the information needed for a consented handoff.
