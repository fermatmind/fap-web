# Floor Layers vs. Tile and Stone Setters: Finishing Around a Column

## What each role delivers

In this example, floor layers fit and join sheet coverings around obstructions. Tile and stone setters plan patterns and check edge cuts and joints.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Floor Layers, Except Carpet, Wood, and Hard Tiles | Tile and Stone Setters |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Sheet flooring fit and seams | Tile layout and edge check |
| Basis | Substrate, obstructions and sheet edge | Centerline, edge width and joints |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $56,460 | $55,690 |

## How the work meets

A fictional retail column is offset from the drawing. The floor layer sees that a sheet seam now hits the corner. The tile setter finds a narrow border piece in the old layout. Both record the affected areas for review.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential for both occupations. It lists moderate-term on-the-job training for this floor-laying role and long-term on-the-job training for tile and stone setters. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Sheet flooring may follow substrate acceptance and room access. Tile work moves between layout, cuts and joint inspection. Ask who approves a field change.

## Moving between roles

From sheet flooring, bring substrate and edge skills and add tile layout and joints. From tile, use layout checks and learn sheet seams and profile fitting.
