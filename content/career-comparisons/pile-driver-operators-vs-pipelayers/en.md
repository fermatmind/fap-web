# Pile driver operators vs Pipelayers

## Follow the pile location or follow the pipe section

Pile driver operators run equipment that drives piles and report status by location. Pipelayers position and join drainage, sewer or water-main pipe and hand off progress by section. Choose pile driving if you prefer heavy equipment work. Choose pipelaying if you prefer tracking how a network comes together section by section.

## Compare what the next shift receives

| Compare | Pile driver operators | Pipelayers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting point | Pile location, equipment and shift assignment | Pipe section, drawing and connection list |
| Setting | Equipment position at a foundation work area | Utility or drainage construction area |
| Handoff | Pile status and equipment exceptions | Section status and connection checks |

[O*NET pile driving](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-2072.00) · [O*NET pipelaying](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-2151.00)

## Two crews hand off work near a bridge

Cuyahoga County’s Pleasant Valley Road Bridges page lists pile driving and drainage structure installation. At a fictional project with similar work, the pile driver operator reports the last pile location worked and flags a missing record. The pipelayer reports a storm drain connection still awaiting inspection. The project supervisor coordinates the work areas and verifies the records with the assigned reviewers. OSHA-required excavation inspections stay with the competent person. [County project](https://cuyahogacounty.gov/publicworks/pleasant-valley-road-bridges---city-of-independence-village-of-valley-view) · [OSHA excavation roles](https://www.osha.gov/etools/construction/trenching/competent-person)

## Check preparation and site authority

BLS lists a high school diploma or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for pile driver operators. For pipelayers it lists no formal educational credential and short-term on-the-job training as typical. Ask each employer what equipment or site training applies to your assignment, and who has authorization to inspect and release work. [BLS preparation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) · [OSHA pile driving standard](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.603) · [OSHA excavation roles](https://www.osha.gov/etools/construction/trenching/competent-person)

## Compare equipment schedules and time outside

BLS gives 2025 national median annual wages of $73,300 for pile driver operators and $49,000 for pipelayers, on an OEWS basis. Ask pile crews how repositioning and equipment downtime affect hours. Ask pipe crews about travel, outdoor work and weather delays. Compare similar local assignments, including hours and travel. [BLS wage table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Use one part of site experience to learn the other

A pile driver operator can bring location and shift records into drainage work and gain practice reading section drawings. A pipelayer considering pile driving can bring experience coordinating with equipment crews, then meet the training and authorization requirements for the machinery.

## Try a thirty-minute project handoff

Use two fictional records. Pile K4 is marked complete, but the review field is blank. Drainage section R2 has a missing inspection record at connection I3. Spend 12 minutes writing the equipment crew note and 13 minutes writing the pipe crew note. Spend five minutes identifying who checks each record and who can confirm the next work assignment.
