# Agricultural Inspector vs. Vegetation Pesticide Applicator

## Inspection and documentation of assigned work

Agricultural inspectors check commodities and related operations against applicable requirements. Vegetation pesticide handlers and applicators apply pesticides to vegetation as assigned and document the work. An inspection may use work records from an applicator’s team.

## Compare the work products

| Work | Agricultural inspector | Vegetation pesticide applicator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First check | Evidence against an applicable requirement | Work order alongside the original log |
| Deliverable | Inspection findings and requests for evidence | Assigned application work, a reviewed log, and any items to clarify |

May 2025 U.S. median hourly wages were $24.01 for agricultural inspectors and $22.28 for pesticide handlers, sprayers, and applicators, vegetation. These OEWS medians apply to covered wage and salary employment. The survey excludes self-employment and most agricultural employment. ## A nursery work order and log list different areas

A fictional nursery has a work order marked North and a log marked South. The applicator asks the crew lead to check the original records and keeps both entries with the follow-up note. The inspector identifies which finding still lacks evidence and follows the assigned review process.

## U.S. training and authorization

O*NET places agricultural inspectors in Job Zone 1–2. Check the hiring agency or employer’s training and qualifications for the kind of inspection involved. BLS describes on-the-job training in grounds maintenance. Check the state licensing agency for requirements that apply to the assigned work. Restricted-use pesticides are subject to certification rules. Where a noncertified applicator is permitted to do the work, federal direct-supervision requirements and applicable jurisdictional rules apply. [O*NET inspector preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/45-2011.00#JobZone) · [BLS grounds maintenance preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/building-and-grounds-cleaning/grounds-maintenance-workers.htm#tab-4) · [EPA certification standards](https://www.epa.gov/pesticide-worker-safety/federal-certification-standards-pesticide-applicators) [Federal direct-supervision rule](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-E/part-171/subpart-C/section-171.201)

## Compare the settings

An inspector may work at inspection facilities or visit a site and document findings. BLS describes mostly outdoor work and some seasonal schedules for the broader grounds maintenance group. Ask how the specific job splits time between field visits, preparation, and records. [O*NET inspection context](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/45-2011.00#WorkContext) · [BLS grounds maintenance setting](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/building-and-grounds-cleaning/grounds-maintenance-workers.htm#tab-3)

## Moving between the roles

Inspectors can bring documentation skills and learn the training and workplace requirements of the application job. Applicators can bring experience keeping work records and learn how to review evidence and write inspection findings.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use three fictional records. The work order says North, the log says South, and the shift note does not confirm the area. Spend ten minutes aligning the records, ten drafting a question for the crew lead, and ten writing an inspection note that identifies what evidence is missing. Work from records only.
