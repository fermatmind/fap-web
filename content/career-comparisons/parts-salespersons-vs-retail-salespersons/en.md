# Parts Salespersons vs. Retail Salespersons: Choosing a Replacement Part

## What each role delivers

Parts salespersons check part numbers and stock and arrange orders. Retail salespersons compare merchandise by shoppers’ needs and process purchases.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Parts Salespersons | Retail Salespersons |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Verified part selection and stock | Product comparison and sales record |
| Basis | Model, connection and catalog | Intended use, features and price |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $38,630 | $35,410 |

[O*NET 41-2022.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/41-2022.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 41-2031.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/41-2031.00#Tasks)

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 41-2022 and 41-2031](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional shopper wants a toolbox and a connector for older equipment. The retail salesperson compares toolboxes and refers the fit question with the equipment model. The parts salesperson finds two similar connectors with different interfaces and reports the catalog result and stock.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential for either occupation, with moderate-term on-the-job training for parts sales and short-term training for retail sales. Ask about catalog training and authority to handle returns. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Parts work may track back orders and supplier status. Retail may follow store traffic, merchandising and promotions. Ask how the counter routes a fit question.

## Moving between roles

Moving into parts adds model and catalog verification to customer communication. Moving into general retail adds product comparisons and merchandising to parts knowledge.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: two toolboxes hold 10 and 15 tools, and two similar connectors carry different model numbers. Spend ten minutes each comparing the boxes, listing fit checks and drafting a reply for each purchase.
