# Instructional Coordinators vs. Elementary Special Education Teachers: Which Materials Change?

## What each role delivers

Instructional coordinators review shared resources, observe implementation and organize training. Elementary special education teachers adapt academic instruction and track student progress.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Instructional Coordinators | Special Education Teachers, Elementary School |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Shared materials review and teacher support | Adapted instruction and progress notes |
| Basis | Implementation evidence across classrooms | Learning and individual support goals |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $77,440 | $65,120 |

## How the work meets

Several fictional classrooms report dense reading directions. The coordinator reviews wording and layout with teachers. The special education teacher adapts presentation under an existing support plan and records progress for the team.

## How to enter

BLS lists a master’s degree and five or more years of related experience as typical for instructional coordinators. Its combined kindergarten and elementary special education row lists a bachelor’s degree. Verify the state credential and assignment. California’s Education Specialist authorization varies by specialty and grade range. [California CTC](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/cl-808ca/) [BLS occupation rows](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Coordinators follow materials and training calendars. Special education teachers follow students’ schedules and team meetings. Ask how individual records are protected when staff discuss shared resources.

## Moving between roles

Moving into special education requires the relevant teaching preparation and credential. Moving into coordination, bring material adaptation experience and develop cross-class implementation review and staff training.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional reading card. Three classes found the directions unclear, and a separate note indicates one student has an existing support plan. Spend ten minutes each on a common materials note, a sample individual progress note, and appropriate recipients.
