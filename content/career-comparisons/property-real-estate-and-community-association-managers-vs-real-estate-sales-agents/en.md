# Property, Real Estate, and Community Association Managers vs Real Estate Sales Agents

## Run the property or move a deal forward

Property managers keep facilities, contracts, and resident services working. Sales agents help buyers and sellers coordinate showings, offers, and transaction deadlines. Choose whether you would rather own a repair and follow it to completion or keep a sale moving as decisions and documents come in.

## Compare the work products

| Work | Property, real estate, and community association managers | Real estate sales agents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focus | Ongoing operation of a site or association | Client and transaction milestones |
| Typical records | Work orders, budget, and contractor completion | Showing plan, offer updates, and document tracker |
| Who approves | The manager acts within the owner's or board's authorization | The client decides offer terms; the agent communicates and tracks them |

[O*NET management tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/11-9141.00) · [O*NET sales agent tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/41-9022.00)

## A showing needs an update from the association

TREC's resale certificate for property with mandatory association membership covers assessments and other association information. In a fictional Texas resale, a buyer asks about a common-area repair and an association assessment. The manager checks the work order and sends the approved association information through the designated channel. The agent records what was received, confirms the revised showing route, and flags any outstanding document for the broker and client. The buyer decides whether to change the offer. [TREC resale certificate](https://www.trec.texas.gov/forms/subdivision-information-including-resale-certificate-property-subject-mandatory-membership)

## Check Texas licensing against the duties

BLS describes high school or equivalent as typical entry education for both occupations and related experience as typical for managers. TREC says ordinary repair coordination generally does not require a Texas real estate license. Check the rules and exceptions if a management job includes paid showing or leasing. For a Texas sales agent, TREC's standard route includes 180 qualifying classroom hours and a licensing exam. An inactive license requires broker sponsorship to become active before brokerage practice. [BLS occupation rows](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) · [TREC management guidance](https://www.trec.texas.gov/article/can-you-use-unlicensed-individuals-help-your-texas-real-estate-transactions-it-depends) · [TREC sales agent application](https://www.trec.texas.gov/become-licensed/sales-agent)

## Compare the schedules and how pay is earned

BLS notes that managers may respond to off-duty emergencies and attend evening meetings. Agents often schedule around client availability and may earn commissions. Ask who covers on-call issues, or how the brokerage splits commissions and handles expenses. May 2025 U.S. median annual employee wages were $69,990 for property, real estate, and community association managers and $52,830 for real estate sales agents. OEWS excludes self-employed earnings. [BLS manager schedules](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/management/property-real-estate-and-community-association-managers.htm) · [BLS agent schedules and pay](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/Sales/real-estate-brokers-and-sales-agents.htm) · [BLS comparable wage rows](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Try a 30-minute showing handoff

Use a fictional Texas address. The west entrance is closed from 9:30 to 11:00 a.m., the association manager confirms the east entrance is open, and the seller can also meet at 11:30. The buyer's 10:00 showing is on the calendar. Spend 12 minutes writing the manager's access update. Spend 13 minutes offering the two times and recording the buyer's reply as pending. Use five minutes to verify that the message and the tracker agree on the entrance, time options, and what remains unconfirmed.
