# Dancers vs. Music Directors and Composers: When the Live Count-In Changes

## What each role delivers

Dancers rehearse and perform movement in time with music and partners. Music directors and composers may write, interpret or rehearse music. The music director confirms the cue with the ensemble here.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Dancers | Music Directors and Composers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Performance on cue and rehearsal feedback | Score, tempo and ensemble cues |
| Basis | Movement timing, audible pickup and partner coordination | Phrasing, conducting gestures and ensemble timing |

## How the work meets

At the first rehearsal with the band for a fictional show, the practice track gives four pickup counts and the band gives two. The dancers identify where their first entrance went wrong. The music director confirms a count-in and cue with the band, and the dancers check both groups’ entrances in a run-through.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and long-term on-the-job training as typical for dancers. It lists a bachelor’s degree and less than five years of related experience for music directors and composers. Check the audition requirements or whether the job emphasizes conducting, composition or rehearsal direction. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Dancers may alternate between training, stage rehearsals and shows. Music work may involve score revisions, rehearsals and performances. Ask who distributes the final cue and whether the cast can hear it onstage.

## Moving between roles

Dancers can bring timing and stage collaboration to music work and build score reading and conducting or composition skills. Music workers can bring rhythmic skills to dance and develop movement technique, conditioning and ensemble practice.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock cues: the recording gives four pickup counts, the band gives two, and the second group cannot hear the drums. Spend ten minutes each writing dancer feedback, a cue option and how to verify both entrances in a run-through.
