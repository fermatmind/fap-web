# Vessel Captain vs. Transportation Inspector: Who Updates the Ship After a Cargo Finding?

## What each role delivers

Captains, mates and pilots contribute to command, navigation or watchkeeping according to assignment. Transportation inspectors examine equipment or goods and document findings. This example uses a marine cargo inspection within the broader inspector occupation.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Captains, Mates, and Pilots of Water Vessels | Transportation Inspectors |
|---|---|---|
| Input | Vessel condition, voyage plans and external reports | Assignment scope, applicable documents and cargo condition |
| Output | Bridge log and verified readiness status | Inspection finding, supporting evidence and follow-up |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $92,460 | $92,100 |

## How the work meets

During loading on a fictional cargo ship, the transportation inspector finds one securing record with missing site evidence. The inspector identifies the compartment, missing item and follow-up contact. The mate logs it as open and informs the captain. Loading staff submit the missing record and the inspector reviews it within the assignment. The bridge then updates that item and checks the other readiness items. If a pilot has been booked, the bridge communicates the revised timing.

## How to enter

BLS lists a postsecondary nondegree award as typical entry education for the combined captains, mates and pilots group, and high school completion or equivalent with moderate-term on-the-job training for the broader transportation inspector group. For inspection, ask which transport mode, requirements and authority the assignment covers. ## Work conditions

Bridge work follows watches and vessel movements. Cargo inspections can depend on loading schedules and site access. Ask how unresolved findings are carried across a shift change.

## Moving between roles

Vessel experience gives inspectors context for ship records and cargo operations. Add inspection documentation and the standards of the assignment. Moving toward the bridge, bring evidence review skills and check the service, training and credential required for the intended assignment.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use mock entries: an inspection finds missing evidence, the new evidence has arrived but has not been reviewed, and the bridge expects an afternoon departure. Spend ten minutes writing the inspection update, ten drafting the bridge handoff and ten marking which statuses need further confirmation. Work from the entries alone.
