# Electrical Engineers vs. Validation Engineers: Testing Configuration A and Releasing Configuration B

## What each role delivers

Electrical engineers design and verify power systems. Validation engineers set criteria and evaluate whether the evidence covers the required configurations.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electrical Engineers | Validation Engineers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Power design and engineering retest | Validation plan and disposition |
| Basis | Load conditions, drawing revision and retest | Predefined criteria, configuration and coverage |

## How the work meets

A fictional sorting unit is being prepared for a second shift. The electrical engineer retests power on configuration A. The validation engineer finds that approved coverage also calls for B and keeps that record open.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for electrical engineers. For validation engineering, show how you handled coverage and deviations and check industry-specific documentation responsibilities. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Electrical work may shift between drawings, equipment and testing. Validation work follows plans, schedules and result review. Ask who owns the configuration list and release decision.

## Moving between roles

Moving to validation, bring specification skills and add coverage and deviation handling. Moving to electrical engineering, bring test records and add design and load calculations.

## Try it in 30 minutes

The fictional plan requires records for A and B. A passes, B has no record and drawing C is approved. Spend ten minutes each on the design retest, validation coverage and evidence and ownership needed to close the gap.
