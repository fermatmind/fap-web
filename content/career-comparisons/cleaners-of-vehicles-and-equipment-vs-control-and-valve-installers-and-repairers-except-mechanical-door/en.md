# Equipment Cleaner vs. Control and Valve Repairer: What Does a Wet Spot Tell You?

## What each role delivers

Equipment cleaners prepare assigned surfaces and report visible damage or unusual conditions. Control and valve installers and repairers inspect, service and test valves or control devices.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Cleaners of Vehicles and Equipment | Control and Valve Installers and Repairers, Except Mechanical Door |
|---|---|---|
| Work object | Assigned surfaces and visible condition | Valves, controls and their functional checks |
| Handoff | Cleaning completion and exception note | Maintenance findings and test record |

## How the work meets

At a fictional rental depot, a water supply unit returns with a wet spot near a regulator. The cleaner completes the assigned exterior work and passes the equipment ID, location and time of the observation to the repairer. The repairer checks the service history and test results and records whether there is a valve fault. The shift lead tracks cleaning and repair on separate lines of the return record.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and short-term on-the-job training for cleaners. It lists high school completion or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for this repair group. Ask which devices, training and authorizations the actual role covers. BLS lists no related work experience as typical for the repair occupation. [BLS Table 1.2](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Cleaning can follow the return schedule. Repairs can wait on test time and parts. Ask how a unit is marked when cleaning is complete and a repair check remains open.

## Moving between roles

Cleaner experience in condition reports can transfer to repair, where reading equipment documentation and interpreting test evidence need additional training. Repairers moving into cleaning can bring equipment recognition and learn the assigned cleaning scope and handoff.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use mock records: cleaning complete, a wet spot near the valve and a blank maintenance entry. Spend ten minutes on the condition report, ten on the repair questions and ten on the current status and its owner. Use the records only.
