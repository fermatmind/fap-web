# Concierge vs. Restaurant Host: From Dinner Reservation to Seating

## What each role delivers

A concierge finds dining options and confirms reservations for guests. A restaurant host greets parties and assigns suitable tables based on arrivals, table availability and server rotation.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Concierge | Restaurant Host |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Reservation with time and guest needs | Waitlist entry or table assignment |
| Basis | Party size, timing and restaurant confirmation | Table capacity, current availability and server sections |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $38,950 | $31,200 |

## How the work meets

In a fictional hotel, four guests want dinner before a show, and one asks for a step-free route. The concierge finds a restaurant that can take the booking, relays the access request and records its reply. At arrival, the host checks the open tables, access route and server sections, then tells the party whether they can be seated in time.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training as typical for concierges. For restaurant hosts, it lists no formal educational credential and short-term on-the-job training. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Concierge requests can extend from dining to transportation. Hosts may face concentrated meal rushes and track table turnover. Ask how waiting times are updated when the dining room is full.

## Moving between roles

Reservation communication transfers from concierge work to hosting; learn seating rotation and waitlist management. A host moving into concierge work can build on guest reception by learning how to verify local services and bookings.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock table sheet: one four-top is upstairs; a step-free four-top is expected to open in 15 minutes; the party can wait 10. Spend ten minutes each on the booking note, a seating decision and an update with the options you can confirm.
