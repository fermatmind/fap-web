# Penetration Testers vs. Validation Engineers: When an Assessment Report Is One Piece of Evidence

## What each role delivers

Penetration testers perform authorized security assessments and report findings. Validation engineers define or execute plans and interpret results against predefined criteria.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Penetration Testers | Validation Engineers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Authorized assessment report and recommendations | Validation plan, coverage and results |
| Basis | Scope, evidence references and ownership | Predefined criteria, revisions and retest records |

## How the work meets

A fictional team receives a completed assessment summary identifying version R1 and an evidence reference. Its validation plan also requires acceptance evidence for R2. The tester clarifies the report coverage. The validation engineer marks R2 open.

## How to enter

For assessment work, show fictional report documentation and ask about the employer’s authorization process. For validation, show a plan, criteria, coverage and deviation records. ## Work conditions

Assessment work can follow approved windows and reporting deadlines. Validation follows versions, coverage and reviews. Ask who approves scope and accepts evidence.

## Moving between roles

Moving to validation, add planning and coverage management to reporting skills. Moving toward assessment, build on review and scope tracking and add security fundamentals, authorization discipline and report writing.

## Try it in 30 minutes
