# Search Marketing Strategists vs. Writers and Authors: Who Owns the Repair Guide?

## The day-to-day choice

A search marketing strategist studies what people look for, checks where existing pages fall short, and decides what to track after publication. A writer researches the subject, organizes the material, and revises the copy through review. An SEO copywriter may do some of both.

## What each role delivers

| Compare | Search marketing strategist | Writer or author |
|---|---|---|
| Inputs | Search queries, existing pages, and traffic data | A brief, interviews, and original documents |
| Deliverable | Page priorities, a content brief, and a performance review | A sourced draft and a revised version ready for review |

## How the handoff works

Suppose an appliance brand sees searches asking whether an in-warranty washer repair includes a service-call fee. The strategist checks the existing warranty page, puts the fee question in the brief, and plans to track clicks from related searches. The writer reads the warranty terms for the applicable models, confirms fee conditions with the service team, and flags unresolved exceptions in the draft. When clicks change, the strategist checks impressions and any simultaneous page changes before deciding what to revise.

For a strategy portfolio, show how a sample query set led you to prioritize a page and specify a follow-up metric. For writing, include a sourced piece and explain one substantive revision. ## Working conditions

Strategy work moves between reports, pages, and team discussions. You may need to wait for enough data to evaluate a change. Writing calls for uninterrupted research and revision time, with some deadlines dependent on a source or editor getting back to you.

## Changing roles

Moving into writing, bring your grasp of audience questions and practice verifying claims and revising full drafts. Moving into strategy, bring your editorial judgment and learn how to interpret impressions, clicks, and conversions when you assess a page change.

## Try both in 30 minutes

Use three sample searches about warranty length, service-call fees, and booking a repair. The fictional terms waive the service-call fee for covered repairs in the city service area. Fees outside that area still need confirmation. Spend 10 minutes deciding where each answer belongs, 10 drafting the fee explanation, and 10 recording the open question and one metric to check after publication.
