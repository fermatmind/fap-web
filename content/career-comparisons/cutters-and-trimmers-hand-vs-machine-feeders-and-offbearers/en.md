# Hand Cutters and Trimmers vs. Machine Feeders and Offbearers: Routing Rework

## What each role delivers

Hand cutters and trimmers shape materials to job orders and identify defects. Machine feeders and offbearers move and tag materials, track counts and may check visible defects.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Cutters and Trimmers, Hand | Machine Feeders and Offbearers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Trimmed pieces and defect record | Supply, removal and routing record |
| Basis | Dimensions, edges and lot | Counts, tags and staging location |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $38,020 | $41,220 |

## How the work meets

Several fictional pads leave a machine with excess edge material. The offbearer records the lot and sends the rework bin to the designated station. The trimmer checks the job order and separates removable excess from a cut that already breaches the finished edge.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and short-term on-the-job training for both. Ask whether the job involves template checks, material routing or both. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Trimming may involve repeated fine hand work. Feeding and offbearing may follow machine pace and staging needs. Check handling, repetition and shift expectations.

## Moving between roles

Moving to material flow, bring defect recognition and add routing records. Moving to trimming, bring identification and inspection skills and add template reading and finishing standards.

## Try it in 30 minutes

A mock lot has 12 pads: three with excess edge material and one with a notch. The job permits removing excess but rejects notches. Spend ten minutes each on routing, disposition and recording the recheck. Use paper only.
