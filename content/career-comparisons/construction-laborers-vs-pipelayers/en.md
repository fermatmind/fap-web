# Construction Laborers vs. Pipelayers: Who Owns the Drainage Run?

## What each role delivers

Construction laborers prepare the area and support the site crew. Pipelayers lay out and join sections of drainage or sewer pipe, check grade against requirements, and hand over the completed run and related records.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Construction Laborers | Pipelayers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Staging and work-area handoff | Installed pipe, joint and grade records |
| Focus | Materials and shared access | Route, elevation and connections |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $47,120 | $49,000 |

## How the work meets

On a fictional drainage project, the laborer records missing fittings for Segment 2. The pipelayer reports that Segment 1’s grade sheet references a different drawing revision. The first issue goes to the site lead for materials coordination and the second to the designated drawing reviewer before the segment is handed off.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and short-term on-the-job training as typical for both occupations. Ask who teaches route and grade checks on the pipe crew and how the employer assigns supervised work. [BLS rows 47-2061 and 47-2151](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

General crews move between site logistics and preparation. Pipelaying follows planned segments and can depend on checks at joints and before handoff. Ask who reviews grade records and when the next crew receives each section.

## Moving between roles

To move into pipelaying, bring staging and material tracking experience, then learn to interpret a pipe plan and keep measurement records. To move into general site work, reuse segment planning and learn to coordinate several crews’ access and materials.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use two fictional pipe segments. S1 has a grade sheet for Revision B and a plan marked Revision C. S2 has matching documents but missing fittings. Spend ten minutes sorting the issues, ten writing two handoff notes, and ten identifying the decision owner and what confirmation would close each item.
