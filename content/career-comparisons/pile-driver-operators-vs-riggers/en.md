# Pile Driver Operator vs. Rigger: When the Pile Tag Does Not Match

## What each role delivers

A pile driver operator checks and runs equipment that installs foundation piles. A rigger checks and prepares loads for movement and coordinates with workers moving them.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Pile Driver Operators | Riggers |
|---|---|---|
| Work object | Pile-driving equipment and foundation location | Load, rigging and movement assignment |
| Handoff | Pile location, identification and installation status | Load identity, rigging check and exceptions |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $73,300 | $62,640 |

## How the work meets

On a fictional foundation job, a rigger sees that a pile tag differs from the lift paperwork and reports both IDs to the site coordinator. The pile driver operator checks the installation order for that location and records the pile identity as unresolved. The coordinator verifies the ID and sends the confirmed record to both stations. Each worker documents the update against the applicable job.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training as typical for both. Ask the employer which equipment, assignments and site rules determine required training and authorization. [BLS Table 1.2](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) In U.S. construction, OSHA generally applies Subpart CC to dedicated pile drivers. For covered equipment, the employer must ensure operator training, certification or licensing, and evaluation, subject to the rule’s exceptions and supervised trainee provisions. [OSHA dedicated pile drivers](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.1439) · [OSHA operator qualifications](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.1427) For covered crane or derrick equipment assembly or disassembly, OSHA requires the rigging work to be done by a qualified rigger. Check the requirements for the task and equipment you will be assigned. [OSHA assembly and disassembly](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.1404)

## Work conditions

Pile work may wait for materials, prepared access or inspections. Riggers can move between jobs or work zones. Ask who records exceptions and makes sure the next crew receives them.

## Moving between roles

Both roles benefit from accurate IDs and clear handoffs. Moving toward rigging, learn assigned load checks and communication. Moving toward pile work, learn location records, construction paperwork and the equipment training the site requires.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use mock documents: the location needs pile A, the tag says B and rigging inspection passed. Spend ten minutes marking what each entry establishes, ten writing the two handoffs and ten listing the records needed before work resumes. Use documents only.
