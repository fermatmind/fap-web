# Occupational Therapy Assistants vs. Physical Therapists: Who Documents What Before a Return to School?

## What each role delivers

An occupational therapy assistant carries out an occupational therapy plan under supervision and documents activity performance. A physical therapist evaluates movement and physical function and develops and reviews a physical therapy plan.

## The same return-to-school goal

Imagine an outpatient student preparing to return to school. The assistant follows the occupational therapy plan, records what the student packs with a checklist, and reports to the supervising occupational therapist. The physical therapist evaluates what assistance the student needs along a practice route to a classroom. At an authorized team meeting, the two therapists compare findings, decide what to reassess in their respective plans, and the occupational therapist relays any changes to the assistant.

## Getting started in the U.S.

Occupational therapy assistants typically need an associate degree from an accredited program and usually need a state-issued license. Physical therapists entering the occupation need a Doctor of Physical Therapy degree, and all states require licensure. Check the relevant state board for role-specific requirements. [BLS assistant entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/occupational-therapy-assistants-and-aides.htm#tab-4) · [BLS physical therapist entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/physical-therapists.htm#tab-4)

## Working conditions

Assistants may document observations between sessions. Physical therapists need time and space for assessment and follow-up. Ask who receives notes from each discipline and when the team reviews them.

## Moving between roles

Assistants can carry clinical observation and documentation into physical therapy education and develop mobility assessment skills. Physical therapists considering assistant work should check the separate credentials and supervision requirements.

## Try both in 30 minutes

Use fictional notes. With a checklist the student packs all three items, and without it they miss one. They reach the practice classroom door with one staff member guarding nearby. The team meets on Friday. Spend 10 minutes writing the assistant's note to the supervisor, 10 distinguishing what the mobility observation shows from what needs reassessment, and 10 assigning handoff items for Friday.
