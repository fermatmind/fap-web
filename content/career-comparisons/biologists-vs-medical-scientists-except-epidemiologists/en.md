# Biologist vs. Medical Scientist, Except Epidemiologist

## Biological processes and human health questions

A biologist can study how living systems work and evaluate what the observations support. A medical scientist can study human disease or health and assess how findings relate to a health outcome or population. Their work can overlap in basic research.

## Compare the evidence each role delivers

| Work | Biologist | Medical scientist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First check | What the measure captures and which observations are comparable | Which population and health outcomes the study covers |
| Deliverable | A biological interpretation and open research questions | A summary of the health evidence and where it applies |

## The team reviews two parts of the same project

A fictional team asks whether a measure from a model could inform later health research. The biologist gives the medical scientist a note defining the measure and showing that the model report has no comparison group. The medical scientist checks the available health surveys, which have baseline data only, and writes a research memo identifying the measure as a lead and the evidence needed to evaluate a health outcome.

## U.S. training and early experience

O*NET puts biologists in Job Zone Five, which often includes graduate preparation. A supervised research project can build experience defining measures and interpreting results. BLS describes a Ph.D. or medical degree as typical for medical scientists, with some positions open to candidates with a master’s degree and experience. A supervised literature review or research assistantship can develop skills in evaluating study designs and outcomes. [O*NET biology preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-1029.04#JobZone) · [BLS medical science preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/medical-scientists.htm#tab-4) BLS says a license generally is unnecessary for research alone. Those who practice medicine must hold the license that applies to their role as a physician or other healthcare practitioner. Ask whether the job includes practicing medicine and verify applicable requirements with the relevant state licensing board.

## Compare settings and responsibilities

Biology work may involve indoor analysis or outdoor observation. BLS says medical scientists typically work in offices and laboratories. Ask how much time goes to research design, data review, or lab work, and how the team manages access to research records. [O*NET biology work context](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-1029.04#WorkContext) · [BLS medical science work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/medical-scientists.htm#tab-3)

## Moving between the roles

A biologist can bring measurement design and evidence review into medical research, then develop skills in health outcomes, research ethics, and interpreting evidence for a defined population. A medical scientist can bring study interpretation skills into broader biology, then build knowledge of the systems and measurements used in the new field.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use two fictional summaries. A reports a model measure rising from 6 to 8 and gives no comparison group. B reports 20 baseline health surveys and no follow-up. Spend ten minutes listing observations, ten writing what each record supports, and ten identifying the additional comparison information needed to discuss the cause of the change or a health outcome.
