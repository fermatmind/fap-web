# Loan Interviewers and Clerks vs. Loan Officers: A Change in the Loan Purpose

## What each role delivers

Loan interviewers and clerks collect and verify records and route the application. Loan officers explain terms and handle decisions within their limits or refer them for approval.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Loan Interviewers and Clerks | Loan Officers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Verified application version and document log | Loan terms and approval status |
| Basis | Applicant confirmation, attachments and revision | Loan products, terms and approval authority |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $50,020 | $76,690 |

## How the work meets

A fictional applicant originally sought equipment financing and now wants to use part of it for inventory. The clerk records the applicant’s confirmed change and attachments and retains the previous version. The officer checks whether both uses fit the product, identifies added documentation and approval needs and sends the verified status back for the file.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and short-term on-the-job training for loan interviewers and clerks. It lists a bachelor’s degree, less than five years of related experience and moderate-term training for loan officers. For U.S. residential mortgage work, check the actual activities and employer for federal registration or state licensing. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [CFPB Regulation G](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1007/103/) [CFPB Regulation H](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1008/103/)

## Work conditions

Intake may follow application, document and closing deadlines. Loan officers may handle consultations and reviews. Ask who stops work on an outdated version.

## Moving between roles

Moving into loan officer work adds terms comparison and applicable authority to file and customer skills. Moving into intake adds indexing, document checks and record updates to product knowledge.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock file: equipment was the original use. The applicant confirms adding inventory purchases, but prior approval only covers equipment. Spend ten minutes each recording the revision, listing items for renewed review and drafting a factual status reply.
