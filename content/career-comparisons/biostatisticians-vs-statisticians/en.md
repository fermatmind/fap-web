# Biostatistician vs. Statistician: Study Context and Method Checks

## What each role delivers

Biostatistics is a specialty within statistics that often works with life science or clinical teams on study comparisons. Statisticians can do the same work. A position may focus on one study or on methods used across projects.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Biostatisticians | Statisticians |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Study outcome, target estimate and analysis documentation | Method checks, estimates and sensitivity report |
| Basis | Study question, collection, follow-up and missingness | Target, assumptions, error and scope |

## How the work meets

A fictional walking program study compares activity minutes recorded at follow-up. The biostatistician agrees on measurement times with the team and distinguishes missing records from zero minutes. A statistician tests the summary on teaching data with complete records and with missing data concentrated in one group. The report records the validation conditions separately from study findings.

## How to enter

BLS lists a master’s degree as typical for statisticians. Applicants for either role can show reproducible inference; for biostatistics, also show how you defined outcomes and aligned analysis with a study protocol. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Study work follows protocols, follow-up and data delivery. Statistical method work can cover several projects and deadlines. Ask who checks whether a method transfers to a new application.

## Moving between roles

Biostatisticians can bring inference and collaboration to other statistics roles and learn the new domain and decision measures. Statisticians can bring estimation to biostatistics and add study design, outcomes and follow-up practices.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock data: group A contains one recorded zero, while group B lacks three records from the same visit. Spend ten minutes each on the study definition, checks for missing data, and what belongs in the two reports.
