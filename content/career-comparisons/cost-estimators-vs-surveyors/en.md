# Cost Estimators vs. Surveyors: Who Updates Costs After a Grade Check?

## What each role delivers

Cost estimators use drawings and cost records to estimate labor, materials and time. Surveyors measure and verify locations, elevations or boundaries and document the results.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Cost Estimators | Surveyors |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Itemized estimate with assumptions | Surveyed points, elevations and report |
| Basis | Drawings, quantities and unit costs | Field observations, datum and records |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $78,740 | $75,440 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 13-1051 and 17-1022](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional site project will haul away excavated soil. A resurvey finds higher ground on the east side than shown in the old base map. The surveyor records the points and elevation datum. Once the revised design grades arrive, the estimator updates excavation and hauling costs for the affected area.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree for both occupations. It lists moderate-term on-the-job training for cost estimators and an internship or residency for surveyors. Surveyors need a license to certify legal documents or serve the public. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [BLS surveyor licensing](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/surveyors.htm)

## Work conditions

Estimating can follow bid deadlines and drawing revisions. Surveying can alternate between site measurements, calculations and review. Ask how staff identify the survey coverage and drawing version.

## Moving between roles

Moving into surveying, build field measurement, datum and review skills on top of drawing experience. Moving into estimating, add quantity takeoff and pricing to your understanding of elevations and coverage.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records show three eastern points higher than the old map, no new western observations and no updated design grade. Spend ten minutes each on a survey handoff, inputs still needed for the estimate and two cost items that could change.
