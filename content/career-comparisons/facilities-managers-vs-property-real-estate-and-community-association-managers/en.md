# Facilities managers vs. property, real estate, and community association managers

## Both may manage repairs

Facilities managers look after space, equipment and maintenance. Property, real estate, and community association managers may also oversee maintenance, alongside owner and occupant communication, property budgets and services. The split is clearest when a site needs both a technical handoff and a decision about service to occupants.

## Compare responsibilities

| Item | Facilities manager | Property, real estate, or community association manager |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deliverable | A documented repair scope, work order and readiness check | A service record, occupant notice and follow-up under the management agreement |
| Work setting to ask about | Facility office, site rounds and mechanical or service areas | Property office, building visits and meetings with occupants or owners |
| Typical U.S. entry education and related work | Bachelor's degree, less than five years of related experience | High school diploma or equivalent, less than five years of related experience |
| 2025 U.S. median annual wage | $106,660 | $69,990 |

Wages cover nonfarm wage and salary workers, excluding self-employment. ## A shared walkway has a leak

At a fictional residential property, the facilities manager coordinates isolation and repair of a leaking walkway and gives the property manager the affected area, alternate route and next inspection time. The property manager notifies residents, tracks questions and communicates service updates. The facilities manager verifies that the area can reopen, and the property manager updates residents and closes the service record.

## U.S. preparation and licensing

A facilities coordinator role can build experience with work orders and contractor oversight. Property operations work can build experience with service agreements and resident communication. For an opening involving regulated activity, check the state requirements for the actual duties. For example, Florida law requires licensure for people acting as community association managers within the scope of its statute. Ask the employer which duties you would perform and verify any applicable requirements with the state. [BLS preparation](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) · [Florida Statutes §468.432](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0468/Sections/0468.432.html)

## Where the hours go

Ask a facilities employer how inspections, response calls and scheduled maintenance are shared. Ask a property employer how resident meetings and complaint escalation are handled, and what spending needs the owner's or board's approval.

## Moving between the roles

A facilities manager can bring a redacted repair log and acceptance checklist. To move into property operations, add experience with service agreements, occupancy communication and expense tracking. A property manager can bring a redacted service record and contractor list, then build skills in asset inventories, preventative maintenance and verifying the contractor's work.

## A 30-minute tabletop exercise

Draw a fictional north walkway closed for a leak and an open south route. The north walkway has no reopening confirmation. Take ten minutes to mark the affected area and route, ten to draft a resident notice with an update time and ten to record the criteria and owner for reopening. Submit a work status note and a resident notice. Keep the closure in force until facilities confirms readiness.
