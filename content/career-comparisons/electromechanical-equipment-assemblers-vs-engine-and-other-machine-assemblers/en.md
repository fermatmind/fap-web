# Electromechanical Assembler vs. Machine Assembler: A Unit Passes Its Test but Does Not Fit

## What each role delivers

Electromechanical equipment assemblers build units that can include wiring, cables or tubing and test them against specifications. Machine assemblers align components and verify the assembled engine or machine. The example uses a plant where those tasks are assigned to separate stations.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electromechanical Equipment Assemblers | Engine and Other Machine Assemblers |
|---|---|---|
| Typical work object | Mechanical units with electrical connections | Components and assemblies in a machine or engine |
| Handoff evidence | Unit ID, interface revision and check results | Build configuration, fit and completed machine checks |

## How the work meets

At a fictional plant, a tested actuator unit reaches final assembly with mounting points that do not match the frame drawing. The machine assembler records the unit ID, frame revision and mismatch. The electromechanical assembler traces the build record, confirms that the unit follows an earlier interface revision and sends the findings to quality staff. Final assembly checks the fit of the replacement unit and records a separate machine-level test.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for machine assembly. It lists the same preparation for its broader electrical, electronic and electromechanical assembly group excluding coil winders, tapers and finishers. Ask the employer which drawings, component checks and station signoffs trainees learn. [BLS Table 1.2](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

The two work areas may rely on the same parts schedule. An interface revision can interrupt both. Ask who controls drawing updates and who handles a mismatch found late on a shift.

## Moving between roles

Moving to machine assembly, bring records of interface checks and learn build order and system-level verification. Moving into electromechanical assembly, bring fit and alignment experience and learn the unit drawings, electrical identification and inspection records.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use fictional tickets for a unit inspected to an earlier revision and a frame requiring a newer interface. Take ten minutes listing the mismatch, ten writing the handoff and ten separating the evidence needed for fit from the final machine check. Use paper records only.
