# Postsecondary Chemistry Teachers vs. Engineering Teachers: Assessing the Same Project

## What each role delivers

Postsecondary chemistry teachers teach chemical concepts and assess students’ interpretation of observations. Postsecondary engineering teachers teach engineering subjects and evaluate work such as design, analysis or lab assignments.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Chemistry Teachers, Postsecondary | Engineering Teachers, Postsecondary |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Chemistry coursework and analytical feedback | Engineering coursework and design evaluation |
| Basis | Mechanism, observations and uncertainty | Constraints, tradeoffs and verification |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $93,250 | $109,270 |

## How the work meets

In a fictional packaging case, the chemistry instructor asks which observation supports a structural explanation. The engineering instructor asks students to weigh cost, weight and durability and identify missing verification.

## How to enter

BLS lists a doctoral or professional degree as typical in both detailed occupation rows. Check each department’s discipline, teaching assignment and research expectations. [BLS occupation rows](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Both roles can teach, assess work, supervise projects and conduct research. Ask how laboratory, design and research responsibilities are assigned.

## Moving between roles

Moving to engineering teaching, carry over lab or materials examples and add engineering design and discipline preparation. Moving to chemistry, deepen the chemistry content and adapt your assessment to chemical reasoning.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional data sheet: material A weighs less, material B has more complete durability records, the structure diagrams differ, and no same-condition durability test is available. Take ten minutes to draft a chemistry evidence question, ten to draft a design decision and ten to distinguish the rubrics. Work from the provided records.
