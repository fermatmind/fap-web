# Photonics Technicians vs. Semiconductor Processing Technicians: The Missing Optical Reference

## What each role delivers

Photonics technicians can maintain optical equipment and record calibration and test data. Semiconductor processing technicians follow work orders and record process and lot inspections.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Photonics Technicians | Semiconductor Processing Technicians |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Optical measurement and calibration record | Process and lot inspection |
| Basis | Instrument, reference part and test conditions | Lot, work order and process steps |

[O*NET 17-3029.08](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-3029.08#Tasks) · [O*NET 51-9141.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/51-9141.00#Tasks)

O*NET maps 17-3029.08 to Engineering Technologists and Technicians, Except Drafters, All Other for wages. This broader series does not establish a separate role median. [O*NET occupational wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/17-3029.08)

## How the work meets

A fictional optical part reads differently in two tests. The photonics technician finds that the second test lacks a reference reading. The semiconductor technician confirms that both samples share a lot and work order. The team requests the missing measurement evidence.

## How to enter

For photonics, show instrument and test documentation. BLS lists high school or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for semiconductor processing technicians. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Photonics work may follow instruments and measurement schedules. Semiconductor processing may follow lots, process steps and shifts. Ask who reviews an exception.

## Moving between roles

Moving to processing, bring measurement discipline and add lot controls. Moving to photonics, bring inspection and traceability and add optical testing and calibration training.
