# Vehicle Cleaner vs. Dishwasher: What Comes Back Ready After a Catering Job?

## What each role delivers

Vehicle and equipment cleaners clean and check vehicles or equipment. Dishwashers clean tableware and kitchen items and return ready items to storage or service.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Cleaners of Vehicles and Equipment | Dishwashers |
|---|---|---|
| Work object | Vehicles, equipment and their surfaces | Tableware, utensils and kitchen work areas |
| Handoff | Vehicle condition and cleaning or damage record | Clean items returned and exceptions logged |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $35,830 | $34,810 |

## How the work meets

A fictional caterer gets back a delivery van and reusable containers. The vehicle cleaner records that the van was cleaned and tells the fleet lead about a loose divider. The dishwasher records how many containers are ready and sets aside a damaged one according to the kitchen's procedure. The shift lead uses separate updates to plan the next van and container assignments.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and short-term on-the-job training for both. Ask whether the vehicle assignment includes moving vehicles and what license and permission apply. For the dish station, check food safety training and local rules for the actual duties. ## Work conditions

Vehicle cleaning can follow fleet returns in a bay or outdoors. Dish work may follow meal periods and involve wet work areas. Ask who tracks repairs and unfinished items across shifts.

## Moving between roles

Bring an example of identifying an item, recording an exception and returning it to service. Moving into vehicle work, learn fleet checks and surfaces. Moving into dish work, learn site sanitation, storage and how clean items return to service.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use mock records: a cleaned van has a loose divider, six containers have been returned and one damaged container is awaiting review. Spend ten minutes writing each handoff and ten listing who can update each status. Work from the records only.
