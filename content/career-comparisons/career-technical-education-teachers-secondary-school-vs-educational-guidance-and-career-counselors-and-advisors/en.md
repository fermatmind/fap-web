# High-School CTE Teachers vs. School and Career Advisors: Planning After Graduation

## What each role delivers

High-school CTE teachers teach knowledge and skills for an occupational field and assess projects. Educational, guidance, and career counselors and advisors help students compare education and career options.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Career/Technical Education Teachers, Secondary School | Educational, Guidance, and Career Counselors and Advisors |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Project score and skill feedback | Program comparison and follow-up |
| Evidence | Student project | Prerequisites, schedule and goals |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $66,270 | $64,330 |

## How the work meets

A fictional student completes an equipment-explanation project and is interested in community college. The teacher provides a project score and one communication goal. The advisor checks two programs’ deadlines, prerequisites and transportation needs with the student.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree and less than five years of related experience as typical for secondary school CTE teachers. For educational, guidance, and career counselors and advisors, its occupation row lists a master’s degree as typical. California’s Designated Subjects CTE credential is one example of authorization for named vocational subjects through grade 12. Check the state and school assignment. [California CTC CTE authorization](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/cl-888/) [BLS occupation rows](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) California’s PPS School Counseling credential applies to the school counseling pathway shown here. Check the requirements for other advising settings separately. [California CTC School Counseling](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/cl-606c/)

## Work conditions

Teachers prepare projects, demonstrations and feedback. Advisors manage appointments and deadlines during application season. Ask how staff share the relevant classroom evidence and who records the student’s decision.

## Moving between roles

Teachers can bring assessment and occupational context into advising and add counseling preparation and confidentiality practices. Advisors moving into teaching need technical demonstration, grading and applicable credentials.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use fictional project feedback and two program cards. A has an early deadline and an unverified prerequisite. B meets on weekends, when the student works. Spend ten minutes on the teacher note, ten comparing options, and ten identifying open questions and their owners.
