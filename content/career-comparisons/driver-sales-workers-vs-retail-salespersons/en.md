# Driver/Sales Workers vs. Retail Salespersons: A Short Delivery at the Store

## What each role delivers

Driver/sales workers deliver on routes and may take orders, collect payments and record transactions under company rules. Retail salespersons identify shoppers’ needs, recommend goods and process purchases.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Driver/Sales Workers | Retail Salespersons |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Route delivery, order or payment record, and delivery receipt | In-store options and sales record |
| Basis | Truck stock, delivery window and receipt | Customer needs, store stock and purchase record |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $38,770 | $35,410 |

## How the work meets

A fictional store orders eight cases of drinks and receives seven. The driver records the quantity and hands the receipt to the receiving lead. Under this store’s policy, the lead posts seven received and follows up on the missing case. The salesperson quotes only the stock the store has confirmed for sale.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent for driver/sales workers and no formal educational credential for retail salespersons, with short-term on-the-job training for both. Check the driver’s license and vehicle requirements for the actual job. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [BLS driving requirements](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/transportation-and-material-moving/delivery-truck-drivers-and-driver-sales-workers.htm#tab-4)

## Work conditions

Route work may start early and depend on loading and delivery windows. Retail work may follow store traffic and shifts. Ask who receives a shortage report.

## Moving between roles

Moving into retail adds display and in-store selling to customer contact and inventory skills. Moving into route work adds the applicable driving credential, route work and delivery records.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: eight cases were ordered and seven received. Five are confirmed for sale. Two remain off the shelf and have not been cleared for sale. A shopper asks for six. Spend ten minutes each on the receipt, the receiving lead’s stock and shortage entry, and the shopper reply. Confirm five for now.
