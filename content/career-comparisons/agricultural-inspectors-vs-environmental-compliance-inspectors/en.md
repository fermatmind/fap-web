# Agricultural Inspector vs. Environmental Compliance Inspector: Two Inspections at One Packing Site

## What each role delivers

An agricultural inspector checks products and handling for applicable health, quality and safety requirements. An environmental compliance inspector compares site activity with environmental requirements, documents findings and follows up.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Agricultural inspectors | Environmental Compliance Inspectors |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Lot inspection and product-handling findings | Permit review, site findings and follow-up record |
| Basis | Product lot, transport and handling records | Permit dates, discharge records, complaints and site observations |

[O*NET 45-2011.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/45-2011.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 13-1041.01](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/13-1041.01#Tasks)

O*NET reports the second role’s wage data under the broader Compliance Officers group. [O*NET wage scope](https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/13-1041.01#WagesEmployment)

## How the work meets

At a fictional produce packing site, the agricultural inspector traces a lot through cold-chain handoffs and finds a receiving timestamp missing. An environmental inspector responding to a discharge complaint checks the current permit and operating records and finds that the discharge log does not identify an outlet. They share site and time information as authorized and record separate findings and follow-up requests.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree and moderate-term on-the-job training as typical for agricultural inspectors. For environmental compliance inspection, ask the hiring agency which permits and media the role covers, what field training it provides and who can issue findings. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Both roles may involve field visits and written records. Agricultural inspection can follow harvest and processing schedules. Environmental inspection can follow complaints and reinspection deadlines. Ask who issues the formal notice.

## Moving between roles

Agricultural inspectors bring field records and evidence review to environmental inspection and can add permit interpretation, monitoring records and enforcement procedures. Environmental inspectors bring compliance tracking and can learn product handling and the relevant health standards.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: a lot has a dispatch slip but no receiving time. A site log exists but uses an old discharge-point number. Spend ten minutes each checking the agricultural records, matching the log to the permit, and writing findings that state what each record actually supports and what remains to verify.
