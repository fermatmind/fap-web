# Endoscopy Technician vs. LPN or LVN

## Room readiness and patient care

Endoscopy technicians prepare rooms, check equipment and assist the procedure team within assigned duties. Licensed practical nurses and licensed vocational nurses provide nursing care and monitor and report changes within their authority.

## Compare what gets handed off

| Item | Endoscopy technician | LPN or LVN |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check | Whether the equipment and room can be handed over | Patient condition and assigned nursing requirements |
| Record | Room and equipment status and discrepancies | Observations, patient concerns and notifications |
| Report to | The designated equipment or procedure lead | The designated nursing or clinical lead |

[O*NET 31-9099.02](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/31-9099.02#Tasks) · [O*NET 29-2061.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-2061.00#Tasks)

## Two issues before one appointment

A fictional endoscopy center finds a missing equipment release entry. The technician reports the open item and verifies who checks it. The patient also tells the LPN or LVN they feel unwell. The nurse documents the concern and reports it according to the facility procedure. Authorized personnel address equipment readiness and the clinical decision through their distinct workflows.

## U.S. entry requirements

BLS describes a state-approved program, licensing exam and state license for LPNs and LVNs. The nursing scope depends on the applicable state and setting. For an endoscopy technician job, verify whether the facility expects procedure support, reprocessing, or both and what training it requires. For reprocessing, HSPA offers a Certified Endoscope Reprocessor credential with hands-on experience and an exam; check whether the employer or applicable rules require it. [BLS practical nursing entry guidance](https://www.bls.gov/OOH/healthcare/licensed-practical-and-licensed-vocational-nurses.htm#tab-4) · [HSPA CER eligibility](https://myhspa.org/certification/certification-overview/certified-endoscope-reprocessor-cer/)

## Ask what drives the shift

For endoscopy support, ask how rooms and reprocessing assignments rotate and who checks equipment status. For nursing, ask how care before and after procedures is assigned and who receives concerns about a patient's condition.

## Moving between roles

Technicians can bring equipment documentation and communication skills to nursing education, then complete approved training and qualify for licensure. Nurses can bring patient safety and handoff experience to endoscopy support, then verify equipment training and the facility's authorization process.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use two fictional notes, one reporting a missing equipment release entry and one reporting a patient concern. Spend ten minutes drafting an equipment verification request, ten on the nursing handoff, and ten naming the recipients and criteria for closing each item. Use records only.
