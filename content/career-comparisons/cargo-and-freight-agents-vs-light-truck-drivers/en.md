# Cargo and Freight Agents vs. Light Truck Drivers When the Receiving Door Changes

## Where responsibilities differ

Cargo and freight agents maintain shipment arrangements and communicate changes. Light truck drivers carry out the trip, check what they are delivering, and verify the receiving handoff on site.

## Compare the handoffs

| Handoff | Cargo and Freight Agents | Light Truck Drivers |
|---|---|---|
| Example | Confirmed receiving instructions and a carrier update | Arrival status, load record, and delivery exception |

U.S. occupational task sources · [O*NET 43-5011.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/43-5011.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 53-3033.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/53-3033.00#Tasks)

## Follow one delivery

In a fictional delivery, the east door has stopped receiving. The driver waits in the designated area, compares the notice with the shipping instructions, and reports that nothing has been unloaded. The freight agent confirms the replacement door and start time with the customer and sends the update to the carrier. Until confirmation, the driver follows the site's waiting process.

## How to prepare

O*NET places cargo and freight agents in Job Zone 2, a broad preparation category. Look for booking or shipment support that lets you practice carrier communication and checking shipment records. [O*NET Job Zone](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/43-5011.00#JobZone)

BLS describes a high school diploma or equivalent as typical for delivery truck work, with some exceptions. It also describes short on-the-job training and a state driver's license and clean record. Verify the license class for the exact vehicle and assignment with the state licensing agency. [BLS driver preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/transportation-and-material-moving/delivery-truck-drivers-and-driver-sales-workers.htm#tab-4)

## What shapes the workday

Freight work moves between calls, booking systems, and transport documents. Local delivery includes driving, site handoffs, and sometimes loading or unloading. Ask what a route requires and who helps with unusual receiving instructions.

## Moving between roles

From driving, use your delivery experience to learn booking and document updates. Moving into driving requires the applicable driver qualification and experience with vehicle, load, and site checks.

## Try it in 30 minutes

A fictional document says east door at 10. A notice says it is closed and no receiver is there. The customer confirms that west door opens at 10:20. Spend ten minutes writing the driver's waiting report, ten writing the revised shipping notice, and ten listing what to recheck before delivery.
