# Retail Salespersons vs. Wholesale Sales Representatives for Nontechnical Products: Replenishing a Sold-Out Item

## What each role delivers

Retail salespersons help shoppers select and purchase goods. Wholesale and manufacturing sales representatives for nontechnical products explain availability, prices and delivery terms to business customers.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Retail Salespersons | Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Except Technical and Scientific Products |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Store availability and unmet demand | Replenishment quote and delivery schedule |
| Basis | Shopper needs, available stock and substitutes | Order quantities, supply and pricing |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $35,410 | $72,080 |

## How the work meets

A fictional stationery store sells out of a notebook. The retail salesperson records the size and unmet demand. The buyer confirms the request and sends it to the supplier. The wholesale representative checks case sizes and delivery dates and sends a quote. Retail continues to offer in-stock alternatives while replenishment is pending.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and short-term on-the-job training for retail salespersons. For nontechnical wholesale sales representatives, it lists high school or equivalent and moderate-term training. Ask who can promise quantities or dates. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Retail may follow store traffic and shifts. Wholesale may follow account and delivery schedules. Ask how shortages turn into replenishment requests.

## Moving between roles

Moving into wholesale adds volume pricing, terms and account management to product knowledge. Moving into retail adds store workflow and live shopper service.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock brief: the store needs 12 notebooks, the supplier sells cases of 10 and Friday is tentative. Spend ten minutes each on the store request, quote conditions and a shopper update.
