# Vessel Captain vs. Dredge Operator: What Does the Bridge Need to Know About Active Work?

## What each role delivers

Captains, mates and pilots contribute to vessel command, navigation or watchkeeping according to their assignments. A dredge operator runs excavation equipment and reports the location and depth of the work. Both may exchange information about the same area.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Captains, Mates, and Pilots of Water Vessels | Dredge Operators |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | Vessel movement, navigation information and watch duties | Excavation limits, equipment status and depth records |
| Output | Navigation, watch or pilotage input within assigned authority | Work location, progress and exceptions |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $92,460 | $49,640 |

## How the work meets

In a fictional port, a vessel approaches a dredging project. The dredge operator reports the active segment, the time of the last reading and the work markers still in place to the vessel's watch. The watch passes the update through the designated channel. The approaching vessel's bridge checks the report against port notices and its own conditions. The captain asks the mate to log the segment that still needs confirmation. A pilot, if assigned, contributes local navigation information.

## How to enter

BLS lists a postsecondary nondegree award as typical entry education for the combined captains, mates and pilots group. It lists high school completion or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for dredge operators. Ask dredging employers whether the assignment includes vessel duties and which equipment training applies. Federal first-class pilotage has a separate, route-specific First Class Pilot endorsement. Maritime pilots may also need a state license. ## Work conditions

Bridge work follows vessel movements, watches and port schedules. Dredging follows project windows, equipment availability and the active work area. Ask which channel carries changes when both teams are on shift.

## Moving between roles

Watch handoffs and clear location reports can transfer. Moving into dredging, learn equipment-specific procedures and excavation records. Moving toward bridge work, verify the desired assignment's credential and training requirements and build experience in navigation records.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use mock cards for one excavation reading, a port restriction still in force and a work boundary awaiting confirmation. Spend ten minutes writing the dredge handoff, ten recording the bridge's questions and ten marking who can update each record. Work from the cards alone.
