# Brokerage Clerks vs. Securities, Commodities, and Financial Services Sales Agents: A Partially Filled Order

## What each role delivers

Brokerage clerks document securities trades and investigate posting issues. Securities, commodities, and financial services sales agents may handle client orders, quotes or execution within their assigned authority. This example concerns securities brokerage.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Brokerage Clerks | Securities, Commodities, and Financial Services Sales Agents |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Confirmation discrepancy and correction status | Confirmed customer instructions and communication log |
| Basis | Trade record, quantity and ledger | Customer instructions, product scope and authority |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $65,750 | $78,660 |

## How the work meets

A fictional order is for 100 shares. Both the execution record and the confirmation show 80 shares filled. The clerk logs that fill and checks the status of the remaining 20. Authorized securities staff explain the confirmed fill and identify which remaining order status still needs verification. If records for the same execution actually disagree, they route the exception for review. [FINRA trade lifecycle](https://syndication.finra.org/content/how-online-stock-trading-works-understanding-trade-lifecycle) [FINRA order types and remaining quantity](https://syndication.finra.org/content/understanding-order-types-can-save-time-and-money)

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent for brokerage clerks and a bachelor’s degree for sales agents, with moderate-term on-the-job training for both. Verify registration by activity with firm compliance. For a U.S. general securities representative role, FINRA requires the SIE and Series 7 exams, and eligibility for Series 7 requires firm association and sponsorship. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [FINRA Series 7](https://www.finra.org/registration-exams-ce/qualification-exams/series7)

## Work conditions

Clerical work may follow trade and posting deadlines. Sales work can follow market hours and customer contact. Ask who records instructions and approves error corrections.

## Moving between roles

Clerks can bring transaction records into client work and build product knowledge, communication and applicable credentials. Sales staff can bring order workflow to operations and add reconciliations and correction records.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records show an order for 100 shares and 80 filled in both the execution record and confirmation. The other 20 have no recorded status. Spend ten minutes each on an operations note, a factual client update and the condition for an error referral.
