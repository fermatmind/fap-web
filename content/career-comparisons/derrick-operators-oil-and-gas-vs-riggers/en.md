# Derrick Operators, Oil and Gas vs Riggers

## Rig equipment and the gear used to move loads

Derrick operators set up rig equipment and operate and monitor drilling-fluid pumps. Riggers set up, inspect, and maintain rigging used to move or support loads. At a drilling site these can be separate assignments, each with its own work record.

## One pump replacement, two handoffs

At a fictional drill site, a crew plans to replace circulation equipment. The derrick operator confirms the equipment ID and sends its status and handoff window to the lift coordinator. The rigger matches the intended load to the task order and sends the rigging inspection record to the lift lead. The drilling lead confirms equipment status and the lift lead reviews the rigging evidence. The coordinator records the two statuses separately.

## What to check before taking a job

Ask derrick employers which rig duties new hires take on, what related experience counts, and how supervision works. For rigging, ask which loads and settings the role covers, how the employer trains workers, and which task-specific credentials or approvals apply.

## What changes the workweek

Derrick work can follow the drilling crew's rotation. Ask whether the site is residential or remote and what monitoring carries across shifts. Rigging is used in settings such as construction, manufacturing, and shipyards. Ask whether workers move between job sites and how the schedule changes when a lift is delayed.

## Supervised starting points

Moving into rigging, bring a fictional equipment traceability record and start with supervised load identification and inspection documentation. Learn the lifting assignment and site requirements. Moving toward derrick work, bring a fictional rigging inspection record and start with supervised circulation log checks. Learn rig crew responsibilities and assigned equipment.

## Try a 30-minute desk exercise

Use a different fictional shift. P-40 identifies pump P4 for replacement. L-40 has a signed rigging check for load P5. U-40 requires the load ID to match the equipment handoff before lift review. Spend ten minutes labeling what each record establishes, ten minutes writing the equipment handoff and rigging evidence request, and ten minutes drafting the coordinator's open items with both review owners. Use only the records.
