# Electrical Engineer vs. Materials Scientist: Is a Passing Insulation Sample Enough?

## What each role delivers

Electrical engineers design equipment and systems and verify that they meet defined requirements. Materials scientists study structure and properties, test materials and assess them for particular uses.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electrical Engineers | Materials Scientists |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence | Equipment design, fit and electrical requirements | Material samples, production lots and test conditions |
| Output | Component specification, verification or change review | Material report, evaluated properties and limits of use |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $120,630 | $117,790 |

## How the work meets

A fictional team proposes replacing an insulating sheet in a device. The materials scientist sends a passing specimen report with thickness and test conditions. The electrical engineer checks the drawing and finds that the sheet is clamped in the assembly, whereas the specimen was flat and unmounted. The materials scientist confirms what the report covers and logs the missing conditions. The team makes a plan for component verification and keeps the substitution open.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor's degree as typical entry education for both. For electrical engineering, show a drawing and the evidence used to verify a requirement. For materials research, show a test report that explains specimen identity and application limits. Ask about laboratory authorization and design signoff for the actual role. [BLS Table 1.2](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) Some chemistry and materials research jobs may require a master’s degree or Ph.D. [BLS research education](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/chemists-and-materials-scientists.htm) BLS describes entry-level electrical engineering as generally not requiring a PE license. Check the state engineering board’s rules for professional sign-off or services to the public. [BLS electrical engineering entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm)

## Work conditions

Electrical design work may move between reviews, test sessions and deadlines. Materials research can depend on available specimens and test environments. Ask how a supplier material report reaches the team reviewing the assembly.

## Moving between roles

Electrical engineers can bring application requirements to materials research and learn characterization and property analysis. Materials scientists can bring insulation or thermal test knowledge and develop circuit, assembly and design verification skills.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use mock records for a flat specimen that passes, a drawing showing a clamped assembly and an empty component test entry. Spend ten minutes writing a material report note, ten drafting the electrical review and ten listing evidence still needed for the substitution. Use the records alone.
