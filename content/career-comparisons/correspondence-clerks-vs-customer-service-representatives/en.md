# Correspondence Clerk vs. Customer Service Representative: A Written Reply to a Billing Dispute

## What each role delivers

A correspondence clerk interprets incoming messages, assembles records and prepares written replies. A customer service representative clarifies inquiries, logs actions and follows up through service channels. Both roles may write to customers.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Correspondence clerks | Customer Service Representatives |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Draft letter, supporting records and dispatch log | Service case, verified facts and status update |
| Basis | Incoming letter, evidence, template and approval status | Customer description, bill, authority and progress |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $46,800 | $44,770 |

## How the work meets

A fictional customer calls about an apparent duplicate charge and asks for a letter. The representative checks the order and records the issue and open questions. The clerk verifies that one item is an authorization hold and the other has posted, drafts a reply based on the payment records and logs its dispatch after approval. The representative updates the case from the dispatch record.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent and short-term on-the-job training as typical for both occupations. Ask when a written reply needs approval. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Correspondence work can follow document readiness and response deadlines. Customer service can follow queue volume and callbacks. Ask how missing evidence is communicated.

## Moving between roles

Clerks bring writing and record retrieval to customer service and can develop live issue handling. Representatives bring intake and service rules to correspondence work and can add evidence citation and version tracking.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock file: two entries have the same amount. One is an authorization hold and the other has posted. The hold release time is unknown. Spend ten minutes each on case notes, facts for the letter and the status update after dispatch.
