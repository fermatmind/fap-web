# Clinical Data Managers vs. Database Administrators When Visit Data Is Missing

## Where responsibilities differ

Clinical data managers check whether study records meet the project's definitions and quality rules. Database administrators keep the database available and maintain its operation and access. When fields in existing records are blank, one investigates what the data should contain and the other investigates what the system received and served.

## Compare the handoffs

| Deliverable | Clinical Data Managers | Database Administrators |
|---|---|---|
| Example | Record queries and data quality findings | Import observations, service status, and recovery evidence |

## One project in practice

A fictional study expects six visit records. Five are in the system, and one of those has a blank field. The clinical data manager uses the study dictionary to decide whether the blank calls for a query. The DBA compares the source delivery and import record to see why the sixth record is absent. They send both findings to the designated study staff, who control any correction.

## How to prepare

BLS describes a bachelor’s degree in computing or a related field as typical for database administrators. Look for roles that give supervised experience in database operations and recovery. [BLS database administration](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/database-administrators.htm#tab-4)

Use fictional datasets to show how you interpret a data dictionary and document a query. For database administration, demonstrate how you verify a load and rehearse recovery in a practice database. Ask U.S. employers which study training and systems experience they expect.

O*NET puts clinical data managers in a category that generally requires considerable preparation. The category describes a broad level of preparation. [Clinical Data Managers Job Zone](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/15-2051.02#JobZone) Look for study data support or data quality assignments that teach you to read a data dictionary, track queries, and follow the study’s correction process. Ask what study-specific training is provided.

## Questions for an employer

Ask the clinical team who reviews open data questions and who can close them. Ask the database team who covers maintenance and how it approves access to the production system.

## Skills that transfer

Move from clinical data into administration by adding system monitoring and recovery work to your quality skills. Move in the other direction by learning how a study defines, verifies and resolves its data.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional six-visit roster, five imported records including one blank field, a short data dictionary, and an import summary. Spend ten minutes deciding whether the blank needs a query, ten comparing source and import totals, and ten drafting separate handoffs for the blank and the missing record.
