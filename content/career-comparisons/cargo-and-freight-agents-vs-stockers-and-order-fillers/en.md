# Cargo and Freight Agents vs. Stockers and Order Fillers When a Shipment Is Short

## Arrange the shipment or assemble the order

Cargo and freight agents arrange transportation, prepare shipping information, and track the handoff. Stockers and order fillers locate and count goods and prepare the physical order for the next step. A shortage matters to both teams for different reasons.

## Compare the two handoffs

| Work | Cargo and Freight Agents | Stockers and Order Fillers |
|---|---|---|
| Short shipment | Booking, confirmed shipping information, and the carrier update | Picked count, location checked, and the shortage record |

## The pickup is booked for more cartons than the floor has

A fictional order calls for 24 cartons, but the warehouse finds only 22. The order filler checks the location and package counts and reports the difference. The freight agent checks the booking, asks the person with authority whether to split the shipment, and updates the carrier and warehouse after approval.

## Prepare for the work itself

O*NET places both occupations in Job Zone 2, a broad category for work requiring very little to some preparation. For freight, look for booking or shipping support and practice checking documents against a confirmed quantity. For stock and order work, start with receiving or picking support and learn how locations and exceptions are recorded. Ask the employer about equipment training and physical tasks. [Freight preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/43-5011.00#JobZone) · [Stock preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/53-7065.00#JobZone)

## Ask what sets the pace

A freight agent may work around carrier pickup times and responses. Order work can be organized in batches on the floor, with walking, standing or handling stock depending on the site. Ask how deadlines, package weights, and staffing are set.

## Build on the handoff you already know

When moving from the floor to freight, keep the habit of checking the actual count and build experience with booking and carrier communication. Moving toward inventory work, learn location checks and the site's process for recording short picks.

## Try the short shipment in 30 minutes

A fictional pickup at 3 p.m. calls for 24 cartons. At 2:20, the count is 22. The other two are expected at 4. The customer permits a split if the shipping supervisor approves. Spend ten minutes recording the count, ten preparing a carrier update and a second pickup request, and ten explaining what would be wrong if the paperwork stayed at 24.
