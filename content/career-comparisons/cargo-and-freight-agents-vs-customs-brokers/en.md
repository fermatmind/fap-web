# Cargo and Freight Agents vs. Customs Brokers When Carton Counts Disagree

## Start with the boundary

Cargo and freight agents arrange transport and maintain shipping records. Customs brokers handle authorized customs business for clients and identify information needed for the entry. A truck can be booked before the records are ready for customs review.

## Compare the handoffs

| Handoff | Cargo and Freight Agents | Customs Brokers |
|---|---|---|
| Example | Booking status, actual pickup count and carrier notes | Entry document questions and a record of client confirmations |

## One shipment in practice

In a fictional shipment the packing list says twelve cartons, the carrier receipt says eleven, and the invoice does not give a carton count. The freight agent checks actual pickup with the shipper and carrier. The broker identifies what evidence is needed from the client and keeps the discrepancy open until it is verified. The importer still has responsibility for compliance.

## Preparing for each role

O*NET places freight agents in Job Zone 2, a broad category of very little to some preparation. Look for supervised booking, documentation or tracking work. [Freight preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/43-5011.00#JobZone) To become an individually licensed broker, check CBP eligibility, pass the broker exam, apply and obtain CBP approval. For client work, learn document preparation and ask which customs tasks you may perform. A broker's employee may conduct business solely for the employer without an individual license only under the applicable authorization and supervision rules. [CBP qualifications](https://www.help.cbp.gov/s/article/Article-1012?language=en_US) [19 CFR 111.2 on licensing and employee authority](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/chapter-I/part-111/subpart-A/section-111.2)

## What shapes the workday

Freight work often follows pickup schedules and carrier replies. Brokerage work can involve repeated document review and waiting for a client's confirmation. Ask who can authorize a change and who prepares or submits an entry.

## Moving between roles

Move toward brokerage by building on shipping records and adding applicable rules, classification, and authorization. Move toward freight by learning carrier schedules and how booking changes reach the people moving goods.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use three fictional records showing twelve packed, eleven picked up, and no carton count on the invoice. Spend ten minutes on carrier questions, ten identifying what the entry team still needs, and ten writing what the client can and cannot confirm yet.
