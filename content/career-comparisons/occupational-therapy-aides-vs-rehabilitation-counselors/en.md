# Occupational Therapy Aides vs. Rehabilitation Counselors: Who Follows Up When a Session Moves?

## What each role delivers

An occupational therapy aide helps prepare equipment, materials, and treatment space and may assist with scheduling. A rehabilitation counselor works with clients on employment or independent living goals, coordinates services, and follows up on barriers.

## The same schedule change

Imagine a client preparing for training at a community workshop when the treatment practice room becomes unavailable. The aide informs the therapist and checks the backup room and materials as directed. The aide gives the proposed time to the therapist. The counselor checks for conflicts and coordinates with the training provider as authorized by the client, then sends the result back to the therapy team before it confirms the appointment.

## Getting started in the U.S.

Aides typically need a high school diploma or equivalent and receive on-the-job training. Rehabilitation counselors typically need a master's degree in rehabilitation counseling or a related field. Some jobs accept a bachelor's degree and some require a license or certification. Check requirements for the specific position. [BLS aide entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/occupational-therapy-assistants-and-aides.htm#tab-4) · [BLS counselor entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/rehabilitation-counselors.htm#tab-4)

## Working conditions

Aides may need to reset materials between closely scheduled appointments. Counselors may wait for responses from clients, trainers, or service providers. Ask who sends schedule changes and who follows up if a conflict remains.

## Moving between roles

Aides can build on room and schedule coordination and learn interviewing, referrals, and employment planning. Counselors can use scheduling and communication experience in support work while learning the site's equipment and preparation procedures.

## Try both in 30 minutes

Use fictional notes. The Tuesday morning practice room is closed. A backup room and materials are available at 10 a.m. Wednesday. The client is due at training at the same time. Spend 10 minutes writing the aide's update to the therapist, 10 listing the counselor's questions and options, and 10 writing the handoff and when to confirm any change.
