# Electrical Engineers vs. Solar Photovoltaic Installers: A Drawing Discrepancy at the Site

## What each role delivers

Electrical engineers design and review electrical systems and distribution interfaces. Solar photovoltaic installers install modules and supports and may perform array wiring or electrical checks when qualified and authorized for the task.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electrical Engineers | Solar Photovoltaic Installers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Distribution interface and drawing review | Module and support installation, with assigned wiring or electrical checks |
| Basis | Distribution limits and connection drawings | Site, drawings, equipment and task authorization |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $120,630 | $53,140 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 17-2071 and 47-2231](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional commercial project has two connection drawings. The installer checks field labels and records the location and revisions, holding affected connection work. The engineer checks existing distribution records and routes any change to the designated design owner for approval.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for electrical engineers and high school or equivalent with moderate-term on-the-job training for photovoltaic installers. Some states require installers to be licensed. Ask the licensing board which tasks and connection work the role may perform. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [BLS licensing overview](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/solar-photovoltaic-installers.htm)

## Work conditions

Engineering may follow design reviews and acceptance. Installation may follow deliveries, weather and work areas. Ask who receives field discrepancies.

## Moving between roles

Moving to installation, bring drawings and inspection skills and add site training. Moving to engineering, bring site experience and develop power distribution design and verification.
