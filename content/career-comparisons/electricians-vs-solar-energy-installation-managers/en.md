# Electricians vs. Solar Energy Installation Managers: An Open Electrical Check

## What each role delivers

Electricians install and check wiring or equipment under assigned authority. Solar energy installation managers coordinate crews, installation and inspections and track open items.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electricians | Solar Energy Installation Managers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Electrical installation and inspection record | Installation schedule and inspection coordination |
| Basis | Drawings, assigned work and inspection | Scope, crew assignments and milestones |

[O*NET 47-2111.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-2111.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 47-1011.03](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-1011.03#Tasks)

O*NET uses the broader First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers wage category for solar energy installation managers. That series does not provide a separate figure for the role. [O*NET occupational profile](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-1011.03)

## How the work meets

A fictional rooftop project has placed its modules but lacks one electrical recheck. The electrician identifies the affected item and drawing. The manager updates the inspection schedule and tells the designated reviewer what is still open.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and apprenticeship for electricians. Most states license electricians, so check the state or local board for the work and responsibility level. High school or equivalent was the most frequent O*NET education response for solar installation managers; ask about site supervision experience. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [O*NET education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-1011.03#Education) [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [BLS licensing overview](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/electricians.htm)

## Work conditions

Electricians may follow installation stages and checks. Managers coordinate deliveries, trades and inspection windows. Ask who tracks inspection status.

## Moving between roles

Moving to management, bring trade knowledge and build scheduling and contractor coordination. Moving into electrical work, bring project knowledge and obtain the required training and credentials.

## Try it in 30 minutes
