# Costume Attendants vs. Fashion Designers: When a Sample Takes Too Long to Change Into

## What each role delivers

Costume attendants prepare garments, assist with fittings and changes and document problems. Fashion designers specify fabrics and construction and modify samples. A wardrobe assignment can also include authorized alterations.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Costume Attendants | Fashion Designers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Fitting notes and change list | Sample revisions and garment specifications |
| Basis | Fitting feedback, steps and change time | Appearance, fabric and construction |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $50,400 | $80,960 |

## How the work meets

At a fictional runway rehearsal, a back zipper requires two people and pushes the change past its time limit. The attendant records time and staffing. The designer checks which parts of the silhouette and closure must stay and proposes a side opening. The attendant times a repeat change with the revised sample.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent and short-term on-the-job training for costume attendants and a bachelor’s degree as typical for fashion designers. Check whether the assignment includes repairs, fittings or on-call show work. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Wardrobe work can follow rehearsals and show calls. Design may cycle through patterns, materials and samples. Ask who receives change notes and which sample goes into rehearsal.

## Moving between roles

Moving into design, bring fitting and dressing feedback and add material, construction and specification skills. Moving into wardrobe, bring sample knowledge and learn labeling, change order and checklists.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: two changes took 38 and 42 seconds, the show allows 30 seconds, and the front silhouette must stay. Spend ten minutes each writing a wardrobe note, listing two construction options and setting retest conditions.
