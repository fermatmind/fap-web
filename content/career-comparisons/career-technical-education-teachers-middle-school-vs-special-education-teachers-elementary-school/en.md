# Middle-School CTE Teachers vs. Elementary Special Education Teachers

## What each role delivers

Middle-school CTE teachers demonstrate and assess technical tasks. Elementary special education teachers adapt academic instruction for students with disabilities and track progress toward assigned learning goals.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Career/Technical Education Teachers, Middle School | Special Education Teachers, Elementary School |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Skill lesson and classroom feedback | Adapted instruction and progress notes |
| Basis | Course goals | Learning goals and support needs |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $65,030 | $65,120 |

National occupation medians. Local and starting pay vary. The special education figure covers kindergarten and elementary school combined. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 25-2023 and 25-2052](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

At a fictional cross-grade workshop, the elementary special education teacher shares the picture-card template and presentation notes approved for workshop use, with no student records attached. The middle-school CTE teacher uses the template for team-role instructions and reports class-level observations. Each teacher adapts classroom materials; any formal change to the elementary student’s support plan goes through the authorized school team.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree and less than five years of related experience as typical for middle school CTE teachers. For special education teachers, elementary school, its occupation row lists a bachelor’s degree for the combined kindergarten and elementary school row as typical. California’s Designated Subjects CTE credential is one example of authorization for named vocational subjects through grade 12. Check the state and school assignment. [California CTC CTE authorization](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/cl-888/) [BLS occupation rows](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) California’s Education Specialist credential has separate authorizations by specialty and grade range. [California CTC Education Specialist](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/cl-808ca/)

## Work conditions

CTE teachers move between demonstration and group work. Elementary special education teachers track individual progress and coordinate with families and school teams. Ask how the team maintains and shares teaching records.

## Moving between roles

Moving into special education, bring step-by-step demonstration and observation skills, then add elementary instruction, disability support methods and applicable authorization. Moving toward CTE, add technical subject knowledge and middle-school teaching.

## Try it in 30 minutes

On paper, use two fictional records. The CTE group sorts correctly but misses roles; the elementary student sorts images correctly but needs extra time with text. Spend ten minutes on each revised task and ten identifying separate progress evidence.
