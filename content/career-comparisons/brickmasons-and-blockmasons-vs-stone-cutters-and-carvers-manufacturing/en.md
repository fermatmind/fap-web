# Brickmasons and Blockmasons vs. Stone Cutters and Carvers: A Part That Meets Its Drawing but Does Not Fit

## What each role delivers

Brickmasons and blockmasons build masonry and hand over measured dimensions. Manufacturing stone cutters and carvers shape and finish parts and check them against drawings or models.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Brickmasons and Blockmasons | Stone Cutters and Carvers, Manufacturing |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Masonry boundary and installation handoff | Finished stone part and measurement record |
| Basis | Wall dimensions, building drawing and finish line | Shop drawing, part dimensions and surface checks |

[O*NET 47-2021.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-2021.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 51-9195.03](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/51-9195.03#Tasks)

O*NET reports the Molders, Shapers, and Casters, Except Metal and Plastic wage series for this role. That category does not establish a separate median for the occupation in this comparison. [O*NET national wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/51-9195.03)

## How the work meets

A fictional exhibit needs a prefabricated stone feature in a masonry opening. The mason records a narrower opening than the drawing. The stone worker checks the part against its shop drawing. The site lead compares both records before requesting a recheck or a change.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and apprenticeship as typical for brickmasons and blockmasons. For manufacturing stone work, ask about shop drawings, finish inspection and employer training. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Masonry follows site progress and wall handoffs. Stone manufacturing may follow shop orders, equipment and part inspection. Ask who confirms shop dimensions and accepts delivery.

## Moving between roles

Moving to stone manufacturing, bring drawing and measurement skills and add shop drawings and finish inspection. Moving to masonry, bring dimensional checks and learn wall layout and site work.
