# Surgical Assistants vs Surgical Technologists

## Choose direct operative assistance or the instrument and field role

Surgical assistants work directly with the surgeon on assigned operative tasks. Surgical technologists prepare the room, help maintain the sterile field and support count and instrument work. Both coordinate with a surgical team, with different assignments during the case.

## Compare the next person in the handoff

| Work | Surgical assistant | Surgical technologist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary focus | Assigned direct assistance to the surgeon | Setup, sterile field and instruments |
| Typical handoff | Status of an assigned task to the surgeon | Supplies or count discrepancy to the supervising team |
| Oversight | Supervising surgeon | Surgical team supervision |

## Follow two issues during a Virginia operating-room handoff

In a fictional Virginia operating room, the surgeon assigns the licensed surgical assistant to help with retraction. The surgical technologist passes an instrument to the assistant as directed and flags an item that does not match the count record to the supervising nurse. The assistant confirms receipt and checks the next assigned task with the surgeon. The surgical team resolves the record discrepancy under facility policy. Tasks such as retraction can overlap depending on the assignment and supervision. ## Check which Virginia credential applies to the job

Virginia generally requires a Board license to practice surgical assisting or use the title, subject to statutory exceptions. One initial pathway uses an accepted current national surgical assistant credential. For surgical technologists, Virginia requires Board certification to use the protected title. One initial pathway combines accredited surgical technology training with a current national credential. BLS describes formal postsecondary preparation for these careers. Check the specific state route before choosing a program. ## Compare national pay and time on the operating-room floor

May 2025 U.S. median annual wages were $66,800 for surgical assistants and $64,650 for surgical technologists. Ask about call, standing time and how long a typical assignment lasts. A fictional handoff log can show that you know how to flag incomplete work. Moving toward first assisting adds direct operative training and applicable credentials; moving toward the technologist role adds training in field, instruments and counts.
