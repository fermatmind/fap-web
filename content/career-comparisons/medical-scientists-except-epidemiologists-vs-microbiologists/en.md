# Medical Scientist vs. Microbiologist

## Health research and interpretation of microbial findings

Medical scientists study questions about health and disease, design research and communicate results. Microbiologists focus on microorganisms and explain what microbial evidence can support. They can work together on a study while interpreting different evidence.

## Compare the work products

| Item | Medical scientist, except epidemiologist | Microbiologist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Define | Study outcomes, inclusion criteria and interpretation limits | What the microbial results represent |
| Verify | Follow-up dates and the completeness of outcome records | Which observations, groups and dates the summary covers |
| Produce | Health research findings and their limitations | Microbial interpretation and its limits |

## The two summaries cover different periods

A fictional study follows changes in digestive comfort and includes an existing microbial summary. The medical scientist verifies that the questionnaire covers four weeks. The microbiologist confirms that the microbial summary covers only week one. They revise the report to describe each time window explicitly and identify what evidence would be needed before interpreting a longer-term relationship.

## U.S. education and training

BLS assigns medical scientists other than epidemiologists a doctoral or professional degree and microbiologists a bachelor's degree in its national table. Neither is assigned related work experience or on-the-job training in that table. The BLS medical scientist profile describes Ph.D. or medical degree routes, and says a master's with experience can qualify for some jobs. Some microbiology employers prefer graduate study. Check the research position's specific education and any separate clinical responsibilities. [BLS detailed training table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/education-and-training-by-occupation.htm) · [BLS medical scientist entry guidance](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/medical-scientists.htm#tab-4) · [BLS microbiologist entry guidance](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/microbiologists.htm#tab-4)

## Ask how research time is divided

Ask a medical science employer about study design, collaboration and publication time. For microbiology, ask how laboratory responsibilities, review and reporting are scheduled and who resolves discrepancies.

## Moving between roles

Medical scientists can bring study design and writing to microbiology, then build knowledge of microbial science and complete the employer's relevant training. Microbiologists can bring technical interpretation and record review into medical research, then develop skills in health research design and pursue the preparation the role requires.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use a fictional four-week questionnaire summary and a microbial summary for week one only. Spend ten minutes describing the outcome and its time period, ten checking the coverage of the microbial summary, and ten drafting two supported statements and one question these records cannot answer.
