# Credit Authorizers, Checkers, and Clerks vs. Loan Officers: A Payment Record Changes During Review

## What each role delivers

Credit authorizers, checkers, and clerks examine credit or payment records. Some apply preset decision rules. Loan officers explain options and handle or refer applications within their assigned authority.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Credit Authorizers, Checkers, and Clerks | Loan Officers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Payment verification and exception note | Loan explanation and approval referral |
| Basis | Payment dates, records and preset standards | Borrower needs, terms and authority limits |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $50,080 | $76,690 |

## How the work meets

A fictional vehicle applicant’s receipt and credit record show different payment statuses. The checker verifies the latest status through an approved channel and records its source and time. The officer updates the application, refers the loan amount that exceeds their limit and reports the confirmed status to the applicant.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for credit authorizers, checkers, and clerks. It lists a bachelor’s degree, less than five years of related experience and moderate-term training for loan officers. For U.S. residential mortgage origination, check the employer and activities for federal registration or state licensing requirements. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [CFPB Regulation G](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1007/103/) [CFPB Regulation H](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1008/103/)

## Work conditions

Checking may follow a discrepancy queue. Loan officers may work across consultations, applications and approval deadlines. Ask who reviews exceptions.

## Moving between roles

Moving into loan work adds customer communication, product knowledge and assigned approval authority to verification skills. Moving into checking adds record comparison and version tracking to application experience.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock file: the payment is confirmed, the amount requested exceeds the officer’s authority and the applicant asks for an update today. Spend ten minutes each on a verification note, an approval referral and a factual status reply.
