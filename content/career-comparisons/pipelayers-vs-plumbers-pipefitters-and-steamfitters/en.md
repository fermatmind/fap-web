# Pipelayer vs. Plumber, Pipefitter, and Steamfitter

## A utility run and the system it connects to

Pipelayers commonly place and join pipe for drains, sewers, and water mains. Plumbers, pipefitters, and steamfitters may install pipe assemblies or connect fixtures, equipment, and industrial systems. Both trades can work on the same project and meet at a connection between systems.

## Compare the deliverables

| Work | Pipelayer | Plumber, pipefitter, or steamfitter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First check | Utility route and pipe position | Piping layout, fittings, and equipment connections |
| Handoff | Utility segment record and open connection questions | System connection notes and any drawing or fitting questions |

U.S. median annual pay in May 2025 was $49,000 for pipelayers and $63,800 for plumbers, pipefitters, and steamfitters. These are occupation-wide OEWS medians. ## U.S. routes into the trades

For pipelaying, look for a utility crew opening with supervised training and ask when new hires start reading plans and logging work. O*NET places pipelayers in Job Zone 1–2. Most plumbers, pipefitters, and steamfitters learn through an apprenticeship, according to BLS. Most states and some localities license plumbers. Check requirements for your trade and job location. [O*NET pipelayer preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-2151.00#JobZone) · [BLS plumbing trades preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/plumbers-pipefitters-and-steamfitters.htm#tab-4)

## Compare the sites and schedules

Pipelayers commonly work outdoors in exposed conditions. The plumbing and fitting trades work in settings such as homes, businesses, and factories. Ask a utility crew how weather affects its schedule. Ask a piping contractor whether the job involves service calls, industrial project work, or scheduled construction shifts. [O*NET pipelayer conditions](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-2151.00#WorkContext) · [BLS plumbing trades work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/plumbers-pipefitters-and-steamfitters.htm#tab-3)

## Moving between the trades

A pipelayer can bring layout and handoff experience to building piping, then learn system drawings, fittings, and equipment interfaces. A building or industrial piping worker can bring drawing and connection experience to utility work, then learn to read route and elevation records and coordinate with civil crews.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

The handoff form says the project lead sends interface questions to design.
