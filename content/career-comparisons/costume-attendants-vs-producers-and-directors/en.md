# Costume Attendants vs. Producers and Directors: Can a Backup Coat Match the Previous Shot?

## What each role delivers

Costume attendants prepare and check garments and record changes. Producers and directors may manage resources and creative decisions. This example concerns timing and shot continuity.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Costume Attendants | Producers and Directors |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Wardrobe status, backups and change log | Shot order and production schedule |
| Basis | Character, take and costume continuity | Story continuity, shot needs and shoot time |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $50,400 | $90,360 |

## How the work meets

For a fictional corridor pickup, the attendant spots different buttons on a backup coat and provides comparison photos and the original coat’s arrival time. The director checks whether the buttons will appear. The producer confirms which scene can be shot first, and wardrobe records the agreed version for the next shot.

## How to enter

BLS lists a high school diploma or equivalent and short-term on-the-job training for costume attendants. It lists a bachelor’s degree and less than five years of related experience for producers and directors. Check which tasks the posting assigns. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Wardrobe may follow cast calls and shot order. Producers and directors may adjust shots, space and resources. Ask who communicates a last-minute costume change.

## Moving between roles

Moving into production or directing, bring continuity awareness and add scheduling or visual storytelling. Moving into wardrobe, apply scene organization and learn labeling, resets and dressing support.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: the previous shot shows silver buttons, the backup has black ones, the original arrives in 20 minutes and one shot does not show the upper body. Spend ten minutes each on wardrobe status, shot order options and updating continuity after approval.
