# Surveying and Mapping Technicians vs. Surveyors: Who Checks an Old Boundary Marker?

## What each role delivers

Surveying and mapping technicians may locate or record survey points and update map layers. Surveyors conduct and review measurements and prepare formal deliverables within the applicable licensing rules.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Surveying and Mapping Technicians | Surveyors |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Points, field notes and map updates | Survey review and formal deliverable |
| Basis | Point IDs, instrument records and coordinates | Control, property records and evaluation of evidence |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $54,240 | $75,440 |

## How the work meets

A fictional project finds an old marker beside a fence. The technician records its ID, coordinates and description and flags a discrepancy with the earlier record. The surveyor compares adjacent markers and property records, then determines what needs to be measured again.

## How to enter

BLS lists a high school diploma or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for technicians. It lists a bachelor’s degree and an internship or residency for surveyors. A surveyor must be licensed to serve the public or certify legal documents. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [BLS surveyor licensing](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/surveyors.htm)

## Work conditions

Technicians may alternate between fieldwork, data entry and inspection. Surveyors may organize fieldwork, verify property records and review deliverables. Ask who defines the follow-up survey when records differ.

## Moving between roles

Technicians can bring instrument and coordinate skills into surveying and add boundary evidence, professional judgment and applicable credentials. Surveyors can apply quality review skills to technical collection and map production.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records show a marker by the fence, two meters from the location in a prior record, with neighboring points still unchecked. Spend ten minutes each on a field note, evidence to compare and a status note for the reviewer.
