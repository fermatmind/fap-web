# Computer and Office Machine Repairers vs. Network Support Specialists: The Offline Copier

## What each role delivers

Computer, automated teller, and office machine repairers service equipment. This example uses an office copier. Network support specialists diagnose connectivity issues and document recovery.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Computer, Automated Teller, and Office Machine Repairers | Computer Network Support Specialists |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Device repair and return record | Network diagnosis and recovery notes |
| Basis | Local device condition | Connectivity and affected scope |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $47,810 | $76,220 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 49-2011 and 15-1231](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional copier still makes local copies, but jobs from three workstations fail. The repairer passes along the local test result and asset ID. Network support compares reports from the area, documents the network issue separately and asks the affected users to verify recovery.

## How to enter

BLS lists some college without a degree and short-term on-the-job training for the repair occupation. It lists an associate’s degree and moderate-term on-the-job training for network support specialists. Check the employer’s specific assignments and training. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Repair work may involve site visits and parts coordination. Network support can involve shifts and incidents spanning multiple users. Ask how each role verifies ticket closure.

## Moving between roles

Moving to network support, build on fault documentation and add networking fundamentals. Moving to repair, add device-specific training, parts tracking and service procedures.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional ticket: local copying works, three workstations fail, and another floor is unaffected. Spend ten minutes each on device status, network impact and the handoff and verification needed. Use records only.
