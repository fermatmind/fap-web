# Choreographers vs. Music Directors and Composers: Syncing the Stage and the Score

## What each role delivers

Choreographers shape movement and formations. Music directors and composers may lead rehearsals, create music or interpret scores. In this example the music lead handles the live cue.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Choreographers | Music Directors and Composers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Movement phrases, counts and blocking | Score, tempo and performance cues |
| Basis | Formation changes, rhythm and space | Tempo, phrasing and ensemble coordination |

## How the work meets

In a fictional theater rehearsal, the band accents count eight, but the dancers reach the new formation on count nine. The choreographer gives the music lead the movement counts and spacing constraints. The music lead checks which accents the phrase can retain. They send the revised cue and blocking to the next rehearsal.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent, five or more years of related experience and long-term on-the-job training for choreographers. It lists a bachelor’s degree and less than five years of related experience as typical for music directors and composers. Check whether the opening emphasizes writing, conducting or rehearsal direction. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Both roles may share rehearsals. Choreography follows movement and stage layout. Music work may alternate among score changes, rehearsals and performance. Ask which role maintains the current cue list.

## Moving between roles

A choreographer can bring rhythm and rehearsal experience to music work and add score reading, composition or conducting. A music lead can bring phrasing and cue design to choreography and add movement sequencing and ensemble blocking.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock cues: the accent is on eight, the formation is ready on nine, and the phrase ends on sixteen. Spend ten minutes drafting movement changes, ten minutes drafting music cues and ten minutes identifying the shared counts to test in rehearsal.
