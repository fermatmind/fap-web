# Coil Winder vs. Timing Device Assembler: A Passed Component and a Failed Module

## What each role delivers

Coil winders, tapers and finishers produce electrical coils to a work order and record what was made. Timing device assemblers and adjusters assemble mechanisms and verify fit and operation.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Coil Winders, Tapers, and Finishers | Timing Device Assemblers and Adjusters |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Wound and finished coil with production records | Assembled timing mechanism and functional inspection result |
| Key checks | Material and component match, lot and production record | Component fit, motion and function |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $48,220 | $62,620 |

## How the work meets

A fictional factory makes a timing module with an electromagnetic drive coil. The winding station transfers the parts with lot and inspection records. The assembler finds one coil without its tag and asks the winding station to verify its identity. A different coil has a matching record and passes its component check, but the module built with it fails its timing test. Assembly records the failing conditions and sends the module for review.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training as typical for both. Ask how much training takes place on winding equipment or at an assembly bench, and which checks the operator signs. [BLS Table 1.2](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Winding can move through a batch at a predictable cadence. Assembly may move between fine parts handling and functional checks. Ask how a missing tag and a failed final result are reported.

## Moving between roles

Moving from winding to timing assembly, bring lot records and learn to document fit and function. Moving into winding, bring the discipline of recording failures and learn material handling, coil specifications and production tracking.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use mock cards: coil A has no tag, coil B has a passing record and the finished module using coil B fails a timing check. Take ten minutes to mark each item's status, ten to draft the handoff and ten to check that your decisions distinguish component and assembly evidence. Use records only.
