# Customs and Border Protection Officers vs. Customs Brokers Reviewing a Shipment Discrepancy

## Start with the boundary

A CBP officer reviews a shipment or its documents under government authority. A customs broker works on behalf of a client within the assigned scope. The broker can prepare and explain the records. The agency retains its own review and decision authority.

## Compare the handoffs

| Handoff | Customs and Border Protection Officers | Customs Brokers |
|---|---|---|
| Example | Official review, request, or decision record under agency procedures | Client document review, verified explanation, and authorization record |

## One shipment in practice

In a fictional entry, an officer sees that two records describe the shipment differently and requests clarification through the applicable process. The broker checks the description with the client, marks facts that are still uncertain, and responds through the authorized channel. The importer remains responsible for compliance.

## Preparing for each role

CBP lists grade-specific experience and education routes for officer applicants. Its GS-5 and GS-7 entrance exam is part of the hiring process. Use the CBP role guidance and check the requirements for the grade you intend to apply for. [CBP Officer qualifications](https://careers.cbp.gov/s/applicant-resources/cbpo-qualification-aid) · [CBP Officer exam](https://careers.cbp.gov/s/applicant-resources/cbpo-entrance-exam) To become an individually licensed broker, check CBP eligibility, pass the broker exam, apply and obtain CBP approval. For client work, learn document preparation and ask which customs tasks you may perform. A broker's employee may conduct business solely for the employer without an individual license only under the applicable authorization and supervision rules. [CBP qualifications](https://www.help.cbp.gov/s/article/Article-1012?language=en_US) [19 CFR 111.2 on licensing and employee authority](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/chapter-I/part-111/subpart-A/section-111.2) Current U.S. government officers and employees do not meet the individual license eligibility rule. If you are considering a move from CBP, check that condition before planning the application. [19 CFR 111.11 individual eligibility](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/chapter-I/part-111/subpart-B/section-111.11) The same federal employment restriction applies on the day of the broker exam. [19 CFR 111.13 examination eligibility](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/chapter-I/part-111/subpart-B/section-111.13)

## What shapes the workday

Officer work can include port coverage and duties at a government inspection site. Broker work can be more concentrated around documents, client communication and entry deadlines. Ask about schedules, supervision and the responsibilities assigned to a new hire.

## Moving between roles

Document review skills can transfer. Moving into an officer role requires qualifying through the federal hiring process. Moving toward brokerage requires learning client representation and meeting the applicable authorization rules. A current federal officer or employee is ineligible to obtain the individual broker license under 19 CFR 111.11.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use fictional records. The manifest describes one equipment set, a product description says two, and the client can verify only that the purchase agreement says one. Spend ten minutes writing an official clarification question, ten drafting the client's document follow-up, and ten separating known facts from what remains open.
