# Wind Energy Development Managers vs. Wind Energy Operations Managers: The Site Handoff

## What each role delivers

Wind energy development managers coordinate development and engineering milestones. Wind energy operations managers oversee site maintenance and staff and track downtime and equipment records.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Wind Energy Development Managers | Wind Energy Operations Managers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Development handoff and open items | Site tickets, maintenance plan and downtime tracking |
| Basis | Agreements, assessments, schedule and budget | Asset register, crews, contractors and downtime |

## How the work meets

A fictional site is preparing for handoff. The development manager provides site and milestone records. The operations manager sees that W4 is in the asset register but has no first-maintenance owner and keeps the item open.

## How to enter

O*NET education responses most often list a bachelor’s degree for wind development. A two-year degree and a bachelor’s degree are the top two responses for wind operations. Ask how the employer weighs development or operating experience. ## Work conditions

Development may follow external agreements and review meetings. Operations may follow sites, shifts and maintenance windows. Ask how open items are passed between teams.

## Moving between roles

Moving to operations, bring project tracking and add asset records and maintenance scheduling. Moving to development, bring operating and maintenance knowledge and learn site assessment and agreements.
