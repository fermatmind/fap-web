# Compliance Managers vs. Security Managers: Who Reviews a Visitor Sign-In Issue?

## How the two jobs use the same record

A compliance manager checks whether organizational requirements are being followed and tracks corrective action. A security manager coordinates site protection, temporary access arrangements, and incident documentation.

## Compare the work

| Compare | Compliance manager | Security manager |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence | Applicable policy and review records | Shift observations, approval status, and incident notes |
| Output | Review findings and corrective action follow-up | Immediate response and shift handoff |

## The same contractor visit

Imagine reception finds a missing host confirmation in a contractor's sign-in record. Under site procedure, the security manager asks the visitor to wait in reception, requests confirmation from the host, and records what happens. The compliance manager checks the policy in effect at the time and reviews other entries from the same group to distinguish missing documentation from missing approval. The compliance manager and process owner agree on what to verify next and when to complete the corrective work.

## Getting started in the U.S.

O*NET lists both occupations in Job Zone Four, which generally calls for considerable preparation. For compliance, show how your review tied a finding to a policy and verified a corrective action. For security, show a de-identified handoff or exercise that demonstrates how you coordinated people and documented decisions. Ask which duties and credentials apply to the particular site. ## Working conditions

Compliance work can involve scheduled review time and follow-up across teams. Security managers may need to coordinate people during a busy arrival period or shift change. Ask who receives escalations and who verifies that corrective work is complete.

## Moving between roles

Moving into compliance, build on incident documentation and learn policy review, sampling, and corrective action. Moving into security, use your process review experience and practice shift handoffs, site communication, and incident coordination.

## Try both in 30 minutes

Use these fictional records. The policy requires host confirmation before admission. At 10 a.m., one sign-in has no confirmation on file. At 10:15 the host says they confirmed. The previous two entries have complete records. Spend 10 minutes drafting the shift handoff, 10 listing the timeline questions and what else compliance should sample, and 10 assigning the next actions.
