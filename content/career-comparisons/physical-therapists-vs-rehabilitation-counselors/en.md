# Physical Therapists vs. Rehabilitation Counselors: What Does a Return-to-Work Plan Still Need?

## What each role assesses

A physical therapist evaluates movement and physical function and plans or adjusts treatment. A rehabilitation counselor works with the client on employment or independent living goals and coordinates support for barriers.

## The same return-to-work goal

Imagine a client wants to return to a library shift. The physical therapist records how the client walks to the clinic door, including the assistance and rest needed. The rehabilitation counselor and client check the commute and find that the distance from the bus stop to the library is longer than the clinic observation. They identify that gap and discuss shift options or transportation support. The teams record what was observed and what remains to be checked.

## Getting started in the U.S.

Physical therapists entering the occupation need a Doctor of Physical Therapy degree and a state license. Rehabilitation counselors typically need a master's degree in rehabilitation counseling or a related field. Some jobs accept a bachelor's degree, and some require licensure or certification. Check the state and job-specific requirements. [BLS physical therapist entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/physical-therapists.htm#tab-4) · [BLS rehabilitation counselor entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/rehabilitation-counselors.htm#tab-4)

## Working conditions

Physical therapists need space and time for evaluations and follow-up. Counselors may need responses from clients, service providers, and employers on different schedules. Ask who handles transportation follow-up and what findings can be shared.

## Moving between roles

Interviewing and team handoffs carry over. Moving into physical therapy requires clinical training in movement and physical function. Moving into rehabilitation counseling requires training in employment planning, referral, and case coordination. Check the credentials for the destination role.

## Try both in 30 minutes

Use fictional notes. The client rested after walking 20 meters on a level clinic floor with a staff member guarding nearby. The bus stop is 100 meters from the library and rest points are unknown. Monday's shift is early, and other shifts may be available. Spend 10 minutes on the mobility note and follow-up question, 10 on the commute and schedule questions, and 10 on a joint handoff.
