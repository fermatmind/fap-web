# Costume Attendants vs. Tailors, Dressmakers, and Custom Sewers: Handing Off a Sleeve Alteration

## What each role delivers

Costume attendants assist with dressing, check fit and record changes. Some handle repairs. Tailors, dressmakers, and custom sewers measure and mark garments, sew alterations and check fit.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Costume Attendants | Tailors, Dressmakers, and Custom Sewers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Fitting record, garment status and return time | Alteration marks, sewing and fit check |
| Basis | Performer, garment ID and next call | Measurements, seam allowance and material condition |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $50,400 | $41,640 |

## How the work meets

At a fictional fitting, a coat cuff covers a watch used as a prop when the performer raises an arm. The attendant logs the garment ID, problem and next call. The tailor measures both sleeves, checks construction and marks the change. After a recheck, the attendant records the returned garment as ready.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and short-term on-the-job training for costume attendants. It lists no formal educational credential and moderate-term on-the-job training for tailors, dressmakers, and custom sewers. Ask what fitting and repair work the assignment covers. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Wardrobe may follow the cast’s schedule. Tailoring may follow fitting appointments and work orders. Ask who signs off on an alteration returned to wardrobe.

## Moving between roles

Moving into tailoring, bring fitting observations and learn measuring, fabrics and sewing. Moving into wardrobe, bring alteration skills and learn scene tracking, labeling and change handoffs.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock fitting: the right sleeve of coat A looks two centimeters long when laid flat, but covers the prop only with the arm raised. The left sleeve is fine and the next call is in two hours. Spend ten minutes each on a fitting note, tailor checks and return acceptance.
