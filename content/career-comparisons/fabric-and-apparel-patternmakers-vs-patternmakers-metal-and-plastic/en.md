# Fabric and Apparel Patternmakers vs. Metal and Plastic Patternmakers: Two Ways to Check a Pattern

## What each role delivers

Fabric and apparel patternmakers develop and revise cutting patterns. Metal and plastic patternmakers make and check patterns and templates against drawings. Both track dimensions and revisions.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Fabric and Apparel Patternmakers | Patternmakers, Metal and Plastic |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Cutting patterns with assembly markings | Industrial pattern checked against dimensions |
| Basis | Fit notes, match marks, sizing and fabric | Drawing datum, tolerances and measuring tools |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $62,750 | $58,000 |

## How the work meets

A fictional theater team needs a fabric cover and a plastic stand for a piece of stage equipment. The apparel patternmaker finds mismatched notches on the cover pattern, checks the revision and hands corrected pieces and match notes to the sample sewing team. The industrial patternmaker finds a hole-spacing discrepancy on the stand pattern and sends the repeated measurements, datum and drawing revision to the quality lead for disposition against the tolerance. The project lead checks both results before scheduling a combined fit check.

## How to enter

BLS lists a high school diploma or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training as typical for both occupations. Check the product and equipment covered by the posting. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Apparel patterns may go through several rounds of fittings. Industrial pattern work may follow shop and inspection schedules. Ask who classifies a discrepancy and how it is logged.

## Moving between roles

Timing and revision habits may transfer in either direction. Industrial work adds engineering drawings, measurement and tolerances. Apparel work adds fabric behavior, piece relationships and fit review.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: the cover notches differ by two millimeters and the stand pattern’s hole spacing differs by two millimeters. There is no sample fit result or tolerance record. Spend ten minutes each on a note for sample sewing, a measurement record for the quality lead and the evidence the project lead needs for a combined fit check.
