# Manufactured Building and Mobile Home Installers vs. Roofers: A Water Mark at the Module Joint

## What each role delivers

Manufactured building and mobile home installers set up units and may also seal joints or address leaks. Roofers inspect and work on roof and seam water protection. The installation drawings and work order assign responsibility where those duties meet.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Manufactured Building and Mobile Home Installers | Roofers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Unit setup and installation handoff | Roof continuity and inspection record |
| Basis | Installation instructions, unit IDs and joints | Roof scope, seams and water protection |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $45,990 | $55,440 |

## How the work meets

In a fictional project, two units are in place and a water mark appears near the roof joint. The work order assigns the installer to check setup and unit sealing records and the roofer to check the roofing seam record. The site lead reviews both before deciding what to verify next.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and short-term on-the-job training for installers, and no formal educational credential with moderate-term training for roofers. For initial installation of a new manufactured home in a state without a qualifying installation program, § 3286.203(a)(1) requires the individual or business directing, supervising or controlling the work to hold the installation license or employ someone who holds it. The rule exempts direct employees of the licensed installer or the company employing that installer when supervised by a licensed installer. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [24 CFR § 3286.203](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/subtitle-B/chapter-XX/part-3286/subpart-C/section-3286.203)

## Work conditions

Unit installation follows arrival and assembly windows. Roofing can depend on weather and access to the work area. Ask who signs the shared joint and later repair work.

## Moving between roles

Moving to roofing, bring module coordination and add roof materials and inspection. Moving to installation, bring interface documentation and add unit setup and handoff skills.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Read a mock record: U1 and U2 are set to the installation drawing, the seam inspection box is blank, and there is a water mark nearby. Spend ten minutes each on the installation record, roofing note and an unresolved cause and review owner. Use documents only.
