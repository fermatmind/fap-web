# Tire Builders vs Tire Repairers and Changers

## Build the tire or handle the vehicle work order

Tire builders assemble or retread tires and check them against production requirements. Tire repairers and changers inspect and service tires on individual jobs. Decide whether you would rather trace one batch across a production shift or track the status of a customer's vehicle.

## Compare what each role checks

| Work | Tire builders | Tire repairers and changers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inputs | Components, casings or process sheet | Vehicle, tire markings, work order |
| Checks | Component or finished tire against production criteria | Tire or wheel condition and the requested service |
| Handoff | Inspection and batch notes | Work-order record and completed service check |

[O*NET tire builder duties](https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/51-9197.00) · [O*NET tire service duties](https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/49-3093.00)

## One recall check, two work records

In a NHTSA-published motorcycle tire recall example, a tire is affected only when its brand, line, size, and identification number match the notice. In a fictional follow-up, a tire builder helps quality staff compare manufacturing records with the listed identifiers. A shop technician records the actual sidewall information on a vehicle ticket, marks an unreadable field for review, and follows the applicable recall process before recording any disposition. [NHTSA recall identification example](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2025/RCONL-25T002-1064.pdf)

## Check the training and job assignment

BLS lists a high school diploma or equivalent as typical for both occupations. The tire builder row lists moderate-term on-the-job training, and the tire repairer and changer row lists short-term training. Ask the plant what is covered in machine and quality training. Ask the service shop which jobs require supervised practice and who checks completed work. [BLS Table 1.2 training rows](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Compare shift conditions and pay

O*NET lists noise exposure for tire builders and standing and repetitive motions for tire service workers. Ask about line rotation, service-bay demand and how exceptions are recorded. May 2025 U.S. median annual employee wages were $57,390 for tire builders and $37,710 for tire repairers and changers. OEWS excludes self-employment earnings. [Builder work context](https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/51-9197.00) · [Service work context](https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/49-3093.00) · [Comparable BLS wage rows](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Bring a sample from your current work

From manufacturing, bring your inspection and batch tracking habits, then build a mock vehicle ticket with service and completion checks. From repair, bring your work-order discipline and add a practice batch record with inspection status and a handoff for a quality exception.

## Try a 30-minute identifier handoff

Use a fictional recall card for brand A, line L, size S1, and identifier T16. Plant record P21 lists A/L/S1/T16; P22 lists A/L/S1/T18. The shop ticket matches brand, line and size but reads T1? for the identifier. Spend 12 minutes writing a plant note that identifies the matching record. Spend 13 minutes logging the three matches and the unreadable field on the shop ticket. In five minutes confirm both records name who will verify the identifier and that the vehicle recall check remains pending.
