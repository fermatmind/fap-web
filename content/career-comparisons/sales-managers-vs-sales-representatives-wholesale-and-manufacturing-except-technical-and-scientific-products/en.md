# Sales Managers vs. Nontechnical Wholesale Sales Representatives: Allocating Stock Across Two Rush Orders

## What each role delivers

Sales managers oversee the team and sales priorities. Nontechnical wholesale representatives handle individual accounts, goods, prices and delivery terms.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Sales Managers | Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Except Technical and Scientific Products |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Team allocation and exception record | Customer quote and delivery terms |
| Basis | Margin, supply and account priorities | Customer needs, terms and confirmed dates |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $148,270 | $72,080 |

## How the work meets

A fictional supplier has 40 cases of standard household supplies. Two accounts want 30 each. The representative records customers’ minimum quantities and dates. The manager sets the allocation based on prior commitments, replenishment and account rules. The representative then sends the approved terms to each account.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree and less than five years of related experience for sales managers. For nontechnical wholesale representatives, it lists high school or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Managers may coordinate teams and supply. Representatives may follow quotes and orders. Ask who acts on approved exceptions.

## Moving between roles

Moving into management adds coaching, forecasting and exception review. Moving into account work adds customer discovery and order follow-through.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: 40 cases available, two requests for 30, and one account needs at least 15. Spend ten minutes each on account needs, allocations and quote conditions.
