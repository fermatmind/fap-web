# Stonemasons vs. Tile and Stone Setters: The Finish Beside a Stone Step

## What each role delivers

Stonemasons lay out and set stone and check joints. Tile and stone setters plan the finish, align pieces and dress joints. Materials can overlap across the two trades.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Stonemasons | Tile and Stone Setters |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Set stone to the masonry layout | Tile finish layout and joints |
| Basis | Stone dimensions, reference line and joints | Pattern, border pieces and edges |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $57,390 | $55,690 |

## How the work meets

In a fictional gallery, a trial tile layout next to a stone step leaves a narrow edge piece. The stonemason provides step measurements. The tile setter reports the border width and finish drawing for review.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and apprenticeship for stonemasons. It lists no formal educational credential and long-term on-the-job training for tile and stone setters. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Stone work may follow delivery and placement sequences. Finish work may wait for substrate acceptance. Ask who confirms the interface levels and edges.

## Moving between roles

Moving to setting, bring site measurement and stone fit and learn finish layout and edging. Moving to masonry, bring joint checks and learn stone placement for walls or steps.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock card: the step edge passes, the trial border is 9 mm and the approved sample calls for at least 15 mm. Spend ten minutes each on the stone handoff, an alternate tile layout and the finish requirements to recheck.
