# Credit Analysts vs. Insurance Sales Agents: Financing and Insuring New Equipment

## What each role delivers

Credit analysts evaluate financial records and repayment risk. Insurance sales agents identify coverage needs, explain policies and sell within their authority.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Credit Analysts | Insurance Sales Agents |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Repayment risk assessment | Coverage needs and policy explanation |
| Basis | Cash flow, debt and credit records | Customer needs, policy terms and authority |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $83,510 | $62,280 |

## How the work meets

A fictional factory finances equipment and asks about damage in transit. The analyst lists coverage documents and the date they are needed. The agent reviews the transit and installation scenarios, explains policy options and flags questions for the insurer. The customer submits confirmed documents to the lender through an approved channel.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree for credit analysts and high school completion or equivalent with moderate-term on-the-job training for insurance sales agents. Insurance sales agents need a license in the states where they work. Ask the state insurance department about the relevant line of authority. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [BLS state licensing](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/sales/insurance-sales-agents.htm)

## Work conditions

Analysis may follow credit-review deadlines. Sales may follow consultations, applications and renewals. Ask how coverage information is verified and shared.

## Moving between roles

Moving into sales, bring financial judgment and add policy knowledge, client communication and licensing. Moving into credit, bring interviewing skills and add statements, cash-flow analysis and risk reporting.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: the borrower pays in installments, equipment travels out of town and the existing policy lists only on-site property. Spend ten minutes each on a credit document list, coverage questions and who should receive open items.
