# Retail Loss Prevention Specialists vs. Retail Salespersons: Two Items Missing From the Shelf

## What each role delivers

Retail loss prevention specialists investigate suspected employee or customer theft, vendor fraud and other loss incidents within their authority. They document and report findings. Sales staff flag shortages under store procedure. Retail salespersons help shoppers find and compare goods and process purchases.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Retail Loss Prevention Specialists | Retail Salespersons |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Discrepancy review log | Shelf status and customer request |
| Basis | Counts, receiving, transfers and returns | Available stock, model and sale status |

[O*NET 33-9099.02](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/33-9099.02#Tasks) · [O*NET 41-2031.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/41-2031.00#Tasks)

O*NET uses the Protective Service Workers, All Other wage series for 33-9099.02. A separate figure for this specialty cannot be derived from the broader series. [O*NET wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/33-9099.02)

## How the work meets

A fictional store shows six units in its system and four on the shelf. The salesperson verifies the product, confirms four are available and logs the difference with the shift lead. The loss prevention specialist checks receiving and transfers and finds two may have gone to another store. They return the finding to the store after verifying the transfer.

## How to enter

In the U.S. O*NET survey, 69% of respondents said a new retail loss prevention specialist would need a high school diploma or equivalent to perform the job. BLS lists no formal educational credential and short-term on-the-job training for retail salespersons. Check state requirements if a loss prevention assignment includes regulated security duties. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [O*NET education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/33-9099.02#Education)

## Work conditions

Retail may follow customer traffic and shifts. Loss prevention may handle inventory differences, incident reports and authorized investigations. Ask who receives the store’s report and who can investigate and close it.

## Moving between roles

Moving into loss prevention, use store knowledge and add documentation and authorized discrepancy review. Moving into retail, add product comparisons and customer communication to inventory knowledge.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: six units in the system, four on the shelf and a transfer receipt for two units that has not posted. Spend ten minutes each on a customer update, a review note and who confirms the change.
