# Mathematician vs. Physicist: Testing a Pendulum Model Against Observations

## What each role delivers

Mathematicians derive relationships and identify the conditions under which they hold. Physicists connect theory with observations and experiments to explain physical phenomena, and some specialize in theory.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Mathematician | Physicists |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Model, derivation and approximation bounds | Observation record, physical explanation and model feedback |
| Basis | Definitions, approximations and boundary conditions | Instruments, measurement error, conditions and mechanism |

## How the work meets

A fictional teaching team compares pendulum records at different starting angles. The mathematician identifies a simplified relation that uses a small-angle approximation. The physicist checks starting angles, timing precision and repeated observations, reports whether the differences exceed measurement variation, and sends the results and conditions back to the modeling team.

## How to enter

BLS lists a master’s degree as typical for mathematicians and a doctoral or professional degree for physicists. Ask whether the role focuses on theory, experiment or instruments. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Mathematics follows derivation and review. Experimental physics may also follow equipment and observation windows, while theoretical work follows other schedules. Ask when comparison data become final.

## Moving between roles

Mathematicians can bring modeling to physics and add physical mechanisms and experimental or theoretical expertise. Physicists can bring modeling questions into mathematics and add formal proof and abstraction.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock file: one set of records uses small angles and the other uses larger angles. The timing precision also differs. Spend ten minutes each on model conditions, observation checks, and what would be needed to judge the model from the difference.
