# Credit Analysts vs. Loan Interviewers and Clerks: A Missing Month of Statements

## What each role delivers

Credit analysts evaluate borrower finances and prepare risk assessments. Loan interviewers and clerks collect and verify applicant information and route the file.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Credit Analysts | Loan Interviewers and Clerks |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Cash-flow analysis and risk note | File index and document request log |
| Basis | Statement basis, debts and repayment capacity | Revisions, application fields and attachments |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $83,510 | $50,020 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 13-2041 and 43-4131](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional business submits a file intended to cover six months. The clerk spots the missing final month, checks the requested dates and follows up. The credit analyst limits the current assessment to five months and tells the reviewer the largest scheduled repayment falls in the missing month.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree for credit analysts and high school completion or equivalent with short-term on-the-job training for loan interviewers and clerks. Ask which team handles document intake and which writes credit opinions. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Intake can follow case queues and document requests. Analysis may follow statement and review deadlines. Ask how late evidence reaches the analyst.

## Moving between roles

Moving into analysis, use file checking and add financial statements, cash flow and risk writing. Moving into intake, use financial document familiarity and add file control and case workflow.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: the file should contain six months, only five are present and a large payment is due in the sixth. Spend ten minutes each on a request, the scope of current analysis and what to check upon receipt.
