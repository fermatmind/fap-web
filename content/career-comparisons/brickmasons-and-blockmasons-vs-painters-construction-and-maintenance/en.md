# Brickmasons and Blockmasons vs. Construction Painters: The Wall Handoff

## What each role delivers

Brickmasons and blockmasons deliver masonry to the drawing. Construction and maintenance painters deliver the specified surface finish. Their work includes checking the surface, protecting adjoining finishes and applying the coating.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Brickmasons and Blockmasons | Construction and Maintenance Painters |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Built masonry and layout record | Prepared and coated surface |
| Checks | Block condition, joints and wall geometry | Substrate, sample color and finish boundaries |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $62,120 | $49,400 |

## How the work meets

In a corridor renovation, the masons hand over their wall list. The painter checks the finish schedule and logs two finds: peeling old paint on a door surround and a damaged block at a corner with no explanation in the wall record. The first goes to the paint lead for surface review. The second goes to the site lead to determine whether masonry work is still open.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and apprenticeship as typical for brick and block masons. It describes on-the-job training as the usual path for painters, with apprenticeship also available. Ask which work you will do under supervision and how the employer checks samples or practice work. [BLS occupational rows](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) · [BLS painter training](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/painters-construction-and-maintenance.htm#tab-4)

## Work conditions

Masonry may involve repeated layout checks and moving heavy materials. Painting can require checking samples and protecting finishes across several rooms on a schedule. Ask how frequently sites remain occupied and how the contractor records room-by-room handoffs.

## Moving between roles

A mason can carry over measurement and defect notes into painting, then learn substrate assessment, sample matching and surface protection. A painter moving to masonry brings finish inspection habits and needs supervised experience checking masonry layout and dimensions.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional corridor punch list. A has an approved color sample, B has peeling old paint on a door surround, C has damage to an undocumented masonry corner, and D has newly finished flooring next door. Spend ten minutes sorting the list, ten writing each crew’s handoff, and ten deciding what to keep open for the site lead and what adjacent work to protect. Submit a written log.
