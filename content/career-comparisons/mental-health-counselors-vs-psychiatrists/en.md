# Mental Health Counselor vs. Psychiatrist

## How their clinical work differs

A mental health counselor uses counseling sessions to understand distress, agree on goals and evaluate progress. A psychiatrist is a physician who interprets medical and psychiatric history, can diagnose mental disorders, and can prescribe medication when appropriate. Both may provide psychotherapy within their practice.

## Compare a follow-up visit

| Decision | Mental health counselor | Psychiatrist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What to review | Concerns, coping strategies and effects on daily routines | Symptoms over time, medical history and treatment response |
| What to document | Goals, progress and the next counseling focus | Medical assessment and clinical plan |
| When to coordinate | Share relevant history when authorized | Request relevant records and explain medical follow-up |

## When a schedule change prompts a referral

A fictional client reports disrupted sleep and trouble getting to work after switching shifts. The counselor can ask what changed in the daily routine, document the effect and help arrange a referral if a medical evaluation is appropriate. The psychiatrist can review the symptom timeline, medication list and relevant health history, then decide what assessment is needed. Authorized coordination can convey the timeline and points that still need verification.

## U.S. training and licensure

BLS describes a typical master's degree and internship for mental health counselors, followed by state licensure requirements that include supervised clinical work and an exam. Psychiatry involves medical school and psychiatry residency. All states require physicians to be licensed. Check the state medical board for licensure rules and verify any board certification requirements with the employer.

## Compare working conditions

Ask a counseling practice about session length, note time and referral support. Ask a psychiatry employer how it splits evaluations and follow-ups and whether the position includes inpatient coverage or on-call shifts.

## Moving between roles

Counselors can bring interviewing and progress-tracking skills to medical training. To practice psychiatry they must qualify as physicians and complete specialty training. Psychiatrists considering a dedicated counseling role can reuse clinical interviewing and check which credentials and supervised counseling experience the employer or state board requires.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use a fictional timeline. A shift changed Monday, the client was late Thursday, reported early waking Friday, and has one medication entry that still needs verification. Spend ten minutes drafting two counseling questions and a follow-up topic. Spend ten minutes sorting the timeline and writing questions for a medical evaluation. Use the last ten minutes to draft a short authorized handoff that marks the unverified entry.
