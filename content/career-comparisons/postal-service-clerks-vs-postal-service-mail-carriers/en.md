# Postal Service Clerks vs Postal Service Mail Carriers

## Accept the mail or complete a delivery route

A Postal Service clerk at a retail counter accepts and weighs items, helps customers choose services, and records transactions. A Postal Service mail carrier sorts assigned mail, delivers it on an established route, and records delivery outcomes. Consider whether you prefer handling the public counter or working through a route and its exceptions.

## Compare outputs and U.S. pay

| Item | Postal Service clerks | Postal Service mail carriers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Example setting | Post office retail counter | Delivery unit and assigned route |
| Typical output | Correct mailing transaction and acceptance record | Delivery record and any undelivered item handed back |
| U.S. median annual wage, 2025 | $62,130 | $60,550 |

The figures are national occupational medians. Ask about the appointment type, schedule, and pay terms in the actual posting. [O*NET postal clerks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/43-5051.00) · [O*NET mail carriers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/43-5052.00) · [BLS Table 1.2](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Follow a piece that needs a signature

In a fictional USPS example, a clerk accepts a domestic item with Signature Confirmation and transfers it to the outgoing mailstream. At the destination the carrier finds no one available to sign, records the unsuccessful attempt, leaves PS Form 3849, and returns the item to the delivery unit. The receiving unit holds the item under the applicable USPS service rules. The notice tells the recipient how to pick it up or request redelivery. If asked, a clerk can explain those options using the recorded status. [USPS redelivery instructions](https://faq.usps.com/s/article/Redelivery-The-Basics)

## Apply for the specific job title

BLS lists no formal educational credential and short-term on-the-job training as typical for both occupations. Check USPS hiring conditions separately. Its exam chart lists VEA 477 for roles such as Sales and Services Associate and VEA 474 for listed Mail Carrier roles. USPS lists minimum age and employment eligibility conditions, background screening, and a safe driving record when the position requires it. Check the announcement for the driving duties and qualifications. Follow the instructions for the exact position you are applying to. [BLS entry data](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) · [USPS assessments](https://about.usps.com/careers/how-to-apply/exams.htm) · [USPS employment requirements](https://about.usps.com/careers/how-to-apply/)

## Ask about the counter schedule and the route

Ask a clerk supervisor when customer volume peaks, who balances transactions, and how accepted mail moves out at closing. Ask a delivery supervisor how often routes change, what portion is on foot or by vehicle, and how the unit handles undelivered items at the end of the day. Ask for a schedule from the unit that is hiring.

## Use what you know about the handoff to switch

A clerk moving toward delivery can show how to pass an accepted item with an accurate status. Then practice route sequencing and learn the delivery unit's review of attempted stops. A carrier moving toward the counter can show how to document an exception and communicate it to a customer. Then learn the counter services, pricing and transaction controls. Check the new role's hiring or assessment requirements. [USPS assessments](https://about.usps.com/careers/how-to-apply/exams.htm)

## Try a 30-minute paper handoff

Make three fictional item cards. A was accepted with Signature Confirmation. On the simulated first route attempt, nobody could sign and the carrier left PS Form 3849. B is waiting on the sender to choose a service. C has a delivery signature on file. Spend eight minutes ordering the events, twelve creating either an acceptance handoff or a route return log, and ten checking your output. The counter record should distinguish A from B. The route log should show that A came back after a notice was left and C was delivered. Write down which type of work you would prefer to own.
