# Customer Service Representatives vs. Parts Salespersons: A Replacement Part Does Not Fit

## What each role delivers

Customer service representatives receive issues and track follow-up. Parts salespersons check part numbers and stock, place orders and report availability.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Customer Service Representatives | Parts Salespersons |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Service case and status | Verified part number and stock or order status |
| Basis | Order, reported issue and service authority | Equipment details, part number and stock |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $44,770 | $38,630 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 43-4051 and 41-2022](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional store receives a call about a part that does not fit. Service records the order, equipment model and package number. The parts salesperson finds two versions in the catalog and returns the missing nameplate details and stock status. Service asks the customer for the missing information.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and short-term on-the-job training for customer service representatives. It lists no formal educational credential and moderate-term on-the-job training for parts salespersons. Check product training and return authority with the employer. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Service may follow case deadlines. Parts work may involve counter orders, stock and supplier requests. Ask who updates the customer when a part is back-ordered.

## Moving between roles

Moving into parts sales adds catalog, fit and inventory skills to issue clarification. Moving into service adds escalation and follow-up to parts knowledge.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: the nameplate is incomplete, the catalog shows two candidates and only one is in stock. Spend ten minutes each on a case note, catalog checks and a reply requesting the missing details.
