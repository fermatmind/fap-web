# Reinforcing Iron and Rebar Workers vs. Structural Iron and Steel Workers: Which Column Base Location Applies?

## What each role delivers

Reinforcing iron and rebar workers position and secure reinforcement inside concrete. Structural iron and steel workers erect, align and connect structural members.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Reinforcing Iron and Rebar Workers | Structural Iron and Steel Workers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Reinforcement placement record | Steel member alignment and connection record |
| Basis | Reinforcement drawing and spacing | Erection drawing, member ID and connections |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $58,970 | $62,780 |

[O*NET 47-2171.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-2171.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 47-2221.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-2221.00#Tasks)

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 47-2171 and 47-2221](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

At a fictional plant, a steel column will sit on a concrete base. The rebar crew supplies its pre-pour placement record. The steel crew finds that the opening location differs from its erection drawing. Both submit their drawing IDs and measurement points for review.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and apprenticeship for both. Ask employers and apprenticeship providers whether the training covers reinforcement, structural erection or both. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Reinforcement follows structural sequences and pre-pour checks. Steel work follows member delivery, alignment and connections. Ask who verifies each handoff and authorizes site tasks.

## Moving between roles

Moving to structural steel, bring drawing and placement skills and learn erection drawings and connections. Moving to reinforcement, add reinforcing plans and pre-pour documentation to alignment work.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: the reinforcement record shows the base opening at B3, the column base drawing shows B4, both revisions are issued, and review is open. Spend ten minutes each on the reinforcement handoff, steel discrepancy and question for the technical lead. Use records only.
