# Tour Guides and Escorts vs. Travel Guides: A Gallery Closure on a Multi-Stop Trip

## What each role delivers

Tour guides and escorts lead visitors and explain points of interest. Travel guides also escort groups, with an emphasis on planning and conducting longer or multi-stop trips, including transport and other logistics. Assignments can overlap.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Tour guides and escorts | Travel guides |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Site route, interpretation and exit update | Multi-stop itinerary, transport handoff and group message |
| Basis | Open exhibits, interpretation and site rules | Group roster, travel route and later bookings |

## How the work meets

A fictional two-day group visits a historic district. The site guide learns that the main hall has closed and, with venue approval, leads an alternative courtyard tour and passes the estimated finish time to the travel guide. The travel guide checks the roster, bus window and next museum booking, then confirms the departure plan with the operator and tells the group.

## How to enter

BLS groups these occupations as tour and travel guides and lists high school completion or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for the combined category. Ask whether the assignment focuses on site interpretation or multi-stop logistics, and check any requirements for the actual location and duties. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Site tours follow visitor questions, admission times and venue rules. Multi-stop trips also follow transport, lodging and handoff times. Ask who takes responsibility for the group roster at each stop.

## Moving between roles

Site guides can build on interpretation and guest communication by learning itinerary planning and transport or lodging handoffs. Travel guides moving into site work can use group management skills and learn site history, rules and interpretation.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock schedule: the alternate tour takes 25 minutes, the coach leaves in 30 and gathering at the exit takes at least eight. Spend ten minutes each on the site route, departure options and a handoff and group message that state what can be confirmed.
