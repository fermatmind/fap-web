# Environmental Compliance Inspector vs. Regulatory Affairs Specialist: Which Permit Version Applies?

## What each role delivers

An environmental compliance inspector compares site activity and evidence with applicable requirements. A regulatory affairs specialist identifies submission routes, coordinates evidence and responds to agency questions.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Environmental Compliance Inspectors | Regulatory affairs specialists |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Inspection findings and comparison to current terms | Submission package, version log and responses to agency questions |
| Basis | Effective date, permit text and field evidence | Filing scope, drawing versions, attachments and agency response |

## How the work meets

A fictional facility assigns its regulatory affairs specialist to submit a revised drainage plan. The applicable authority decides whether to approve the change. The regulatory affairs specialist organizes the drawings and confirmation of receipt. Without an approval date, the specialist keeps the current permit indexed for operations. The inspector checks the permit and outlet records and records the filing status and any evidence still needed.

## How to enter

Inspection candidates can show a permit comparison and field record. Regulatory affairs candidates can show a submission inventory and response tracker. Ask which regulator and product or permit the role covers.

## Work conditions

Inspection follows field visits and follow-up. Regulatory affairs follows filing, information requests and review milestones. Ask who communicates a formally effective change.

## Moving between roles

Inspectors bring interpretation skills to regulatory affairs and can add filing structure and version control. Regulatory affairs staff can bring documentation skills into inspection and learn field evidence and inspection authority.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock file: the old permit is current, the agency acknowledged receipt of new drawings but issued no approval, and the site uses the original outlet. Spend ten minutes each on the filing status, the inspection basis and the evidence needed to update the operative version.
