# Plumber, Pipefitter, and Steamfitter vs. Stationary Engineer and Boiler Operator

## Piping work and operating the equipment

Plumbers, pipefitters, and steamfitters may install or repair the piping that serves a facility. Stationary engineers and boiler operators run boilers and auxiliary equipment and hand off operating status. A steam-line maintenance job can involve both crews.

## Compare the work products

| Work | Piping trades | Stationary engineers and boiler operators |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check | Piping scope, connections, and work records | Equipment status and shift records |
| Handoff | Completed piping work and open acceptance items | Equipment status and items for the next shift |

In May 2025, U.S. median annual wages were $63,800 for plumbers, pipefitters, and steamfitters and $78,620 for stationary engineers and boiler operators. Both are BLS OEWS occupation-wide medians. [BLS piping wages](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/plumbers-pipefitters-and-steamfitters.htm#tab-5) · [BLS stationary engineer and boiler operator wages](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/production/stationary-engineers-and-boiler-operators.htm#tab-5)

## U.S. training and qualification

BLS describes apprenticeship as the usual route for the piping trades. Most states and some localities license plumbers. For stationary engineers and boiler operators, a high school diploma or equivalent plus supervised training or apprenticeship is typical. Some jurisdictions require licensing, and employers may require a competency exam before independent work. Check the rules for the equipment and location. [BLS piping entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/plumbers-pipefitters-and-steamfitters.htm#tab-4) · [BLS boiler operator entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/production/stationary-engineers-and-boiler-operators.htm#tab-4)

## Compare worksites and shifts

Piping jobs can involve travel to homes, businesses, and industrial sites. Stationary engineers and boiler operators typically work in boiler rooms and mechanical rooms. Facilities that run continuously may use rotating shifts. Ask which sites you cover, when shifts change, and who reviews a handoff. [BLS piping work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/plumbers-pipefitters-and-steamfitters.htm#tab-3) · [BLS boiler operator work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/production/stationary-engineers-and-boiler-operators.htm#tab-3)

## Moving between the roles

A pipefitter can bring experience reading steam-system drawings into operating work, then learn shift documentation and complete equipment-specific training. An operator can bring facility knowledge and careful handoff records into piping, then build installation skills and check the trade requirements.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use three fictional records. The work order says pipe section P7 is complete. The acceptance signature is blank. The shift log lists P7 as awaiting formal handoff.
