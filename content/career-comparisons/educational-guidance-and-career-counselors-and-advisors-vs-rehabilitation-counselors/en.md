# Educational, Guidance, and Career Counselors and Advisors vs. Rehabilitation Counselors: Who Helps With an Internship Choice?

## What each role delivers

Education and career counselors and advisors help students or job seekers compare programs and career paths. Rehabilitation counselors work with clients with disabilities on employment or independent living goals and coordinate support for barriers.

## The same internship decision

Imagine a community college student who uses a wheelchair and is considering two internships. The career advisor checks the duties, credit requirements, and application dates. With the student's permission, the rehabilitation counselor checks site access and transportation with the student. One site has yet to confirm a step-free entrance. The counselor identifies who will ask and when to check back, and the student uses both sets of findings to decide.

## Getting started in the U.S.

Most school counselors need a master's degree and a state-issued credential. Some states require career counselors or advisors to be licensed. Rehabilitation counselors typically need a master's degree in rehabilitation counseling or a related field. Some jobs accept a bachelor's degree and some require a license or certification. Check the requirements for the particular role and setting. [BLS school and career counselor entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/school-and-career-counselors.htm#tab-4) · [BLS rehabilitation counselor entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/rehabilitation-counselors.htm#tab-4)

## Working conditions

Advising may follow enrollment and application deadlines. Rehabilitation counseling may depend on responses from a client and several providers. Ask who keeps follow-up records and what the student may agree to share with a site.

## Moving between roles

Advisors can carry over interviewing and comparing options and learn disability-related employment support. Rehabilitation counselors can carry over case coordination and learn education requirements and internship pathways. Verify destination-role credentials.
