# School and Career Counselor vs. Marriage and Family Therapist

## Academic decisions and family relationships

Educational, guidance, and career counselors and advisors help students make education and career choices and respond to barriers at school. Marriage and family therapists develop treatment goals that account for relationships and patterns of interaction.

## Compare the work each person documents

| Decision | School or career counselor and advisor | Marriage and family therapist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assess the situation | Course requirements, student preferences and scheduling constraints | How family members negotiate and respond to one another |
| Document | Advising options, an approval owner and follow-up | Treatment goals, progress and issues to revisit |
| Coordinate | Student and authorized academic staff | Participating clients and authorized care partners |

[O*NET 21-1012.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/21-1012.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 21-1013.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/21-1013.00#Tasks)

## Family pickup conflicts with a make-up lab

A fictional student can make up a lab on Tuesday but is due to pick up a younger sibling. The school advisor can confirm make-up rules and find out who can approve another time. If the family is already in therapy, its therapist can explore how discussions repeatedly end without a clear agreement and help the family try a new way to confirm responsibilities. With authorization, the teams can coordinate on availability while keeping the details of family sessions within the care setting.

## U.S. education and credentials

BLS says school counselors typically need a master's degree and state-issued credential, and counseling graduate programs usually include supervised experience. Career counseling licensure and employer expectations for advising vary. Marriage and family therapists typically need a master's degree. All states require licensure, including postdegree supervised clinical experience and a state-recognized exam. Verify the state and employer rules for the position. [BLS counselor and advisor entry guidance](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/school-and-career-counselors.htm#tab-4) · [BLS marriage and family therapist entry guidance](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/marriage-and-family-therapists.htm#tab-4)

## Ask how appointments and coverage are scheduled

Ask a school about appointment demand during registration or graduation, and who handles exceptions. Ask a family therapy practice about evening appointments, documentation time and supervision or case consultation.

## Moving from one track to the other

School and career advisers can bring interviewing, referral and progress-tracking skills, then pursue training in family dynamics and qualifying clinical experience. Family therapists can bring experience with shared goals and complex conversations to advising, then learn course rules, career information and school record procedures.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use a fictional lab calendar, a Tuesday pickup duty and a family note saying no one has confirmed who can cover it. Spend ten minutes listing two advising options with facts to verify, ten drafting two open questions about how the family agrees on responsibilities, and ten marking who approves a change and what could be shared with authorization.
