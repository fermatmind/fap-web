# Customer Service Representative vs. Postal Service Clerk: When a Postage Estimate Becomes a Price

## What each role delivers

A customer service representative answers questions and tracks cases. A postal service clerk checks mailing conditions, determines postage and accepts mail at the counter.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Customer Service Representatives | Postal Service Clerks |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Service explanation, estimate inputs and case note | Parcel check, postage and acceptance record |
| Basis | Sender information, options and authority | Measured weight, size, destination and mailing conditions |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $44,770 | $62,130 |

National employee wage medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2 rows 43-4051 and 43-5051](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional sender asks by phone how much it costs to mail two books. The representative explains options using the weight given and notes that packaging is excluded. The clerk weighs the packed parcel, checks the destination and selected service and provides the final price for the customer to accept before recording the mailing.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent as typical for customer service representatives and no formal educational credential for postal clerks. It lists short-term on-the-job training for both. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) For USPS employment, its general age rule is 18 at appointment or 16 with a high school diploma. Check the posting for other requirements. [USPS employment requirements](https://about.usps.com/careers/how-to-apply/welcome.htm)

## Work conditions

Service roles follow contact queues. Counter jobs also involve handling mail, payments and receipts as customers arrive. Ask how an estimate discrepancy gets recorded.

## Moving between roles

Representatives can bring explanation skills to counter work and add postage and acceptance checks. Clerks moving into service can bring mailing knowledge and add remote case handling and follow-up.
