# Brickmasons and Blockmasons vs. Carpet Installers: The Doorway Handoff

## What each role delivers

Brickmasons and blockmasons lay out and build masonry. Carpet installers plan seams for traffic and fit carpet at edges and openings.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Brickmasons and Blockmasons | Carpet Installers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Masonry partition to drawing | Carpet layout and edge finish |
| Basis | Wall line, opening and dimensions | Traffic, seams and cuts |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $62,120 | $50,340 |

## How the work meets

In a fictional office, a partition moves. The mason hands over opening measurements. The carpet installer sees that the old seam plan runs through the main traffic path and revises it after the measurements and drawing are confirmed.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and an apprenticeship for brickmasons and blockmasons. It lists no formal educational credential and short-term on-the-job training for carpet installers. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Masonry handoffs can follow completion of wall sections. Carpet work depends on measured room edges and traffic paths. Ask who accepts the interface.

## Moving between roles

Moving to carpet, bring drawing and measurement skills and add layout and seam work. Moving to masonry, bring opening measurements and learn wall layout through supervised training.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock drawing A gives a 900 mm opening, the field record shows 930 mm and the former carpet seam crosses the main traffic path. Spend ten minutes each on a masonry handoff, two seam options and who must confirm the opening before cutting.
