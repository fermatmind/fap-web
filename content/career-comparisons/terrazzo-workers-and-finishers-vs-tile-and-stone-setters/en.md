# Terrazzo Workers and Finishers vs. Tile and Stone Setters: Joining Two Floor Patterns

## What each role delivers

Terrazzo workers and finishers set dividers, place material and finish the surface. Tile and stone setters fit individual pieces and align and finish joints.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Terrazzo Workers and Finishers | Tile and Stone Setters |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Terrazzo panels and finish record | Tile placement and joint record |
| Basis | Dividers, color fields and surface | Substrate, tile width and joints |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $76,170 | $55,690 |

[O*NET 47-2053.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-2053.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 47-2044.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-2044.00#Tasks)

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 47-2053 and 47-2044](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional corridor changes from terrazzo at the entrance to tile. The terrazzo crew records the fixed dividers. The tile crew finds only one joint lines up in the trial and asks the lead which alignment is required.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and apprenticeship for terrazzo workers and finishers. It lists no formal educational credential and long-term on-the-job training for tile and stone setters. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Terrazzo crews may follow panel and finish stages. Tile crews may work by area, piece and joint. Ask who accepts the transition and protects each finished surface.

## Moving between roles

Moving to tile, bring substrate and layout skills and learn piece placement and border cuts. Moving to terrazzo, bring finish inspection and learn dividers, material placement and polishing.
