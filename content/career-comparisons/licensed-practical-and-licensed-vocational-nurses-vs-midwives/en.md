# LPN or LVN vs. Midwife

## Assigned nursing care and midwifery care

Licensed practical and licensed vocational nurses provide assigned care and monitor and report changes. This comparison uses O*NET's Midwives occupation, which includes direct-entry midwife and Certified Professional Midwife titles. Midwives assess and coordinate pregnancy and birth care within their jurisdiction and authority.

## Compare the records

| Item | LPN or LVN | Midwife |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verify | Assigned care, reported concerns and observations | Prenatal information, care plan and issues needing assessment |
| Document | What was reported, when and to whom | Midwifery assessment and next steps within scope |
| Coordinate | Notify the designated supervising clinician | Coordinate care or referral under state and facility rules |

[O*NET 29-2061.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-2061.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 29-9099.01](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-9099.01#Tasks)

## One new concern at a prenatal visit

At a fictional prenatal practice, an LVN assists with intake within state and facility rules, under the direction of a licensed nurse or physician authorized to supervise that work. The LVN documents a new concern and alerts both the responsible midwife and the nursing supervisor through the designated process. The midwife reviews the information and coordinates follow-up within their own scope. [BLS practical nursing duties and supervision](https://www.bls.gov/OOH/healthcare/licensed-practical-and-licensed-vocational-nurses.htm#tab-2)

## U.S. education and credentials

For practical or vocational nursing, BLS describes a state-approved nursing program, an exam and state licensure. For midwifery under this occupational profile, identify the specific credential pathway and state authorization. For example, NARM sets eligibility for the Certified Professional Midwife examination and credential. State regulators determine licensure eligibility separately. O*NET lists Nurse Midwives under a separate occupation. [BLS LPN and LVN entry](https://www.bls.gov/OOH/healthcare/licensed-practical-and-licensed-vocational-nurses.htm#tab-4) · [NARM CPM certification](https://narm.org/certification-recertification/) For CPM certification, candidates document education and supervised clinical experience through a NARM-recognized pathway, meet its eligibility criteria, pass the NARM exam and satisfy any remaining credential requirements. [NARM certification process](https://info.narm.org/article/23-overview-of-the-cpm-certification-process)

## Ask about the work schedule

LPN or LVN applicants can ask about assignments, charting time and supervision. Midwifery applicants can ask how the practice covers prenatal visits, births and follow-up, and who handles referrals outside its scope.

## Moving between the roles

LPNs or LVNs can bring patient communication and handoff skills to midwifery study. They need to verify the required education, clinical practice and state authorization for the intended pathway. A midwife moving into practical nursing can bring patient documentation skills and must qualify for the applicable nursing license.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use a fictional prenatal schedule, a new patient concern and a record with one missing field. Spend ten minutes drafting a nursing handoff, ten writing a verification list for the midwife, and ten naming both the midwifery recipient and the nursing supervisor, with a follow-up owner for each item. Use records only.
