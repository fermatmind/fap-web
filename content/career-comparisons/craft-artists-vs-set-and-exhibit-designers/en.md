# Craft Artist vs. Set and Exhibit Designer: Fitting a Handmade Piece Into a Gallery Layout

## What each role delivers

A craft artist delivers a finished handmade piece and material notes. A set and exhibit designer develops a spatial layout and drawings, making revisions for function, site constraints and budget.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Craft artists | Set and Exhibit Designers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Finished work, dimensions and display precautions | Scaled layout, pedestal specifications and installation notes |
| Basis | Form, finish and object dimensions | Floor plan, viewing direction and fabrication constraints |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $46,080 | $75,240 |

## How the work meets

A fictional traveling show will display a woven piece. The craft artist supplies its dimensions, intended front and surfaces to avoid touching. The exhibit designer sees that the original pedestal restricts the aisle, checks a narrower pedestal with venue staff and asks the artist to verify where the piece can bear on it. The designer then updates the layout for the installers.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and long-term on-the-job training as typical for craft artists and a bachelor’s degree for set and exhibit designers. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Craft production follows materials and fabrication steps. Exhibit design often follows approval, fabrication and load-in dates. Ask who tells installers when an object’s dimensions change.

## Moving between roles

Craft artists can bring material and object knowledge to exhibits and add scale drawings, visitor circulation and budgets. Exhibit designers can bring prototyping and fabrication communication to craft and add specific handwork and finishing skills.
