# Retail Loss Prevention Specialists vs. Security Managers: Who Follows Up on a Return Discrepancy?

## The work each role delivers

A retail loss prevention specialist checks store procedures, reports shortages and documents discrepancies. A security manager coordinates site protection and can organize follow-up across shifts and review operating procedures.

## Compare the work

| Compare | Retail loss prevention specialist | Security manager |
|---|---|---|
| Focus here | What differs between return and handoff records? | Who takes the next check, and does the procedure need revision? |
| Output | Discrepancy summary and supporting records | Review scope, owner, and deadline |

## The same discrepancy

Suppose a store compares its return log with the shift handoff and sees two returns logged in the system, with only one confirmed in the shift handoff. The specialist checks return IDs and shift notes, then reports which item is confirmed and which still needs a receiving record. The manager coordinates with store operations to assign the next check and reviews similar cases before proposing a change to the handoff form.

For management, show how you assigned follow-up and verified completion. Check state and employer requirements for the actual duties. ## Working conditions

Specialists may document issues during store shifts. Managers also coordinate reviews across teams and follow up on the result. Ask when handoffs are due and who takes over an issue at shift change.

## Moving between roles

Specialists can bring store documentation and audit experience into management, then build skills in coordinating shifts and corrective work. Managers can bring procedural knowledge into store-level work and practice reconciling entries and writing item-level discrepancy notes.

## Try both in 30 minutes

Use three fictional records. The system lists one unit each for returns A and B. The day shift confirmed receipt of A and left B blank. The evening shift has yet to submit its record. Spend 10 minutes listing confirmed and open items, 10 assigning a review owner and deadline, and 10 writing a handoff for the next shift.
