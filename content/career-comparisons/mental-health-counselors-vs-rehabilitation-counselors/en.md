# Mental Health Counselors vs. Rehabilitation Counselors: Who Helps With a Return-to-Work Meeting?

## What each role delivers

A mental health counselor provides counseling and develops and reviews a treatment plan. A rehabilitation counselor works with clients with disabilities on employment or independent living goals and coordinates services.

## Compare the work

| Compare | Mental health counselor | Rehabilitation counselor |
|---|---|---|
| Focus here | Distress about the meeting and its effect on daily life | Agenda, job information, and practical supports |
| Output | Counseling plan and follow-up notes | Return-to-work support plan and follow-up |

[O*NET 21-1014.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/21-1014.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 21-1015.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/21-1015.00#Tasks)

## The same return-to-work meeting

Suppose a client worries about explaining their needs in a return-to-work meeting and has yet to receive an agenda. The mental health counselor explores how the concern affects daily life and agrees on what to revisit in counseling. The client tells the rehabilitation counselor they would like a written agenda in advance and gives permission to share that request. The rehabilitation counselor asks the employer for the agenda and follows up with the client about the response and next steps.

## Getting started in the U.S.

BLS says mental health counselors typically need a master's degree and an internship, and all states require them to be licensed. Rehabilitation counselors typically need a master's degree in rehabilitation counseling or a related field. Some jobs accept a bachelor's degree, and some require a license or certification. Check the state and role you are considering. [BLS mental health counselor entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/substance-abuse-behavioral-disorder-and-mental-health-counselors.htm#tab-4) · [BLS rehabilitation counselor entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/rehabilitation-counselors.htm#tab-4)

## Working conditions

Mental health counseling includes sessions, confidential notes, and time for supervision where applicable. Rehabilitation counseling may involve responses from clients, employers, and service providers. Ask who maintains records and who follows up outside the organization.

## Moving between roles

Both use interviewing and goal-setting skills. Moving into mental health counseling requires clinical training and supervision. Moving into rehabilitation counseling requires training in employment services and coordination. Verify the destination role's qualifications.

## Try both in 30 minutes

Use three fictional notes. Thinking about the meeting makes it hard for the client to focus. The employer has yet to send an agenda. The client consents to share only their request for a written agenda. Spend 10 minutes writing counseling questions and a follow-up note, 10 listing practical supports to verify, and 10 drafting the employer inquiry.
