# Concierge vs. Travel Guides: Who Takes Charge at the Tour Pickup?

## What each role delivers

A concierge helps guests arrange local services and get to a pickup point. Travel guides assemble groups, lead the itinerary and handle disruptions along the route.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Concierge | Travel guides |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Individual transfer and verified pickup directions | Group check-in, departure plan and itinerary update |
| Basis | Guest location, transfer time and operator instructions | Roster, route, transport capacity and stop times |

[O*NET 39-6012.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/39-6012.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 39-7012.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/39-7012.00#Tasks)

## How the work meets

A fictional tour operator moves a coach pickup because the hotel’s east gate stop is closed. The concierge contacts two guests still at the hotel, checks walking and transfer times and shares their estimated arrival through the approved channel. At the new stop the guide checks the roster, reports the missing guests to the operator and follows its policy on holding or departing. The group already at the stop gets an update.

## How to enter

BLS lists a high school diploma or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training as typical for concierges and for its combined tour and travel guides category. Ask employers which trips you would lead and check whether the specific location or activity requires credentials. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

A concierge coordinates requests from a service desk or similar point. A travel guide works with the group and responds to delays en route. Ask who publishes pickup changes and who follows up with late guests.

## Moving between roles

Concierge experience transfers in guest intake and transport coordination. Travel guides add roster checks, route execution and credentials applicable to the work. Guides moving into concierge work can build on guest communication by learning hotel services and local provider procedures.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock notice: the coach leaves the new stop at 9 a.m. and can wait until 9:05. At 8:55, a guest is still at the hotel. Walking takes eight minutes; waiting for a transfer and riding takes twelve. Spend ten minutes each on directions, the guide’s roster note and an update for the late guest. Distinguish an expected arrival before 9:05 from a confirmed seat.
