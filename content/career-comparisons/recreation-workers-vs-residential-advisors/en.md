# Recreation workers vs. residential advisors

## Lead an activity or support a residence

Recreation workers plan and lead activities. Residential advisors coordinate day-to-day residence needs, address resident concerns and may refer residents to other support. Both can organize social events. A residential advisor's work continues between events and across shifts.

## Compare the roles

| Item | Recreation worker | Residential advisor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deliverable | An activity plan and record of participation | A shift handoff and follow-up on resident needs |
| Setting to ask about | A center, activity room or outdoor program space | A dormitory, group home or similar residence |
| Typical U.S. entry education | High school diploma or equivalent | High school diploma or equivalent |
| Typical U.S. on-the-job training | Short-term | Short-term |
| 2025 U.S. median annual wage | $36,560 | $42,240 |

Wages cover nonfarm wage and salary workers and exclude self-employment. ## A game night runs close to quiet hours

At a fictional residence hall, a recreation worker wants to extend a board game. The residential advisor checks the house rule, explains that the common room must be cleared by 9 p.m. and reports a resident concern about noise. The recreation worker chooses a shorter round and arranges cleanup by 9. The advisor updates the resident and passes any open issue to the next shift.

## Getting started in the U.S.

For recreation work, prepare an activity plan and ask how the employer trains staff to lead groups. For residential work, practice a mock shift handoff and ask about the duties, site-specific hiring conditions, and referral or escalation training. Ask the employer whether the role serves a college residence or another type of facility. [BLS entry preparation](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Ask what happens after the event

Ask the recreation team how many sessions run in a shift and when room cleanup is due. Ask the residential team how duty rotates, how concerns are logged and who responds when another department needs to take over.

## Moving between the roles

A recreation worker can bring a redacted activity log and participant communication record, then learn residence rules, shift handoffs and referral procedures. A residential advisor can bring a redacted residence event brief and handoff, then develop activity design, material planning and group facilitation.

## A 30-minute tabletop exercise

Use a fictional residence hall. The common room must be clear by 9 p.m., and at 8:45 the last round has not started. A full round takes twenty minutes, a shortened one takes ten, and either needs five minutes for cleanup. Spend ten minutes comparing the options, ten drafting the activity leader’s wrap-up, and ten writing the residential advisor’s update and handoff. Mark the time cutoff and who implements each part.
