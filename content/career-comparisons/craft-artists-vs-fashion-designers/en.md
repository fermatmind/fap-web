# Craft Artist vs. Fashion Designer: Choosing a Handmade Trim for a Collection

## What each role delivers

A craft artist makes objects and samples with particular materials and finishes. A fashion designer develops apparel or accessories for an intended wearer, writes specifications and adjusts designs after reviewing samples.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Craft artists | Fashion Designers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Trim samples and material and finish notes | Collection choices, placement and sample specifications |
| Basis | Craft finish, physical weight and making time | Garment style, intended use, cost and sample fit |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $46,080 | $80,960 |

## How the work meets

A fictional label plans a small run of jackets. The craft artist makes two woven trims and reports their weights. The fashion designer tries both at the shoulder and pocket on a sample, checks drape, movement and cost, and sends the selected placement and specifications to the sample team. The craft artist confirms how long the run would take.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and long-term on-the-job training as typical for craft artists, and a bachelor’s degree as typical for fashion designers. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Craft work can follow material arrivals and handwork schedules. Fashion design can follow seasonal deadlines, samples and reviews. Ask which other styles are affected by replacing a trim.

## Moving between roles

Material development transfers from craft into fashion; add body proportions, garment construction and collection planning. Designers moving into craft can build on material selection by learning hand techniques, finishes and quality checks.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock design: trim A weighs 18 grams and 20 pieces are available. Trim B weighs 30 grams and 12 are available. Ten jackets need two pieces each with a combined weight of no more than 40 grams per jacket. Spend ten minutes each on supply notes, a viable trim choice and checks still needed on the sample.
