# Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers vs. Team Assemblers: An Unfinished Check at Shift Change

## What each role delivers

Electrical and electronic equipment assemblers install and check electrical components. Team assemblers can rotate between tasks in a production process and verify work against specifications.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers | Team Assemblers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Electrical assemblies and inspection records | Station rotation and cross-station handoff |
| Basis | Wiring specifications and electrical checks | Work orders, assignments and quality checks |

## How the work meets

At shift change on a fictional line, packaging is ready but one wiring recheck is missing. The electrical assembler identifies the open item. The team assembler uses the station qualification record to coordinate the handoff and keep the check open.

## How to enter

For electrical assembly, show wiring and inspection work. For team assembly, show checks and handoffs from more than one station. Ask how the employer verifies readiness by station.

## Work conditions

Electrical jobs may focus on components and connections. Team assembly may change with a rotation schedule. Ask which stations rotate and which checks require further authorization.

## Moving between roles

Moving to team assembly, carry over inspection discipline and train on other stations. Moving to electrical assembly, build on rotation experience and learn wiring drawings and circuit checks.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a mock station card: A finished wiring but lacks a recheck, B is waiting to package, and the incoming worker is authorized for packaging only. Spend ten minutes each on the electrical exception, the shift handoff and who must confirm the open check before packaging.
