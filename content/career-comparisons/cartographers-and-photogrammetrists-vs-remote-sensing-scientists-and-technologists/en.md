# Cartographers and Photogrammetrists vs. Remote Sensing Scientists and Technologists: From Classification to a Wetland Map

## What each role delivers

Cartographers and photogrammetrists compile imagery and survey sources and check map products. Remote sensing scientists and technologists analyze sensor data and document the quality and limits of results.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Cartographers and Photogrammetrists | Remote Sensing Scientists and Technologists |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Map and feature quality record | Remote sensing classification and quality notes |
| Basis | Features, scale and representation | Image date, classes and validation |

[O*NET 17-1021.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-1021.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 19-2099.01](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-2099.01#Tasks)

O*NET uses the Physical Scientists, All Other wage series for 19-2099.01. A separate figure for this specialty cannot be derived from the broader series. [O*NET wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/19-2099.01)

## How the work meets

A fictional team updates a wetland map. The remote sensing specialist provides a classification and notes seasonal flooding. The cartographer finds an unverified stretch would read as a confirmed boundary and flags it for review in the legend and map.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for cartography and photogrammetry; it notes that some jobs may require licensure. A bachelor’s degree was the most frequent O*NET education response for remote sensing scientists and technologists. Ask which work the employer assigns and verify licensing for the actual task by jurisdiction. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [O*NET education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-2099.01#Education) [BLS licensing overview](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/cartographers-and-photogrammetrists.htm)

## Work conditions

Map work may follow data revisions and publication checks. Remote sensing research may follow image batches, sampling and validation. Ask who maintains mapped features and who reviews classification results.

## Moving between roles

Moving to remote sensing, combine spatial data skills with sensor and classification validation. Moving to cartography, bring imagery interpretation and learn feature representation and map delivery.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: both A and B are classified as wetland. A has field validation, while B has only wet-season imagery. Spend ten minutes each on a classification note, a map legend and the missing evidence for B.
