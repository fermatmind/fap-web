# School and Career Counselor vs. Residential Advisor

## Academic options and residence life

Educational, guidance, and career counselors and advisors help students make academic and career decisions. Residential advisors address residence rules, roommate concerns and issues that need follow-up from housing staff.

## Compare the records and who can act

| Decision | School or career counselor and advisor | Residential advisor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verify | Student goals, course requirements and options | Residence policy, reported concerns and agreements |
| Document | Advising choices and a follow-up date | Residence follow-up and the person responsible |
| Escalate | Ask who approves a course change or exception | Ask who approves a room change or handles an appeal |

[O*NET 21-1012.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/21-1012.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 39-9041.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/39-9041.00#Tasks)

## A residence conflict affects lab attendance

At a fictional college, roommates keep different hours and one misses a morning lab. The residential advisor can hear both accounts, discuss quiet hours and record a follow-up. An academic advisor can verify make-up options, a different lab section and any effect on prerequisites. Each office follows its own process and shares only information it is authorized to pass on.

## U.S. education and training

BLS says school counselors typically need a master's degree and a state-issued credential. Career counselor licensure applies in some states, and employers can set different requirements for academic or career advisors. BLS assigns residential advisors high school completion or equivalent, no related work experience and short-term on-the-job training. Ask the housing employer whether its opening is a student appointment or a staff job and what orientation and eligibility it requires. The national BLS table assigns the combined education and career counselor category a master’s degree and no related work experience or on-the-job training. Compare that broad category with the requirements for the position you want. [BLS school and career counselor entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/school-and-career-counselors.htm#tab-4) · [BLS detailed training table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/education-and-training-by-occupation.htm)

## Ask how the role covers peak hours

Academic advisors may have heavier appointment loads near registration and graduation. Residential advisors should ask about night and weekend coverage, on-site expectations and whom to contact when a concern requires follow-up.

## Moving between roles

Residential advisors can bring student communication and escalation skills into advising, then learn degree requirements, career resources and student-record rules. Advisors can bring interviewing and case follow-up experience into residence life, then learn housing policy, conflict mediation and shift procedures.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use a fictional quiet-hours policy, a missed lab entry and a make-up notice. Spend ten minutes writing a housing follow-up with one date, ten drafting two advising options and conditions to confirm, and ten marking recipients, approval owners and what can be shared with authorization.
