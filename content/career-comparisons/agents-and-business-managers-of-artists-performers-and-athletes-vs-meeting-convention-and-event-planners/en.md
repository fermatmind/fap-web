# Talent Agent or Business Manager vs. Event Planner: Who Confirms a New Performance Time?

## What each role delivers

Agents and business managers represent clients in negotiations and arrange engagements. Meeting, convention, and event planners turn an event brief into venue bookings, supplier arrangements and an operating schedule.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Agents and business managers of artists, performers and athletes | Meeting, Convention, and Event Planners |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Client availability, fee and authorized terms | Approved run of show and vendor notice |
| Basis | Client preferences, existing terms and delegated authority | Room time, equipment, budget and adjacent sessions |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $82,890 | $61,160 |

## How the work meets

At a fictional convention, the production team asks to move a performance from 4 to 5 p.m. The planner checks the room, sound crew, next session and overtime cost. The agent checks the performer’s travel schedule and appearance terms, then confirms within their authority whether the performer can accept and which terms would change. The planner circulates the updated schedule once the parties confirm.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for both occupations and less than five years of related work experience for agents and business managers. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) California requires a Labor Commissioner license to operate a talent agency. Check whether the role involves obtaining artist engagements. Obtaining recording contracts alone does not trigger licensing under this chapter. [California Division of Labor Standards Enforcement](https://dir.ca.gov/dlse/talent_agency_license.html)

## Work conditions

Agents may work around negotiations and client availability. Planners may face peaks during preparation, setup and the live event. Ask who confirms late schedule changes.

## Moving between roles

Vendor communication transfers to planning, where you add event budgeting, equipment and site operations. A planner moving into representation can build on delivery records by learning client authority, contract negotiation and long-term scheduling.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock constraints: the room is open at 4 p.m. but sound staffing is unconfirmed. At 5 p.m. both are available, but the performer must leave at 5:30 and needs 45 minutes to perform and clear the stage. Spend ten minutes each on the agent’s reply, planning options and the checks required before announcing a change.
