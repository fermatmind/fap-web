# Electronics Engineers vs. Robotics Engineers: The Pick Signal and the Robot’s Next Move

## What each role delivers

Electronics engineers may deliver a sensor interface and circuit verification. Robotics engineers integrate sensor data, programs and motion and test whether the system performs its task.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electronics Engineers, Except Computer | Robotics Engineers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Sensor interface and circuit verification | Robot behavior and system tests |
| Basis | Signal and interface specifications | Sensor data, movement states and task requirements |

## How the work meets

A fictional picking cell displays the same notice at two steps. The electronics engineer checks interface records for when the signal arrived. The robotics engineer matches it to task states and simulation logs and flags a step for authorized verification.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for electronics engineering. In robotics, check whether the employer expects research, application integration or commissioning, and show evidence from the relevant work. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Electronics work may follow changes to boards and interfaces. Robotics work may follow simulation, integration and site acceptance windows. Ask how the team divides authorized verification.

## Moving between roles

Moving into robotics, bring sensor interface skills and add motion and task logic. Moving into electronics, bring system integration experience and add circuit design and signal verification.
