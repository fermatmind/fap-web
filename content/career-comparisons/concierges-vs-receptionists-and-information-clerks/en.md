# Concierge vs. Receptionists and Information Clerks: An Early Visitor

## What each role delivers

A concierge arranges services that make a visit easier. Receptionists and information clerks determine why visitors have arrived, contact the host, give directions and maintain appointment information.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Concierge | Receptionists and Information Clerks |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Service options while the visitor waits | Appointment or visitor record, host response and routing |
| Basis | Timing, public-area options and provider information | Visitor name, appointment time, purpose and host confirmation |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $38,950 | $38,010 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2 rows 39-6012 and 43-4171](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

At a fictional office building, a visitor arrives 20 minutes early. Reception checks the location, contacts the host and records that clearance is pending. The concierge points out the public waiting area and water station, including the step-free route. Once the host confirms they are ready, reception updates the visit status and directs the visitor under site procedure.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent as typical for both roles, with moderate-term on-the-job training for concierges and short-term training for receptionists and information clerks. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Reception may switch between calls, visitors and calendars. A concierge may coordinate requests across several service areas. Ask how the site routes appointment inquiries and public service requests during busy periods.

## Moving between roles

Guest communication transfers to reception; learn appointment records, call routing and visitor procedures. A receptionist moving into concierge work can build on directions and intake by learning how to verify local services.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock schedule: two visitors share a name, with appointments at 10 a.m. and 2 p.m. A visitor arrives at 9:40 a.m., and the host has yet to reply. Spend ten minutes each on the reception checks, public waiting options and a handoff that separates a matching appointment from host clearance.
