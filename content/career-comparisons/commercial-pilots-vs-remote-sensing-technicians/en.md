# Commercial Pilots vs. Remote Sensing Technicians: A Gap in the Aerial Survey

## What each role delivers

Commercial pilots fly aircraft and carry out required operating checks. Remote sensing technicians collect, process and verify data. This example uses a crewed imagery mission.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Commercial Pilots | Remote Sensing Technicians |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Flight assignment and status record | Imagery batch and quality note |
| Basis | Flight assignment, aircraft and operating conditions | Coverage, acquisition time and data quality |

[O*NET 53-2012.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/53-2012.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 19-4099.03](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-4099.03#Tasks)

O*NET uses the Life, Physical, and Social Science Technicians, All Other wage series for 19-4099.03. A separate figure for this specialty cannot be derived from the broader series. [O*NET wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/19-4099.03)

## How the work meets

After a fictional river survey flight, the imagery covers only the eastern reach. The technician marks the gap and the pilot reports completed work to operations. The project team looks for alternative data or requests another flight. The operator handles the arrangements, and the pilot in command is directly responsible for the operation of the aircraft and has final authority over it. [14 CFR §91.3(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-F/part-91/subpart-A/section-91.3)

## How to enter

BLS lists a postsecondary nondegree award and moderate-term on-the-job training as typical for commercial pilots in Table 1.2. In the U.S., verify the FAA certificate and ratings, the limits on commercial pilot privileges and the operating rules for the assignment. For remote sensing technicians, a bachelor’s degree was the most frequent O*NET education response. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [BLS pilot certification](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/transportation-and-material-moving/airline-and-commercial-pilots.htm) [O*NET technician education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-4099.03#Education) [14 CFR §61.133](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-61/subpart-F/section-61.133)

## Work conditions

Flights follow assignments, weather and operating conditions. Imagery work can follow acquisition and quality batches in the field or office. Ask who receives the flight status and authorizes follow-up.

## Moving between roles

Moving to remote sensing, use mission records and learn coverage and data quality. Moving to pilot work, bring mission coordination and obtain applicable training and credentials.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records show an ended flight, imagery only for the east and no new flight assignment. Spend ten minutes each on the flight handoff, the request for missing imagery and what the project team, operator and pilot in command each determine.
