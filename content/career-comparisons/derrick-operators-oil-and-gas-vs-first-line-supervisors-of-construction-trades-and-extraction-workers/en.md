# Oil and Gas Derrick Operators vs. First-Line Extraction Supervisors

## What each role delivers

Derrick operators check the derrick and related equipment, document conditions and may instruct crew members. First-line extraction supervisors coordinate the wider crew plan and follow up on issues that require another person’s verification.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Derrick Operators, Oil and Gas | First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Derrick or circulation-system status report | Crew plan, assignments and unresolved issues |
| Scope | Equipment and the assigned rig activity | Coordination across people and tasks |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $58,620 | $79,920 |

## How the work meets

On a fictional land rig, the derrick operator logs an equipment exception without a review response. The supervisor also receives two requests for the same time slot. The operator keeps the status visible. The supervisor resolves the scheduling issue and keeps the related work pending the assigned review.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential, less than five years of related work experience and short-term on-the-job training as typical for oil and gas derrick operators. For first-line construction and extraction supervisors, it lists high school or equivalent and five or more years of related work experience. Ask the employer which field tasks qualify and how training is documented. [BLS rows 47-5011 and 47-1011](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Derrick work follows rig shifts, equipment conditions and outdoor exposure. Supervisors spend more time coordinating people and assignments. Ask who handles handoff and how an open equipment item is tracked across shifts.

## Moving between roles

Move toward supervision by bringing rig handoff and training experience and adding crew scheduling and follow-up. To move toward derrick work, match your training to the assigned rig equipment and work under supervision.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional rig handoff. Item T has no review response and Crew A and Crew B requested the same time slot. Spend ten minutes on the equipment status, ten on the crew log and ten assigning the decision owners. Keep it on paper.
