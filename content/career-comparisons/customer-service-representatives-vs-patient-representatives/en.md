# Customer Service Representative vs. Patient Representative: A Rescheduled Appointment and a Care Concern

## What each role delivers

Customer service representatives log and follow up on service requests. Patient representatives identify concerns about the care experience and coordinate communication among patients, families and care or administrative teams.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Customer Service Representatives | Patient Representatives |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Service case, verified booking and referral | Patient concern, coordination record and follow-up |
| Basis | Booking data, disclosure limits and deadlines | Patient account, facility procedures and authorized contacts |

[O*NET 43-4051.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/43-4051.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 29-2099.08](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-2099.08#Tasks)

O*NET reports patient representative wages under the wider Health Technologists and Technicians, All Other category. [O*NET wage scope](https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/29-2099.08#WagesEmployment)

## How the work meets

A fictional hospital reschedules a clinic visit. The patient calls to verify the new time and says they previously received no explanation of the waiting procedure. The representative verifies the appointment and records both concerns. The patient representative hears the concern, checks who can review the notice and waiting procedure, and follows up on who will respond.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent and short-term on-the-job training as typical for customer service. For patient representative roles, check employer training and the scope of patient-facing duties. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) In the O*NET education survey for patient representatives, 58% of respondents selected a bachelor’s degree and 23% selected an associate degree or other two-year degree as needed for a new hire. [O*NET patient representative education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-2099.08#Education)

## Work conditions

Service teams may follow call and scheduling volume. Patient representatives may need to follow up across departments. Ask which questions a role can answer and which go to clinical staff.

## Moving between roles

Representatives can bring intake and follow-up into patient relations and learn care workflows, permissions and team coordination. Patient representatives can bring communication skills into customer service and add multichannel case work.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock file: the new appointment is confirmed. The system shows a notice was sent but gives no confirmation of delivery. The patient asks about waiting procedures and their condition. Spend ten minutes each on a booking update, routing the concern and identifying questions for clinical staff.
