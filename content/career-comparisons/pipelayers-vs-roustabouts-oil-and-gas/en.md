# Pipelayers vs Roustabouts, Oil and Gas

## Work on the pipe run or cover the oilfield crew’s list

A pipelayer focuses on placing and joining pipe for storm drains, sanitary sewers and water mains, and documenting the installed section. A roustabout works across assigned oilfield tasks, which may include pipe work along with equipment repair and materials support. Compare what the crew expects you to finish each shift.

## Compare the two closeout records

| Compare | Pipelayers | Oil and gas roustabouts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work focus | Drainage, sewer or water-main sections and joints | Field equipment, repair and crew support |
| Setting | Trench or pipeline work area on a construction site | Well sites and field work areas |
| Handoff | Section status and connection checks still due | Materials status, assigned tasks and open work orders |

## A drainage segment and a pad equipment order

Federal permitting records for Piney Woods describe well pads and associated gathering pipelines. Consider a fictional site with separate storm drainage work beside the pad. A pipelayer records the last completed section and flags a connection awaiting review. A roustabout records which materials arrived for the pad’s oilfield equipment and who still needs to confirm receipt. The designated project reviewer checks the connection record, while any excavation inspections required by OSHA remain with the assigned competent person. This handoff is illustrative. [Federal permitting record](https://www.permits.performance.gov/permitting-project/piney-woods-development/agency-postings?pid=126231) · [OSHA competent-person guidance](https://www.osha.gov/etools/construction/trenching/competent-person)

## Ask what the crew teaches and who can sign off

BLS lists no formal educational credential or related experience as typical for either occupation. Its typical training is short-term on the job for pipelayers and moderate-term for roustabouts. Ask which tasks you will learn under supervision and how the site assigns inspection authority. [BLS preparation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) · [OSHA role guidance](https://www.osha.gov/etools/construction/trenching/competent-person)

## Compare local schedules alongside national pay

BLS reports 2025 national OEWS median annual wages of $49,000 for pipelayers and $46,960 for oil and gas roustabouts. Ask pipe crews about travel between projects and weather delays, and ask oilfield crews about rotation, driving and callouts. Compare offers at similar seniority and hours. [BLS wage table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Turn a closeout record into a work sample

Pipelayers can bring experience tracing sections and open checks to oilfield crew work, then learn equipment and materials records. Roustabouts can bring accurate work-order updates to a pipe crew while learning to read project drawings and track section acceptance.

## Practice a thirty-minute handoff on paper

Use fictional forms. Storm drain section P7 is marked complete, but connection I2 has no review entry. An oilfield equipment work order lists materials delivered with no receiving signature. Spend 12 minutes writing a pipe section report and 13 minutes writing the pad crew update. Spend five minutes naming the reviewer and the receiving contact and checking which statements the forms support.
