# Environmental Compliance Inspector vs. Environmental Economist: What Can Corrective-Action Records Show?

## What each role delivers

An environmental compliance inspector checks permits, site conditions and corrective-action evidence. An environmental economist compares the costs and benefits of policies or investments and who they affect.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Environmental Compliance Inspectors | Environmental Economists |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Inspection finding, evidence and follow-up status | Options analysis, assumptions and sensitivity |
| Basis | Applicable terms, time, location and evidence | Baseline, scope, costs and environmental effects |

## How the work meets

At a fictional industrial park, an inspector finds that one corrective-action record lacks a location identifier and specifies what needs verification. The planning team receives only data authorized for that use. The economist includes required work in the baseline, compares two additional investments and explains how the evidence gap affects estimated benefits.

## How to enter

Inspection candidates can show a permit-to-evidence comparison. Economics candidates can show a cost-benefit analysis and its uncertainty. Check which agency training and delegated authority the inspection role requires.

## Work conditions

Inspection follows site access and reinspection deadlines. Environmental economics often follows project budgets and policy reviews. Ask which records can be shared in aggregate.

## Moving between roles

Inspectors can bring regulatory context to economics and learn valuation and sensitivity analysis. Economists moving into inspection can add permit interpretation, field documentation and inspection procedures.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock file: a required repair will be checked in two weeks. Additional option A costs less but has a wide range of estimated effects. Option B costs more and has operating records. Spend ten minutes each on the follow-up evidence, economic assumptions and separate conclusions.
