# Survey Researcher vs. Urban and Regional Planner: Using Resident Input in a Route Plan

## What each role delivers

A survey researcher designs sampling and questionnaires and reports response and coverage. An urban and regional planner combines land use, travel needs, public input and delivery constraints into planning alternatives.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Survey researchers | Urban and regional planners |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Questionnaire, response records and population coverage | Spatial alternatives, constraints and recommendation |
| Basis | Target population, contact channels and sample coverage | Land use, travel, safety, budget and public needs |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $69,460 | $89,320 |

## How the work meets

A fictional city plans a pedestrian connection from a station to a neighborhood. The survey researcher finds that online responses came mainly from one side of the station and reports the coverage gap. The planner considers the responses alongside school access, parcel boundaries and street conditions and presents two routes and groups to consult further.

## How to enter

BLS lists a master’s degree as typical for both roles. Survey candidates can show pretesting and response checks. Planning candidates can show maps, land-use analysis and implementation tradeoffs. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Surveys follow fieldwork, response and analysis dates. Planning can also depend on site visits, review meetings and public consultation. Ask when new survey results can enter a plan revision.

## Moving between roles

Survey researchers can bring community needs assessment into planning and add spatial analysis, land use and design communication. Planners moving into surveys can build on community engagement and learn sampling, measurement and fieldwork controls.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock file: 50 of 60 responses came from east of the station. Route A crosses the school entrance and route B needs a parcel access check. Spend ten minutes each on coverage limits, route constraints and who should collect each piece of missing evidence.
