# Art Therapists vs. Rehabilitation Counselors: Who Helps a Client Return to a Community Studio?

## What each role does

An art therapist uses art-making to support expression and treatment goals and documents the client's responses. A rehabilitation counselor works with the client on employment or independent living goals and coordinates services.

## Compare the work

| Compare | Art therapist | Rehabilitation counselor |
|---|---|---|
| Focus here | How the client expresses their preferences during treatment | How the client can reach and join the studio |
| Output | Treatment observations and progress notes | Referral and follow-up plan |

[O*NET 29-1129.01](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-1129.01#Tasks) · [O*NET 21-1015.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/21-1015.00#Tasks)

## The same community goal

Imagine a client who wants to return to a community art studio. In therapy, the client uses collage to express what would help them feel comfortable joining. The art therapist records the preferences the client describes. The client tells the rehabilitation counselor they would like to visit during a quiet period. The counselor uses that preference to check appointments and transportation and, with permission, asks the studio about a quiet-time visit. The counselor and client agree when to check in after the first visit.

## Getting started in the U.S.

The American Art Therapy Association describes a master's degree as necessary for entry-level art therapy practice. Verify the state's rules. In New York, practicing creative arts therapy generally requires licensure unless an exemption applies. Rehabilitation counselors typically need a master's degree in rehabilitation counseling or a related field, though some jobs accept a bachelor's degree and some require a license or certification. [AATA education guidance](https://arttherapy.org/becoming-art-therapist/) · [New York license requirements](https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/creative-arts-therapists/license-requirements) · [BLS rehabilitation counselor entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/rehabilitation-counselors.htm#tab-4)

## Working conditions

Art therapists prepare materials, conduct sessions, and document the process. Rehabilitation counselors may wait for community providers to confirm arrangements. Ask who follows up on referrals and how the team handles artwork and treatment records.

## Moving between roles

Interviewing and case communication carry over. Moving into art therapy takes education in therapeutic use of art and supervised practice. Moving into rehabilitation counseling takes training in referrals, individual planning, and follow-up. Check the destination role's credentials.

## Try both in 30 minutes

Use three fictional notes. The client wants to visit during a quiet period. Saturday is busy, and the studio offers appointments on Tuesday. The client agrees to share their timing preference. Spend 10 minutes writing a treatment observation and a follow-up question, 10 making a referral checklist, and 10 drafting a brief inquiry to the studio.
