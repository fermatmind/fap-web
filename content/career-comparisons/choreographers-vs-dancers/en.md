# Choreographers vs. Dancers: Restaging a Routine on a Smaller Stage

## What each role delivers

Choreographers create movement patterns, lead rehearsals and document changes. Dancers practice and perform their parts, coordinate with the ensemble and report spacing problems.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Choreographers | Dancers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Revised blocking and movement notes | Rehearsed performance and feedback |
| Basis | Space, coordination and movement effect | Movement, spacing and music cues |

In May 2025, the U.S. median hourly wage was $26.59 for choreographers and $24.80 for dancers. Show schedules and billed hours vary. [BLS hourly wages](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/entertainment-and-sports/dancers-and-choreographers.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional show moves to a smaller rehearsal stage. On count eight, two dancers approaching from opposite sides reach the same mark. They report the conflict. The choreographer changes the entrance order and checks the new sequence with the ensemble before recording it.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and long-term on-the-job training as typical for dancers, with substantial practice common. It lists a high school diploma or equivalent, five or more years of related experience and long-term on-the-job training for choreographers. Check the audition or portfolio requirements for the actual role. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Both roles can have evening or weekend rehearsals. Dancers repeat and coordinate movement; choreographers may track revisions across the ensemble. Ask how schedule and venue changes are communicated.

## Moving between roles

A dancer moving into choreography can add blocking, notation and feedback skills to performance technique. A choreographer taking a performance role can check the physical and technical demands of the part and rehearse with the ensemble.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock blocking: on count eight, the dancers used to reach marks A and B, but the new stage has no mark B. Spend ten minutes describing the conflict, ten minutes drafting an ensemble adjustment and ten minutes listing the counts and spacing to check.
