# Compliance Manager vs. Environmental Compliance Inspector: Who Closes a Finding?

## What each role delivers

A compliance manager coordinates internal policies, investigations, corrective actions and required reporting. An environmental compliance inspector applies inspection authority to review conditions, evidence and follow-up.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Compliance Managers | Environmental Compliance Inspectors |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Internal investigation, action record and submission | Inspection record, finding and follow-up status |
| Basis | Internal controls, owners, deadlines and records | Applicable permit, inspection scope and verifiable facts |

## How the work meets

A fictional factory must supply evidence after an environmental inspection. Its compliance manager checks operations records, retains the original versions, coordinates corrections and submits both sets of records. The inspector compares the submission with the permit and field record. One identifier cannot be matched, so that item remains open with a request for evidence.

## How to enter

Compliance managers can show a corrective-action tracker and an internal investigation summary. Inspection candidates can show a permit comparison and a clear finding. Ask the employer about training, delegated authority and who signs formal documents.

## Work conditions

Compliance management follows reporting deadlines and internal coordination. Inspection also follows site visits and verification schedules. Ask how the external response reaches the internal owners.

## Moving between roles

Compliance managers bring rule interpretation to inspection and can add environmental permits, field verification and inspection authority. Inspectors can bring evidence review into compliance and add internal workflows, training and corrective-action tracking.

Mock file: the company marks two actions complete. Item one has linked versions and a field verification record against the current permit. Item two has a site-point identifier different from the one in the inspection record. Spend ten minutes each on the company submission, the inspection status and assigning the follow-up.
