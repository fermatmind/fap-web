# Construction Painters vs. Roofers: Tracing a Water Stain

## What each role delivers

Construction and maintenance painters prepare and coat surfaces. Roofers inspect roof problems and perform roof repair or waterproofing work within the assignment.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Construction and Maintenance Painters | Roofers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Wall coating and surface record | Roof repair and assessment record |
| Checks | Sample and surface scope | Roof condition and repair scope |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $49,400 | $55,440 |

## How the work meets

On a fictional public building, the paint crew marks the exterior stain and peeling finish. The roof crew has the location but has yet to verify the source. The project lead assigns separate checks for the roof and wall surface before updating the work orders.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and moderate-term on-the-job training as typical for both roles. Ask the employer what training and fall protection apply to the specific roof assignment. [OSHA roofing provisions](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.501) [BLS rows 47-2141 and 47-2181](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Painting follows surface readiness, color samples and protection of finished work. Roofing depends more directly on outdoor and access conditions. Ask how the teams communicate after rain and who receives the findings.

## Moving between roles

To move to roofing, bring defect records and learn roofing systems under supervision. To shift to painting, bring area documentation and learn surface preparation, sample matching and coating checks.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional sketch. The north wall is stained and its roof review is outstanding. The east wall’s sample is approved but finishes nearby need protection. In 30 minutes write a wall record, a roof query and a list of items that can be confirmed now. Use paper only.
