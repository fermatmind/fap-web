# Fabric and Apparel Patternmakers vs. Shoe and Leather Workers and Repairers: Can a Worn Strap Serve as a Template?

## What each role delivers

Fabric and apparel patternmakers develop and revise cutting patterns. Shoe and leather workers and repairers cut, join or repair parts and may work from a pattern.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Fabric and Apparel Patternmakers | Shoe and Leather Workers and Repairers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Cutting pattern and match marks | Repaired item and repair record |
| Basis | Fabric, pattern relationships and dimensions | Wear, existing joints and replacement material |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $62,750 | $37,800 |

## How the work meets

A fictional theater group needs consistent shoulder straps on new performance vests and a repair to an old leather bag. The patternmaker updates the vest strap pattern and marks where it joins the body. The repairer finds that the old strap has stretched and checks the matching strap and attachments before making a replacement.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training as typical for both occupations. Ask whether the opening covers pattern development, footwear production or leather repairs. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Pattern work may follow samples and deadlines. Repair can depend on the state of each item and the work order. Ask who confirms the scope and accepts returned work.

## Moving between roles

Moving into leather work, use pattern and joining skills and add material inspection and repair methods. Moving into apparel pattern work, bring fabrication experience and add sizing, specifications and pattern revision.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records show that the new vest needs symmetrical shoulder straps and the old leather strap is stretched but its attachments remain. Spend ten minutes each on pattern marks, a repair inspection list and checks before returning each item.
