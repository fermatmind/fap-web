# Construction Laborers vs. Roofers: Who Takes the Leak Report?

## What each role delivers

Construction laborers stage materials and report which rooms are affected. Roofers inspect roofing, determine what the work order needs, and perform the assigned repair.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Construction Laborers | Roofers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Staging and indoor observation log | Roof assessment and repair record |
| Checks | Access and affected rooms | Roof details, materials and the repair extent |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $47,120 | $55,440 |

[O*NET 47-2061.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-2061.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 47-2181.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-2181.00#Tasks)

National occupation medians can help compare typical pay. Local pay and starting offers vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 47-2061 and 47-2181](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

After rain at a fictional school, the laborer logs two ceiling stains by room, time and affected area. The roofer sees that one room ID differs between the plan and summary and asks the site lead to verify it. The designated crew checks the roof after the project confirms access and applicable fall protection.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and short-term on-the-job training as typical for construction laborers. For roofers, it lists no formal educational credential and moderate-term on-the-job training. [BLS rows 47-2061 and 47-2181](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) Ask the employer how it trains new roofers and provides required fall protection for the actual roofing work. U.S. federal OSHA distinguishes low-slope roofing and steep-roof work in its construction fall-protection rule. [OSHA 1926.501(b)(10)–(11)](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.501)

## Work conditions

Laborers may move between floors and delivery points. Roofers’ schedules are more directly affected by outdoor conditions and access windows. Ask who decides when work resumes after rain and how roof findings are handed to the interior crew.

## Moving between roles

For a roofing transition, bring clear location and material handoffs and learn roof inspection documentation under supervision. For general site work, reuse your defect reporting and add materials coordination across trades.

## Try it in 30 minutes

On paper, use a fictional floor plan and two leak notes. A201 matches the plan, but A204 is listed as A240 on the summary. Roof access is still unscheduled. Spend ten minutes each on an indoor handoff, questions for the roofer and a note assigning the unresolved checks. Keep the exercise indoors on paper.
