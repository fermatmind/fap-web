# Cartographers and Photogrammetrists vs. Remote Sensing Technicians: An Imagery Gap in a Trail Map

## What each role delivers

Remote sensing technicians collect, process and verify imagery or other sensor data. Cartographers and photogrammetrists compile sources, capture features and verify the finished map.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Cartographers and Photogrammetrists | Remote Sensing Technicians |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Map features and product checks | Verified imagery and batch exception record |
| Basis | Mapping extent, legend and location | Coverage, quality and acquisition date |

[O*NET 17-1021.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-1021.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 19-4099.03](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-4099.03#Tasks)

O*NET uses the Life, Physical, and Social Science Technicians, All Other wage series for 19-4099.03. A separate figure for this specialty cannot be derived from the broader series. [O*NET wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/19-4099.03)

## How the work meets

A fictional trail map needs an update. The technician flags a gap in the northern image and provides the coverage and batch ID. The map team compares the coverage with the delivery area and identifies where the older source still applies pending review.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for cartographers and photogrammetrists, with licensure required for some work. A bachelor’s degree was the most frequent O*NET response for remote sensing technicians. Check the assigned work and local licensing requirements with the employer or relevant authority. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [O*NET education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-4099.03#Education) [BLS licensing overview](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/cartographers-and-photogrammetrists.htm)

## Work conditions

Technical imagery work may follow acquisition batches and QA. Mapping work may follow feature updates and product checks. Ask how source status passes between teams.

## Moving between roles

Moving to mapping, build on imagery handling and learn feature representation and map inspection. Moving to remote sensing, bring spatial checks and learn acquisition, coverage and processing.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock card: new imagery covers the southern area only; the northern source is dated and not verified for current conditions. Spend ten minutes each describing the imagery gap, marking the area that can be updated and writing a source note for the map.
