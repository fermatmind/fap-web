# Compliance Manager vs. Regulatory Affairs Manager

## Internal controls and product submissions

A compliance manager may investigate a breakdown in an operating procedure and track corrective action. A regulatory affairs manager may set the plan for a product submission, review the supporting material, and coordinate responses to agency questions. The two can work together when a process gap affects the evidence behind a filing.

## Compare their work on a product change

| Work | Compliance manager | Regulatory affairs manager |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First check | Whether the change followed the required review | Which version the submission describes |
| Deliverable | Findings, corrective actions, and follow-up | Filing plan and a list of evidence needed |
| Collaboration | Process owners and internal reviewers | Product, quality, and submission teams |

## A specification changes just before filing

At a fictional manufacturer, a quality lead finds a revised specification and a missing review record. The compliance manager traces the change through the internal process and assigns follow-up under the company’s procedures. The regulatory affairs manager identifies which parts of the filing depend on the changed specification and requests evidence for the correct version. The authorized decision-makers use both findings when deciding whether the submission is ready.

## Building relevant U.S. experience

Build compliance experience through internal control testing or compliance program support, then take responsibility for reviews and corrective actions. For regulatory affairs management, start with submission coordination or document review and build toward ownership of filing plans and deadlines. O*NET places both occupations in Job Zone Four, where a bachelor’s degree and relevant experience are common. Check the requirements for the specific opening. ## Find out what drives the calendar

Ask the compliance team how it schedules routine reviews when urgent concerns arrive. Ask the regulatory affairs team how it handles late material from product or quality when a filing is due. For either team, find out when work requires escalation and whether deadline coverage rotates.

## Switching between roles

An investigation log can demonstrate evidence tracking when moving into regulatory affairs. Build on it by learning how to map product versions and technical evidence to submission requirements. A reviewed submission can show coordination and document review when moving into compliance. Add experience testing controls and verifying that a corrective action holds up in practice.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Create two fictional product specifications, a short internal change procedure, and a note saying one review record is missing. Spend ten minutes identifying the process gap and the affected filing material, ten minutes drafting a follow-up for each, and ten minutes adding an owner and a clear completion criterion to each request.
