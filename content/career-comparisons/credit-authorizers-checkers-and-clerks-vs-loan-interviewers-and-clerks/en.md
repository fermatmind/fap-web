# Credit Authorizers, Checkers, and Clerks vs. Loan Interviewers and Clerks: Two Addresses in One File

## What each role delivers

Loan interviewers and clerks collect and verify application information. Credit authorizers, checkers, and clerks obtain and organize credit records, with some decisions made under assigned limits.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Credit Authorizers, Checkers, and Clerks | Loan Interviewers and Clerks |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Credit verification and discrepancy record | Application, interview notes and document index |
| Basis | Credit history, sources and authority | Application fields, attachments and applicant confirmation |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $50,080 | $50,020 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 43-4041 and 43-4131](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional applicant moved, but the credit record shows the old address. Intake records the move date and attachment location and sends the file to credit checking. The checker retains the prior source, compares dates through approved channels and reports whether the discrepancy is resolved and what supports that status.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent for both roles. It lists moderate-term on-the-job training for credit authorizers, checkers, and clerks and short-term training for loan interviewers and clerks. Ask about access and decision authority. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Intake can follow appointments and document queues. Checking can depend on when external records arrive. Ask who tracks source dates and open items.

## Moving between roles

Moving into checking adds credit sources, discrepancy review and authority records to intake experience. Moving into intake adds applicant communication and file management to source verification.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock file: the application shows a new address, the credit record shows the old one, the move date is known and the attachment is undated. Spend ten minutes each on the file index, verification note and conditions for closing the discrepancy.
