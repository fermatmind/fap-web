# Data entry keyers vs. word processors and typists

## A clean record and a usable document

Data entry keyers record values in defined fields and check them against the source. Word processors and typists format and proof supplied material for readers. A class roster makes the distinction clear when it also appears in a handbook.

## Compare the deliverables

| Item | Data entry keyer | Word processor or typist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Output | Structured record and exceptions linked to their sources | A formatted document and a proof with open queries |
| Work setting | Computer work with forms and validation screens | Computer work with draft files, templates and page proofs |
| Typical U.S. preparation | High school diploma or equivalent, short-term on-the-job training | High school diploma or equivalent, short-term on-the-job training |
| 2025 U.S. median annual wage | $41,340 | $49,280 |
The wage estimates cover U.S. nonfarm wage and salary workers and exclude self-employment. [O*NET data entry](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/43-9021.00#Tasks) · [O*NET word processing](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/43-9022.00#Tasks) · [BLS Table 1.2](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## An instructor's name differs across files

A fictional training office has Dana Lee on a registration form and Dana Leigh on two pages of the draft handbook. The keyer records the conflicting spellings and their sources. The word processor marks both locations in the proof and holds the export. The program lead confirms the spelling. The keyer updates the roster and the word processor revises the proof from the confirmed roster. The program lead authorizes release.

## Starting out in the U.S.

For data entry, practice validating fields and logging changes. Ask how employers test transcription accuracy and train you to report exceptions. For word processing, prepare a sample document with consistent styles, page breaks and clear revision marks. Ask whether the role includes transcription, proofing or preparing final files. [BLS typical preparation](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Ask how work reaches the desk

Ask a data entry team what happens when a form arrives twice and how records are checked before import. Ask a word processing team how many revisions are typical, who may approve changes and which file format the next team needs.

## Moving between the roles

From data entry, bring a redacted exception log and field verification sheet to show attention to the source. Learn page styles, cross-references and proofing. From word processing, bring a marked proof and revision log. Learn field validation, duplicate handling and accuracy checks across records.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Make three fictional items: a registration form showing Dana Lee, an unsigned instructor confirmation, and a handbook proof showing Dana Leigh in two places. The program lead must confirm the name before release. Take ten minutes to create a source-linked roster entry, ten to mark both proof locations and ten to ask for confirmation in a handoff note. Leave the name conflict open on both outputs.
