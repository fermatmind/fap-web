# Property, Real Estate, and Community Association Managers vs Residential Advisors

## Run the property or support the people on shift

Property managers keep a building or community operating across contracts, maintenance and budgets. Residential advisors help residents navigate daily issues and follow up on needs during and between shifts. Think about whether you would rather own a vendor work order or make sure a resident knows what happens next.

## Compare the decisions and deliverables

| Work | Property, real estate, and community association managers | Residential advisors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focus | Property operations across units or sites | Day-to-day issues in a residence |
| Record | Vendor work order, spending decision, and completion check | Resident request, referral, and shift log |
| Decision | Who does the work and what may be approved | Which request needs routing and when to follow up |

## The same laundry machine fails twice

UT Austin describes resident assistants who support students and a separate Building Services team that provides maintenance assistance. In an independent fictional student-housing complex with a property manager, a washer breaks again the day after a service visit. The residential advisor logs the new failure and links it to the previous ticket, tells residents which machines remain available, and leaves the next shift a callback time. The manager checks the vendor's repair record, schedules a return visit under the service agreement, and verifies the machine before closing the work order. ## Check the assignment and the Texas scope

BLS describes a high school diploma and relevant experience as typical for property management entry. In Texas, TREC says arranging repairs generally does not require a license. If duties include compensated leasing or showing for an owner, check TREC's rules and applicable exceptions before accepting those duties. For residential advisors nationally, BLS lists a high school diploma or equivalent, no typical related work experience, and short-term on-the-job training. At UT Austin specifically, RAs are full-time students in part-time roles. Ask about enrollment, training and the duty rotation for the specific position. ## Ask about after-hours coverage and pay

BLS notes that property managers may respond after hours and attend evening meetings. UT Austin's student RAs rotate through desk shifts and after-hours or weekend duty. Ask which calls go to a supervisor, how shift coverage works and whether housing is included. In May 2025, U.S. median annual employee wages were $69,990 for property, real estate, and community association managers and $42,240 for residential advisors. These estimates exclude self-employed earnings. BLS generally uses a 2,080-hour work year when converting hourly wages to annual rates. For a part-time campus role, compare the actual stipend or pay, expected hours, and any housing benefit in the offer. ## Show the work you can carry across

From residence life, bring a maintenance handoff and add a sample vendor work order with a completion check and approval limit. From property operations, bring a closing record and practice a resident update that names the next contact and makes the status clear in one paragraph.

## Try a 30-minute repeat-ticket handoff

Use fictional ticket W17, marked complete yesterday. At 7:10 p.m. the same washer stops again, and the vendor can give an update by 8:00 p.m. Spend 12 minutes writing a shift log with the resident update and next contact. Spend 13 minutes preparing a work order with the vendor follow-up and who can authorize any extra charge. In five minutes verify both records point to W17 and leave the repair open until someone checks it.
