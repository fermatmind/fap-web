# Judges, Magistrate Judges and Magistrates vs Probation Officers and Correctional Treatment Specialists

## Decide whether you want to rule on a case or build the record

Judges conduct proceedings and make decisions within their authority. Probation officers investigate and may supervise people in the community. Correctional treatment specialists can help build rehabilitation or reentry plans. The BLS category combines the last two roles, even though the work varies by assignment.

## Compare responsibilities at the handoff

| Work | Judge or magistrate | Probation officer or correctional treatment specialist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main output | Ruling, order or decision within jurisdiction | Investigation report, supervision notes or service plan |
| Common setting | Courtroom and chambers | Court agency, community or corrections setting |
| Review task | Evaluate the record and applicable law | Verify facts and document gaps or service needs |

## Follow a disputed source before federal sentencing

In a fictional federal district court case, the probation officer finds that an interview date conflicts with an agency record. The officer checks the sources, updates the report and states what remains unresolved. The district judge reads the report and the parties' submissions, decides any dispute that requires a ruling, and determines the sentence. The officer's work provides evidence and a recommendation for that decision. [U.S. Courts presentence process](https://www.uscourts.gov/about-federal-courts/probation-and-pretrial-services/presentence-investigations)

## Check the qualifications for the exact office

Federal judicial offices have different appointment routes. For example, federal magistrate judges are appointed by district judges and must meet criteria that include at least five years as a member in good standing of the highest court bar of a state or qualifying jurisdiction. Check the authority and eligibility rules for the office you want. BLS lists a bachelor's degree and moderate-term on-the-job training as typical for probation officers and correctional treatment specialists. Federal probation officers also face agency-specific hiring and screening requirements. [Types of federal judges](https://www.uscourts.gov/about-federal-courts/types-federal-judges) · [BLS entry characteristics](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) · [Federal probation hiring](https://www.uscourts.gov/careers/who-works-judiciary/us-probation-pretrial-services-careers)

## Compare wages and the shape of the work week

The 2025 U.S. median annual wages were $153,990 for judges, magistrate judges and magistrates, and $66,270 for probation officers and correctional treatment specialists. Ask about hearing calendars, writing time, field visits and on-call work. A fictional source log can demonstrate record-checking skills in either setting. Moving toward a judicial office involves legal practice and meeting its selection rules. Moving into probation or treatment work adds interviewing, case management and agency training. [BLS national wage table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) · [Federal probation duties](https://www.uscourts.gov/careers/who-works-judiciary/us-probation-pretrial-services-careers)

## Spend 30 minutes comparing two case records

Use two fictional records about interview R14: the interview note gives Tuesday as the meeting date, and the signed agency log gives Wednesday as the date of the same meeting. Spend eight minutes listing what each source supports, 12 minutes drafting a factual discrepancy note and a separate question for judicial review, and ten minutes checking that a reader can tell which point remains open and who handles it. Keep the exercise to written records.

## Ask what gets reviewed and by whom

Ask someone in a court what makes a written decision ready to issue. Ask a probation or treatment professional what evidence closes a follow-up item. Compare which kind of responsibility you would like to practice.
