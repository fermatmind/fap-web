# Roofers vs. Sheet Metal Workers: The Parapet Flashing Does Not Fit

## What each role delivers

Roofers inspect roofs and seams for water protection. Sheet metal workers can fabricate and join metal flashing. Project assignments determine who checks each part of the interface.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Roofers | Sheet Metal Workers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Roof and seam inspection record | Fabricated and installed metal flashing |
| Basis | Roof edges, water marks and inspection criteria | Shop drawing, finished dimensions and joints |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $55,440 | $61,800 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 47-2181 and 47-2211](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

At a fictional school renovation, the roof crew finds that a metal flashing piece does not meet the approved roof finish line at the parapet. The sheet metal crew records part dimensions and the shop drawing revision. The site lead receives both accounts.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and moderate-term on-the-job training for roofers. It lists high school or equivalent and apprenticeship for sheet metal workers. Ask which shop and site tasks the employer expects. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Roof work can follow weather and access. Sheet metal work can span shop fabrication and site assembly. Ask who confirms field measurements and the interface.

## Moving between roles

Moving to sheet metal, bring roof detail experience and learn fabrication and inspection. Moving to roofing, bring seam and handoff skills and learn roof systems and inspection.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: roof detail D calls for 420 mm of flashing, the shop made a 400 mm piece to revision C, and the site has not accepted the part. Spend ten minutes each on roof interface evidence, the fabrication recheck and the revision discrepancy for the site lead.
