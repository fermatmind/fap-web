# Fashion Designers vs. Set and Exhibit Designers: Making a Garment Read on Display

## What each role delivers

Fashion designers specify materials and appearance and review samples. Set and exhibit designers plan layouts, backdrops and built elements. This example concerns a garment display.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Fashion Designers | Set and Exhibit Designers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Garment sample and material specifications | Display layout, backdrop and drawings |
| Basis | Silhouette, swatches and appearance | Viewing distance, circulation and backdrop material |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $80,960 | $75,240 |

## How the work meets

A fictional brand sets up a display and finds that a dark backdrop hides the shoulder line of a dark sample. The fashion designer marks the detail that needs to be visible and supplies a swatch. The exhibit designer tests two backgrounds under the same lights, preserves the aisle and shares comparison photos for review.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for both occupations. Build a garment and material portfolio for fashion roles or show scaled layouts and fabrication notes for exhibit roles. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Fashion design may follow fit reviews and collection deadlines. Exhibit work may span site measurements, drawings and installation. Ask when the team can review the garment under actual display lighting.

## Moving between roles

Moving into exhibits, bring material and visual judgment and add spatial layout, circulation and fabrication coordination. Moving into fashion, bring composition skills and add garment construction, textiles and fittings.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock display: the sample is navy. Backdrop A matches it, while light-gray backdrop B reflects the lighting. Visitors stand about three meters away. Spend ten minutes each on garment priorities, a backdrop comparison and conditions for the on-site check.
