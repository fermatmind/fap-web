# High-School CTE Teachers vs. Tutors: Project Grades and Targeted Practice

## What each role delivers

High-school CTE teachers organize technical coursework and assess projects. Tutors review specific material with students and track progress during sessions.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Career/Technical Education Teachers, Secondary School | Tutors |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Course project and assessment | Practice plan and progress note |
| Basis | Course objectives and project work | Current understanding and practice |

## How the work meets

In a fictional technical class, a student omits units from a project sheet. The teacher gives rubric-based feedback. A learning-center tutor uses a parallel worksheet to practice checking units and records whether the student corrects the next one independently.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree and less than five years of related experience as typical for secondary CTE teachers and some college without a degree as typical for tutors. California’s Designated Subjects CTE credential authorizes named vocational subjects through grade 12. Verify the assignment and any employer requirements for tutoring. [California CTC](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/cl-888/) [BLS occupation rows](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Teaching follows the course and class schedule. Tutoring follows appointments and specific learning goals. Ask who receives progress notes and whether the tutor follows up after another practice session.

## Moving between roles

Teachers can bring feedback and lesson structure into tutoring and develop one-to-one coaching. Tutors moving to school teaching need technical subject experience, classroom practice and applicable authorization.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional project sheet where a student mixed centimeters and millimeters twice. Spend ten minutes drafting rubric feedback, ten planning a parallel practice and check, and ten choosing what can be shared with the teacher.
