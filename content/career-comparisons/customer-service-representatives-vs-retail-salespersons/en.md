# Customer Service Representatives vs. Retail Salespersons: A Return and a New Purchase

## What each role delivers

Customer service representatives document and follow up on issues. Retail salespersons identify needs, recommend merchandise and process purchases. Store duties can overlap.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Customer Service Representatives | Retail Salespersons |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Post-sale case, policy and status | Product options and sales record |
| Basis | Original order, return terms and customer feedback | Use, features, price and stock |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $44,770 | $35,410 |

[O*NET 43-4051.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/43-4051.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 41-2031.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/41-2031.00#Tasks)

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 43-4051 and 41-2031](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional shopper wants to return a desk lamp and buy a narrower model. Service checks the original order and submits the return, then shares the shopper’s dimensions with sales as permitted by store policy. The salesperson checks floor and system stock and provides two options. Service explains the return and new order statuses separately.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent for customer service representatives and no formal educational credential for retail salespersons, with short-term on-the-job training for both. Ask who can authorize refunds or discounts. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Service can follow queue deadlines. Retail may follow store traffic and shifts. Ask when an exchange needs a handoff.

## Moving between roles

Moving to sales adds product presentation and checkout skills to customer communication. Moving to service adds case records, order checks and escalation to product knowledge.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: the original lamp can be submitted for return, model A fits but stock is unverified, and model B is in stock but wider. Spend ten minutes each on the case, an option comparison and the next step you can confirm.
