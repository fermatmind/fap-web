# Orthotists and Prosthetists vs Podiatrists

## Choose between the device and the medical problem

Orthotists design and fit orthoses such as braces, and prosthetists fit artificial limbs. Podiatrists diagnose and treat foot and ankle disorders within their scope. Their work can meet when a clinician refers someone for an orthosis and needs feedback from the fitting team.

## Compare what each appointment produces

| Work | Orthotist or prosthetist | Podiatrist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main output | Device evaluation, design, fitting and adjustment record | Medical examination, diagnosis and care plan |
| Setting | Patient-facing practice and fabrication or laboratory setting | Podiatry office, clinic or hospital |
| Typical partner | Referring clinician and rehabilitation team | Other clinicians and orthotic services |

[Orthotic and prosthetic tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-2091.00) · [Podiatry tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-1081.00)

## Follow one orthosis referral in Florida

In a fictional Florida clinic, a podiatrist completes a foot assessment and refers the patient for a new orthosis with a stated functional goal. The referral includes a written prescription from a licensed physician, as required for the orthotist's work in this example. The orthotist evaluates fit and documents the device observations and open questions. The orthotist sends a progress note back to the podiatrist, who reviews it as part of medical follow-up. Each works within the scope of the relevant license. ## Compare education and credential routes

BLS lists a master's degree and residency as typical preparation for orthotists and prosthetists. ABC certification has discipline-specific education, residency and exam steps. Florida licenses orthotics and prosthetics separately. The combined prosthetist-orthotist license is available to applicants who meet both sets of requirements. Check which discipline the job calls for and use that application pathway. BLS describes a DPM degree, three-year residency and state license for podiatrists. Florida has its own podiatric physician education, clinical experience and exam rules. [BLS O&P](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/orthotists-and-prosthetists.htm) · [ABC certification](https://www.abcop.org/individual-certification/get-certified/orthotist-prosthetist/application) · [Florida O&P licensing](https://floridasorthotistsprosthetists.gov/licensing-renewals/) · [BLS podiatry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/podiatrists.htm) · [Florida podiatry licensing](https://floridaspodiatricmedicine.gov/podiatric-physician/) · [Florida license categories](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/Statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0468/Sections/0468.803.html)

## Compare pay along with the work you want to do

May 2025 U.S. median annual wages were $81,110 for orthotists and prosthetists and $160,300 for podiatrists. Ask about fitting visits and fabrication time in one setting, and clinic caseload and procedure time in the other. An orthotist can build referral collaboration by reporting fit observations to a podiatry clinic. A podiatrist can learn device handoffs through joint reviews. Crossing into either profession requires its own formal training and license where applicable. [BLS O&P pay](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/orthotists-and-prosthetists.htm) · [BLS podiatry pay](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/podiatrists.htm)
