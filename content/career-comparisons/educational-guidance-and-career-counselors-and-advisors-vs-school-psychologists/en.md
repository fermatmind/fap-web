# School Counselor vs. School Psychologist

## Course planning and school-based assessment

Educational, guidance, and career counselors and advisors help students make course and career choices and address barriers to education. School psychologists assess learning, behavioral and developmental needs and explain assessment findings to authorized school partners and families.

## Compare records and decisions

| Stage | School or career counselor and advisor | School psychologist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gather | Student preferences, course requirements and concerns | Observations, educational records and assessment information |
| Document | Advising options, referrals and follow-up | Assessment findings and support recommendations |
| Coordinate | Students and staff who can arrange support | Families, educators and the assessment team |

[O*NET 21-1012.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/21-1012.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 19-3034.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-3034.00#Tasks)

## Reading demands start affecting course choices

In a fictional middle school, a student avoids classes with heavy reading but wants to keep an elective. The counselor can discuss options, verify the schedule and identify supports to ask about. A school psychologist assigned to evaluate can examine classroom observations and other evidence and explain findings. If the school evaluates eligibility under IDEA, the parent and a group of qualified professionals make the eligibility decision. [U.S. Code of Federal Regulations, IDEA §300.306](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/cfr/34/300?link-type=pdf&sectionnum=306&year=mostrecent)

## U.S. education and credentials

BLS says school counselors typically need a master's degree and a state-issued credential. Some states license career counselors, and employer requirements for career or academic advising vary. BLS says school psychologists typically need at least a master's in school psychology and commonly need a state credential. Ask the state education agency which degree, internship or supervised experience and credential it accepts for the specific setting. [BLS school and career counselor entry guidance](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/school-and-career-counselors.htm#tab-4) · [BLS school psychologist entry guidance](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/psychologists.htm#tab-4)

## Ask how the workweek is divided

For a counseling job, ask about appointment peaks before registration and time for advising, groups and referrals. For school psychology, ask how many campuses the position serves and how evaluations, reports and parent meetings fit into the schedule.

## Moving between roles

Counselors can bring experience interviewing students, coordinating referrals and understanding class schedules, then build school psychology assessment skills through the required training. School psychologists can bring family consultation and support planning experience, then learn academic advising, career research and the relevant credential rules.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use a fictional class schedule, two classroom notes and one student preference form. Spend ten minutes writing two advising options and facts to verify. Spend ten listing what evidence the evaluation team still needs and who might provide it. Spend the last ten identifying recipients for each record and the people involved in an IDEA eligibility decision.
