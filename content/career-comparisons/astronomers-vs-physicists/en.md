# Astronomer vs. Physicist

## Celestial observations and tests of physical explanations

Astronomers study celestial objects and phenomena, including by planning observations and analyzing measurements. Physicists study physical phenomena and can use measurements and models to test explanations. Astrophysics can involve both kinds of work.

## Compare the deliverables

| Work | Astronomer | Physicist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First check | The target, observation times, and comparable records | The model assumptions and predictions that differ |
| Deliverable | Measurement summary with observing conditions | Model comparison and a request for discriminating evidence |

BLS reported May 2025 U.S. median annual wages of $128,820 for astronomers and $172,250 for physicists. These OEWS figures cover wage and salary employment and exclude self-employment. [BLS pay](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/physicists-and-astronomers.htm#tab-5) · [OEWS methods](https://www.bls.gov/oes/methods_25.pdf)

## Two nights, two different readings

A fictional team observes one target in different filters on consecutive nights. The astronomer documents the filters and any available calibration details and flags the limits of comparing the readings. The physicist explains what each model would predict if the difference came from change over time or from using different filters. The team uses both notes to choose follow-up observations.

## U.S. education and early experience

BLS describes a doctorate as typical for research or academic positions in either field. Entry-level federal physicist roles typically require a bachelor’s degree in physics. An astronomy project can build experience documenting observations and comparing datasets. A physics project can build experience checking whether a measurement distinguishes two predictions. [BLS preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/physicists-and-astronomers.htm#tab-4)

## Compare work settings

Astronomers may analyze data in offices and sometimes observe at night or visit observatories. Physicists may work in labs and also spend time planning, analyzing, or writing outside the lab. Ask how often the team works at a facility, whether it uses archived data, and what travel or night work is expected. [BLS work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/physicists-and-astronomers.htm#tab-3)

## Moving between the roles

An astronomer can bring data analysis and calibration experience to a physics specialty while building relevant modeling or laboratory skills. A physicist can bring modeling and measurement experience to astronomy while learning observational constraints and use of astronomical archives.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use two fictional entries for the same target: a blue-filter reading of 12 on Monday and a red-filter reading of 9 on Tuesday, with no calibration notes. Spend ten minutes listing known facts, ten writing a comparability note, and ten requesting one same-filter time comparison and one same-time comparison across filters.
