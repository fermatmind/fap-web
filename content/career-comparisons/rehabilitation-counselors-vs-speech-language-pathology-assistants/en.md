# Rehabilitation Counselors vs. Speech-Language Pathology Assistants: Who Helps With Workplace Check-In?

## What each role delivers

A rehabilitation counselor helps clients address barriers to employment and coordinates services. A speech-language pathology assistant carries out communication practice under a speech-language pathologist's direction and reports observations.

## Compare the work

| Compare | Rehabilitation counselor | Speech-language pathology assistant |
|---|---|---|
| Focus here | What communication support is available at check-in? | How does the client perform with the assigned prompts? |
| Output | Support and follow-up plan | Practice data and a report to the supervisor |

[O*NET 21-1015.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/21-1015.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 31-9099.01](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/31-9099.01#Tasks)

## The same workplace check-in

Imagine a client worries about following verbal directions at reception when they report for training. The counselor reviews check-in with the client and, with permission, asks whether the site can give the next steps in writing. Under the speech-language pathologist's protocol, the assistant guides practice in asking for repetition and records which attempts needed a prompt. After review and with the client's permission, the supervisor shares relevant suggestions with the counselor, who confirms the arrangements.

## Getting started in the U.S.

Rehabilitation counselors typically need a master's degree in rehabilitation counseling or a related field. Some jobs accept a bachelor's degree, and some require a license or certification. Check the state rules for speech-language pathology assistants. California lists three education routes: a Board-approved program, a qualifying bachelor's degree, or a qualifying out-of-state associate degree in speech-language pathology assisting. For the bachelor's route, the Board may consider 36 weeks of qualifying full-time work in place of the 100-hour fieldwork or clinical experience requirement. Check the Board's out-of-state work criteria if relying on that option. [BLS counselor entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/rehabilitation-counselors.htm#tab-4) · [California assistant registration](https://speechandhearing.ca.gov/applicants/slp_assistant/index.html) · [California adopted regulation](https://www.speechandhearing.ca.gov/laws_regs/pdf/1399_170_order_of_adoption.pdf)

## Working conditions

Counselors may wait for employers or trainers to confirm an arrangement. Assistants may record several practice sessions before the supervisor reviews the notes. Ask who checks in with the training site and when the assistant can expect feedback.

## Moving between roles

Assistants can build on progress documentation and learn referrals and employment planning. Counselors can bring interviewing experience into assistant work and learn supervised practice and documentation. Check the destination role's credentials and supervision requirements.

## Try both in 30 minutes

Use fictional notes. Reception can provide written directions. The client needs a verbal prompt on two of three attempts to ask for repetition. The client consents to sharing their preferred support. Spend 10 minutes writing the assistant's observation, 10 writing the counselor's follow-up questions, and 10 drafting separate updates for the supervisor and training site.
