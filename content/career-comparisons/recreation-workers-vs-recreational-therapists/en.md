# Recreation Worker vs. Recreational Therapist

## What the activity is for

Recreation workers organize activities around group interests and available facilities. Recreational therapists design activities around assessed needs and treatment goals, then track individual progress.

## Compare what each person records

| Stage | Recreation worker | Recreational therapist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan | Group size, schedules, materials and rules | Individual objective, activity adaptations and observation plan |
| Lead | Keep the activity accessible and the group on schedule | Track participation and adjust within the treatment plan |
| Document | Attendance and program logistics | Individual response, assistance and progress |

[O*NET 39-9032.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/39-9032.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 29-1125.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-1125.00#Tasks)

## A community game with a treatment goal

Imagine a community center hosting a rolling-ball game. The recreation worker sets up two stations and keeps groups rotating. A recreational therapist supporting one patient observes whether the patient gives a ready signal when their turn begins and records what prompting was needed. The therapist can share an authorized adjustment to the rotation with program staff while keeping the individual progress record within the appropriate care workflow.

## U.S. entry and training

The BLS detailed table assigns recreation workers a high school diploma or equivalent, no related work experience, and short-term on-the-job training. Recreational therapists typically need a bachelor's in a relevant field. Employers may prefer or require certification. Check state licensure and employer requirements for the setting. Employers may also require CPR or first aid certification, a driver’s license or a background check for recreation workers. The same BLS table assigns no related work experience or on-the-job training to recreational therapists as typical of national entry. The BLS occupation handbook says recreational therapy degree programs usually include an internship. One certification route pairs a bachelor’s degree in recreational therapy with a supervised internship and a passing exam result. A small number of states require licensure or certification. [BLS detailed training table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/education-and-training-by-occupation.htm) · [BLS recreational therapist entry requirements](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/recreational-therapists.htm#tab-4) · [BLS recreation worker hiring guidance](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/personal-care-and-service/recreation-workers.htm#tab-4)

## Ask about the working week

For a recreation job, ask about evening or weekend programs, staffing and time to set up. For therapy work, ask about caseload, time for progress notes and how often the team reviews each treatment plan.

## Moving between the two jobs

Recreation workers can bring skill in adapting activities and coordinating groups. They need to build assessment, treatment planning and documentation skills through the relevant degree and supervised training. Recreational therapists can bring activity design and communication skills to community roles, then learn the program's scheduling, outreach and site operations.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use two fictional stations that each hold six people and an individual goal of giving a ready signal at the start of a turn. Spend ten minutes outlining the rotation, ten writing an individual observation sheet with independent, prompted and absent responses, and ten marking what the program lead and care team each need to receive.
