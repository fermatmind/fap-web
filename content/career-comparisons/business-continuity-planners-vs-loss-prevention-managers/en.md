# Business continuity planners vs. loss prevention managers

## Keep work moving and keep inventory accountable

Business continuity planners organize and test plans for interruptions. Loss prevention managers oversee inventory and loss controls, review discrepancies and train staff. Both may support risk decisions during a disruption. One team asks which service should resume first; the other asks what inventory evidence supports the decision.

## Compare the roles

| Item | Business continuity planner | Loss prevention manager |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deliverable | Service priorities, dependencies and an exercise or recovery record | A discrepancy review, control action and follow-up record |
| Setting to ask about | Cross-team exercises and operations readiness reviews | Store, warehouse or regional inventory and loss reviews |
| U.S. preparation evidence | O*NET Job Zone Four, considerable preparation | O*NET Job Zone Four, considerable preparation |

## A maintenance window delays receiving

At a fictional retail warehouse, maintenance runs long when replenishment loads arrive. The continuity planner checks the time-sensitive deliveries and recommends resuming receiving for the batch with a confirmed count. The loss prevention manager compares the inventory and transfer records, and recommends keeping the discrepant batch unchanged until it is reconciled. The operations lead approves the split, receiving staff carry it out, and the two teams document recovery progress and reconciliation separately.

## Getting started in the U.S.

Both occupations are in O*NET Job Zone Four. Most occupations in the zone require a four-year bachelor's degree, with exceptions. Operations coordination or exercise support can build experience mapping dependencies and readiness for continuity work. Inventory operations or audit support can build experience reviewing discrepancies for loss prevention. Ask what industry experience and decision authority the employer expects. [O*NET continuity preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/13-1199.04#JobZone) · [O*NET loss prevention preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/11-9199.08#JobZone)

## Ask what drives the schedule

Continuity work may cluster around exercises and disruptions. Loss prevention work can include recurring inventory reviews and unscheduled exceptions. Ask who attends exercises, how many sites are covered, and who approves a change to receiving or inventory records.

## Moving between the roles

A planner can bring a redacted exercise report and action list, then learn inventory reconciliation and control follow-up. A loss prevention manager can bring a redacted discrepancy review and training record, then learn dependency mapping, recovery priorities and exercise design.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use fictional records. Batch A has a matching count and needs to be received by noon. Batch B has nine items against ten on its transfer sheet and can be reviewed later. The example allows phased receiving, and discrepancies must be reconciled before posting. Take ten minutes setting the priority, ten writing a discrepancy and verification note, and ten writing a recommendation to the operations lead. State which batch can proceed, who approves the split and who verifies the unresolved count.
