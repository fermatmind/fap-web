# Occupational Therapists vs. Elementary Special Education Teachers: Who Handles Classroom Participation?

## What each role delivers

Occupational therapists evaluate functional abilities and support participation within their professional scope. Elementary special education teachers adapt academic instruction and track progress.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Occupational Therapists | Special Education Teachers, Elementary School |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Functional evaluation and service note | Adapted instruction and academic progress |
| Basis | Participation in school activities | Learning goals and support plan |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $100,330 | $65,120 |

National occupation medians. The special education figure combines kindergarten and elementary school. Local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 29-1122 and 25-2052](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional student answers questions orally but takes longer to complete written work. The teacher records academic observations. The school team receives a separate participation concern, and the therapist follows its authorization and evaluation process.

## How to enter

BLS lists a master’s degree as typical for occupational therapists and notes that all states require licensure. Its combined kindergarten and elementary special education row lists a bachelor’s degree as typical. Check state teacher authorization and the actual school position. Under IDEA, occupational therapy can be a related service. [BLS occupational therapy preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/occupational-therapists.htm#tab-4) · [IDEA 34 CFR 300.34](https://sites.ed.gov/idea/regs/b/a/300.34) [BLS occupation rows](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Therapy work follows service and evaluation schedules. Teachers follow instruction and classroom progress. Ask how the team routes and protects records.

## Moving between roles

Therapists moving to special education need academic teaching preparation and credentials. Teachers moving to therapy need occupational therapy training, supervised fieldwork and licensure.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional note: the student answers orally but writes slowly. Spend ten minutes on an academic observation, ten on appropriate information for the authorized team, and ten distinguishing permitted uses. Keep the exercise on paper.
