# Marriage and Family Therapist vs. School Psychologist

## Relationship-focused therapy and school support

A marriage and family therapist may provide therapy to individuals, couples, or families and consider how relationships affect their concerns. A school psychologist evaluates needs related to learning, behavior, and adjustment at school and works with staff and families on support.

## Compare the work products

| Work | Marriage and family therapist | School psychologist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First check | The concerns and interactions the family wants to address | School participation and existing support |
| Deliverable | Shared therapy goals and a review plan | School support recommendations and criteria for follow-up |

U.S. median annual wages in May 2025 were $66,940 for marriage and family therapists and $95,990 for school psychologists. Both figures are national occupation-wide OEWS medians. [BLS therapy pay](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/marriage-and-family-therapists.htm#tab-5) · [BLS school psychologist pay](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/psychologists.htm#tab-5)

[BLS therapist duties](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/marriage-and-family-therapists.htm#tab-2) · [BLS school psychology duties](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/psychologists.htm#tab-2)

## A student has trouble getting to class in the morning

A fictional family describes arguments while getting ready to leave home. The therapist asks family members how they experience that routine and what they want to change. The school psychologist reviews arrival and classroom entry records and asks staff what arrival support is available. With appropriate consent and according to applicable rules, the team and family share the arrival plan and agree on when to review it.

## U.S. education and credentials

BLS describes a master’s degree as typical for marriage and family therapists. States require licensure, including supervised clinical experience after the degree and an accepted exam. School psychologists typically need at least a master’s degree and preparation usually includes an internship. Check the education agency’s state credential for the school role and the relevant state licensing board for therapy. [BLS therapist preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/marriage-and-family-therapists.htm#tab-4) · [BLS school psychologist preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/psychologists.htm#tab-4) California applicants, for example, can check requirements with the Board of Behavioral Sciences for therapy and the Commission on Teacher Credentialing for school psychology. [California LMFT information](https://www.bbs.ca.gov/exams/lmft) · [California school psychology credential](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/cl-606c/)

## Compare settings and schedules

Therapists may work in mental health centers or private practice, with some evening or weekend appointments. School psychologists work in educational settings. Ask about appointment hours, school coverage, and time for team meetings. [BLS therapist work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/marriage-and-family-therapists.htm#tab-3) · [BLS psychologist work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/psychologists.htm#tab-3)

## Moving between the roles

A therapist can bring experience talking with families into school work, then build supervised school assessment and consultation experience. A school psychologist can bring experience working with families into therapy preparation, then build supervised practice specific to couple and family therapy and meet the applicable state requirements.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use these fictional notes. On Monday the student arrives 20 minutes late. On Wednesday the student arrives on time but waits ten minutes outside the classroom. The parent says the morning routines differed. Spend ten minutes listing facts, ten writing one home question and one school question, and ten drafting a short arrival support note containing only the information the team needs.
