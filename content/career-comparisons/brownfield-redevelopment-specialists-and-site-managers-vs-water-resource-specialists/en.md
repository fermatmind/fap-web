# Brownfield Site Manager vs. Water Resource Specialist: Checking a Downstream Connection

## What each role delivers

Brownfield redevelopment specialists and site managers coordinate investigations, remediation and site work. Water resource specialists evaluate flows, water quality and receiving systems against watershed goals.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Brownfield redevelopment specialists and site managers | Water resource specialists |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Site plan, work stages and access conditions | Drainage assessment, downstream constraints and recommendations |
| Basis | Site history, cleanup scope, construction and approvals | Hydrologic records, receiving system and watershed goals |

## How the work meets

A fictional warehouse property is being redeveloped. The site manager supplies revised surface plans, access restrictions and the sequence of work. The water resource specialist checks the proposed outlet and downstream branches, finds a gap in one branch’s data and asks for verification. The site manager records this dependency and its owner before confirming the relevant work date.

## How to enter

For redevelopment roles, show a site investigation timeline and an example of coordinating project teams. For water resources, show a hydrologic analysis with data limitations. Ask whether the role includes engineering sign-off or other duties with specific credential requirements.

## Work conditions

Site work follows access and construction milestones. Water resource studies also depend on monitoring records, review and watershed coordination. Ask who can move the construction date when the evidence is incomplete.

## Moving between roles

Site staff can bring coordination into water resources and add hydrology, water quality and watershed analysis. Water resource staff moving into redevelopment can bring systems analysis and add site procedures, contractor coordination and scheduling.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock schedule: a drainage plan is due this month. Of two candidate downstream branches, one has complete records and the other lacks recent flow data. A restricted area remains closed. Spend ten minutes each on the site handoff, evidence for the two routes and which work dates can be confirmed.
