# Proofreaders and Copy Markers vs. Writers and Authors: Resolving Conflicting Numbers in a Proof

## What each role delivers

Proofreaders and copy markers flag errors and discrepancies and recheck corrections. Writers and authors research, draft and revise text.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Proofreaders and Copy Markers | Writers and Authors |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Marked and rechecked proof | Confirmed copy and revision notes |
| Basis | Approved copy, figures and layout changes | Sources, argument and meaning |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $51,120 | $76,910 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 43-9081 and 27-3043](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional publication has conflicting figures in a caption and article. The proofreader locates the discrepancy in both the proof and approved copy. The writer checks interview notes and a source table, confirms the year and value with the editor, and the proofreader checks the value, unit and year in the next proof.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for both occupations and long-term on-the-job training for writers and authors. Ask whether the posting includes content editing or work on approved proofs. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Proofreading may follow proof rounds and publication dates. Writing may include research, drafting and revision deadlines. Ask who resolves factual queries and how approved changes are circulated.

## Moving between roles

Moving into writing, bring close reading and develop source research and structure. Moving into proofreading, bring language judgment and build style, proof tracking and recheck skills.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock proof: the caption shows 16, the body 61 and the source copy has the same conflict. A reference table has results from two years. Spend ten minutes each on a query, fields to verify and a checklist for the corrected proof.
