# Search Marketing Strategists vs. Web Administrators: Who Handles a Broken Landing Page?

## What each role owns

A search marketing strategist tracks search traffic, identifies affected landing pages, and prioritizes campaign adjustments. A web administrator diagnoses site and server problems, restores service within their authority, and records any changes.

## Compare the work

| Compare | Search marketing strategist | Web administrator |
|---|---|---|
| Inputs | Queries, landing pages, clicks, and conversions | Error records, alerts, and recent changes |
| Deliverable | Affected entry pages and a search performance review | Diagnosis, restoration checks, and a change log |

## The handoff after launch

Suppose a museum opens early-access reservations. Search clicks hold steady, but completed reservations fall. The strategist sends the administrator the affected landing page and the time the drop began. The administrator checks alerts and recent changes and follows the team's restoration procedure. In an authorized test environment, they open the landing page, select a time slot, and submit a mock reservation. The check passes when the confirmation page appears and the reservation record has a reference number. The strategist separately tracks actual search visits and completed reservations after recovery.

## Working conditions

Strategy work follows campaign deadlines and reporting cycles. Site administration can include interruptions when an alert fires. Ask who is on call, who can approve a production change, and who signs off once service returns.

## Changing roles

Moving into administration, carry over your ability to describe a symptom by URL and time, then practice monitoring and recovery. Moving into strategy, use your knowledge of site structure and logs while learning to distinguish search impressions, visits, and bookings.

## Try both in 30 minutes

Use three fictional records for `/early-access`: 80 search clicks in each of the 9–10 and 10–11 a.m. windows, 16 and 2 completed reservations in those windows respectively, and a reservation submission timeout alert at 10:04 a.m. Spend 10 minutes writing the handoff with the URL and timing, 10 defining a mock submission and pass criteria, and 10 drafting a recovery update with the metric still to monitor.
