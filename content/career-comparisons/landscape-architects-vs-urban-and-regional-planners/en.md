# Landscape Architects vs Urban and Regional Planners

## Design the site or coordinate the district

Landscape architects design built and planted outdoor spaces around site conditions and use. Urban and regional planners compare options for land use, transport, and public facilities across places. On a shared project, a planner may identify connections to preserve, and the landscape architect works out how to fit those connections into the site.

## Work products and U.S. pay

| Item | Landscape architects | Urban and regional planners |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Example setting | Landscape or multidisciplinary design studio | Planning agency or consulting team |
| Typical output | Site plan, sections and planting design | Land use and access maps, alternatives and recommendations |
| U.S. median annual wage, 2025 | $79,870 | $89,320 |

These are national occupational medians. Compare each role's employer, hours, and responsibilities. ## Connect the bridge to a raised park

New York City’s East Side Coastal Resiliency project raises East River Park for flood protection and identifies the Delancey Street Bridge among access improvements. Using the public documents as a work example, a planner could trace neighborhood walking links and give the site team the bridge connection to preserve. A landscape architect could draw a section from the bridge landing to the raised path and flag grades and drainage to verify. The project team would review that section alongside engineering and accessibility requirements. [NYC flood protection design](https://www.nyc.gov/site/escr/about/resiliency-and-flood-protection.page) · [NYC transportation and access plan](https://www.nyc.gov/site/escr/about/transportation-and-access.page)

## Compare education and professional requirements

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree and internship or residency for typical landscape architecture entry. States license landscape architects, with education, supervised experience and an exam commonly involved. New Jersey’s licensing committee explains its education and LARE process. BLS lists a master’s as typical for planning, and its handbook also describes accredited bachelor’s routes. Most states do not require planners to hold a state license. New Jersey is an exception with Professional Planner licensure. Employers may prefer or require professional certification, so check the posting as well as the state rules. [BLS landscape entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/landscape-architects.htm#tab-4) · [BLS planning entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/urban-and-regional-planners.htm#tab-4) · [New Jersey landscape architecture](https://www.njconsumeraffairs.gov/land/Pages/Licensee-Frequently-Asked-Questions.aspx) · [New Jersey Professional Planners](https://www.njconsumeraffairs.gov/plan/Pages/applications.aspx)

## Ask how the office and field fit into the week

Landscape architects work in offices and at project sites. Planners may inspect sites and attend some evening or weekend meetings. Ask a landscape firm how often field conditions prompt drawing changes. Ask a planning team who carries meeting feedback into the next proposal. [BLS landscape work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/landscape-architects.htm#tab-3) · [BLS planning work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/urban-and-regional-planners.htm#tab-3)

## Transfer the map you already know how to make

A landscape architect moving toward planning can reuse a site access analysis and learn district GIS, land use rules and engagement. A planner moving toward landscape architecture can use a connection map to define a site problem, then learn grading, planting, section drawing and the licensing path.

## Try a 30-minute bridge connection exercise

Use three fictional records. Network map N shows a north neighborhood using a bridge to reach a park. Site card S says the park path is one meter above the landing. Field note T says the available connection length is unknown. Spend eight minutes classifying the evidence, twelve drawing either a district connection map or a site section, and ten checking it. The map should preserve the link. The section should label the rise and the missing measurement, leaving accessibility to be verified.
