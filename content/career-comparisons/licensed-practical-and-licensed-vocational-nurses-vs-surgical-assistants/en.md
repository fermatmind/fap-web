# LPN or LVN vs. Surgical Assistant

## Patient care and assigned surgical assistance

Licensed practical nurses and licensed vocational nurses provide assigned nursing care, observe patients and report changes. Surgical assistants support the operating surgeon within their assigned authority, including work in the operative field.

## Compare the responsibilities

| Item | LPN or LVN | Surgical assistant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check | Assigned nursing care and patient observations | Assigned work and instructions from the surgical team |
| Communicate | Patient concerns, changes and pending care items | Status of surgical assistance and issues requiring clarification |
| Verify with | The designated nursing or clinical lead | The surgeon or the designated operating-room lead |

[O*NET 29-2061.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-2061.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 29-9093.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-9093.00#Tasks)

## A case start time changes

A fictional surgery center moves a procedure to a new start time. The LPN or LVN assigned to preprocedure care checks which information the patient is still waiting for and updates the nursing handoff. The surgical assistant checks with the surgeon or designated lead whether the assisting assignment still stands. The responsible scheduler coordinates the timing after the clinical and operating-room teams confirm their own items.

## U.S. education and credentials

BLS describes a state-approved program and passing an exam for LPN or LVN licensure. Its detailed national table assigns both roles a postsecondary nondegree award and no related experience or on-the-job training as typical categories. The surgical handbook describes accredited certificate or associate programs, including surgical-assisting pathways and additional training for some technologists moving into first assisting. Certification may be required by employers, and some states regulate this work. Verify state and facility rules. [BLS nursing entry guidance](https://www.bls.gov/OOH/healthcare/licensed-practical-and-licensed-vocational-nurses.htm#tab-4) · [BLS surgical assisting entry guidance](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/surgical-technologists.htm#tab-4) · [BLS detailed training table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/education-and-training-by-occupation.htm)

## Ask what sets the schedule

Ask a nursing employer how it handles shift handoffs and care assignments when case times move. Ask a surgical-assisting employer about case assignments, operating-room rotations and on-call expectations.

## Moving between roles

LPNs and LVNs can bring clinical communication and patient documentation to surgical-assisting training, then verify the needed education and procedural authority. Surgical assistants can bring teamwork and handoff experience to nursing training, then complete the applicable licensure process.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use a fictional schedule showing a new start time, a patient note awaiting clarification and an unconfirmed assisting assignment. Spend ten minutes drafting the nursing handoff, ten writing the assistant's clarification request and ten identifying who receives each message and who confirms the schedule. Use the provided records only.
