# Coil Winders vs. Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers: The Coil That Does Not Fit

## What each role delivers

Coil winders, tapers, and finishers make and finish coils to a work order. Electrical and electronic equipment assemblers install components and inspect assemblies.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Coil Winders, Tapers, and Finishers | Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Finished coil and process check | Installed component and assembly check |
| Basis | Job ticket, coil dimensions and insulation | Parts list, fit and inspection results |

[O*NET 51-2021.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/51-2021.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 51-2022.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/51-2022.00#Tasks)

O*NET reports the Electrical, Electronic, and Electromechanical Assemblers, Except Coil Winders, Tapers, and Finishers wage series for this role. That category does not establish a separate median for the occupation in this comparison. [O*NET national wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/51-2022.00)

## How the work meets

A fictional coil will not sit in a module housing. The winder supplies diameter and insulation inspection results. The assembler checks the coil lot against the housing revision and flags an unverified revision for the lead.

## How to enter

BLS lists a high school diploma or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for coil winding. For electrical and electronic assembly, ask which components and inspection steps the employer covers. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Coil work may repeat by part and lot. Equipment assembly follows parts lists, sequences and checks across components. Ask about shifts, rework and release responsibility.

## Moving between roles

Moving to assembly, add component placement and inspection to specification reading. Moving to coil work, build on fit checks and get station-specific winding and taping training.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a mock card: coil diameter and insulation pass, the case is labeled B and the assembly order calls for C. Spend ten minutes each on the coil record, assembly exception and who verifies the case revision before release.
