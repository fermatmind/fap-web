# Equipment Cleaner vs. Power Tool Repairer: Who Clears a Tool With an Open Fault?

## What each role delivers

Equipment cleaners clean and check assigned surfaces. Motor and power tool repairers investigate faults, repair equipment and verify its operation.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Cleaners of Vehicles and Equipment | Electric Motor, Power Tool, and Related Repairers |
|---|---|---|
| Starting point | Equipment ID, assigned surfaces and visible condition | Reported fault, unit history and checks |
| Handoff | Cleaning record and any exception | Diagnosis, repair and verification record |

## How the work meets

A fictional tool rental shop receives a tool reported to stop intermittently. The cleaner finishes the assigned exterior work and leaves the fault tag in place, adding the tool ID to the record. The repairer receives the user's description and the cleaning note and tracks diagnosis and post-service checks. The rental desk checks the repair status before changing availability.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and short-term on-the-job training for cleaners. For motor and power tool repair, it lists high school completion or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training. Ask how the employer assigns work involving electrical equipment. BLS lists less than five years of related work experience as typical for the repair occupation. [BLS Table 1.2](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Cleaning may follow busy return periods. Repair schedules can shift with faults, parts and verification. Ask how the desk distinguishes cleaned items from items with unresolved faults.

## Moving between roles

Cleaners can bring traceable IDs and condition notes to repair and add diagnostic and verification skills. Repairers can bring equipment familiarity to cleaning and learn surface care, exceptions and handoff procedures.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use mock entries: cleaning has finished, an intermittent fault has not been checked and the desk asks whether it can rent the tool. Spend ten minutes writing the cleaning handoff, ten listing evidence needed for the repair ticket and ten drafting the desk's update. Use records only.
