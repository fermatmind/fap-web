# Event Planner vs. Travel Guides: A Delayed Conference Field Trip

## What each role delivers

Meeting, convention, and event planners fit a field trip into the event and coordinate its suppliers. Travel guides assemble the group and lead the trip, checking timing and service conditions along the route.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Meeting, Convention, and Event Planners | Travel guides |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Confirmed event timeline and supplier record | Roster, route confirmation and trip change record |
| Basis | Program, contract, site and organizer authority | Roster, travel time, admissions and visit windows |

[O*NET 13-1121.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/13-1121.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 39-7012.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/39-7012.00#Tasks)

## How the work meets

A fictional conference coach is expected to arrive 25 minutes late. The guide checks the roster, admission slot and route, then asks the operator about shortening or rebooking the visit. The planner confirms whether the host and organizer can use a later slot and checks how the contract treats the cost. Once confirmed, the planner updates attendees and the guide.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for event planners. Its combined tour and travel guides category lists a high school diploma or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training. Check applicable requirements for the trips and locations you would cover. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Planners track the overall program and supplier deadlines. Guides stay with the group and coordinate each transfer. Ask who reports delays and who can authorize cost changes.

## Moving between roles

Planners can bring schedules and supplier communication into guiding and add group leadership and applicable credentials. Guides moving into planning can build on live decisions by learning budgets, contract changes and supplier coordination.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock itinerary: the coach is 25 minutes late and can leave at 9:40 a.m. The ride takes 30 minutes. The site will admit the group at its original 10 a.m. slot only, unless an unconfirmed 10:30 opening becomes available. Spend ten minutes each on trip options, approval and cost questions, and a group update.
