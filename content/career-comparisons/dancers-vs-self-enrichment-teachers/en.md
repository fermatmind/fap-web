# Dancers vs. Self-Enrichment Teachers: What Comes After the Demonstration?

## What each role delivers

Dancers practice and perform movement in time with music and partners. Self-enrichment teachers set learning goals, observe students and adapt instruction.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Dancers | Self-Enrichment Teachers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Demonstration in rhythm and formation | Practice tasks learners can repeat |
| Basis | Timing, technique and ensemble coordination | Lesson targets, observations and next steps |

## How the work meets

A fictional community program offers an adult dance workshop. A guest dancer demonstrates a side step and identifies the weight shift. The teacher sees that some learners keep the beat but travel the wrong way. The teacher splits practice into an in-place weight shift and a directional step, then requests a slower demonstration.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and long-term on-the-job training as typical for dancers. For self-enrichment teachers, it lists a high school diploma or equivalent and less than five years of related experience. Check audition and teaching demonstration requirements separately. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Dance training and performances may follow production calls. Classes may follow enrollment and fixed meeting times. Ask how preparation, demonstration and teaching hours are counted.

## Moving between roles

Moving into teaching, use demonstration skills and add lesson goals, progression checks and individual feedback. Moving into performance, use practice design skills and develop technique, regular rehearsal and audition preparation.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock class: eight learners hear the beat, five travel in the wrong direction and 12 minutes remain. Spend ten minutes each writing a slower demonstration cue, a two-part practice plan and an observable end-of-class check.
