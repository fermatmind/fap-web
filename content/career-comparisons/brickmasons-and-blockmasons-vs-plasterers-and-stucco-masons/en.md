# Brickmasons and Blockmasons vs. Plasterers and Stucco Masons: The Wall Corner Handoff

## What each role delivers

Brickmasons and blockmasons lay out and build walls. Plasterers and stucco masons prepare substrates, protect adjacent finishes and apply coatings.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Brickmasons and Blockmasons | Plasterers and Stucco Masons |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Wall dimensions and masonry handoff | Substrate preparation and finish check |
| Basis | Wall line, openings and deviations | Substrate, adjacent finishes and coating |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $62,120 | $57,660 |

## How the work meets

At the handoff of a fictional classroom partition, the mason measures a corner outside the drawing line and submits the field measurement. The plasterer sees that the planned finish would interfere with the door trim and records the area for the site lead to review.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and an apprenticeship for brickmasons and blockmasons. It lists no formal educational credential and long-term on-the-job training for plasterers and stucco masons. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Masonry follows wall layout and handoff. Plaster work follows substrate readiness, protection of adjoining finishes and finish acceptance. Ask who releases the work area.

## Moving between roles

Moving to plaster, build on drawings and substrate awareness and add finish work. Moving to masonry, bring geometry checks and learn wall layout and placement.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock card: the wall corner limit is 4 mm, the measured deviation is 7 mm and the door trim is already installed. Spend ten minutes each on a masonry exception, a finish and protection note and the person who must approve the disposition.
