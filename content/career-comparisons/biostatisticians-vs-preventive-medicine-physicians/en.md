# Biostatistician vs. Preventive Medicine Physician

## Statistical comparisons and prevention program decisions

Biostatisticians plan analyses, estimate differences, and describe uncertainty. Preventive medicine physicians may direct prevention or health education programs and use evidence to recommend how the program should proceed. The jobs can overlap in evaluation.

## Compare the work products

| Work | Biostatistician | Preventive medicine physician |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First check | Measures, denominators, and comparable conditions | Population, reach, and service constraints |
| Deliverable | Statistical findings and limits | Prevention program recommendation and follow-up |

## Attendance rose. What share of invitees attended?

A fictional community runs two health education sessions. The biostatistician reports more attendees at the second session but cannot compare attendance among invitees because the second invitation count is missing. The physician requests that count and asks for the size of the target population and the number of unique people from it who attended before assessing program reach.

## U.S. preparation and authorization

O*NET describes biostatistics as a Job Zone Five occupation, where graduate preparation is common. A supervised evaluation project can build practice specifying a measure and reporting uncertainty. For physician work, plan for medical education, postgraduate training, and state physician licensure. ABPM describes an accredited residency pathway for its Public Health and General Preventive Medicine specialty certification. Check the state medical board and employer for the requirements that apply to the job. [O*NET biostatistics preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/15-2041.01#JobZone) · [ABPM residency pathway](https://www.theabpm.org/become-certified/specialties/public-health-general-preventive-medicine/) [FSMB physician licensure](https://www.fsmb.org/guide-to-medical-regulation-in-the-united-states/about-physician-licensure)

## Compare the workday

O*NET describes indoor work and communication with others across these roles. Ask how time is divided among analysis, meetings, and program oversight. ## Moving between the roles

A biostatistician can bring evaluation skills into prevention programs. Working as a physician requires the relevant medical training and licensure. A physician can bring knowledge of program goals into biostatistics, then build skills in study design, inference, and reproducible analysis.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use fictional records from two sessions. The first invited 100 people and recorded 40 attendees. The second recorded 55 attendees and no invitation count. Spend ten minutes listing comparable measures, ten explaining whether attendance proportions can be compared, and ten writing a request for the missing information and a condition for reviewing the decision. For target-population reach, list the size of that population and the number of unique people reached within it as separate information needs.
