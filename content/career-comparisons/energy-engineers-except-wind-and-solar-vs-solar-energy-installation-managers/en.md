# Energy Engineers, Except Wind and Solar vs. Solar Energy Installation Managers: From a Baseline to a Site Plan

## What each role delivers

Energy engineers in this occupation track use, build baselines and recommend efficiency measures. Solar energy installation managers coordinate installation, crews and inspections.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Energy Engineers, Except Wind and Solar | Solar Energy Installation Managers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Demand baseline and savings analysis | Solar installation and inspection plan |
| Basis | Energy baseline, demand and operating hours | Approved drawings, materials, crews and schedule |

[O*NET 17-2199.03](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.03#Tasks) · [O*NET 47-1011.03](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-1011.03#Tasks)

O*NET uses the Engineers, All Other wage series for 17-2199.03. A separate figure for this specialty cannot be derived from the broader series. [O*NET wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/17-2199.03)

O*NET uses the broader First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers wage category for solar energy installation managers. That series does not provide a separate figure for the role. [O*NET occupational profile](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-1011.03)

## How the work meets

A fictional office finishes an energy study before the solar installation team plans site work. The engineer provides operating hours and the metering boundary. The installation manager confirms crew readiness but flags an unfiled approved drawing.

## How to enter

A bachelor’s degree is the most frequent O*NET education response for energy engineers in this occupation. For solar installation managers, high school or equivalent was the most frequent response. Ask how each employer assesses technical or site experience. [O*NET education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-1011.03#Education) [O*NET energy engineering education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.03#Education)

## Work conditions

Energy engineering can follow measurements and reviews. Installation work can follow deliveries, weather and inspections. Ask who confirms data and readiness.

## Moving between roles

Moving to installation management, bring demand analysis and build scheduling and site supervision. Moving into energy engineering, bring field records and learn measurement and savings evaluation.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock card: the baseline covers weekdays only, materials are available and the approved installation drawing is missing from the file. Spend ten minutes each on the baseline limits, site readiness and the evidence needed before a decision.
