# Office Machine Operators vs. Printing Press Operators: Two Jobs for One Meeting

## What each role delivers

Office machine operators prepare and check document orders. Printing press operators verify print specifications and use proofs to check production quality.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Office Machine Operators, Except Computer | Printing Press Operators |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Finished office document order | Press run with approved proof |
| Basis | Count, page order and due time | Stock, color and registration |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $40,960 | $45,780 |

[O*NET 43-9071.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/43-9071.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 51-5112.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/51-5112.00#Tasks)

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 43-9071 and 51-5112](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional meeting needs black-and-white packets that day and a batch of covers with a specified color. The office operator checks copies and page sequence. The press operator checks stock and reviews a proof for color and alignment before approval.

## How to enter

BLS lists a high school diploma or equivalent for both, with short-term on-the-job training for office machine operators and moderate-term training for press operators. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Office orders may follow same-day requests. Press work may follow run schedules and proof approvals. Ask about shifts, deadlines and rework.

## Moving between roles

Moving to press operation, bring job-ticket and output checks and learn materials and proof inspection. Moving to office operations, bring quality checks and learn to prioritize simultaneous internal orders.

## Try it in 30 minutes

The packet ticket calls for 20 black-and-white sets of four pages, and the sample lacks page 3. The cover ticket calls for 100 copies and a client-approved color proof. The current proof has misaligned marks. Spend ten minutes each on packet checks, proof checks and exception notes for a missing page and misregistration.
