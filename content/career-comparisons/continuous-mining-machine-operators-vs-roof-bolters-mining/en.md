# Continuous Mining Machine Operator vs Roof Bolters, Mining

## Extraction and roof support

Continuous mining machine operators run extraction equipment, check existing supports in the work area within their assignment, and report equipment and assignment status. Roof bolters install underground roof support and document the areas completed or still open. The two crews need matching records when handing off a section.

## The support record covers a different section

At a fictional mine, handoff M-16 lists the east section as the next extraction assignment. Support record S-16 marks west complete and east awaiting verification. The machine operator leaves east pending and shares the discrepancy with the roof bolter and shift lead. The roof bolter returns the section-specific status and remaining items. Under this mine's example rules, the shift lead checks the applicable records and confirms whether the next assignment can proceed.

## Training and assignment

Ask the mine about the equipment, supervised practice, and who authorizes the assignment. At underground mines covered by Part 48, federal new-task training applies to the categories listed in §48.7, with the exceptions described there. Paragraph (c) requires health and safety instruction before miners perform new tasks outside the listed categories. Verify the mine's training plan and the requirements for the specific work. [30 CFR §48.7](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/chapter-I/subchapter-H/part-48/subpart-A/section-48.7)

## What to ask about shifts

Ask the operator team who coordinates equipment delays and transfers open items to the next shift. Ask the support team who verifies completed sections and how delays are communicated when another crew is waiting.

## Skills to carry between roles

A fictional shift record can demonstrate clear reporting of section and equipment status. Moving into extraction calls for training on the assigned machine. Moving into roof support calls for training on the support work and the mine's documentation and review responsibilities.

## Try a 30-minute desk exercise

Use fictional M-16 and S-16. Spend ten minutes marking east and west separately, ten minutes writing each crew's handoff, and ten minutes drafting a summary for the shift lead. State what the west record proves, what remains open for east, and who confirms the next assignment in the example.
