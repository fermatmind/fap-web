# Solar Energy Installation Managers vs. Solar Energy Systems Engineers: The Layout Revision at the Site

## What each role delivers

Solar energy installation managers coordinate crews, materials and inspections. Solar energy systems engineers use site information to design systems and provide technical support during installation and acceptance.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Solar Energy Installation Managers | Solar Energy Systems Engineers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Installation schedule and site handoff | Solar design and technical review |
| Basis | Approved drawings, crews, materials and inspections | Site information, layout and performance |

[O*NET 47-1011.03](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-1011.03#Tasks) · [O*NET 17-2199.11](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.11#Tasks)

O*NET uses the Engineers, All Other wage series for 17-2199.11. A separate figure for this specialty cannot be derived from the broader series. [O*NET wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/17-2199.11)

O*NET uses the broader First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers wage category for solar energy installation managers. That series does not provide a separate figure for the role. [O*NET occupational profile](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-1011.03)

## How the work meets

A fictional rooftop project receives equipment, but its layout conflicts with a maintenance access path. The manager identifies work areas to hold and revises the schedule. The systems engineer compares site and design records and sends a proposed revision to the designated approver.

## How to enter

High school or equivalent was the most frequent O*NET education response for installation managers, and a bachelor’s degree was the most frequent for systems engineers. Check how employers weigh site supervision and design experience. [O*NET education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-1011.03#Education) [O*NET 17-2199.11 education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.11#Education)

## Work conditions

Installation management follows site access, deliveries, weather and inspection. Engineering follows site information, design and revision review. Ask how approvals reach the crew.

## Moving between roles

Moving to engineering, bring site judgment and add design and performance verification. Moving to installation management, bring technical interfaces and add crew planning and contractor coordination.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: S4 places the array across an access path, equipment has arrived and proposed S5 is unapproved. Spend ten minutes each on the installation hold, technical review and approval evidence needed to resume work.
