# Roofers vs. Solar Photovoltaic Installers: The Roof Seam Next to a Mount

## What each role delivers

Roofers inspect roofs and seams and maintain water protection. Solar photovoltaic installers evaluate placement and install modules and supports according to drawings.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Roofers | Solar Photovoltaic Installers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Roof condition and interface record | Array placement and installation record |
| Basis | Seams, defects and roof boundary | Array location, supports and approved drawings |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $55,440 | $53,140 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 47-2181 and 47-2231](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional warehouse is planning a rooftop array. A roofer records a water mark near an existing seam. The photovoltaic installer sees a proposed support near that seam. Both send records to the project lead before work proceeds in the area.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential for roofers and high school or equivalent for photovoltaic installers, with moderate-term on-the-job training for both. Some states license photovoltaic installers. Check with the relevant licensing board about the exact job and who may handle any electrical connection. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [BLS licensing overview](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/solar-photovoltaic-installers.htm)

## Work conditions

Both may work outdoors and depend on weather. Roofing follows surface readiness and inspection. Photovoltaic work also follows equipment and project milestones. Ask who owns the handoff.

## Moving between roles

Moving to solar, bring roof interface knowledge and add array drawings and installation training. Moving to roofing, bring handoff records and learn roof materials and condition checks.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use mock documents: drawing P3 puts mount R2 next to seam J4, where an old water mark is logged and the recheck is blank. Spend ten minutes each on the roof record, array location and two items for the designated lead to verify.
