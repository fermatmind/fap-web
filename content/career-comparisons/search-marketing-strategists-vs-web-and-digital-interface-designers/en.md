# Search Marketing Strategists vs. Web and Digital Interface Designers: Who Fixes Course Selection?

## The decision each role makes

A search marketing strategist works out what visitors expect when they arrive from search and how to measure a change. A web and digital interface designer translates those needs into navigation, layout, and interactions, then tests whether visitors can use them.

## Compare the deliverables

| Compare | Search marketing strategist | Web and digital interface designer |
|---|---|---|
| Inputs | Queries, entry pages, and subsequent behavior | User observations, interviews, and prototype tests |
| Outputs | Search brief, success measure, and follow-up analysis | Interface design, interaction specs, and test notes |

## The same mobile course catalog

Imagine a community arts school sees visits from searches for weekend pottery classes, but few of those visitors open a class listing. The strategist puts a concrete need in the brief: find a beginner class that meets on a weekend. The proposed measure is the share of visitors who open a class detail page. In a mobile test, the designer sees participants miss the filter, so they prototype a visible weekend option and a message for when nothing matches. The designer checks whether people can find a class in the prototype. After launch, the strategist reviews the measure by entry page.

## Getting started in the U.S.

For strategy work, build a portfolio case that ties anonymized queries to a page decision and follow-up measure. For interface work, show two prototypes, a test task, and one change made after observation. BLS says education requirements vary and projects or previous work can demonstrate the skills some employers seek. [BLS education guidance](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/web-developers.htm#tab-4)

## Working conditions

Campaign dates and reporting cycles shape strategy work. Designers may have to schedule sessions with participants and settle interface states with engineers. Ask whether the team can recruit test participants and who maintains the data after launch.

## Changing roles

From strategy into design, carry over your understanding of search intent and practice navigation design, prototyping, and usability interviews. From design into strategy, use your skill in forming hypotheses and learn to break out search performance by entry page.

## Try both in 30 minutes

Use three sample queries: “weekend pottery class,” “beginner pottery,” and “weekend pottery for families.” The catalog has a Saturday beginner class, a Sunday advanced class, and a Tuesday family class. Spend 10 minutes writing a search brief and measure, 10 sketching the filter before selection, with results, and without results, and 10 defining a test task and a completion criterion.
