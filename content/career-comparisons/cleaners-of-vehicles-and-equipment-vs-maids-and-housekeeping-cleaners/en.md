# Vehicle Cleaner vs. Housekeeping Cleaner: What Gets Handed Back to a Hotel?

## What each role delivers

Vehicle and equipment cleaners clean and check vehicles or equipment. Housekeeping cleaners clean rooms and common areas, replenish supplies and report conditions that affect use.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Cleaners of Vehicles and Equipment | Maids and Housekeeping Cleaners |
|---|---|---|
| Main checks | Vehicle or equipment ID, surfaces and condition | Room ID, cleanliness, linens and supplies |
| Handoff | Completed cleaning and damage report | Room status and issues for follow-up |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $35,830 | $35,510 |

## How the work meets

A fictional hotel has a group arriving early. After cleaning its shuttle, the vehicle cleaner finds a damaged seat and sends the vehicle ID and a note to the fleet lead. A housekeeper reports one room clean but short of linens and notifies the housekeeping supervisor. The desk offers the group a waiting area, asks fleet staff to confirm a backup shuttle and waits for the housekeeping supervisor to confirm that linens have been restocked before updating the room status.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and short-term on-the-job training as typical for both. Ask about equipment training, whether the vehicle role includes driving and what license and employer authorization apply. For housekeeping, ask how you learn room checks and handle found property. [BLS Table 1.2](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Vehicle cleaners may work outside or in a wash bay. Housekeeping work may follow checkout and check-in windows. Ask how the employer allocates time for inspections when the schedule gets busy.

## Moving between roles

Bring an example of a checklist and an exception report. Moving into vehicle work, learn surfaces and bay procedures. Moving into housekeeping, learn linen and supply checks, privacy and room status updates.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use three mock entries: the shuttle is clean but has a damaged seat, a cleaned room lacks linens, and a guest group wants to check in early. Spend ten minutes writing each handoff and ten identifying who can update each status. Use records only.
