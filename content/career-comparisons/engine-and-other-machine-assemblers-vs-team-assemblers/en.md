# Machine Assembler vs. Team Assembler: Who Picks Up an Unfinished Check?

## What each role delivers

An engine or machine assembler interprets specifications, positions parts and verifies the build. Team assemblers rotate through work assigned to a team and check products or parts along the way. One production line may use both arrangements.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Engine and Other Machine Assemblers | Team Assemblers |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | Build and verify a machine or engine | Rotate among assigned tasks on a team's process |
| Handoff | Machine ID, build stage and whole-unit check | Part ID, completed steps and unfinished checks |

## How the work meets

At a fictional plant, workers rotate at the end of a shift. One subassembly has a recorded visual check but no ID check. The outgoing team assembler logs its status for the next worker and informs final assembly. The incoming worker verifies the identification, records it and transfers the part. The machine assembler checks that it matches the build revision and completes the machine's functional check.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for machine assemblers, and the same preparation for its broader miscellaneous assemblers and fabricators category. Ask about qualification at each station and how the employer records rotation. [BLS Table 1.2](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Machine builds can depend on model-specific parts and inspection slots. Rotating teams also plan around staffing and coverage between stations. Ask how someone taking over a station learns what is still open on a ticket.

## Moving between roles

From machine assembly, bring reading and inspection skills and learn additional stations and shift handoffs. From team assembly, bring varied production experience and learn the specific machine's sequence, alignment and final checks.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use three mock parts: one has both checks, one lacks an ID check and one lacks a traceable part number. Spend ten minutes classifying them, ten writing the rotation handoff and ten listing the records final assembly needs before accepting them. Use mock paperwork only.
