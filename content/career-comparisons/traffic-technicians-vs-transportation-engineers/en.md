# Traffic Technicians vs. Transportation Engineers: When a Left-Turn Queue Spills Back

## What each role delivers

Traffic technicians measure traffic and delays and organize study records. Transportation engineers compare and review designs against engineering criteria and project constraints.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Traffic Technicians | Transportation Engineers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Turn counts and queue logs | Intersection design alternatives and review notes |
| Basis | Direction, interval and record completeness | Geometry, observations and design criteria |

## How the work meets

At a fictional intersection, a left-turn queue extends beyond its lane. The technician logs turns and queue extent for two peak periods and marks an interval with an obstructed view. The engineer identifies what needs another count and compares the implications of changing lane space or existing operations.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for traffic technicians. A bachelor’s degree accounted for 77% of U.S. O*NET education responses for transportation engineers. Check the state board for duties that require a PE license. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [O*NET education data 17-2051.01](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2051.01#Education) [BLS civil engineering licensing](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/civil-engineers.htm)

## Work conditions

Studies may involve peak-hour field shifts. Design may involve site visits, calculations and drawing review. Ask how the design team requests a repeat count.

## Moving between roles

Moving into design, use traffic observation experience and build engineering analysis, standards and applicable credentials. Moving into field studies, use design context and develop consistent measurement and raw-record review.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records show Monday spillback, an incomplete Tuesday queue record and a marked crossing close to the turn lane. Spend ten minutes each on the observation gap, two design considerations and a follow-up survey request.
