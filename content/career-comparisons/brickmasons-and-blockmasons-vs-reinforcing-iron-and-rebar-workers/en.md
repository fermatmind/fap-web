# Brickmasons and Blockmasons vs. Reinforcing Iron and Rebar Workers: When the Drawings Conflict

## What each role delivers

Brickmasons and blockmasons lay out and build masonry. Reinforcing iron and rebar workers locate and secure reinforcement according to drawings and check placement.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Brickmasons and Blockmasons | Reinforcing Iron and Rebar Workers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Built wall and layout record | Reinforcement placement record |
| Basis | Wall, opening and masonry drawing | Reinforcement drawing, location and spacing |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $62,120 | $58,970 |

## How the work meets

In a fictional equipment room, a masonry partition meets a concrete edge beam. The mason marks the wall line from the architectural drawing. The rebar worker checks the structural drawing. They document conflicting locations and send the measurements and drawing IDs to the technical lead.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and apprenticeship as typical for both. Ask apprenticeship providers and employers how they assess drawing reading, placement and site training. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Masonry follows wall sections and handoffs. Reinforcement work follows structural sequences and pre-pour checks. Ask who reviews discrepancies and clears the next operation.

## Moving between roles

Moving to reinforcement, bring drawing and measurement skills and learn reinforcement plans and pre-pour checks. Moving to masonry, use placement experience and add wall layout and masonry practice.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock card: architectural drawing A2 shows a wall clearance that overlaps reinforcement on S3. Both are issued drawings, and there is no approved resolution. Spend ten minutes each on a wall handoff, reinforcement check and a question for the technical lead. Use records only.
