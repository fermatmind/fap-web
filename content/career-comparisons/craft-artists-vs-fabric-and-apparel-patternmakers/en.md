# Craft Artist vs. Apparel Patternmaker: Turning a Handmade Vest Into a Size Range

## What each role delivers

A craft artist selects materials and makes an object or prototype by hand. A fabric and apparel patternmaker translates the garment into pattern pieces that the team can cut, fit and grade.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Craft artists | Fabric and Apparel Patternmakers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Craft sample and notes on materials and finish | Versioned pattern with sizing and construction marks |
| Basis | Material feel, form and technique | Measurements, stretch, seams and fit feedback |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $46,080 | $62,750 |

## How the work meets

A fictional studio wants to make a handmade patchwork vest in three sizes. The craft artist delivers the sample, marks the direction of the motif and explains which seam lines shape the look. The patternmaker drafts all three sizes and marks grain and joins. After a fitting shows restricted shoulder movement in the largest size, the patternmaker revises it and both check the new sample.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and long-term on-the-job training as typical for craft artists. For fabric and apparel patternmakers it lists a high school diploma or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Craft work can follow material and prototype schedules. Patternmaking often follows sampling, fittings and production deadlines. Ask who records and signs off on fitting changes.

## Moving between roles

Craft experience with materials and templates transfers to patternmaking; add measurements, grading and fitting revisions. Patternmakers bring accurate cutting to craft work and can add hand forming, finishing and object development.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock packet: a vest sketch for body chest measurements of 88, 96 and 104 centimeters, a motif that must keep its direction, and restricted shoulder movement in the largest fitting. Check the required ease with the fitting team. Spend ten minutes each on the visual constraints, pattern checks and movements to retest, and a version handoff.
