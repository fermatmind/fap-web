# Compliance Manager vs. Regulatory Affairs Specialist

## Follow the process or trace the figure

A compliance manager may direct an investigation into how an error passed internal review and follow up on the fix. A regulatory affairs specialist may trace a figure to its source, review the supporting material, and help prepare a response to an agency question.

## Compare the work products

| Work | Compliance manager | Regulatory affairs specialist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First check | How the figure passed internal review | Where the submitted figure came from |
| Deliverable | Findings and a plan to verify corrective action | Source references and a draft response |
| Collaboration | Process owners and internal reviewers | Data owners, technical reviewers, and submission leads |

## A reviewer flags two different numbers

At a fictional company, a filing table differs from its supporting report. The specialist traces the value back to the report, asks its owner whether a unit conversion explains the difference, and records what still needs verification in a draft reply. The compliance manager checks whether other tables went through the same review and asks the process owner to test a revised check. The company's authorized representative handles the formal response under its procedures.

## Preparing for U.S. openings

Look for compliance support or internal control work where you can gather evidence and track findings before taking on review leadership. For regulatory affairs, look for work supporting submissions where technical reviewers can check your source references and draft responses. O*NET places both occupations in Job Zone Four, where a bachelor’s degree and relevant experience are common. Check the requirements for the specific opening. [O*NET Compliance Managers preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/11-9199.02#JobZone) · [O*NET Regulatory Affairs Specialists preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/13-1041.07#JobZone)

## Ask what disrupts the schedule

Ask compliance teams how an urgent investigation affects planned reviews and who can secure time from other departments. Ask regulatory affairs teams who sets response deadlines and who reviews the technical details when several requests arrive together.

## Moving between the jobs

A compliance reviewer can show how they document sources and approvals, then learn how to map technical evidence to a filing. A regulatory affairs specialist can show how they resolved a document discrepancy, then build experience testing how widely a process issue occurred and verifying the corrective action.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Make up a data table, a supporting report, and a short request about conflicting figures. Spend ten minutes listing the unit and version questions, ten minutes drafting a reply with any unresolved points marked, and ten minutes writing a follow-up for the process owner with a sample to check and a criterion for closing the issue.
