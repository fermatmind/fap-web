# Loan Officers vs. Securities, Commodities, and Financial Services Sales Agents: Routing an Account Question

## What each role delivers

Loan officers explain loan terms and process applications. Securities, commodities, and financial services sales agents handle assigned products or transactions within their authority. This example focuses on a securities account inquiry.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Loan Officers | Securities, Commodities, and Financial Services Sales Agents |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Loan terms and application record | Securities inquiry and referral record |
| Basis | Purpose, repayment and approval authority | Client instructions, registration and product scope |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $76,690 | $78,660 |

## How the work meets

A fictional business asks whether a brokerage statement can support a loan file and how to change the brokerage account contact. The officer explains what version the lender accepts and, with the customer’s consent, refers the account question to authorized securities staff. They clarify the request and log the responses separately.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree and moderate-term on-the-job training for both and less than five years of related experience for loan officers. Check federal registration or state licensing for covered residential mortgage activity. For the FINRA general securities representative role, check SIE, Series 7 and firm sponsorship for the representative exam. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [CFPB Regulation G](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1007/103/) [CFPB Regulation H](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1008/103/) [FINRA Series 7](https://www.finra.org/registration-exams-ce/qualification-exams/series7)

## Work conditions

Loan work may follow application and approval deadlines. Securities work may follow market activity and account requests. Ask what the team records after a referral.

## Moving between roles

Moving into securities adds product, order and applicable registration knowledge. Moving into credit adds application, repayment assessment and approval rules to customer communication skills.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock file: the client sends a two-month-old brokerage statement for a loan and asks to update a brokerage account contact. Spend ten minutes each on a loan documentation reply, an authorized referral and which items require separate follow-up.
