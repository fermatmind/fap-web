# Dispatchers (Except Police, Fire and Ambulance) vs. Power Distributors and Dispatchers

## The responsibility boundary

General dispatchers assign people or equipment to jobs. Power distributors and dispatchers monitor electric system conditions and, within their authority, coordinate or adjust power distribution and issue operating directions. A crew being available does not establish system readiness.

## How to prepare

O*NET groups general dispatch in Job Zone 1–2. Look for scheduling or service coordination work to practice assigning resources and recording changes. [Dispatch preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/43-5032.00#JobZone)

BLS describes at least high school or equivalent and extensive on-the-job training for the operator, distributor and dispatcher group. NERC PER-003-2 requires the covered real-time reliability positions to be staffed by system operators with the appropriate valid credential. The standard allows certain tasks under direct supervision of the certified operator. Check whether the target job falls within the standard and what the employer requires. [BLS preparation](https://www.bls.gov/OOH/production/power-plant-operators-distributors-and-dispatchers.htm#tab-4) · [NERC certification program](https://www.nerc.com/programs/system-operator-certification--credential-maintenance) [NERC PER-003-2, R1–R3](https://www.nerc.com/globalassets/standards/reliability-standards/per/per-003-2.pdf)

## Work conditions

Service dispatch may handle several crews and requests on one shift. Power dispatch can involve sustained monitoring and rotating schedules. Ask employers how they structure training, shift handoffs and escalation.

## Moving between roles

From service dispatch, transfer your handoff and tracking experience, then build electrical knowledge and supervised operating preparation. Moving the other direction, use accurate handoffs to coordinate fleet or field service work.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use fictional notes only. A planned job needs one crew, with Team A free at 2 and Team B at 3. The system-side status is unknown. Spend ten minutes listing staffing options, ten recording the information the authorized team must confirm, and ten writing the shift note. Keep the exercise at the information level.
