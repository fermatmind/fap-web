# Lodging Manager vs. Property, Real Estate, and Community Association Manager: A Shared Garage Closure

## What each role delivers

A lodging manager coordinates rooms, check-in and guest services. A property, real estate, or community association manager coordinates building operations, maintenance and relationships with tenants and owners.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Lodging Manager | Property, real estate, and community association managers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Hotel guest parking and arrival plan | Tenant notice, contractor schedule and owner record |
| Basis | Arrivals, alternate parking and hotel policy | Closure scope, leases, owner authority and contractor timeline |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $69,250 | $69,990 |

## How the work meets

In a fictional mixed-use building, one garage level closes for repairs. The property manager checks the closure window and affected stalls with the contractor, notifies tenants under the leases and owner’s authority, and passes the confirmed hotel parking allocation to the lodging manager. The lodging manager directs arriving guests to the available alternatives and briefs the front desk.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent and less than five years of related experience as typical for both occupations. It lists short-term on-the-job training for property, real estate, and community association managers. In California, paid leasing or rent collection for another can require a real estate broker license. Section 10131.01(a)(1) exempts specified roles from §10131(b), including hotel managers and resident apartment managers. [California Department of Real Estate, §§10131(b), 10131.01(a)(1)](https://dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/relaw/relaw.pdf) [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Lodging work follows arrival and checkout waves and shift coverage. Property work also follows contractor schedules, maintenance and owner or tenant communications. Ask who approves changes to shared areas.

## Moving between roles

Guest-service coordination transfers to property management; add property contracts, budgets and owner communications. Property managers moving into lodging can bring vendor coordination and learn room inventory and arrival operations.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock garage record: 18 stalls close, including 12 assigned to tenants. The hotel has confirmed six off-site spaces and expects eight guest vehicles tonight. Spend ten minutes each on the tenant notice and open approvals, use of the six hotel spaces, and separate updates for guests and tenants.
