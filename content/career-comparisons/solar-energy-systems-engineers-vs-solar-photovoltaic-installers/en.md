# Solar Energy Systems Engineers vs. Solar Photovoltaic Installers: When the Site Conflicts with the Drawing

## What each role delivers

Solar energy systems engineers design systems from site information and support verification. Solar photovoltaic installers fit modules and supports to approved plans and record field differences.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Solar Energy Systems Engineers | Solar Photovoltaic Installers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Site information and system design | Installation status and field deviations |
| Basis | Survey, layout, components and verification criteria | Approved drawings, parts, locations and progress |

[O*NET 17-2199.11](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.11#Tasks) · [O*NET 47-2231.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-2231.00#Tasks)

O*NET uses the Engineers, All Other wage series for 17-2199.11. A separate figure for this specialty cannot be derived from the broader series. [O*NET wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/17-2199.11)

## How the work meets

A fictional roof has a new access path that conflicts with the planned array. The installer supplies measurements and a drawing ID. The engineer reviews access and design constraints and sends a proposed revision for approval.

## How to enter

A bachelor’s degree was the most frequent O*NET education response for solar systems engineers. BLS lists high school or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for photovoltaic installers. Some states require photovoltaic installers to be licensed. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [BLS licensing overview](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/solar-photovoltaic-installers.htm) [O*NET 17-2199.11 education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.11#Education)

## Work conditions

Engineering can move between site assessment, design and acceptance support. Installation can follow site access, weather and deliveries. Ask who communicates revisions to the crew.

## Moving between roles

Moving to installation, bring drawing and verification skills and seek site training. Moving to engineering, build on field records and add site modeling, design and performance verification.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: approved drawing S6 puts array A in a newly confirmed access path. Proposed S7 awaits approval. Spend ten minutes each on the field exception and hold, engineering review and which drawing, conditions and approval owner must be confirmed before work resumes.
