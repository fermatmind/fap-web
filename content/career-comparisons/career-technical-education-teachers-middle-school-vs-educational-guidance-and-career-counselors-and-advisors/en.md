# Middle-School CTE Teachers vs. School and Career Advisors

## What each role delivers

Middle-school CTE teachers teach and assess technical work. Educational, guidance, and career counselors and advisors help students compare education and career options and navigate course choices.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Career/Technical Education Teachers, Middle School | Educational, Guidance, and Career Counselors and Advisors |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Skills observation and feedback | Course alternatives and follow-up |
| Evidence | Classroom work | Goals, prerequisites and schedule |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $65,030 | $64,330 |

National occupation medians. Local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 25-2023 and 21-1012](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional student enjoys a drawing project but is undecided about an advanced elective. The teacher reports what the student demonstrated. The advisor checks prerequisites and scheduling and helps the student compare two options under the school’s enrollment process.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree and less than five years of related experience as typical for middle-school CTE teachers. For educational, guidance, and career counselors and advisors, its occupation row lists a master’s degree as typical. California’s Designated Subjects CTE credential is one example of authorization for named vocational subjects through grade 12. Check the state and school assignment. [California CTC CTE authorization](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/cl-888/) [BLS occupation rows](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) For California school counseling, separately check the PPS School Counseling credential. Career advising outside schools can have different requirements. [California CTC School Counseling](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/cl-606c/)

## Work conditions

Teachers work around class periods, demonstrations and grading. Advisors work around appointments, student records and registration deadlines. Ask how staff share only the information needed and who processes course changes.

## Moving between roles

Teachers moving to advising can use student feedback skills and build counseling, program navigation and recordkeeping experience. Advisors moving to teaching need classroom instruction, technical assessment and applicable authorization.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional student’s two classroom notes and two electives. Advanced Drawing meets Thursday but has an unverified prerequisite. Creative Drawing meets Tuesday. The student is available only on Thursday. Spend ten minutes on teacher feedback, ten on an advising note, and ten identifying an unknown prerequisite and who submits the choice.
