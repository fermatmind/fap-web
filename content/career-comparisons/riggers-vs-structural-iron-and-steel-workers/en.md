# Riggers vs. Structural Iron and Steel Workers: The Beam Handoff

## What each role delivers

Riggers inspect rigging and coordinate load movement. Structural iron and steel workers align and connect members to erection drawings. Assignments and authorizations can overlap.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Riggers | Structural Iron and Steel Workers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Lift handoff and rigging inspection record | Steel alignment and connection record |
| Basis | Member ID, lift plan and handoff point | Erection drawing, member location and connection checks |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $62,640 | $62,780 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 49-9096 and 47-2221](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional warehouse beam reaches the documented handoff point. The rigging crew returns the lift record. The steel crew finds the connection check blank and flags the member ID and open item for review.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent for both, with moderate-term on-the-job training for riggers and apprenticeship for structural iron and steel workers. For covered construction crane work when employees hook, unhook, guide or initially connect a load within the fall zone, OSHA requires the materials to be rigged by a qualified rigger. Verify the employer’s qualification process for the specific task. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [OSHA 1926.1425(c)](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.1425)

## Work conditions

Rigging can follow lift windows. Steel work can follow member delivery and connection checks. Ask who documents handoff and approves the next operation.

## Moving between roles

Moving to steel work, bring member identification and coordination skills and learn erection drawings and connection checks. Moving to rigging, bring site handoff skills and obtain training and qualification appropriate to the work.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Read a mock record: B7 has reached the handoff point, the rigging record is signed and the connection check on drawing C is blank. Spend ten minutes each on the handoff, connection exception and who verifies closure. Use records only.
