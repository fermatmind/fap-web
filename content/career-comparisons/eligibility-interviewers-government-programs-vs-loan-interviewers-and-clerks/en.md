# Eligibility Interviewers, Government Programs vs. Loan Interviewers and Clerks: Checking Income for Different Decisions

## What each role delivers

Eligibility interviewers collect information and may determine eligibility or assistance under program authority. Loan interviewers and clerks gather and verify application records and route them for review.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Eligibility Interviewers, Government Programs | Loan Interviewers and Clerks |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Eligibility record and program status | Loan file and handoff record |
| Basis | Program rules, household evidence and authority | Loan fields, deadlines and attachments |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $54,210 | $50,020 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 43-4061 and 43-4131](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional household applies for public assistance and a loan at separate organizations. The eligibility interviewer reviews the program’s income period. The loan clerk finds that the loan requires a more recent month and requests it through the lender’s approved channel. Each route records its time period and outstanding documents.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent for both. It lists moderate-term on-the-job training for eligibility interviewers and short-term training for loan interviewers and clerks. Check the hiring agency or lender for the actual duties and approval authority. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Eligibility work may follow filing and recertification cycles. Loan intake may follow application, document and closing deadlines. Ask how teams track source dates.

## Moving between roles

Moving into loan intake, add loan documents and handoffs to interview and verification skills. Moving into public program review, add program criteria, recertification and eligibility records to file management experience.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock file: the benefit program requests last quarter, the loan file needs the latest three months and the newest month is missing. Spend ten minutes each on a program note, a loan document request and where the two decisions belong.
