# Middle-School CTE Teachers vs. Postsecondary Education Teachers

## What each role delivers

Middle-school CTE teachers teach technical concepts and check students’ classroom performance. Postsecondary education teachers help college students study teaching methods and design instruction, and may also supervise fieldwork or conduct research.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Career/Technical Education Teachers, Middle School | Education Teachers, Postsecondary |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Demonstration and student skill checks | Methods coursework and feedback |
| Learners | Middle-school students | College education students |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $65,030 | $75,350 |

## How the work meets

In a fictional skills lesson, most groups can name the tools but only one records their roles. The middle-school teacher revises the next demonstration. The college instructor uses fictional observation notes to ask teacher candidates to justify a lesson redesign.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree and less than five years of related experience as typical for middle-school CTE teachers. For education teachers, postsecondary, its occupation row lists a doctoral or professional degree and less than five years of related experience as typical. California’s Designated Subjects CTE credential is one example of authorization for named vocational subjects through grade 12. Check the state and school assignment. [California CTC CTE authorization](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/cl-888/) [BLS occupation rows](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

School periods structure setup, demonstrations and feedback. College education faculty may divide time among coursework, feedback, field supervision and research. Ask how field supervision counts toward workload.

## Moving between roles

Moving to college, bring classroom evidence and develop education research and methods coursework. Moving to middle school, add adolescent classroom management, technical instruction and the relevant state authorization.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use fictional notes from four groups: three identify tools and one records roles. Spend ten minutes creating a middle-school observation goal, ten writing the college assignment, and ten writing separate scoring criteria.
