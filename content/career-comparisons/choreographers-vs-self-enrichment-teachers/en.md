# Choreographers vs. Self-Enrichment Teachers: Teaching a Turn to Adult Beginners

## What each role delivers

Choreographers create movement and formations and guide rehearsals. Self-enrichment teachers set learning goals, demonstrate skills and adapt to participants.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Choreographers | Self-Enrichment Teachers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Performance sequence and formations | Lesson goals, practice options and feedback |
| Basis | Performance intent, spacing and phrasing | Learner experience, progress and practice conditions |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $55,310 | $46,800 |

## How the work meets

A fictional adult class borrows a movement phrase from a show. The choreographer identifies the accent that should stay and the spacing that can change. The teacher demonstrates the turn in place, then uses practice feedback to decide whether to add travel. The teacher reports where learners lost the beat so both can revise the class version.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent for both roles. It lists five or more years of related experience and long-term on-the-job training for choreographers and less than five years of related experience for self-enrichment teachers. Check the actual audition or teaching demonstration requirements. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Choreography can follow production and rehearsal deadlines. Hobby classes may happen on evenings or weekends. Ask whether teaching time includes lesson preparation and feedback.

## Moving between roles

Moving into teaching, use movement and rhythm skills and build tiered practice and individual feedback. Moving into choreography, use demonstration skills and add ensemble blocking and rehearsal direction.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock class: six of 12 adults turn in place on the beat and three can stay on the beat while traveling. Spend ten minutes each on a performance priority, two practice levels and an observable condition for moving up.
