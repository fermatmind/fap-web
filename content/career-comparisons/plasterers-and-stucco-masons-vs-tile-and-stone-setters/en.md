# Plasterers and Stucco Masons vs. Tile and Stone Setters: Who Checks the Wall Before Tiling?

## What each role delivers

Plasterers and stucco masons prepare and coat walls and protect adjacent surfaces. Tile and stone setters check readiness, lay out and cut pieces and inspect the finish.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Plasterers and Stucco Masons | Tile and Stone Setters |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Substrate preparation and finish record | Tile pattern, alignment and joints |
| Basis | Substrate condition, flatness and protection | Layout, edges and joints |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $57,660 | $55,690 |

## How the work meets

At a fictional changing room, one patch of plaster exceeds the project tolerance for the tile substrate. The plaster crew records its location. The tile crew leaves the area unaccepted and flags the doorway layout for review after substrate confirmation.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and long-term on-the-job training for both. Ask whether the employer trains and assigns substrate preparation, finish work or both. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Plaster work follows areas and substrate handoffs. Tile work follows readiness, layout and edge cuts. Ask who signs the substrate record and checks the installed finish.

## Moving between roles

Moving to tile, bring substrate and measurement skills and learn layout, joints and cutting. Moving to plaster, bring handoff and inspection skills and learn material and finish procedures.
