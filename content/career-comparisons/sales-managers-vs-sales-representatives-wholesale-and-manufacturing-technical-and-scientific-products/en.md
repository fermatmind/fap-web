# Sales Managers vs. Technical and Scientific Wholesale Sales Representatives: Scheduling a Product Pilot

## What each role delivers

Sales managers oversee teams and sales priorities. Technical and scientific wholesale representatives evaluate application needs, product specifications and commercial terms.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Sales Managers | Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Technical and Scientific Products |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Pilot resource allocation and escalation decisions | Technical requirements and pilot proposal |
| Basis | Team capacity, cost and priorities | Sample conditions, specifications and acceptance criteria |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $148,270 | $104,920 |

## How the work meets

A fictional buyer asks to test an instrument on the same material at two temperatures. The representative records the conditions and checks feasibility with the applications team. The manager reviews demo equipment, support and conflicts and approves an available slot. The representative communicates the schedule and identifies the criteria still to be tested.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree for both occupations. It lists less than five years of related experience for managers and moderate-term on-the-job training for technical wholesale representatives. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Managers may coordinate resources and reviews. Representatives may visit sites and organize demos. Ask who documents validated test results.

## Moving between roles

Moving into management adds staffing and forecasting to product experience. Moving into technical sales adds application assessment and validation to business coordination.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock brief: two temperatures are requested, the device is available for half a day, and support confirmed only one temperature. Spend ten minutes each on technical questions, scheduling options and what can be promised.
