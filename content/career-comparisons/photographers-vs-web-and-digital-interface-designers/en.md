# Photographer vs. Web and Digital Interface Designer: Fixing a Mobile Homepage Crop

## What each role delivers

A photographer captures, selects and processes images for a brief. A web and digital interface designer organizes content and interactions around user needs and verifies how the page works across screens.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Photographers | Web and digital interface designers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Contact sheet, edited images and alternate framing | Responsive layout, image specifications and test notes |
| Basis | Lighting, subject scale, negative space and delivery specifications | User task, container ratio, text and action placement |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $44,660 | $104,000 |

## How the work meets

A fictional museum builds an online exhibition homepage. The photographer sends a landscape image with the exhibit detail on the right. In the phone prototype the designer finds that a centered crop loses the detail and the title is hard to read over a bright area. The designer specifies the phone framing and title position. The photographer selects a vertical frame from the shoot, and the designer verifies the phone and desktop layouts before handoff.

## How to enter

BLS lists a high school diploma or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training as typical for photographers. It lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for web and digital interface designers. Ask who defines image specifications and who verifies the implementation. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Photography follows shoot windows and editing batches. Interface design follows review, implementation and checks across devices. Ask which layouts must be checked before launch.

## Moving between roles

Photographers can bring visual hierarchy and asset management to interface design and learn user research, interaction and responsive testing. Designers can bring visual briefs to photography and learn capture, lighting and editing workflows.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock layout: the desktop container is 16:9 and the phone container is 4:5. Image A places the subject on the right; image B is vertical with the subject centered. The phone title needs separation from the bright background. Spend ten minutes each on image selection, two layout sketches, and checks for subject visibility, text and the primary action.
