# Costume Attendants vs. Set and Exhibit Designers: A Costume Catches in a Set Doorway

## What each role delivers

Costume attendants prepare garments, assist with dressing and record problems. Set and exhibit designers plan layouts and draw construction details. This example uses a theater set.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Costume Attendants | Set and Exhibit Designers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Costumed walk-through notes and dressing setup | Stage layout and revised set drawing |
| Basis | Garment width, dressing status and change location | Openings, dimensions and scene requirements |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $50,400 | $75,240 |

## How the work meets

At a fictional rehearsal, a performer’s full skirt catches in a set doorway. The attendant records the garment version, direction and point of contact. The designer compares widening the opening with shifting the route, sends the options to the stage team and requests another walk-through under the same conditions.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent and short-term on-the-job training for costume attendants and a bachelor’s degree as typical for set and exhibit designers. Ask about dressing calls or drawing and installation responsibilities. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Wardrobe may follow cast calls, rehearsals and performances. Set design may move between drafting, site checks and installation coordination. Ask how the team schedules a follow-up walk-through.

## Moving between roles

Moving into set design, use performer and garment awareness and build scale drawing, materials and coordination skills. Moving into wardrobe, bring backstage layout knowledge and learn garment identification, dressing and change routines.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock layout: the opening is 85 centimeters, the dressed skirt is about 95 centimeters wide and an alternate 110-centimeter route crosses the changing area. Spend ten minutes each logging the issue, sketching layout or route options and specifying the walk-through conditions.
