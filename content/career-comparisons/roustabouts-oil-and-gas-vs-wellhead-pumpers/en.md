# Roustabouts, Oil and Gas vs Wellhead Pumpers

## Follow the crew assignment or follow the wells

A roustabout assists with equipment, assembly and maintenance tasks. A wellhead pumper operates well pumps and related equipment within their assignment, performs routine maintenance, and documents production and exceptions. If you prefer varied crew assignments and tangible tasks, start with roustabout work. If you prefer tracking how the same wells perform over time, explore wellhead pumping.

## Compare the handoffs at one site

| Compare | Roustabouts, oil and gas | Wellhead pumpers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting point | Work order, tools and crew assignments | Well roster, production log and equipment status |
| Work setting | Field sites with the assigned crew | Well sites for pump operation and routine maintenance, plus production reporting |
| Shift output | Work status and things still needed | Production and exception log by well |

## One well, two records after a field visit

SLB describes a Permian Basin operator dealing with downhole pump downtime and field interventions. In an illustrative case at another site, the pumper records when production data went missing and reports that the well’s current status needs verification. The roustabout confirms that assigned materials were delivered and reports that the work order is still open. The pumper and crew each check their records. The site’s authorized supervisor determines how to proceed with operation. ## Use the BLS categories to compare entry paths

BLS classifies roustabouts as typically needing no formal educational credential or prior related work, and wellhead pumpers as typically needing a high school diploma or equivalent and less than five years of related work experience. Both list moderate-term on-the-job training. Ask employers which shop or field assignments are supervised and what task-specific qualifications they require. [BLS Table 1.2](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Compare field schedules alongside pay

In 2025 the national median annual wage was $46,960 for roustabouts and $69,960 for wellhead pumpers, according to BLS OEWS. Ask how each employer handles travel between sites, callouts and overnight assignments. Compare two offers using similar seniority, hours and location. [BLS Table 1.2](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Bring a work record you can explain

A roustabout can show how a work order and materials log helped the crew see what remained open, then practice well production records and reporting. A pumper can bring incident and routine field logs into a crew support role and learn work-order tracking and the facility’s maintenance training requirements.

## Try two paper notes in thirty minutes

Use fictional records for well W12. There is a production log gap from 9 to 10 a.m. Work order M3 says the parts arrived at 10:15 a.m., with no acceptance entry. Spend 12 minutes writing a crew update and 13 minutes writing the well handoff. Take five minutes to assign the data check and work-order check, and state who can confirm whether operations resumed.
