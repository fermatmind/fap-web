# Family Medicine Physicians vs Midwives

## Primary care over time and care around birth

Family physicians provide diagnosis, treatment, and preventive care for patients of different ages. Midwives focus on care around pregnancy, labor, and the postpartum period. Both may collect health histories, document changes, and coordinate referrals. Which tasks a particular clinician can perform depends on their privileges or authorized scope.

## What each practice records

| Item | Family medicine physicians | Midwives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work product | Visit note, prevention or treatment plan, follow-up record | Prenatal assessment, labor record, postpartum assessment |
| Example setting | Family practice or community primary care clinic | Birth center, where the role and setting are authorized |
| Questions to ask | Who tracks ongoing care across visits? | Who takes over when a birth spans a shift or requires referral? |

## Who takes over when care is transferred

In a fictional California case, a licensed midwife at a birth center recognizes that a postpartum concern meets the conditions for transfer, initiates the transfer, and provides the records to the receiving hospital physician. In this example the hospital confirms receipt and its physician takes responsibility for evaluating the referred concern. The family physician separately confirms a follow-up date for a longstanding issue already managed by the family practice. [Medical Board of California transfer guidance](https://www.mbc.ca.gov/Licensing/Licensed-Midwives/Practice-Information/)

## Compare U.S. training and state requirements

Physicians typically complete medical school and specialty residency training. Check the state medical board for licensure. For Certified Professional Midwife (CPM) certification, NARM lists routes including its Portfolio Evaluation Process. NARM has provisional rules for eligible students already enrolled in programs accredited by the Midwifery Education Accreditation Council (MEAC) from October 1, 2026 through March 31, 2028. Before enrolling, ask NARM how your enrollment and expected completion dates affect eligibility. State licensing is a separate step. For example, the Medical Board of California is the regulator for licensed midwives in California. [BLS physician training](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/physicians-and-surgeons.htm) · [NARM application routes](https://narm.org/certification-recertification/cpm-application/) · [NARM transition FAQ](https://info.narm.org/article/88-meac-transition-public-faq) · [Medical Board of California](https://www.mbc.ca.gov/Licensing/Licensed-Midwives/Practice-Information/)

## Ask for a sample schedule

At a family practice, ask who handles messages and results when the appointment schedule is full, and who covers your patient panel during leave. At a birth center, ask how call windows work, who takes over if labor continues after the scheduled shift, and how postpartum visits are assigned following an overnight call.

## Moving between the two roles

A family physician exploring a direct-entry midwifery role can show how they document continuity of care. Ask the training program and licensing authority what prior experience they recognize and what supervised birth experience or credentials the role still requires. A midwife pursuing family medicine can bring experience with history-taking and handoffs, then check medical school admission, family medicine residency, and state medical licensure requirements. [NARM application routes](https://narm.org/certification-recertification/cpm-application/) · [BLS physician training](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/physicians-and-surgeons.htm)

## Try a 30-minute records exercise

Use this fictional record: the birth center sent the records and is still waiting for confirmation from the hospital. The family practice confirmed a Thursday visit for a longstanding concern. Spend eight minutes marking what each team has confirmed. Spend twelve minutes drafting either a midwife’s handoff note with the receiving team, records sent, and confirmation still due, or a family physician’s follow-up entry with the visit purpose, date, and team responsible for follow-through. Use the last ten minutes to check that the notes distinguish records sent from receipt confirmed. Write which record you would prefer to own and one question you would ask a supervisor about it.
