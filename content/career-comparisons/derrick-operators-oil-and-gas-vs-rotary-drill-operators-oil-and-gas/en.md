# Oil and Gas Derrick Operator vs. Rotary Drill Operator

## Start with what each operator tracks

Derrick operators monitor equipment such as mud pumps and prepare mud reports. Rotary drill operators use drill controls, maintain drilling records, and direct crew activities on assigned work.

## Compare the shift records

| Record | Derrick operator | Rotary drill operator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine entry | Circulation equipment observations and mud report | Drilling progress, time and tools used |
| Handoff item | Pump observation, time and whom the operator notified | Related point in the drilling log and items for the next crew to verify |

[O*NET 47-5011.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-5011.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 47-5012.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-5012.00#Tasks)

## A pump observation at shift change

Imagine a crew handoff where a derrick operator reports unusual pump noise and a pending maintenance response. The rotary drill operator can link the report to the affected part of the drilling log and flag it for the incoming crew. The site's procedures and assigned authority govern any operational decision.

## U.S. education and training

BLS's detailed occupation table lists no formal educational credential as typical for either occupation. It assigns short-term on-the-job training to derrick operators and moderate-term training to rotary drill operators. Check the employer's requirements for prior crew experience, equipment training, and authorization before independent work. [BLS education and training table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/education-and-training-by-occupation.htm) BLS assigns less than five years of related experience to rotary drill operators. It assigns the same related-experience category to the two occupations.

## What to ask about a rig schedule

Ask how inspections and work around the circulation system fit into a derrick operator's shift. For rotary drill work, ask how console time and crew coordination are divided. Compare rotation length, shift overlap and the process for passing open issues to maintenance.

## Moving from one role to the other

Derrick operators can bring experience documenting equipment changes and communicating with a crew. Rotary drill work adds drilling logs, control systems and crew coordination. Rotary drill operators can transfer handoff and monitoring skills toward derrick work, then learn the specific circulation equipment and inspection responsibilities.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use fictional notes: unusual pump noise just before handoff, no maintenance response yet, and the shift's progress already recorded. Take ten minutes to draft the derrick report and ten to write the relevant drilling-log handoff. Use the final ten to identify who receives each record and which fact still needs verification. Use paperwork only.
