# Interior Designers vs Landscape Architects

## Plan the room or design the outdoor site

Interior designers work on indoor layout, circulation, finishes and lighting. Landscape architects design outdoor paths, grades, planting and use areas. Their drawings meet at an entrance, where the outdoor arrival must connect with an indoor route.

## Compare the work products and U.S. wages

| Item | Interior designers | Landscape architects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Example setting | Interior design or architecture team | Landscape or multidisciplinary design studio |
| Typical output | Interior plan, finishes and lighting notes | Site plan, sections and planting details |
| U.S. median annual wage, 2025 | $67,190 | $79,870 |

These are national occupational medians. Compare the position’s actual sector and pay terms. ## Follow the entrance ramp into a library

New York City’s Eastern Parkway Library renovation includes a new entrance ramp and elevator. Using that public project as an example, a landscape architect could draw the walk from sidewalk to door and flag landing elevations and drainage for review. An interior designer could trace the route from the door to the elevator and check for furniture conflicts. They would exchange the threshold elevation and route drawings so the architectural and engineering team can review the full connection. [NYC library project announcement](https://www.nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2026/08/mayor-mamdani-breaks-ground-on--36-million-transformation-of-eas)

## Compare education and state rules

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical entry education for both. Interior design requirements vary by state and scope. In New Jersey, the Certified Interior Designer title requires certification, with an NCIDQ exam and qualifying education and experience. BLS reports that all states license landscape architects. In New Jersey, check approved education, documented work experience and the Landscape Architect Registration Examination. Ask each board how its rules apply to the duties you want. [BLS interior entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/arts-and-design/interior-designers.htm#tab-4) · [BLS landscape entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/landscape-architects.htm#tab-4) · [New Jersey interior design statute](https://www.njconsumeraffairs.gov/Statutes/interior-design-law.pdf) · [New Jersey interior certification](https://www.njconsumeraffairs.gov/int/Pages/FAQ.aspx) · [New Jersey landscape licensure](https://www.njconsumeraffairs.gov/land/Pages/Licensee-Frequently-Asked-Questions.aspx)

## Ask what pulls you away from the desk

Both work on drawings and visit project sites. Ask interior teams who reviews finish samples, field dimensions and client revisions. Ask landscape teams who verifies grades, conditions for planting, and changes during construction. [BLS interior work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/arts-and-design/interior-designers.htm#tab-3) · [BLS landscape work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/landscape-architects.htm#tab-3)

## Bring one side of the threshold to the next role

An interior designer moving toward landscape architecture can show a circulation plan, then learn grading, stormwater and planting and check the licensure path. A landscape architect moving toward interiors can show an entry section, then learn programming, lighting, finishes and the applicable interior design rules.

## Try a 30-minute entrance handoff

Use three fictional records. Site plan S connects a sidewalk to the door. Interior plan I shows furniture beside the elevator. Field sheet T gives only the threshold elevation. Spend eight minutes marking the shared interface, twelve drafting either a site section or an indoor circulation plan, and ten reviewing it. The section should show the ramp landing and missing elevations. The interior drawing should show the door-to-elevator route and the potential furniture conflict.
