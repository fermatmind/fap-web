# Medical Scientists, Except Epidemiologists vs Urologists

## What each role delivers

A medical scientist turns a question about human disease into a study and a conclusion readers can check. A urologist evaluates a patient with a urinary or male reproductive condition and documents a care plan. In an academic department, the same person can do both kinds of work. Look at which responsibility the job expects you to own and how much of the week it assigns to each.

## Work, setting, and preparation

| | Medical scientist | Urologist |
|---|---|---|
| Main deliverable | An analysis with its methods and limitations | An assessment and follow-up plan for a patient |
| Work setting | Research institute, university, or hospital research group | Clinic, hospital, or operating room |
| Typical U.S. preparation | Ph.D. or medical degree, with research experience | Medical degree, urology residency, and state medical licensure |

## When a follow-up visit is missing

Imagine an approved study of follow-up attendance at a stone clinic. A missed visit appears in the clinical scheduling queue and in the study data. The urologist reviews the chart and records who should contact the patient to arrange follow-up. The scientist checks how the protocol defines a missed visit and asks the authorized study data steward to verify its source status. The clinician owns the patient's care plan. The study team records how the case enters the aggregate analysis and what remains uncertain. Each decision goes in its own record.

## Training and authority in the United States

Medical scientists typically pursue a Ph.D. or medical degree and develop experience in study design and analysis. Research work generally does not require professional licensure; treating patients requires the relevant clinical license. The urologist route usually involves medical school, urology residency and a medical license in the state of practice. Check your state medical board for its requirements. Separate from licensure, the American Board of Urology’s initial certification process includes a qualifying examination, practice and licensure criteria, and an oral certifying examination. Check hospital credentialing requirements for the duties you want. [BLS research entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/medical-scientists.htm) · [BLS physician entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/physicians-and-surgeons.htm) · [ABU initial certification](https://abu.org/initial-certification/)

## How the workweek differs

Ask a research group what share of the week goes to data review, study meetings and writing, and who covers deadlines during a grant submission. Ask a urology department how it splits clinic, operating time and call, and who reviews follow-up items when a physician is away.

## Moving between research and urology

A urologist can bring a clinical question and experience documenting follow-up outcomes to an approved study, then develop methods, statistics and protocol-writing skills. A scientist can join urology research as a methods collaborator and contribute a reproducible analysis. For clinical practice, the scientist would need to complete medical and specialty training and meet medical licensing requirements.

## Try both decisions in 30 minutes

Use fictional clinic cards C1 (visit missed, clinical follow-up owner blank) and C2 (visit completed, owner recorded), plus study cards S1 (status unverified) and S2 (source-verified completed visit). Under the mock clinic rule, flag C1 to the urologist to assign a contact owner and leave C2 unchanged. Under the mock study rule, hold S1 outside the attendance count until the data steward verifies its source status, and count S2 as completed. Spend ten minutes sorting the cards, ten writing a clinic handoff and a study query, and ten checking the assigned owner, the needed evidence, and the two outputs against the rules.
