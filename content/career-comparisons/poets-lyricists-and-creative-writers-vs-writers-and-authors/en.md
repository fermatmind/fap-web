# Poets, Lyricists and Creative Writers vs. Writers and Authors: Reading the Assignment Brief

## What each role delivers

Poets, lyricists and creative writers are a specialty within the broader writers and authors wage category. The broader occupation can include fiction, nonfiction and creative work.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Poets, Lyricists and Creative Writers | Writers and Authors |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Original poem, lyric or creative text | Commissioned manuscript and revision notes |
| Basis | Form, point of view and language rhythm | Audience, sources and assignment scope |

[O*NET 27-3043.05](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/27-3043.05#Tasks) · [O*NET 27-3043.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/27-3043.00#Tasks)

## How the work meets

A fictional exhibition commissions an opening poem and an object background note. The background writer shares the verified object name, dated sources and an unresolved year with the poet. The poet works from the verified facts, revises the rhythm after a read-through and routes the date question to the editor. The editor reviews the poem for how it reads aloud and the background note for traceable sources.

## How to enter

For the specialty, 32% of U.S. O*NET education responses cited a bachelor’s degree and 27% cited some college without a degree. BLS lists a bachelor’s degree and long-term on-the-job training for the broader writers and authors occupation. [O*NET writing education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/27-3043.05#Education) [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Creative work can involve development and read-throughs. Broader assignments can include interviews, writing and review. Ask which checks the client or editor applies.

## Moving between roles

Moving into other writing, bring form and rhythm and develop audience research or fact-checking. Moving into a creative specialty, bring structure and revision and learn the relevant forms and voice.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock brief: deliver an eight-line poem and a 150-word note. The object name is verified. Two possible dates remain unconfirmed. Spend ten minutes each on a source handoff to the poet, a poem concept and separate acceptance checks for the editor.
