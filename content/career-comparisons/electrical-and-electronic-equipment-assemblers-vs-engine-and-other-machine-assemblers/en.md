# Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers vs. Engine and Other Machine Assemblers: The Pump Module Check

## What each role delivers

Electrical and electronic equipment assemblers install and check electrical components. Engine and other machine assemblers align components, verify clearances and inspect machine function.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers | Engine and Other Machine Assemblers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Wiring and component inspection | Machine fit and functional check |
| Basis | Connection points and electrical results | Clearances and functional status |

[O*NET 51-2022.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/51-2022.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 51-2031.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/51-2031.00#Tasks)

## How the work meets

Before a fictional pump module’s trial, the electrical connector label matches the drawing but the recorded coupling clearance is outside specification. The electrical assembler returns the wiring check. The machine assembler documents the clearance and revision for review.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for engine and other machine assemblers. For electrical assembly, ask which components, inspection steps and on-site training the employer expects. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Electrical work follows component and wiring steps. Machine assembly may also coordinate placement and mechanical inspection. Ask how handling, shifts and retest responsibilities are assigned.

## Moving between roles

Moving to machine assembly, bring work-order checks and learn mechanical drawings and measurements. Moving to electrical assembly, bring process tracking and learn wiring diagrams and electrical inspection records.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a mock card: connector X1 matches, clearance should be 2–3 mm, the reading is 4 mm and the trial has not started. Spend ten minutes each on the wiring record, the fit exception and who must confirm readiness. Note that a matching label does not establish electrical function.
