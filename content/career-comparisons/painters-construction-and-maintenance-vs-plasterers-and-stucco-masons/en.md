# Construction Painters vs. Plasterers and Stucco Masons: The Wall Surface Handoff

## What each role delivers

Plasterers and stucco masons prepare the substrate and create the specified finish. Construction and maintenance painters check the surface and sample, protect nearby work and apply the coating.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Construction and Maintenance Painters | Plasterers and Stucco Masons |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Coating and color record | Specified plaster finish |
| Checks | Paint-ready surface and sample | Substrate, texture and handoff |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $49,400 | $57,660 |

## How the work meets

In a fictional meeting room, W1 has a completed plaster handoff but no approved color sample. W2 has an approved color but still needs a plaster texture review. Each crew updates its record so the lead can sequence the two areas separately.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential as typical for either role. It lists moderate-term on-the-job training for painters and long-term on-the-job training for plasterers and stucco masons. [BLS rows 47-2141 and 47-2161](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Both roles involve repeated surface checks. Plasterers pay more attention to base preparation and texture; painters focus on coating and color. Ask who verifies readiness and who protects the finished work.

## Moving between roles

Move toward painting with your surface-check and protection experience, then learn samples and coatings. Move toward plastering with sample comparison experience and add supervised substrate and texture practice.

## Try it in 30 minutes

On paper, W1 has a complete plaster handoff and missing color approval. W2 has the opposite. Spend ten minutes each on a plaster note, a paint note and a list assigning the remaining decisions.
