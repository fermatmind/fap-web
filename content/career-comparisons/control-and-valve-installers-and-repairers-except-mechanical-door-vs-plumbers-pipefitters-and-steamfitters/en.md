# Control and Valve Installer and Repairer vs. Plumber, Pipefitter, and Steamfitter

## The piping run and the regulating device

Plumbers, pipefitters, and steamfitters may install valves as part of a piping system. Control and valve installers and repairers specialize in mechanical regulating devices and may diagnose, service, or test them. On a shared work order, responsibility depends on the equipment and the assigned scope.

## Compare the deliverables

| Work | Control and valve installer and repairer | Plumbing or fitting trade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First check | Device identity, condition, and test records | Piping drawings and connection requirements |
| Handoff | Device findings and questions about the replacement | Connection records and questions about the drawing |

May 2025 U.S. median annual wages were $74,340 for control and valve installers and repairers, except mechanical door, and $63,800 for plumbers, pipefitters, and steamfitters. Both are occupation-wide BLS OEWS medians. ## The stock record lists a different valve

At a fictional site, the work order lists valve V12 and the stock record lists V13. The valve technician checks the device records and flags the model question. The pipefitter checks the piping drawing and notes which connection information is missing for the replacement. Both send their findings to the technical lead for a decision.

## U.S. entry routes

O*NET lists the valve trade in Job Zone Three, where vocational training, related work experience, or an associate degree are common preparation paths. Look for supervised maintenance work involving device records and testing. BLS describes apprenticeship as the usual route into the piping trades. Most states and some localities license plumbers; check your trade and location. [O*NET valve preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/49-9012.00#JobZone) · [BLS piping trades preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/plumbers-pipefitters-and-steamfitters.htm#tab-4)

## Compare the sites and schedules

O*NET respondents in the valve trade often report outdoor exposure. BLS lists homes, businesses, and factories as worksites for piping trades. Ask which valve jobs go to the field or shop and how piping projects balance planned work and service calls. [O*NET valve work context](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/49-9012.00#WorkContext) · [BLS piping work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/plumbers-pipefitters-and-steamfitters.htm#tab-3)

## Moving between the trades

A valve technician can bring device records and fault analysis to piping while learning system drawings, connections, and jobsite handoffs. A pipefitter can bring drawing and fitting experience to valve service while learning regulator behavior and how to interpret device test records.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use three fictional entries. The work order lists V12 on page 1, the stock record lists V13, and the connection drawing has an entry for V12 only. Spend ten minutes documenting the mismatch, ten writing a device identification question, and ten writing a request for connection information. Leave the replacement decision to the technical lead.
