# Administrative Services Managers vs. Postmasters and Mail Superintendents During a Mailing Rush

## Where the work differs

Administrative services managers organize office support, which can include collecting material from departments and arranging a handoff. Postmasters and mail superintendents oversee mail operations and service work. A mailing rush makes the handoff between those responsibilities clear.

## Compare the handoffs

| Work product | Administrative Services Managers | Postmasters and Mail Superintendents |
|---|---|---|
| Example | Department collection list, timing and contacts | Staff assignments and a plan for processing or customer updates |

## A shared situation

A company needs to send materials from several departments on the same day. One department may finish late. The office manager confirms how much is coming and obtains a contact for missing information. The postal supervisor reviews staffing and processing arrangements, then explains how the site will handle a later arrival. The office manager uses that information to update the departments.

## How to prepare

BLS describes a bachelor’s degree and related experience as typical for administrative services managers. [BLS occupational preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/management/administrative-services-managers.htm#tab-4) O*NET classifies postmasters and mail superintendents as work that generally requires very little to some preparation. [O*NET Job Zone](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/11-9131.00#JobZone) Job Zones describe a broad level of preparation. Ask the employer which education, training and experience count for the specific job.

Build office services experience by coordinating an internal shipment or service desk. For postal supervision, ask the employer how it fills supervisory positions and which operational and staffing experience it expects. A shift plan can show how you balance the counter and processing workload.

## What to ask an employer

The office manager's schedule can follow information and materials arriving from several departments. At a postal site the supervisor may need to balance counter and processing coverage in the same shift. Ask the office team which departments can move collection deadlines. Ask the postal team how it covers peaks, handles customer complaints and hands work over at the end of a shift.

## Moving between roles

Office handoff records transfer to postal operations, where you also need to understand how the site processes mail. Postal staffing experience can support an administrative application if you show how you would balance requests from multiple office teams.

## Try it in 30 minutes

For a fictional collection, department A has twelve items at 1:30, B has twenty at 2:45, and C has eight at 3:10. The company's handoff cutoff is 3. The receiving site can assign two people between 2:40 and 3:20. Spend ten minutes on collection, fifteen on receiving coverage and a question about late items, and five writing an early update.
