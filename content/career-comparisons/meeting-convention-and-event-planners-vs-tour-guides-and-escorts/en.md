# Event Planner vs. Tour Guides and Escorts: A Conference Gallery Visit

## What each role delivers

Meeting, convention, and event planners coordinate schedules, venues and attendee services. Tour guides and escorts lead visitors on site, interpret what they see and communicate venue rules.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Meeting, Convention, and Event Planners | Tour guides and escorts |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Confirmed event program and attendee update | Visit route, interpretation and on-site feedback |
| Basis | Session timing, venue services and headcount | Open galleries, group size and venue rules |

[O*NET 13-1121.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/13-1121.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 39-7011.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/39-7011.00#Tasks)

## How the work meets

A fictional industry conference includes a gallery visit. The guide learns that the east gallery has closed and confirms a route through the west gallery with staff. The guide reports the expected length of the visit and walk back. The planner checks the meeting point and the next session time, then updates attendees after the plan is confirmed.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for planners. For the combined tour and travel guides category, it lists high school completion or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training. Check venue and local requirements for the duties you would perform. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Planning work follows venue and speaker deadlines. On-site guiding follows visitor questions, gallery access and time on the route. Ask who relays opening and closure notices.

## Moving between roles

Planners bring timing skills into guiding and add interpretation, visitor observation and any applicable credentials. Guides bring venue communication into planning and add budgets, vendors and program management.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock visit: 30 attendees, up to 15 in the gallery at one time, 18 minutes per visit and eight minutes back to the meeting room. The next session starts in 40 minutes. Spend ten minutes each on the group plan, whether the second group can return in time, and a status message before a change is approved.
