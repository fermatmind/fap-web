# Purchasing Managers vs. Supply Chain Managers on a Minimum Order

## Which decisions differ?

Purchasing managers oversee supplier selection and buying terms. Supply chain managers work out what the choice means for demand, inventory, and downstream capacity. Some roles include both responsibilities.

## Compare the handoffs

| Work product | Purchasing Managers | Supply Chain Managers |
|---|---|---|
| Example | Supplier terms and order recommendation | Demand, stock, and delivery scenarios |

## The supplier raises the minimum

A fictional supplier lowers its price but raises its minimum. The purchasing manager confirms the terms and whether delivery can be staggered. The supply chain manager checks whether the shipments fit expected demand and available space. Both send their findings to the person who can commit to the purchase.

## How to prepare

BLS describes a bachelor’s degree and several years of related work experience as typical for purchasing managers. O*NET assigns supply chain managers to Job Zone 4, a broad category of considerable preparation. Build experience in supplier evaluation for purchasing, or in materials planning and inventory for supply chain work. [BLS preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/business-and-financial/purchasing-managers-buyers-and-purchasing-agents.htm#tab-4) · [O*NET Job Zone](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/11-3071.04#JobZone)

## Work conditions

Purchasing can be paced by supplier answers and approval deadlines. Supply planning changes as demand, stock, and shipments change. Ask which updates arrive reliably and who handles exceptions spanning several teams.

## Moving between roles

Move toward supply chain by adding demand and capacity checks to a buying example. Move toward purchasing by showing how you evaluate vendors and document a proposed change.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use fictional demand of 70 units each week for two weeks, starting stock of 20, and room for 150. Deliveries arrive before each week's use. Supplier A ships at least 150 at once. Supplier B can send 60 each week. Spend ten minutes comparing terms, fifteen calculating stock and space, and five writing what to renegotiate and who would approve it.
