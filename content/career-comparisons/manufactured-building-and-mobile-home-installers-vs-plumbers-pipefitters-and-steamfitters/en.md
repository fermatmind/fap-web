# Manufactured Building and Mobile Home Installer vs. Plumber, Pipefitter, and Steamfitter

## Setting the home and preparing the piping connection

Manufactured building and mobile home installers set up building units and may make assigned connections. Plumbers, pipefitters, and steamfitters install pipe assemblies, fixtures, and equipment connections. Responsibility for a shared connection depends on the project scope and applicable rules.

## Compare the deliverables

| Work | Manufactured building and mobile home installer | Plumbing or fitting trade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First check | Unit placement and the unit's connection drawing | Site water plan and the connection point |
| Handoff | Placement record and observed inlet location | Site piping record and discrepancies for review |

May 2025 U.S. median annual wages were $45,990 for manufactured building and mobile home installers and $63,800 for plumbers, pipefitters, and steamfitters. These are occupation-wide BLS OEWS medians. ## The home is in place and the plans disagree

At a fictional site, the placement record shows an inlet on the east side. The site water drawing shows a connection point on the south side. The installation crew checks the unit model and placement record. The piping crew checks the site drawing and its connection label. The project lead takes the findings to the design team for clarification.

## U.S. training and licensing

For home installation, look for supervised setup work that teaches you to read manufacturer documents and prepare handoffs. O*NET places this occupation in Job Zone 1–2. For an initial installation covered by 24 CFR Part 3286 in a state without a qualifying installation program, the installer must meet the federal program’s certification or licensing requirements. Check the state program and the scope of work. BLS describes apprenticeship as typical for the piping trades. Most states and some localities license plumbers. ## Compare sites and project schedules

Manufactured building installers may work outdoors while setting a unit. Plumbing and fitting work can take place in homes, commercial facilities, and factories. Ask how installation dates depend on delivery and site readiness, and whether the piping crew follows project schedules or handles service calls. [O*NET installer work context](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/49-9095.00#WorkContext) · [BLS piping work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/plumbers-pipefitters-and-steamfitters.htm#tab-3)

## Moving between the trades

An installer can bring experience coordinating the unit and site crews, then build skills in piping drawings, fittings, and inspection handoffs. A piping worker can bring connection and drawing knowledge, then learn to verify unit placement and work from manufacturer documents.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use three fictional records. The placement form lists the inlet on the east side. Sheet 4 of the site plan shows a connection on the south side. The manual cover says model M2 and the placement form says M3. Spend ten minutes listing the discrepancies, ten writing a question for each crew, and ten writing a request for the project lead to confirm the relevant documents. Use paper records only.
