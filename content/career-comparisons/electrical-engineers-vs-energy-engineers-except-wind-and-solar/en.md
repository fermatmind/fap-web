# Electrical Engineers vs. Energy Engineers, Except Wind and Solar: Capacity or Energy Use?

## What each role delivers

Electrical engineers design and verify equipment and power systems. Energy engineers in this occupation collect and analyze energy-use data and recommend efficiency improvements.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electrical Engineers | Energy Engineers, Except Wind and Solar |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Power design and capacity review | Energy baseline and savings review |
| Basis | Load, distribution limits and equipment specs | Operating hours, metering and comparison conditions |

[O*NET 17-2071.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 17-2199.03](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.03#Tasks)

O*NET uses the Engineers, All Other wage series for 17-2199.03. A separate figure for this specialty cannot be derived from the broader series. [O*NET wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/17-2199.03)

## How the work meets

A fictional facility uses more electricity and plans to bring in equipment. The energy engineer compares use over operating hours. The electrical engineer checks the proposed load against available capacity and flags the missing peak-load data.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for electrical engineers. O*NET education responses for energy engineers most frequently report a bachelor’s degree. Check the employer’s actual assignment and ask about engineering licensure if it includes regulated design or signing responsibilities. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [O*NET education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.03#Education)

## Work conditions

Electrical work may follow design, installation and handoffs. Energy work may follow measurement and analysis. Ask who verifies savings and capacity.

## Moving between roles

Moving into energy, bring metering and power-system knowledge and develop baseline and savings verification skills. Moving into electrical engineering, bring load analysis and add power distribution and installation design.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records show 1,200 kWh in 120 hours this month and 1,000 kWh in 100 hours last month. Existing peak load is unchanged and new equipment peak is missing. Spend ten minutes each on usage, missing capacity records and the limits of each conclusion.
