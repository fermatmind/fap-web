# Retail Loss Prevention Specialists vs. Stockers and Order Fillers on a Count Discrepancy

## Where the duties differ

Stockers and order fillers check goods, locations and reservations. Retail loss prevention specialists look for evidence that explains differences and follow the organization's review process. A shortage alone does not establish a cause or responsibility.

## Compare the handoffs

| Work product | Retail Loss Prevention Specialists | Stockers and Order Fillers |
|---|---|---|
| In this case | Verified observations, limits, and follow-up for an authorized reviewer | Physical count, location, and stock exception |

## One workplace example

A fictional store shows nine units in the system and seven on the shelf after a recount. The stocker checks location and current orders and records the count. The loss prevention specialist reviews authorized transfer and adjustment information, then sends what is known and what is still missing to the designated reviewer.

## How to prepare

O*NET places both in Job Zone 1–2. For loss prevention, practice documenting authorized inventory reviews and communicating the limits of the evidence. For stock, build experience with receiving, picking and counting. Ask the employer what training and information access each job has. [Stock preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/53-7065.00#JobZone) [Loss prevention preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/33-9099.02#JobZone) This example covers inventory review. If a job adds security duties, check the state regulator against the actual assignment. In California, for example, workers who meet the state definition of a proprietary private security officer need registration before doing that work. Check the definition and conditions with BSIS. [California BSIS registration guidance](https://www.bsis.ca.gov/forms_pubs/ppso_fact)

## What shapes the workday

Stock work can require a recount between orders. Loss reviews may involve several shifts or teams. Ask who can request a recount, change inventory, or decide on a follow-up.

## Moving between the roles

Move toward loss prevention by adding a timeline and evidence limits to count records. Move toward picking by learning location, reservation and transfer procedures.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use fictional numbers. The system lists nine, the shelf count is seven, and a request lists two for transfer but has no confirmation that they moved. Spend ten minutes recording the count, ten writing transfer questions, and ten identifying what remains unknown for the supervisor.
