# Occupational Therapy Assistants vs Speech-Language Pathologists

## Choose the kind of progress you want to document

Occupational therapy assistants carry out assigned therapy that helps clients take part in daily activities, under an occupational therapist's supervision. Speech-language pathologists assess and treat communication and swallowing disorders. Both track progress, with different responsibilities for assessment and treatment planning.

## Compare the handoff each role owns

| At work | Occupational therapy assistant | Speech-language pathologist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main work | Supervised activity practice and progress documentation | Evaluation, treatment planning and progress documentation |
| Setting | Therapy clinics, hospitals or nursing facilities | Schools, clinics or hospitals |
| Next handoff | Supervising occupational therapist | Client, family and healthcare or education team |

[OTA tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/31-2011.00) · [SLP tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-1127.00)

## Follow one observation through a California rehab team

In a fictional California rehab clinic, the OTA records that a client completed the assigned activity but could not say where they needed help. The supervising occupational therapist reviews the note and decides whether to change the occupational therapy plan. The SLP receives the communication referral and assesses that concern within their scope before making recommendations to the team. [California OT supervision](https://bot.ca.gov/forms_pubs/supervision_faqs.shtml) · [SLP duties](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/speech-language-pathologists.htm)

## Compare education and California licensing

BLS lists an associate degree from an accredited OTA program as typical. In California, apply through the Board of Occupational Therapy and check education, fieldwork and examination requirements. For SLP practice, California's standard route includes a qualifying master's degree or equivalent, clinical practicum, supervised professional experience and the national exam. For supervised experience completed in California on the RPE route, the board requires the temporary license during that experience. [BLS OTA training](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/occupational-therapy-assistants-and-aides.htm) · [California OTA application](https://www.bot.ca.gov/applicants/application.shtml) · [California OTA requirements](https://www.bot.ca.gov/forms_pubs/2026_sunsetreport.pdf) · [California SLP license](https://speechandhearing.ca.gov/applicants/slp/index.html)

## Compare pay alongside the work setting

May 2025 national median annual wages were $72,300 for occupational therapy assistants and $97,870 for speech-language pathologists. Ask about productivity expectations and standing time for OT work, and assessment, documentation and caseload for speech-language work. A fictional progress note can show observation skills for either path. A move into SLP requires distinct graduate and supervised clinical training. [BLS OTA pay](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/occupational-therapy-assistants-and-aides.htm) · [BLS SLP pay](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/speech-language-pathologists.htm)

## Try a 30-minute record handoff

Use fictional note A (R5 completed the assigned activity but did not explain what was difficult) and note B (communication referral sent, evaluation pending). Spend eight minutes separating the observations, 12 minutes writing separate handoffs to the occupational therapist and SLP, and ten minutes checking whether a colleague can identify who owns each next step. Use only the paper records.

## Ask what each team reviews at the end of a session

Ask an OTA what the supervisor wants to see in a progress note and ask an SLP what makes a referral ready to assess. Compare which work you would want to repeat across a full week.
