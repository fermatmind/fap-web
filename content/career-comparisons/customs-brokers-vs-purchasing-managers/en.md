# Customs Brokers vs. Purchasing Managers When a Supplier Description Is Too Vague

## Start with the boundary

A customs broker reviews the information needed for authorized customs business. A purchasing manager is responsible for what was ordered, what the supplier agreed to provide, and what to do about a mismatch.

## Compare the handoffs

| Handoff | Customs Brokers | Purchasing Managers |
|---|---|---|
| Example | Entry document questions and information for client review | Specification confirmation, revised buying plan, or escalation |

## One shipment in practice

A fictional order asks for alloy brackets, but the invoice says only hardware. The broker asks the client for material and intended use before relying on the description. The purchasing manager checks the order terms, asks the supplier for written confirmation, and identifies any effect on delivery or acceptance. The importer retains responsibility for compliance.

## Preparing for each role

BLS describes a bachelor's degree and several years of related experience as typical for purchasing managers. Buying or supplier coordination can develop experience comparing specifications and terms. [BLS purchasing preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/business-and-financial/purchasing-managers-buyers-and-purchasing-agents.htm#tab-4) To become an individually licensed broker, check CBP eligibility, pass the broker exam, apply and obtain CBP approval. For client work, learn document preparation and ask which customs tasks you may perform. A broker's employee may conduct business solely for the employer without an individual license only under the applicable authorization and supervision rules. [CBP qualifications](https://www.help.cbp.gov/s/article/Article-1012?language=en_US) [19 CFR 111.2 on licensing and employee authority](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/chapter-I/part-111/subpart-A/section-111.2)

## What shapes the workday

Brokerage can involve repeated reviews while documents are clarified. Purchasing may also have supplier meetings and decisions about continuity, price, and delivery. Ask who owns each supplier request and who confirms the entry information.

## Moving between roles

Move toward purchasing by adding supplier evaluation and contract work to your document skills. Move toward brokerage by adding applicable customs rules and verification under appropriate supervision.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional order for aluminum brackets, an invoice saying components, and a photograph with no material label. Spend ten minutes on the entry questions, ten drafting a supplier confirmation for purchasing, and ten separating what is known from what cannot be decided yet.
