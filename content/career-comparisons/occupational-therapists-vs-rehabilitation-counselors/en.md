# Occupational Therapists vs. Rehabilitation Counselors: Who Helps With What Before a Return to Work?

## What each role delivers

An occupational therapist evaluates how a person carries out work or daily activities and records the demands that affect performance. A rehabilitation counselor discusses employment or independent living goals with the client, identifies barriers, and coordinates support.

## The same return-to-work goal

Imagine a client preparing to return to sorting small packages. At a practice station, the occupational therapist sees that the client can scan and sort items into a nearby bin but pauses to rearrange items when using a distant bin. The rehabilitation counselor compares shift options with the client's bus schedule and finds the first bus arrives after the early shift starts. With the client's permission, the counselor checks shift and workstation options with the employer and records both issues in the return-to-work plan.

## Getting started in the U.S.

Occupational therapists typically need a master's degree in occupational therapy, and all states require licensure. Rehabilitation counselors typically need a master's degree in rehabilitation counseling or a related field. Some jobs accept a bachelor's degree, and some require a license or certification. Check the state board and the job duties for the position you are considering. [BLS occupational therapist entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/occupational-therapists.htm#tab-4) · [BLS rehabilitation counselor entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/rehabilitation-counselors.htm#tab-4)

## Working conditions

Occupational therapists need a place to observe tasks and time to write up findings. Rehabilitation counselors follow up with clients, agencies, and employers, often on different schedules. Ask who owns follow-up and which observations may be shared with the employer with consent.

## Moving between roles

Occupational therapists can carry over task observation and goal-setting skills, then develop case coordination and referral skills. Counselors can carry over interviewing and interagency coordination, then train in clinical assessment and activity analysis. Verify the education and credentials required for the destination role.

## Try both in 30 minutes

Use three fictional notes: sorting into a nearby bin went smoothly, the distant bin required a pause to rearrange items, and the first bus arrives at 7:20 a.m. for a 7 a.m. shift. The client has consented only to sharing work-task conditions with the employer. Spend 10 minutes writing the observation, 10 listing options and contacts, and 10 drafting an internal handoff and one question you can ask the employer.
