# Manufacturing Stone Cutters and Carvers vs. Stonemasons: Two Corner Pieces with Unclear Labels

## What each role delivers

Manufacturing stone cutters and carvers shape and verify pieces against shop drawings. Stonemasons lay out, set and align stone and finish joints.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Stone Cutters and Carvers, Manufacturing | Stonemasons |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Finished stone part and inspection record | Site layout and joint record |
| Basis | Part dimensions, tolerance and finish | Wall reference, pattern and joints |

[O*NET 51-9195.03](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/51-9195.03#Tasks) · [O*NET 47-2022.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-2022.00#Tasks)

O*NET reports the Molders, Shapers, and Casters, Except Metal and Plastic wage series for this role. That category does not establish a separate median for the occupation in this comparison. [O*NET national wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/51-9195.03)

## How the work meets

Two corner pieces arrive for a fictional plinth. Both have passing dimension records, but the site layout does not match the labels. The lead reviews the part IDs, dimensions and drawings before authorizing relabeling or replacement.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and apprenticeship for stonemasons. For manufacturing stone work, ask about drawing interpretation, finish inspection and shop training. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Manufacturing follows shop orders and inspection. Site masonry follows layout, handoff and sample approval. Ask who confirms identity when a label is missing.

## Moving between roles

For site masonry, use dimension checks and learn field layout and joints. For manufacturing, use material and fit judgment and learn shop drawings and inspection.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock card: both pieces pass dimensions, S1 is marked as a right corner, S2 has no tag, and the left corner must have a polished exposed face. Spend ten minutes each on a shop query, a site layout note and the identity or finish evidence required before acceptance.
