# Cartographers and Photogrammetrists vs. Surveying and Mapping Technicians: The Missing Survey Point

## What each role delivers

Cartographers and photogrammetrists compile imagery and survey sources and check map features and finished products. Surveying and mapping technicians collect or enter points and field records and may also prepare map data.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Cartographers and Photogrammetrists | Surveying and Mapping Technicians |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Map features and final product check | Survey points, field notes and data records |
| Basis | Version, features and legend | Point ID, location and source |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $81,390 | $54,240 |

## How the work meets

A fictional park updates its trail map. The technician supplies three point records, but P3 has an ID and no coordinates. The cartographer identifies the northern segment that depends on it and uses verified records for the southern update.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for cartographers and photogrammetrists. It lists high school or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for surveying and mapping technicians. Check whether the specific mapping assignment requires a license. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [BLS cartography licensing](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/cartographers-and-photogrammetrists.htm)

## Work conditions

Technicians may alternate between fieldwork and data entry. Map production may follow revisions and review feedback. Ask how teams report missing records.

## Moving between roles

Moving to mapping, bring coordinate and field source knowledge and add feature representation and product inspection. Moving to technical work, bring spatial checks and seek field and survey record training.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: P1 and P2 have coordinates and support the southern segment. The northern segment relies on P3, which has no coordinates and is only shown on an older map. Spend ten minutes each on the field gap, update boundary and the reviewer to consult.
