# Residential Advisors vs Social and Community Service Managers

## Support residents on shift or run a service program

Residential advisors help people navigate daily living in a residence. Social and community service managers organize the people and money that keep a service available. Think about which result you want to own at the end of the week: a resident got timely help, or a program met its commitments to the people it serves.

## What each role hands off

| Compare | Residential advisor | Social and community service manager |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typical setting | Student residence or other group living facility | Nonprofit, government agency, or residential services program |
| Work product | Shift log, referral, or maintenance follow-up | Staffing plan, budget, or service outcomes report |
| Decisions | What to handle during the shift and whom to contact | How to staff a service and use its approved resources |

## When the laundry room closes

At UT Austin, resident assistants support students on rotation, and Building Services provides maintenance assistance. The university routes maintenance concerns through a request system and urgent needs through the 24-hour desk. [UT Austin residence hall staff](https://housing.utexas.edu/housing/residence-halls/residence-hall-resources/essential-info-forms/residence-hall-staff)

Imagine a separate housing nonprofit with a similar division of duties. When a shared laundry room closes, the residential advisor records the work order and tells residents which laundry room is open. When several residents cannot reach the alternate site, the advisor passes a count and the access barrier to the resident services team. The program manager checks approved funding and who qualifies for a temporary ride, assigns a staff member to coordinate pickup, and reviews how many eligible residents received help. Facilities handles the repair.

## Check the job posting before choosing training

Campus resident assistant jobs may require student status. UT Austin describes its role as part-time work for full-time students and requires an information session to access the following year's application. Other residential settings can have different requirements. For community service managers, BLS describes a bachelor's degree and related work experience as typical. A program assistant assignment can give you experience tracking referrals, scheduling coverage, and reporting service results. [UT Austin application route](https://housing.utexas.edu/housing/residence-halls/residence-hall-resources/essential-info-forms/residence-hall-staff) · [BLS education and experience](https://www.bls.gov/OOH/management/social-and-community-service-managers.htm)

## Compare the schedule and the pay basis

UT Austin's live-in RAs rotate through desk shifts and after-hours and weekend duty. Ask about on-call frequency and whether housing is part of the offer. Most community service managers work full time, and some work over 40 hours. Ask who covers service gaps and when budget or outcome reports come due. In the May 2025 national OEWS survey, median hourly wages were $20.31 for residential advisors and $38.65 for social and community service managers. Use the posting to compare actual hours, stipends, and benefits for specific offers. ## Build a sample for the role you want

From residence life, show how you spotted a pattern in referrals and turned it into a proposal with costs, staffing, and a way to check who received help. From program administration, practice a short shift note with the current work-order status, the person taking over, and when residents should hear back.

## Try a 30-minute service interruption exercise

Assume the laundry room is closed for three days, five residents need a ride, and the program has $60 left. Spend 12 minutes writing a shift log with the repair status and next update. Spend 13 minutes drafting a service plan with the ride capacity, who signs off on the expense, and one way to count completed rides. Use five minutes to check whether the next shift and the program team can each act on what they receive.
