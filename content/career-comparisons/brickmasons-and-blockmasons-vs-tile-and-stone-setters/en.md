# Brickmasons and Blockmasons vs. Tile and Stone Setters: The Narrow Tile at a Wall Niche

## What each role delivers

Brickmasons and blockmasons build walls and openings to drawings. Tile and stone setters lay out, cut and align finishes and check joints. This example follows a tile finish over masonry.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Brickmasons and Blockmasons | Tile and Stone Setters |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Wall layout and masonry opening | Finish layout and joint checks |
| Basis | Building dimensions and substrate | Pattern, obstructions and finish joints |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $62,120 | $55,690 |

## How the work meets

A fictional changing room niche passes its masonry measurement. A trial tile layout leaves a border narrower than the approved sample. The tile setter sends the proposed layout and substrate dimensions to the site lead.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and apprenticeship for brickmasons and blockmasons. It lists no formal educational credential and long-term on-the-job training for tile and stone setters. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Masonry handoffs commonly follow wall progress. Finish work can start after substrate review and may need coordination around fixtures, corners and surface protection.

## Moving between roles

Moving to finishes, bring drawings and measurements and learn joint layout, edge pieces and substrate checks. Moving to masonry, bring opening measurements and add wall layout and masonry practice.
