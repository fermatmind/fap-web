# Wind Energy Development Managers vs. Wind Turbine Service Technicians: A Missing Record at Handoff

## What each role delivers

Wind energy development managers coordinate assessments, agreements and project milestones. Wind turbine service technicians diagnose, maintain and verify equipment under assigned authority.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Wind Energy Development Managers | Wind Turbine Service Technicians |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Project milestones and handoff requirements | Turbine service and recheck record |
| Basis | Milestones, handoff list and costs | Asset ID, work order and equipment checks |

[O*NET 11-9199.10](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/11-9199.10#Tasks) · [O*NET 49-9081.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/49-9081.00#Tasks)

O*NET uses the Managers, All Other wage series for 11-9199.10. A separate figure for this specialty cannot be derived from the broader series. [O*NET wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/11-9199.10)

## How the work meets

A fictional project is close to handoff. Its milestone package is complete, but turbine T6 has no service recheck. The technician confirms the asset and ticket and flags the gap. The manager updates handoff status.

## How to enter

A bachelor’s degree is the most frequent O*NET education response for development managers. BLS lists a postsecondary nondegree award and long-term on-the-job training for wind turbine service technicians. Ask about site, equipment and authorization requirements. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [O*NET 11-9199.10 education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/11-9199.10#Education)

## Work conditions

Development work may follow approvals and external coordination. Turbine service can depend on location, weather and service windows.

## Moving between roles

Moving to service, bring handoff skills and obtain turbine training. Moving to development, bring maintainability experience and build site, agreement and schedule skills.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock card: the site and construction milestones are complete, T6 has a repair note but no recheck, and handoff is planned for Friday. Spend ten minutes each on the open milestone, service handoff and the evidence needed to proceed or defer.
