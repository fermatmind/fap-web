# Postsecondary Education Teachers vs. School and Career Advisors: Choosing a Placement

## What each role delivers

Postsecondary education teachers teach methods courses and assess work such as lesson plans and fieldwork. Educational, guidance, and career counselors and advisors help students navigate programs and future paths.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Education Teachers, Postsecondary | Educational, Guidance, and Career Counselors and Advisors |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Education coursework and feedback | Advising comparison and follow-up |
| Basis | Lesson plans and fieldwork | Goals, program requirements and constraints |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $75,350 | $64,330 |

## How the work meets

A fictional teacher candidate completes a microteaching exercise but is undecided about two placements. The instructor gives feedback on questioning techniques. The advisor checks schedule, prerequisites and student preferences and records any eligibility item to verify.

## How to enter

BLS lists a doctoral or professional degree and less than five years of related experience as typical for postsecondary education teachers, and a master’s degree for the broad advisor occupation. For counseling in California schools, verify the PPS School Counseling pathway. College and community advising may use different requirements. [California CTC](https://www.ctc.ca.gov/credentials/leaflets/cl-606c/) [BLS occupation rows](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Faculty follow a teaching and grading cycle; advisors manage appointments and application dates. Ask what student information each role can access and whether advising is centralized.

## Moving between roles

Moving to advising, carry over learning feedback and build interviewing and program-navigation skills. Moving to teaching, turn advising cases into methods coursework and build disciplinary preparation.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional teaching score and two placement cards. A has an unverified prerequisite and B conflicts with work hours. Spend ten minutes each on faculty feedback, options and who verifies and submits the final choice.
