# Precision Agriculture Technicians vs. Surveyors: Is the Equipment Track a Property Line?

## What each role delivers

Precision agriculture technicians track worked areas and maintain field management records. Surveyors collect and verify measurements and prepare survey records that may include boundaries.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Precision Agriculture Technicians | Surveyors |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Field management zones and operating records | Survey and boundary records |
| Basis | Worked area, yield records and sampling | Property records, control and field measurements |

[O*NET 19-4012.01](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-4012.01#Tasks) · [O*NET 17-1022.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-1022.00#Tasks)

O*NET uses the Agricultural Technicians wage series for 19-4012.01. A separate figure for this specialty cannot be derived from the broader series. [O*NET wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/19-4012.01)

## How the work meets

On a fictional farm, the equipment track jogs around a ditch and lies several feet from a line in the property records. The precision agriculture technician documents where and when the equipment ran. The surveyor checks control, boundary records and field evidence to decide what needs further investigation.

## How to enter

For precision agriculture, 35% of O*NET education responses cited a bachelor’s degree and 30% cited a two-year degree. BLS lists a bachelor’s degree and internship or residency as typical for surveyors. Surveyors must be licensed to offer services to the public or certify legal documents. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [O*NET education data 19-4012.01](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-4012.01#Education) [BLS surveyor licensing](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/surveyors.htm)

## Work conditions

Agricultural work can follow busy seasons and equipment availability. Boundary surveying can alternate between fieldwork, records and review. Ask how each team labels the purpose and date of a line.

## Moving between roles

Moving to surveying, use field positioning experience and build skills in survey control, boundary evidence and formal documentation. Moving to precision agriculture, add equipment and crop management data to coordinate and accuracy skills.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records show a recent equipment track around a ditch, an older straight line on an operating map and no survey deliverable. Spend ten minutes each on the source of each line, records to request and which line shows the equipment’s recent route.
