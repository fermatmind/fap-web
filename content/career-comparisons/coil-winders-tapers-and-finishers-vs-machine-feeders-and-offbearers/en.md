# Coil Winders vs. Machine Feeders and Offbearers: The Wrong Bin at the Station

## What each role delivers

Coil winders, tapers, and finishers process coils to specifications. Machine feeders and offbearers move material into and away from equipment and document identity and counts.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Coil Winders, Tapers, and Finishers | Machine Feeders and Offbearers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Finished coils and exceptions | Lot movement and counts |
| Basis | Coil specifications and insulation | Labels, quantities and destinations |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $48,220 | $41,220 |

## How the work meets

A fictional station receives bin X2 for an X1 order. The feeder records the tag and route for the lead. The coil winder can complete inspection records for the previous lot while awaiting the right material.

## How to enter

BLS lists a high school diploma or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for coil winding. For feeders and offbearers, it lists no formal educational credential and short-term on-the-job training. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Coil work may involve close visual checks. Material handling may move between stations at the line’s pace. Ask about shifts, handling and exception reporting.

## Moving between roles

Moving to material handling, bring lot traceability and add routing and count tracking. Moving to coil work, bring tag and specification habits and learn coil processing and inspection.

## Try it in 30 minutes

The mock ticket requests 20 X1 items, but 20 X2 items arrive. Two coils from the previous lot have insulation damage. Spend ten minutes each on the routing exception, the coil quality note and closure criteria for both.
