# Credit Analysts vs. Loan Officers: A Request for a Longer Loan Term

## What each role delivers

Credit analysts assess financial and credit risk. Loan officers communicate with applicants, explain terms and handle decisions within authorized limits.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Credit Analysts | Loan Officers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Risk assessment and sensitivity assumptions | Loan option explanation and decision record |
| Basis | Repayment capacity, statements and credit policy | Borrower needs, terms and approval limits |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $83,510 | $76,690 |

## How the work meets

A fictional business requests more time to repay an equipment loan. The analyst compares cash flow and total interest under two terms and sends the assumptions to the officer. The officer confirms the borrower’s needs, explains what can be submitted and routes the term outside their limit for approval before reporting the outcome.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree for both occupations. For loan officers, it lists less than five years of related experience and moderate-term on-the-job training. For U.S. residential mortgage origination, check whether the actual activities and employer require federal registration under Regulation G or state licensing under Regulation H. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [CFPB Regulation G](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1007/103/) [CFPB Regulation H](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1008/103/)

## Work conditions

Analysts may follow documentation and review milestones. Officers may combine borrower meetings, terms explanations and coordination. Ask how the institution records decision limits.

## Moving between roles

Moving into loan origination or client work adds communication and applicable qualifications. Moving into analysis adds sensitivity checks and risk writing to application experience.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock request: a six-month extension, updated statements and an officer authorized for only three months. Spend ten minutes each on analysis assumptions, a factual borrower update and the exception referral.
