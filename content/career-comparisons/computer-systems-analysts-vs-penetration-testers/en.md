# Computer Systems Analysts vs. Penetration Testers: Reading the Assessment Report

## What each role delivers

Systems analysts define workflows and feature acceptance. Penetration testers evaluate security weaknesses within documented authorization and report findings and recommendations.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Computer Systems Analysts | Penetration Testers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Business acceptance record | Authorized assessment report and remediation recommendations |
| Basis | Workflow and agreed acceptance criteria | Documented scope, approvals and evidence |

## How the work meets

A fictional project receives a completed assessment summary. The security lead flags two items for follow-up. The tester clarifies evidence references and scope. The systems analyst links each issue to affected workflow criteria, and the project owner assigns follow-up.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for systems analysts. For assessment roles, verify employer expectations for security fundamentals, scope governance, evidence handling and clear report writing. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Analysis follows requirements and release reviews. Testing follows assessment windows and report deadlines. Ask who authorizes the scope and who accepts remediation evidence.

## Moving between roles

Moving toward assessment work, bring business impact documentation and build security fundamentals, authorization discipline and reporting. Moving to systems analysis, bring evidence and impact tracking and build workflow and acceptance design.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Read two fictional report summaries. A names the employee booking status page, which is in approved scope, cites record E-01 and leaves open whether booking status still displays as required. B mentions the support notification workflow, which is absent from the approval record, and gives a conclusion without an evidence reference. Neither item has a remediation owner. Spend ten minutes checking report fields, ten writing the business acceptance check for A and marking the impact of B as unverified, and ten routing B’s scope gap and both ownership gaps. Review documents only.
