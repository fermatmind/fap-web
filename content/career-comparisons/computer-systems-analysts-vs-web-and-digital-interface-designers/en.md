# Computer Systems Analysts vs. Web and Digital Interface Designers: What Does “Uploaded” Mean?

## What each role delivers

Systems analysts specify business states and transitions. Web and digital interface designers use research and visual design to help people understand the interface.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Computer Systems Analysts | Web and Digital Interface Designers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Business states and acceptance criteria | Interface prototype and test notes |
| Basis | Rules, roles and status changes | User research, information hierarchy and feedback |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $105,850 | $104,000 |

## How the work meets

A fictional registration site leads applicants to think uploading materials completes submission. The analyst confirms that a review step separates the two states. The designer sketches two progress treatments and observes which action participants choose when shown each mockup.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical entry education for both roles. An analysis portfolio can show state and acceptance criteria. A design portfolio can show how research changed a prototype. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Analysts work with business owners on exceptions. Designers work with users and development teams on prototypes and implementation reviews. Ask who verifies the final interface.

## Moving between roles

Moving into interface design, build on state modeling and add observation and prototyping. Moving into analysis, bring problem framing and develop workflow and acceptance design.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use this mock workflow. After upload, the applicant selects Send for review to enter Awaiting confirmation. Only staff confirmation moves it to Submitted. Case A is uploaded only. Case B is awaiting staff confirmation. Spend ten minutes each mapping the transitions, sketching status cards and asking readers who acts next. In A the applicant sends it for review. In B the applicant waits for staff confirmation.
