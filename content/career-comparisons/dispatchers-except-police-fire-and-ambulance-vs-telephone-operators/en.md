# Dispatchers (Except Police, Fire and Ambulance) vs. Telephone Operators on a Facilities Call

## Connecting a call and assigning work

Telephone operators connect callers with the appropriate department or person. Dispatchers turn a confirmed request into a work assignment using crew and equipment availability. A connected call does not tell the caller that a visit has been booked.

## A routine workplace service call

A fictional office calls to report that an equipment room light is out. The operator confirms location and callback details and uses the directory to route the call to facilities service. The dispatcher checks who can respond, then sends the feasible time back to the front desk. Actual inspection belongs to the assigned staff.

## Prepare for each role

O*NET puts both roles in Job Zone 1–2, a broad range of preparation. For operator work, learn directories, basic call routing and accurate callback notes. For dispatch, look for service scheduling and learn to record availability and assignments. Ask whether the employer's role is part of any specialized response system. [Dispatch preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/43-5032.00#JobZone) · [Operator preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/43-2021.00#JobZone)

## What interrupts the work

Operator work moves among incoming calls, transfers and callbacks. Dispatch also follows staff already assigned to jobs. Ask how a transfer, an assignment and a completed job show up in the employer's system.

## Moving between roles

Moving toward dispatch, build crew scheduling and assignment experience on top of good intake notes. Moving toward switchboard work, use your knowledge of service departments and practice concise messages and connection records.

## Try it in 30 minutes

A fictional call comes at 2:10 about a light in the east equipment room. Facilities extension 2203 does not answer. A callback log is available. A technician is busy until 2:30 and can visit after 3. Spend ten minutes recording the attempted transfer, ten outlining assignment options, and ten drafting a caller update. Use paper only.
