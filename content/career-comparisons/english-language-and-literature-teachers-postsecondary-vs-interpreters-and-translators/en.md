# Postsecondary English Teachers vs. Interpreters and Translators: One Text, Two Assignments

## What each role delivers

Postsecondary English language and literature teachers teach literature, language or writing and assess textual work. Interpreters and translators render messages for a specific setting and research terminology.

## Compare the work

| Work item | English Language and Literature Teachers, Postsecondary | Interpreters and Translators |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Coursework and feedback | Written translation or interpreted message |
| Basis | Text and learning goals | Source, audience, terminology and confidentiality |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $78,760 | $60,170 |

## How the work meets

A fictional exhibit line uses wordplay. The instructor asks students to explain how two translations change the tone. The translator checks the exhibit audience, space limit and glossary, then supplies a proposed line and a query for review.

## How to enter

BLS lists a doctoral or professional degree as typical for postsecondary English language and literature teachers, and a bachelor’s degree for interpreters and translators. Verify language proficiency, subject expertise and any setting-specific credential with the employer or commissioning body. [BLS occupation rows](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Teaching follows terms and revision cycles. Translation and interpreting follow project or meeting schedules. Ask who reviews terminology and what commitments govern source materials.

## Moving between roles

Moving to translation, bring close reading and feedback skills and add project scoping and terminology workflows. Moving to teaching, turn authorized examples into assignments and build assessment and disciplinary preparation.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional exhibit line, “a bright idea in a dim room,” and an audience note for families. Spend ten minutes writing a class question, ten drafting a translator query and ten listing separate criteria for the two deliverables.
