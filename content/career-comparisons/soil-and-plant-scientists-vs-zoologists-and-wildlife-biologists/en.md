# Soil and Plant Scientists vs Zoologists and wildlife biologists

## Follow the soil and plants, or follow the animals

Soil and plant scientists study how site conditions affect plants and how management changes those conditions. Zoologists and wildlife biologists study animals, their populations and their relationships with habitats. If you like tracking what changed in the soil or vegetation, start with soil and plant science. If you want to know which animals use the site and when, start with wildlife biology.

## Compare the two records a project needs

| Compare | Soil and plant scientists | Zoologists and wildlife biologists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typical evidence | Soil observations and vegetation or crop measurements | Animal observations, survey protocols and habitat data |
| Work setting | Field sites, labs and analysis desks | Field sites, labs or offices, depending on assignment |
| Deliverable | An analysis of soil or vegetation change with limits | A population or habitat-use analysis with survey context |

## When restored grassland looks different

USGS catalogs a northern Iowa study that compared habitat structure and breeding birds in native and restored grasslands. Imagine a similar project. The soil and plant scientist records vegetation cover, plot IDs and survey dates, then provides the plot records to the wildlife biologist. The wildlife biologist checks whether bird observations were made under comparable conditions and reports where wildlife use appears to differ and what further evidence the team needs. The job assignments here are illustrative. [USGS study](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/70024202)

## Build a field record and learn how it will be reviewed

BLS describes at least a bachelor’s degree for agricultural and food scientists and notes that some soil and plant science work is subject to state licensure. Look up the specific duties in the state where you intend to work. For wildlife roles, BLS identifies a bachelor’s degree as typical at entry, with graduate training for some positions and a doctorate typically needed to lead independent research. Ask the project who is responsible for site access and any required research permits. [BLS soil and plant science entry](https://www.bls.gov/OOH/life-physical-and-social-science/agricultural-and-food-scientists.htm) · [BLS wildlife entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/zoologists-and-wildlife-biologists.htm)

## Compare reporting deadlines, field seasons and pay

In May 2025 the national median annual wage was $78,850 for soil and plant scientists and $76,780 for zoologists and wildlife biologists, based on BLS OEWS. Local compensation can vary with the employer and role. Ask about the length of the field season, overnight travel and what proportion of time goes to sampling, analysis and writing. When looking at seasonal and year-round offers, compare pay against the months and hours of work. [BLS soil and plant pay](https://www.bls.gov/OOH/life-physical-and-social-science/agricultural-and-food-scientists.htm) · [BLS wildlife pay](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/zoologists-and-wildlife-biologists.htm)

## Make a shared dataset your bridge

A soil or plant scientist can use experience with paired plots and repeated observations to move toward habitat monitoring. Add species identification and learn how survey conditions affect detection. A wildlife biologist can use georeferenced surveys and site histories to move toward vegetation monitoring. Add practice in describing vegetation and interpreting soil measurements.

## Try a thirty-minute evidence handoff

Use a fictional plot record. Vegetation cover increased from 55% to 65%. Bird observations increased from five to eight, and observation time increased from 15 to 30 minutes. Spend 12 minutes writing the vegetation note with the site ID and measurement change. Spend 13 minutes writing the wildlife note that flags the unequal observation effort. Spend five minutes combining the notes and naming one additional record that would make a comparison more defensible.
