# Pump Operator vs. Oil and Gas Rotary Drill Operator

## Two jobs keep records for different operations

A pump operator in this occupation monitors transfer equipment and records what was moved, in what quantity and when. A rotary drill operator runs assigned drilling equipment, coordinates crew work and logs drilling activity.

## Compare where the records are used

| Item | Pump operator, except wellhead pumper | Rotary drill operator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary record | Transfer batch, quantity and operating times | Drilling work order, tools and shift progress |
| Verification | Check which delivery or receiving record belongs to the transfer | Check which supporting records belong to the open drilling assignment |
| Handoff | Transfer status and unmatched documentation | Progress and dependencies the incoming crew needs to verify |

[O*NET 53-7072.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/53-7072.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 47-5012.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-5012.00#Tasks)

## A receipt references yesterday's work order

Suppose the transfer and drilling teams share a work-order system. A pump operator notices that today's receipt refers to a closed order and checks the batch identifier and receiving record. The rotary drill operator sees that the current drilling assignment is waiting for confirmation and flags it for the incoming shift. The designated owner verifies the link between the records under site procedures.

## U.S. entry and training

The BLS education and training table assigns a high school diploma or equivalent to pump operators except wellhead pumpers, and no formal educational credential to rotary drill operators. It assigns moderate-term training on the job to both. Ask employers which experience, site training and equipment authorization apply. [BLS education and training table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/education-and-training-by-occupation.htm) BLS assigns less than five years of related experience to rotary drill operators. For the comparison occupation, the table assigns no prior related experience.

## Ask about transfer blocks and rig rotations

Ask a pump employer about batch scheduling, monitoring time and the coverage for transfer records. Ask a drilling employer about crew rotation, time to review the log and who handles unresolved dependencies during handoff.

## Skills that carry across

A pump operator can bring equipment monitoring and careful transfer records to drill training, then learn drill equipment, crew coordination and drilling logs. A drill operator can bring shift handoff and equipment observations to a transfer role, then learn the site's material tracking and documentation.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use a fictional batch A transfer sheet and a receipt for batch A referencing order 17. The current order is 18. Spend ten minutes listing fields for the pump operator to check, ten minutes writing a drilling handoff entry, and ten minutes naming the person who can verify the link and the proof they need. Work from records only.
