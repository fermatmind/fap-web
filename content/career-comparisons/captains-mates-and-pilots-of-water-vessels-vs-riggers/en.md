# Vessel Captain vs. Rigger: When a Loading Delay Reaches the Bridge

## What each role delivers

Captains, mates and pilots contribute to command, navigation or watchkeeping according to assignment. Riggers check and prepare loads for movement and coordinate with the workers moving them.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Captains, Mates, and Pilots of Water Vessels | Riggers |
|---|---|---|
| Work object | Vessel status and navigation plan | Load, rigging gear and assigned movement |
| Handoff | Readiness, schedule and open items | Load identity, checks and exceptions |

## How the work meets

A fictional cargo vessel expects to load a piece of equipment before departure. The rigger finds different load weights on the shipping document and the lift order and sends both records to the lift coordinator. The lift stays on hold, and the coordinator informs the bridge. The mate logs loading status separately from the proposed departure time. The coordinator reports back after the work order and checks are confirmed, and the bridge updates its plan.

## How to enter

BLS lists a postsecondary nondegree award as typical entry education for the combined captains, mates and pilots group. It lists high school completion or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for riggers. Ask rigging employers which training and authorization apply to the assignment. Federal first-class pilotage has a separate, route-specific First Class Pilot endorsement. Maritime pilots may also need a state license. ## Work conditions

Bridge schedules follow watches and vessel movements. Rigging schedules can follow equipment availability and work windows. Ask who sends the next status update when work pauses.

## Moving between roles

Both roles use clear location, time and status reports. Moving toward rigging, build experience with load documentation and assigned checks. Moving toward bridge work, verify the service, watch training and endorsement needed for the actual role.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use mock entries: the shipping document and lift order list different weights, and the vessel had planned to leave in the afternoon. Spend ten minutes writing the rigging report, ten making a bridge handoff and ten assigning the two status updates. Work from the entries alone.
