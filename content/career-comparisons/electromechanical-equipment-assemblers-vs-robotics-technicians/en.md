# Electromechanical Equipment Assemblers vs. Robotics Technicians: The End Tool Handoff

## What each role delivers

Electromechanical equipment assemblers position, connect and inspect parts to specification. Robotics technicians troubleshoot and service robots and peripherals under site authorization.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electromechanical Equipment Assemblers | Robotics Technicians |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Assembled unit and final inspection | Robotic equipment service record |
| Basis | Drawings, clearances and wiring | Authorized equipment status and service evidence |

## How the work meets

A fictional handling cell receives a new end tool. The assembler returns the drawing revision and fit check. The technician sees a missing feedback status in the test summary and records the tool and robot IDs for authorized follow-up.

## How to enter

For assembly, show a drawing and final inspection record. For robotics service, show a sanitized service ticket and ask about equipment scope, site authorization and training.

## Work conditions

Assembly follows station sequences. Robotics service can follow incidents and maintenance windows. Ask who coordinates downtime and confirms return to service.

## Moving between roles

Moving to service, bring drawings and fit checks and add system diagnosis and recordkeeping. Moving to assembly, bring component familiarity and learn drawing revision and lot inspection.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a mock ticket: tool T2 is installed to revision B and passes the fit check. The robot record lacks feedback status for T2. Spend ten minutes each on the assembly handoff, service follow-up and separate closure conditions. Use records only.
