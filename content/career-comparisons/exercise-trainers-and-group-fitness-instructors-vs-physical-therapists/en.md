# Exercise Trainers and Group Fitness Instructors vs. Physical Therapists: Who Helps With a Return to Class?

## What each role delivers

Exercise trainers and group fitness instructors plan workouts or classes and monitor participation. Physical therapists evaluate movement and physical function and develop and review clinical treatment plans.

## Compare the work

| Compare | Exercise trainer or group fitness instructor | Physical therapist |
|---|---|---|
| Focus here | Available class options and participation | Physical function and clinical follow-up |
| Output | Class plan and observations the participant can share | Evaluation, treatment plan, and reassessment |

[O*NET 39-9031.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/39-9031.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 29-1123.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-1123.00#Tasks)

## The same return to class

Imagine a client wants to return to group fitness. The physical therapist documents the assessment and confirms with the client which activity conditions they can share. The client explains those conditions to the instructor, who identifies available class options and records participation. The client tells the therapist they stopped midway through the first class, and the therapist uses that information in follow-up.

## Getting started in the U.S.

BLS reports that fitness trainers and instructors typically need a high school diploma, with training and certification expectations varying by specialty and employer. Physical therapists entering the occupation need a Doctor of Physical Therapy degree and all states require licensure. [BLS fitness entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/personal-care-and-service/fitness-trainers-and-instructors.htm#tab-4) · [BLS physical therapy entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/physical-therapists.htm#tab-4)

## Working conditions

Group fitness follows a class schedule and can involve several participants at once. Physical therapy needs time for evaluation, documentation, and follow-up. Ask who receives observations outside the session.

## Moving between roles

Fitness instructors can carry over communication and observation and pursue clinical education in physical therapy. Physical therapists can carry over movement observation and learn group instruction and facility requirements. Verify destination-role qualifications.

## Try both in 30 minutes

Use fictional notes. The client can share that they need the option to pause during class. Class A allows breaks, while Class B has yet to confirm. The client attended only the first half of their first class. Spend 10 minutes writing the instructor's class checks, 10 listing the therapist's follow-up questions, and 10 drafting a handoff the client could share.
