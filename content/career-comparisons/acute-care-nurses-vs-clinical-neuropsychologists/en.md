# Acute Care Nurses vs Clinical Neuropsychologists

## Bedside observations and a cognitive assessment

Acute care nurses provide advanced nursing care for acute conditions, including perioperative assignments in some settings. Clinical neuropsychologists integrate cognitive and behavioral evidence in an assessment report. The nursing example below uses a bedside RN assignment. The occupation also includes acute care nurse practitioner titles.

## Outputs, settings, and U.S. preparation

| Item | Acute care nurses | Clinical neuropsychologists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Output | Nursing record, observed changes, and shift handoff | Cognitive and behavioral assessment report with limitations and recommendations |
| Setting examples | Acute hospital units and perioperative care | Hospital assessment services, rehab, or multidisciplinary clinics |
| U.S. preparation | Bedside RN example: approved nursing education, qualifying exam, and state licensure | Doctoral psychology training and supervised specialty preparation; check state licensure |

## Two different observations for the same patient

A fictional U.S. inpatient team reviews two records. In B-31, the patient asked twice for an activity schedule to be repeated when it was presented aloud in a room with several people talking. In B-32, the patient could repeat the time from a written schedule shown later in a quiet area. The RN signs the bedside observations and sends both notes to the neuropsychologist. The neuropsychologist explains that the conditions changed, identifies what history is still needed, and returns the formal assessment plan and signed report to the team. Under the clinic's example rules, the nursing lead arranges ongoing bedside observation and the neuropsychologist signs the formal assessment.

## Training and professional authorization

For a bedside RN role, check approved education, examination and state licensure, then ask about the unit's orientation. An acute care nurse practitioner role has separate graduate preparation and state APRN requirements. For clinical neuropsychology, check the state psychology board and supervised specialty preparation. ABPP describes supervised postdoctoral training for specialty certification. [BLS APRN entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/nurse-anesthetists-nurse-midwives-and-nurse-practitioners.htm#tab-4)

## Ask when the work happens

Ask nurses when observations are documented and how details carry over between shifts. Ask neuropsychology employers how interviews, assessment and report writing are scheduled and how collateral information is obtained.

## What transfers between the roles

Both roles use careful observation and concise handoffs. Someone moving into clinical neuropsychology needs supervised training in assessment and interpreting evidence. Someone moving into nursing needs approved nursing education, licensure and supervised unit preparation.

## Try a 30-minute desk exercise

Use fictional B-31 and B-32. Spend ten minutes separating behavior from timing, setting, and how the schedule was presented. Spend ten minutes deciding whether the two notes establish improved memory and identifying what would need to be checked. Spend ten minutes writing the nurse's handoff and the assessor's response. Mark who signs each record and who arranges bedside follow-up.
