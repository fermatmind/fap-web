# Construction Laborers vs. Plasterers and Stucco Masons: From Clear Room to Finished Wall

## What each role delivers

Construction laborers clear work areas and support staging. Plasterers and stucco masons assess and prepare the substrate, protect nearby surfaces and deliver the specified plaster or stucco finish.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Construction Laborers | Plasterers and Stucco Masons |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Cleared work area and logistics note | Prepared substrate and finished plaster or stucco |
| Typical issue | Shared access or unfinished staging | Base condition, texture and nearby finishes |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $47,120 | $57,660 |

## How the work meets

During a stairwell renovation, the laborer reports that material movement is complete but another crew still uses the door. The plasterer compares the wall to the finish schedule and flags a substrate issue by the window for the project’s designated reviewer before starting that section.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential as typical for both roles. Its occupation rows distinguish short-term on-the-job training for laborers from long-term on-the-job training for plasterers and stucco masons. Ask the trade employer who teaches substrate checks and evaluates sample finishes. [BLS rows 47-2061 and 47-2161](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Laborers may move between floors and materials. Plaster crews repeat surface work and check texture across areas. Ask which surfaces must be protected and how the employer handles sample approval and correction.

## Moving between roles

Moving toward plastering, bring room handoffs and surface-protection records and add supervised practice in substrate assessment and finish checks. Moving into site work, bring your area-based tracking and add multi-crew logistics.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional stairwell sketch. The door is shared, the window-side substrate is unverified, the east wall’s sample is approved, and the old railing stays. Spend ten minutes writing the room handoff, ten preparing a surface check list, and ten marking what can be staged, what awaits review and what needs protection.
