# Electrical Engineers vs. Wind Energy Engineers: A Turbine Moves on the Site Plan

## What each role delivers

Electrical engineers design and verify electrical equipment and systems. Wind energy engineers may coordinate turbine positions, roads and collector system layout and evaluate site effects.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electrical Engineers | Wind Energy Engineers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Collector-system and electrical interface review | Wind farm layout and infrastructure plan |
| Basis | Loads, equipment and distribution limits | Turbine positions, access and site interfaces |

[O*NET 17-2071.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 17-2199.10](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.10#Tasks)

O*NET uses the Engineers, All Other wage series for 17-2199.10. A separate figure for this specialty cannot be derived from the broader series. [O*NET wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/17-2199.10)

## How the work meets

A fictional wind farm moves proposed turbine T4. The wind engineer updates the layout and checks access boundaries. The electrical engineer sees that the collector drawing still references the old location and sends both revisions for review.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for electrical engineers. O*NET lists a bachelor’s degree among reported education requirements for wind energy engineers. Ask the employer which site, design and review work the role covers. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [O*NET education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.10#Education)

## Work conditions

Electrical work may follow distribution design and equipment interfaces. Wind engineering may follow site design and infrastructure coordination. Ask who maintains both drawings.

## Moving between roles

Moving to wind, bring collector-system experience and learn wind farm layout and site constraints. Moving to electrical engineering, bring interface experience and add electrical design and verification.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: layout L3 moves T4 and keeps access within limits. Collector drawing E2 still shows the old position. Spend ten minutes each on layout changes, electrical follow-up and who verifies and approves updates.
