# Cartographers and Photogrammetrists vs. Urban and Regional Planners: Using the Same Map for Two Site Options

## What each role delivers

Cartographers and photogrammetrists compile and check spatial data and map products. Urban and regional planners evaluate land use, policy and alternatives and present recommendations.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Cartographers and Photogrammetrists | Urban and Regional Planners |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Mapped layers and quality notes | Land-use options and planning report |
| Basis | Source revision, extent and representation | Policy, feasibility and public input |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $81,390 | $89,320 |

[O*NET 17-1021.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-1021.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 19-3051.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-3051.00#Tasks)

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 17-1021 and 19-3051](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional neighborhood compares two sites for a public facility. The map team finds that the walking entrance came from an older source and updates its source note. The planner considers it alongside accessibility, cost and community feedback.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for cartographers and photogrammetrists and a master’s degree for urban and regional planners. For mapping, check jurisdiction-specific licensing for the assigned work. For planning, look for supervised work comparing alternatives and engaging stakeholders. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [BLS mapping licensing](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/cartographers-and-photogrammetrists.htm)

## Work conditions

Map work may follow data revisions and quality review. Planning may involve research, meetings and public input. Ask who verifies sources and records feedback.

## Moving between roles

Moving to planning, use spatial evidence and build policy analysis and engagement skills. Moving to mapping, bring land-use knowledge and add geospatial production and quality review.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock card: Site A has a verified entrance. Site B uses an entrance from an older map and has not been rechecked. Costs differ. Spend ten minutes each writing a map source note, planning comparison and what must be checked before selection.
