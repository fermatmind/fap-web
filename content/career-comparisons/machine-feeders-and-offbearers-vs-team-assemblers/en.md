# Machine Feeders and Offbearers vs. Team Assemblers: A Line Change Handoff

## What each role delivers

Machine feeders and offbearers move materials between equipment and staging and record the movement. Team assemblers rotate through production tasks and check parts or finished work.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Machine Feeders and Offbearers | Team Assemblers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Lot movement and quantity record | Cross-station assembly and inspection |
| Basis | Labels, counts and destinations | Orders, rotation and quality checks |

[O*NET 53-7063.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/53-7063.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 51-2092.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/51-2092.00#Tasks)

O*NET maps 51-2092.00 to Miscellaneous Assemblers and Fabricators for wages. This broader series does not establish a separate role median. [O*NET occupational wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/51-2092.00)

## How the work meets

After a fictional changeover, the tote count matches the order but the product check has no sign-off. The feeder reports quantities and staging. The team assembler routes the open check to a qualified worker using the station roster.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and short-term on-the-job training for feeders and offbearers. For team assembly, ask how the employer trains and qualifies people for rotation and checks. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Feeding and offbearing may move between machines and staging. Team assembly changes with the rotation schedule. Ask how the site tracks station qualifications.

## Moving between roles

Moving to team assembly, bring lot tracking and add drawing and multi-station skills. Moving to material handling, use handoff skills and learn routing and counts.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a mock record: 16 parts arrive and the tag matches the order, station 3 has no output check, and the incoming worker is trained only on station 1. Spend ten minutes each on the material record, rotation gap and confirmation owner.
