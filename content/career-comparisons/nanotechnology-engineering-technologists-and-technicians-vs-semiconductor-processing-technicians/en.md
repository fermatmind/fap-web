# Nanotechnology Engineering Technologists and Technicians vs. Semiconductor Processing Technicians: Linking Sample and Lot Records

## What each role delivers

Nanotechnology engineering technologists and technicians may characterize samples and record instrument status. Semiconductor processing technicians follow work orders and document lot and inspection records.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Nanotechnology Engineering Technologists and Technicians | Semiconductor Processing Technicians |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Sample characterization and instrument record | Process inspection and lot record |
| Basis | Sample ID and instrument status | Lot, work order and inspection sequence |

[O*NET 17-3026.01](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-3026.01#Tasks) · [O*NET 51-9141.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/51-9141.00#Tasks)

O*NET maps 17-3026.01 to Industrial Engineering Technologists and Technicians for wages. This broader series does not establish a separate role median. [O*NET occupational wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/17-3026.01)

## How the work meets

A fictional wafer lot sends one sample for characterization. The measurement is traceable, but the lot’s surface inspection sign-off is still missing. The teams match sample and lot IDs and retain both records.

## How to enter

For characterization, show measurement and calibration documentation. BLS lists high school or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for semiconductor processing technicians. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Characterization may follow instrument schedules and samples. Processing follows lot and inspection steps. Ask who verifies instrument status and closes inspection items.

## Moving between roles

Moving to process work, bring measurement discipline and add work-order control. Moving to characterization, bring lot traceability and add instrument training and sample analysis.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: sample S7 has a complete characterization report and belongs to lot W7. The W7 work order requires surface inspection and its sign-off is blank. Spend ten minutes each on the sample record, process exception and who confirms readiness.
