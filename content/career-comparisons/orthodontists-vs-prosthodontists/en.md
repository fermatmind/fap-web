# Orthodontists vs Prosthodontists

## Choose alignment work or restorative planning

Orthodontists assess and treat alignment and bite. Prosthodontists plan and deliver care that restores or replaces teeth and related oral structures. Choose orthodontics if you want to follow alignment and bite across a course of visits. Choose prosthodontics if you prefer planning around a missing or damaged structure and coordinating a restoration.

## Compare the information each specialty hands off

| Compare | Orthodontists | Prosthodontists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focus | Tooth alignment, spacing and bite | Restoration, replacement and function |
| Setting | Dental visits and ongoing reassessment | Dental visits, lab coordination and follow-up |
| Handoff | Findings about space and tooth position | Restorative goals and information needed to meet them |

## What the team needs before agreeing on a shared plan

A University of Kentucky research record describes interdisciplinary care for an adult with spacing and a congenitally missing tooth. In a fictional case review, the orthodontist summarizes the space and bite findings and flags a missing measurement. The prosthodontist summarizes the functional goals and identifies information that the restorative plan still needs. Both clinicians review the record and discuss a coordinated plan with the patient. These assignments illustrate a handoff. [University of Kentucky case record](https://scholars.uky.edu/en/publications/interdisciplinary-management-of-an-adult-orthodontic-patient-a-ca/)

## Check dental school, residency and the state board

Both paths generally start with dental school and a DDS or DMD. BLS describes postgraduate residency for dental specialties. Compare accredited specialty programs and verify the dentist license and any applicable specialty requirements, including rules on representing yourself as a specialist, with the state dental board where you intend to practice. [BLS dental training and licensing](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/dentists.htm)

## Compare patient follow-up and lab work alongside pay

BLS reports May 2025 national median annual wages of $289,140 for orthodontists and $311,180 for prosthodontists in OEWS, which excludes self-employed workers. Ask orthodontic practices about follow-up schedules and continuity of care. Ask prosthodontic practices who coordinates lab work and how revisions affect the schedule. Compare actual offers at the same career stage and hours. [BLS dental compensation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/dentists.htm)

## Build on interdisciplinary work when changing paths

An orthodontist can bring documentation about space and bite into a restorative team and learn how restorations are planned and reviewed. A prosthodontist can bring case planning and communication experience while pursuing orthodontic education and supervised training. Check the required residency and credentialing before seeking independent specialty work.

## Try a thirty-minute paper case conference

Use two fictional notes. Note A says a spacing record is missing. Note B says the patient wants to improve chewing and appearance. Spend 12 minutes drafting the orthodontic handoff and 13 minutes drafting the restorative handoff. Use five minutes to identify what each clinician needs to verify and who documents the agreed next discussion. Work from the paper notes only.
