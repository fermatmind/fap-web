# Manicurists and Pedicurists vs Shampooers

## Work on a nail finish or support a hair appointment

Nail technicians turn a shape and finish request into a detailed hand or foot service. Shampooers prepare the wash station, confirm what the guest expects, and wash and rinse hair. Think about whether you would rather spend the appointment on a precise finish or handle the wash and handoff to the next person.

## Compare what each station delivers

| Work | Manicurists and pedicurists | Shampooers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Materials | Nail products, tools, and style samples | Hair products, towels, and a wash station |
| Guest check | Hand or foot service, shape, and finish | Shampoo or conditioning scope and preferences |
| Handoff | Service and tool-status record | Wash completed, station reset, and any request to route |

## A nail add-on during a hair appointment

The Ritz-Carlton, Dallas lists both a hair salon and a nail salon. Imagine a two-station appointment at a fictional property with both services. While checking in for a shampoo, a guest asks to add polish removal to the nail appointment. The shampooer records the request for the desk and completes the booked wash. The nail technician checks the current booking, verifies whether the add-on fits, and updates the service record after the guest confirms the change. ## Check the service scope in Texas

TDLR's Texas-school route to a manicurist license requires 600 hours at a licensed school and written and practical exams. TDLR lists which Texas licenses cover the nail services. Shampooing and conditioning alone do not require a Texas license. BLS lists short-term on-the-job training as typical for shampooers. Ask the employer what else the job includes, then verify any additional scope with the state regulator. ## Compare shift demands and employee pay

O*NET reports repetitive movements for nail work and extended standing for shampooers. In a nail interview, ask about appointment length and between-client cleanup. At a wash station, ask about peak bookings and whether shifts rotate. BLS lists May 2025 U.S. median annual employee wages of $35,760 for manicurists and pedicurists and $32,600 for shampooers. Self-employment earnings are excluded. ## Build a sample for the next role

Moving from nails to shampooing? Reuse your intake checklist and add a supervised wash-and-handoff example. Moving from shampooing to nails? Keep your station reset habit and build a practice portfolio showing consistent shaping and a clear tool-status log. Verify the applicable license before serving nail clients.

## Try a 30-minute scheduling handoff

In a fictional salon, a 2:00 p.m. wash takes 20 minutes, and a 2:30 p.m. nail appointment takes 35 minutes. Polish removal adds 15 minutes, and the nail station is booked again at 3:05. Spend 12 minutes writing the wash card with the original booking and the guest's request. Spend 13 minutes writing the nail card with tool status, the time conflict, and the staff member who will offer another slot. In five minutes verify that the add-on is pending and the 3:05 booking is intact.
