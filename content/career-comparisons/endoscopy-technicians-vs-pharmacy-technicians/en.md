# Endoscopy Technicians vs Pharmacy Technicians

## Choose between equipment readiness and pharmacy workflow

Endoscopy technicians help prepare rooms and equipment, support the procedure team and may work on instrument reprocessing. Pharmacy technicians support prescription fulfillment, records and stock within their authorized duties. Both track what is ready and what still needs review.

## Compare what you hand off

| Work | Endoscopy technician | Pharmacy technician |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typical output | Equipment readiness or reprocessing record | Prepared and labeled items or technical work record |
| Next reviewer | Designated procedure or processing lead | Supervising pharmacist |
| Workplace | Procedure rooms or processing departments | Community pharmacies or hospitals |

[Endoscopy tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/31-9099.02) · [Pharmacy tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-2052.00)

## Follow two open checks at an outpatient center

In a fictional California center, the endoscopy technician sees an equipment status entry still pending and reports it to the designated procedure lead, who confirms what can be used. At the center's separate outpatient pharmacy, a technician completes assigned labeling under pharmacist supervision and presents the prepared item for pharmacist verification. The two teams record their separate signoffs before moving their assigned work forward. [Endoscopy responsibilities](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/31-9099.02) · [California pharmacy rules](https://www.pharmacy.ca.gov/laws_regs/lawbook.pdf?preview=true&site_id=816)

## Check whether the role is based on equipment or prescriptions

O*NET places endoscopy technicians in Job Zone 1–2, where a high school diploma or equivalent is common and training varies. For a reprocessing assignment, ask whether the employer requires a credential such as HSPA's CER and how it documents competence. BLS lists high school or equivalent as typical entry education for pharmacy technicians. California requires a pharmacy technician license; check the Board's current qualification routes and application. [O*NET preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/31-9099.02) · [CER credential](https://myhspa.org/certification/certification-overview/certified-endoscope-reprocessor-cer/) · [BLS pharmacy training](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/pharmacy-technicians.htm) · [California application](https://www.pharmacy.ca.gov/applicants/tch.shtml)

## Compare how the shift is paced

Ask an endoscopy department how much time is spent preparing rooms versus processing instruments. Ask the pharmacy how time is split between records, stock and supervised technical tasks. A fictional status log shows how you identify incomplete work. Moving into endoscopy adds equipment handling and reprocessing training where assigned. Moving into pharmacy adds prescription system training and the applicable credential.
