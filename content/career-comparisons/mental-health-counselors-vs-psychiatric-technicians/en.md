# Mental Health Counselor vs. Psychiatric Technician

## Treatment goals and shift observations

Mental health counselors develop treatment plans and review progress through counseling. Psychiatric technicians work under clinical direction to support patients' daily care and activities, record observations and report changes to medical staff.

## Compare what the two records do

| Task | Mental health counselor | Psychiatric technician |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gather information | Ask how the client understands a change and its effect on daily life | Record when an observable change happened and what was reported |
| Produce a record | Progress note linked to counseling goals | Shift note for continuity of patient care |
| Hand it off | Share relevant goals with authorized team members | Report the observation to the designated nurse or clinical lead |

## At the door to a group activity

In a fictional inpatient setting, a patient who attended group yesterday returns to their room today after saying the area is too loud. The technician can document the time and the patient's words and report the change through the shift's assigned channel. A counselor meeting with the patient can ask what felt different about today's group and review whether the current treatment goals still make sense.

## U.S. education and supervision

BLS says psychiatric technicians typically enter with a postsecondary certificate, receive on-the-job training and need a license in some states. Check the state regulator and the facility's job description for approved training and the scope of assigned duties. Mental health counselors typically complete graduate education and an internship. BLS reports that all states require mental health counselor licensure, including supervised clinical work and an exam.

## Calendar and shift differences

In a facility operating around the clock, technician schedules may include off-hours shifts. Ask how long staff overlap for handoff and how much time goes to activity support. In counseling, ask how the clinic schedules sessions, documentation and case supervision.

## Moving between roles

A technician can bring experience writing observations and communicating changes to a team, then seek qualifying counseling education and supervised practice. A counselor moving toward technician work can apply communication skills while learning the facility's observation and reporting practices and meeting state or employer training requirements.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use a fictional shift note: yesterday the patient attended group for 20 minutes. Today at 10 a.m. they said the area was too loud and returned to their room. Spend ten minutes writing a factual technician handoff with the intended recipient. Spend ten minutes drafting two counseling questions and one goal to revisit. In the last ten minutes, mark what may be shared with authorization and what would need to be confirmed directly.
