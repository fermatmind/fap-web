# Electrical Engineers vs. Solar Energy Systems Engineers: Connecting the Array to Existing Distribution

## What each role delivers

Electrical engineers design and evaluate electrical systems. Solar energy systems engineers use site information to design photovoltaic or solar thermal systems and can support installation and performance review.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electrical Engineers | Solar Energy Systems Engineers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Distribution capacity and interface record | Solar system design and siting review |
| Basis | Existing loads, connection point and power limits | Site conditions, layout and system goals |

[O*NET 17-2071.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2071.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 17-2199.11](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.11#Tasks)

O*NET uses the Engineers, All Other wage series for 17-2199.11. A separate figure for this specialty cannot be derived from the broader series. [O*NET wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/17-2199.11)

## How the work meets

A fictional commercial building plans rooftop solar. The solar engineer provides site information and an array layout. The electrical engineer compares existing distribution drawings and finds conflicting revisions for the connection point.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for electrical engineers. A bachelor’s degree is the most frequently reported education for solar systems engineers in O*NET. Ask whether the employer’s assignment includes design approval, commissioning or installation support. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [O*NET education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.11#Education)

## Work conditions

Electrical work can follow equipment, distribution review and acceptance. Solar design can involve site visits and coordination with installers. Ask who owns the connection and layout drawings.

## Moving between roles

Moving to solar, bring electrical interface skills and add site and system performance work. Moving to electrical engineering, bring project interfaces and add distribution and equipment design.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: array layout S2 is submitted and the roof information is available. Old drawing P1 and proposed drawing P2 show different connection points. Spend ten minutes each on a solar design handoff, an electrical discrepancy and what must be confirmed before approval.
