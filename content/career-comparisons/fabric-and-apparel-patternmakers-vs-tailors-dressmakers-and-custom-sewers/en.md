# Fabric and Apparel Patternmakers vs. Tailors, Dressmakers, and Custom Sewers: When Should a Fit Change Go Back Into the Pattern?

## What each role delivers

Fabric and apparel patternmakers develop and revise patterns for cutting. Tailors, dressmakers, and custom sewers make or alter garments to fit individuals. Some also draft custom patterns.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Fabric and Apparel Patternmakers | Tailors, Dressmakers, and Custom Sewers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Base pattern and sizing revision | Custom fitting order and altered garment |
| Basis | Pattern, sizing and repeat fitting records | Individual measurements, fit and seam allowance |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $62,750 | $41,640 |

## How the work meets

A fictional studio adjusts one customer’s waist and fits three sample garments of the same size. The tailor records the individual measurements and result. The patternmaker sees the same excess in all three samples, checks the garment and pattern versions and proposes a trial change for another fitting.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for fabric and apparel patternmakers. It lists no formal educational credential and moderate-term on-the-job training for tailors, dressmakers, and custom sewers. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Patternmaking may follow sample and production milestones. Custom work often follows measurements, appointments and delivery dates. Ask who approves individual alterations and changes to the base pattern.

## Moving between roles

Moving into custom sewing, build measuring, client communication and fitting skills on top of pattern knowledge. Moving into pattern work, bring fit diagnosis and add grading and records across fittings.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: customer A requests a narrower waist, and three samples of the same size are loose at the same point. Spend ten minutes each on an individual order, a pattern hypothesis and what fitting results would support changing the base pattern.
