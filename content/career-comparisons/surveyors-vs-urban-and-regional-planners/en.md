# Surveyors vs. Urban and Regional Planners: A Park Entrance Near a Property Line

## What each role delivers

Surveyors check field measurements and property records and prepare survey deliverables under applicable rules. Urban and regional planners evaluate land use, service needs and alternatives and make recommendations.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Surveyors | Urban and Regional Planners |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Boundary survey and unresolved segments | Site comparison and planning recommendation |
| Basis | Control, parcel records and measurements | Land-use rules, service needs and site feasibility |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $75,440 | $89,320 |

## How the work meets

A fictional community proposes a corner entrance to a park. The surveyor finds that part of the proposed walkway extends onto the adjacent parcel and reports the verified line and source version. The planner compares shifting the path within the site with investigating access rights, recording what each option would mean for visitors.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree and internship or residency as typical for surveyors and a master’s degree as typical for urban and regional planners in Table 1.2. Surveyors need a license to certify legal documents or provide services to the public. Check local requirements for the position. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [BLS surveyor licensing](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/surveyors.htm)

## Work conditions

Survey work may involve field measurements, records and deliverable review. Planning may involve site visits, review meetings and public input. Ask when the project needs a verified line.

## Moving between roles

Moving into planning, use boundary and records experience and add land-use policy and evaluation. Moving into surveying, use site analysis skills and build fieldwork, evidence review and applicable qualifications.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records show entrance A crosses the neighboring parcel without confirmed access rights. Entrance B stays within the site but adds walking distance. Spend ten minutes each on a survey handoff, an options comparison and checks needed before a recommendation.
