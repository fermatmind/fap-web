# Construction Laborers vs. Manufactured Building Installers: The Delivery Handoff

## What each role delivers

Construction laborers prepare the area and record access and staging issues. Manufactured building and mobile home installers verify the unit and its documentation, perform setup within their assigned scope, and record checks for handoff.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Construction Laborers | Manufactured Building and Mobile Home Installers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Ready staging area and site handoff | Unit verification, setup and inspection records |
| Typical issue | An occupied zone or changed delivery window | A mismatch between unit ID and project documents |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $47,120 | $45,990 |

## How the work meets

Two prefabricated site buildings are due to arrive. The laborer logs that the staging area is still occupied and confirms when it can be cleared. The installer finds that one unit’s delivery slip has a different revision from the project list and sends the discrepancy to the installation lead. The site lead rearranges delivery while the installation lead checks the paperwork.

## How to enter

BLS lists short-term on-the-job training for both roles and high school or equivalent as typical for installers. When a business directs, supervises or controls the covered initial installation of new manufactured homes in a state without a qualifying program, it must hold a HUD installation license or employ a license holder. Direct employees of the licensed installer or the company that employs one can work under that installer’s supervision without holding their own HUD installation license. Ordinary site preparation outside Part 3285 is one listed exception. Ask who supervises your work and which installation program applies to the home. ## Work conditions

Laborers move between work areas and handle changing site needs. Installation crews coordinate with deliveries, document revisions and inspections. Ask whether jobs require travel, who controls the installation documents, and who arranges required inspections.

## Moving between roles

To move into installations, use your delivery and site handoff records, then learn document and unit checks under supervision. To move into general site work, bring your inventory and handoff habits and add coordination across several trades.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional delivery sheet. M1 has matching documents, but its staging zone is occupied. M2’s label disagrees with the project list. Take ten minutes to write the site status, ten to log the document discrepancy, and ten to assign each next decision to the site lead or installation lead. Submit a desk-only handoff without planning equipment operations.
