# First-Line Construction Supervisors vs. Terrazzo Workers: When Can the Gallery Reopen?

## What each role delivers

First-line construction supervisors coordinate the floor crew and the facility schedule. Terrazzo workers and finishers deliver the specified floor and document progress and finish issues by area.

## Compare the work

| Work item | First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers | Terrazzo Workers and Finishers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Reopening plan and next-crew assignment | Area finish and joint check |
| Open issue | Access commitment | Finish discrepancy awaiting review |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $79,920 | $76,170 |

## How the work meets

In a fictional gallery, the east side has completed the project’s review, and the west side has a joint appearance item outstanding. The terrazzo crew reports both statuses. The supervisor proposes a phased reopening and keeps west-side access tied to the designated review.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent for both roles. An apprenticeship is typical for terrazzo workers and finishers, while five or more years of related work experience is typical for first-line supervisors. [BLS rows 47-1011 and 47-2053](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Terrazzo crews may stay in one area through successive stages. Supervisors coordinate the facility, materials and other trades. Ask how the site marks areas under finish review and who issues reopening notices.

## Moving between roles

To move into supervision, use finish-review and protection records to build experience coordinating turnover. To move into terrazzo, pair your scheduling experience with supervised layout and finish training.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Assume opening is Friday. East is approved, west still needs a joint review, and the west door is also a delivery route. Spend ten minutes on the floor handoff, ten on access planning, and ten on the conditions you would attach to a reopening promise.
