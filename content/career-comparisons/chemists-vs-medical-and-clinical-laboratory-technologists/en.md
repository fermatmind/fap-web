# Chemist vs. Clinical Laboratory Technologist: Can a Research Method Support a Clinical Report?

## What each role delivers

Chemists study composition and properties and may develop analytical methods. Clinical laboratory technologists conduct clinical tests and check the accuracy and quality of results under the lab's procedures.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Chemists | Medical and Clinical Laboratory Technologists |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence | Method, validation data and scope | Clinical specimen records, local quality checks and review status |
| Output | Analytical or method report | Clinical lab findings managed through the lab's procedures |

[O*NET 19-2031.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-2031.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 29-2011.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-2011.00#Tasks)

BLS reports wages for clinical laboratory technologists and technicians together. The combined figure does not isolate technologists. [BLS occupation profile](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/clinical-laboratory-technologists-and-technicians.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional U.S. hospital lab is considering introducing an in-house, nonwaived test system for clinical use. The chemist provides reference-solution data and states exactly which samples the research covered. The technologist notices that the handoff contains no validation record for the lab's intended specimen type and raises the gap with the laboratory lead. The chemist clarifies the study scope. The lab keeps the method under evaluation. For this nonwaived in-house method, 42 CFR § 493.1253(b)(2) requires the lab to establish the applicable performance specifications before reporting patient results. A research report can inform that evaluation. [42 CFR § 493.1253(b)(2)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-G/part-493/subpart-K/subject-group-ECFRc96daead380f6ed/section-493.1253)

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor's degree as typical entry education for chemists. BLS says clinical laboratory technologists typically earn a bachelor's degree in medical technology or a related life sciences field. Some states require a license, and employers may prefer certification. Check the relevant state agency and the employer's credential requirements. [BLS Table 1.2](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) · [BLS clinical laboratory entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/clinical-laboratory-technologists-and-technicians.htm) Some chemistry and materials research jobs may require a master’s degree or Ph.D. [BLS research education](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/life-physical-and-social-science/chemists-and-materials-scientists.htm)

## Work conditions

Chemistry work can depend on research and instrument schedules. Clinical laboratory work may follow specimen queues, quality checks and shifts. Ask who controls method versions and retains evaluation records.

## Moving between roles

Chemists can bring method analysis into clinical work and build the necessary credentials and quality-system experience. Clinical technologists can bring result review and learn research design and method development.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use mock records: results for reference solutions and a clinical checklist missing validation for the intended specimen type. Spend ten minutes noting method limits, ten drafting the lab handoff and ten separating conclusions for the research or internal evaluation record from patient results that can be reported only after the lab establishes the applicable performance specifications. Use paper records alone.
