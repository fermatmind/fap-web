# Procurement Clerks vs. Wholesale Sales Representatives for Nontechnical Products: A Quote Changes Before Ordering

## What each role delivers

Procurement clerks prepare and track purchase orders. Wholesale and manufacturing sales representatives for nontechnical products explain products, prices and delivery terms.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Procurement Clerks | Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Except Technical and Scientific Products |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Purchase order and quote revision record | Supplier quote and delivery confirmation |
| Basis | Requisition, approval and quantities | Products, supply, price and delivery terms |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $50,580 | $72,080 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 43-3061 and 41-4012](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional office supplier splits one delivery and adds shipping to the later batch. The sales representative sends a revised quote with a version number and expiration date. The procurement clerk compares the charge and delay against the approved requisition and updates the purchase order after authorization, then asks the supplier to acknowledge it.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for both occupations. Ask which role can approve changes, issue quotes or release orders. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Purchasing may follow requisitions, approvals and receiving deadlines. Sales may follow quote validity and dispatch. Ask where revised terms are recorded.

## Moving between roles

Moving into sales adds product and account work to order knowledge. Moving into procurement adds requisitions, approval routing and reconciliation to quoting experience.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records: the original order is 40 boxes in one delivery. The new quote sends 25 first and 15 later, with an added freight charge. Spend ten minutes each on the quote, the buyer’s discrepancy record and confirmations needed before the order changes.
