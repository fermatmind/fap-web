# Sheet Metal Workers vs. Structural Iron and Steel Workers: A Column Cover That Does Not Fit

## What each role delivers

Sheet metal workers fabricate and assemble metal parts. Structural iron and steel workers erect, align and connect structural members. This example concerns a nonstructural column cover and the steel column behind it.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Sheet Metal Workers | Structural Iron and Steel Workers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Fabricated sheet metal enclosure | Erected and aligned structural members |
| Basis | Shop dimensions, edges and joints | Erection drawing, member ID and connections |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $61,800 | $62,780 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 47-2211 and 47-2221](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

In a fictional lobby, a cover will not fit. The sheet metal crew checks dimensions and the shipment revision. The steel crew supplies the column measurement and erection drawing. The column passes its check and the cover was made to an old revision.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and apprenticeship for both. Ask whether training covers shop fabrication, field installation or structural erection. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Sheet metal work can span shop and site. Steel erection follows delivery, alignment and connection windows. Ask who coordinates the shared interface.

## Moving between roles

Moving to structural steel, bring drawing and assembly skills and learn erection drawings and alignment. Moving to sheet metal, bring field measurement and learn fabrication and edge details.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock card: column S4 passes against erection revision E, its cover is marked C, the purchase order calls for D and the part has not been accepted. Spend ten minutes on each trade’s record and ten naming who checks the revision and approves the handoff.
