# Food Batchmakers vs. Machine Feeders and Offbearers: A Lot That Does Not Match the Ticket

## What each role delivers

Food batchmakers process ingredients to a recipe and document the batch. Machine feeders and offbearers feed materials into equipment or remove output and check labels, quantities and destinations.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Food Batchmakers | Machine Feeders and Offbearers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Recipe batch and production record | Machine loading, unloading and routing record |
| Basis | Recipe, ingredient lots and process records | Labels, quantities and destinations |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $42,290 | $41,220 |

## How the work meets

At a fictional mixing station, the machine feeder checks two boxes before loading the mixer. Lot L3 is absent from the batch ticket. The feeder keeps that box in the designated hold area and alerts the assigned reviewer. The batchmaker leaves L3 out of the ingredient record and notes that the batch still needs one eligible box.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for food batchmakers. For feeders and offbearers it lists no formal educational credential and short-term on-the-job training. Ask about the employer’s training and task scope. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Batchmaking can follow product and batch changes. Feeding and offbearing follow material movements and station demand. Ask about handling, sanitation assignments and shifts.

## Moving between roles

Moving to material flow, bring traceability and learn routing and inventory checks. Moving to batchmaking, bring lot tracking and add recipe records and food-production training.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock station card: the mixer order calls for two boxes of L1. Before loading, the feeder finds one L1 box and one L3 box. L3 is not approved for this batch. Spend ten minutes logging the loading exception and hold, ten recording the missing L1 box for the batch, and ten listing which lot and ticket the designated reviewer must check.
