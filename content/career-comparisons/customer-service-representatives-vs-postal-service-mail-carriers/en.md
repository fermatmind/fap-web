# Customer Service Representative vs. Postal Mail Carrier: A Delivered Scan and a Missing Parcel

## What each role delivers

A customer service representative receives inquiries and tracks resolution. A postal service mail carrier delivers and collects mail along a route and records scans or receipts required for the service.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Customer Service Representatives | Postal Service Mail Carriers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Inquiry, verified status and follow-up | Route actions, scans or delivery receipt |
| Basis | Customer account, tracking ID, service and status | Route, address, time and field record |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $44,770 | $60,550 |

## How the work meets

A fictional recipient reports a parcel marked delivered that they cannot find. The representative checks the tracking ID and service and routes the issue to the delivery unit. The carrier checks the day’s route record and note within their authority and confirms that it records a delivery location without a signature. The representative communicates the finding and what is still open.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent as typical for customer service representatives and no formal educational credential for postal carriers. It lists short-term on-the-job training for both. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) For USPS employment, its general age rule is 18 at appointment or 16 with a high school diploma. Check the posting for other requirements, including a safe driving record where applicable. [USPS employment requirements](https://about.usps.com/careers/how-to-apply/welcome.htm)

## Work conditions

Service work follows inquiries and callbacks. Delivery work follows route conditions, weather and mail volume. Ask how unclear scans are investigated.

## Moving between roles

Representatives can bring exception logging to carrier work and learn routes, safe work and physical handoffs. Carriers bring tracking context to customer service and can add case systems and callbacks.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock file: tracking says delivered at 2:20 p.m., and the service does not require a signature. The route note says lobby, but the recipient reports two lobbies. Spend ten minutes each on the inquiry, route checks and an update separating what is known from what needs review.
