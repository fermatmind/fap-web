# Psychiatric Technicians vs Psychiatrists

## Choose between ongoing observation and medical decision-making

Psychiatric technicians observe and support patients in daily care under professional direction. Psychiatrists are physicians who assess mental health conditions, diagnose and make medical treatment decisions. Both rely on accurate records from the unit.

## Compare the two records each shift depends on

| At work | Psychiatric technician | Psychiatrist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main output | Observations, routine care and handoff notes | Medical assessment and treatment decisions |
| Common setting | Behavioral health facility or hospital unit | Hospital, clinic or private practice |
| Working relationship | Reports through the unit's supervision structure | Coordinates medical decisions with the care team |

[Technician tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-2053.00) · [Psychiatrist tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-1223.00)

## Follow a missing detail through a California unit

In a fictional California inpatient unit, a psychiatric technician records that patient P9 missed a scheduled activity and leaves the reason open. The technician reports the observation to the supervising nurse. The psychiatrist reviews the information with the team and determines whether further medical assessment is needed. The next shift receives the documented observation and the confirmed follow-up. California defines who may direct technicians; this example uses a nurse as the immediate supervisor. [California technician scope](https://www.bvnpt.ca.gov/licensees/psychiatric_technician.html)

## Check education, examination and authority

BLS lists a postsecondary nondegree award as typical for psychiatric technicians. California requires a technician license and an exam; completing a board-approved program is one qualifying route. Psychiatrists complete medical school and specialty residency. For the California physician pathway administered by the Medical Board, check the board's education, postgraduate training and examination requirements. [BLS entry education](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) · [Technician licensing](https://bvnpt.ca.gov/applicants/summary_pt.html) · [Physician licensing](https://www.mbc.ca.gov/Licensing/Physicians-and-Surgeons/Apply/Physicians-and-Surgeons-License/)

## Read pay beside the shifts and responsibility

BLS reports 2025 U.S. median annual wages of $45,130 for psychiatric technicians and $281,870 for psychiatrists. Ask how many overnight or weekend shifts each job entails, and who reviews documentation. A fictional observation log can show your ability to separate facts and open questions. Moving toward psychiatry adds medical school, residency and licensure; moving into a technician role requires checking that role's own training and credential rules. [BLS wage table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Try the two handoffs in 30 minutes

Use mock record A (P9 missed a scheduled activity; reason unknown) and record B (supervisor notified; medical follow-up unconfirmed). Spend eight minutes sorting facts, 12 minutes writing the observation note and separate follow-up handoff, and ten minutes asking another reader to identify the next owner. Work from the fictional notes only.

## Ask how long each role owns the record

Ask a technician what makes a shift note complete. Ask a psychiatrist which questions a handoff cannot settle without a medical assessment. Compare which responsibility you would want across an ordinary week.
