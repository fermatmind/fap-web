# LPN or LVN vs. Surgical Technologist

## Nursing care and surgical preparation

Licensed practical and licensed vocational nurses provide assigned nursing care and report changes. Surgical technologists prepare instruments and supplies, maintain the sterile field and participate in the surgical team's counts.

## Compare what needs to be documented

| Item | LPN or LVN | Surgical technologist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verify | Assigned care and patient observations | Instrument and supply readiness |
| Document | Patient concerns and whom the nurse notified | Preparation status and count discrepancies |
| Report to | The designated clinical or nursing lead | The designated operating-room team member |

[O*NET 29-2061.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-2061.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 29-2055.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-2055.00#Tasks)

## The operating room and receiving unit need separate confirmations

In a fictional surgical center, a surgical technologist identifies a discrepancy in the team count record and reports it to the designated operating-room lead. An LPN or LVN assigned to subsequent care finds that the receiving location changed and checks the update with the designated nursing lead. The teams resolve the count and receiving arrangements through their own processes.

## U.S. education and credentials

LPNs and LVNs complete an approved nursing program and pass an exam to obtain a state license. Scope and supervision vary by state and setting. BLS describes a certificate or associate degree from an accredited program as typical for surgical technologists. Employers may prefer or require certification and some states regulate the work. Check applicable state and facility requirements. In its national table, BLS assigns both occupations a postsecondary nondegree award and no related work experience or on-the-job training. Individual entry routes, degree programs and authorization still vary. [BLS practical nursing entry guidance](https://www.bls.gov/OOH/healthcare/licensed-practical-and-licensed-vocational-nurses.htm#tab-4) · [BLS surgical technologist entry guidance](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/surgical-technologists.htm#tab-4) · [BLS detailed training table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/education-and-training-by-occupation.htm)

## Ask about workflow changes

For a nursing job, ask how preprocedure care, observation and documentation are assigned. For surgical technology, ask about operating-room rotation, time to prepare instruments and who handles discrepancies when the schedule changes.

## Moving between roles

An LPN or LVN can bring team communication and documentation skills into surgical technology training, then learn instrument and sterile-field responsibilities. Surgical technologists can bring handoff and discrepancy reporting skills to nursing school, then complete the required path to licensure.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use a fictional team count record, patient handoff and schedule update. Spend ten minutes recording a count discrepancy, ten writing a request to confirm the receiving location, and ten identifying who receives each message and what closes each issue. Work with the fictional records only.
