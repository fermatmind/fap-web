# Occupational Therapy Aides vs Personal Care Aides

## Choose the setting and the work you want to support

Occupational therapy aides help prepare treatment areas and carry out routine delegated tasks. Personal care aides assist with daily living and report observations so the next worker knows what to follow up. The setting determines who assigns and checks the work.

## Compare the two work records

| Work | Occupational therapy aide | Personal care aide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typical record | Room and supplies readiness | Daily care and observations |
| Team | Occupational therapist and occupational therapy assistant | Care supervisor and other caregivers |
| Setting | Therapy department or clinic | Home or residential care setting |

[OT aide tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/31-2012.00) · [Personal care tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/31-1122.00)

## Follow a schedule change at a California residence

In a fictional residence with an OT service, the OT aide records that a room is still being prepared and alerts the occupational therapist. The therapist determines whether the session can proceed. The personal care aide checks the resident's daily schedule, reports the conflict to the care supervisor, and documents the updated arrangement. California's OT regulations classify room preparation as a non-client-related task. Certain routine client-related tasks may be delegated under specified conditions. Evaluation, treatment initiation and treatment adjustment stay with qualified professionals. [California OT regulations](https://bot.ca.gov/board_activity/laws_regs/cc_regulations.shtml)

## Check training and the exact job category

BLS describes high school education and on-the-job training as typical for occupational therapy aides. California requires training and demonstrated competence for assigned OT aide tasks, with supervision that depends on the task. For personal care work, BLS says a high school diploma or equivalent is typical for its home health and personal care aide group, with some jobs requiring no formal credential. Training may be on the job. For work as an affiliated Home Care Aide through a licensed California Home Care Organization, check state registration and background clearance, and plan for state-required entry training before client contact and annual training. [BLS OT aides](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/occupational-therapy-assistants-and-aides.htm) · [BLS personal care entry](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/home-health-aides-and-personal-care-aides.htm) · [California OT regulations](https://bot.ca.gov/board_activity/laws_regs/cc_regulations.shtml) · [California Home Care Aide registration](https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/community-care/home-care-services/resources-for-home-care-aides) · [California affiliated aide training](https://cdss.ca.gov/portals/9/regs/hcscpman1.pdf?ver=2017-12-29-082929-037)

## Compare shifts and build on your handoff skills

Ask a therapy department how many appointments an aide prepares for and ask a care agency about travel, schedule changes and reporting. Bring a fictional handoff log to show how you record incomplete work. Moving toward OT support adds site procedures and delegation training. Moving toward personal care adds practice documenting daily living support and meeting applicable agency requirements.

## Try a 30-minute paper exercise

Use two fictional records: R8 has a 10 a.m. appointment, and the room checklist still has an open item. Spend eight minutes separating confirmed information from open items. Spend 12 minutes writing a note for the therapist and another for the care supervisor. Use the final ten minutes to test whether a reader can identify the owner and status of each item.

## Ask who checks the next step

Ask an OT team which tasks a new aide can perform after training. Ask a care supervisor what goes into a good daily handoff. Compare which setting and set of responsibilities you want to explore.
