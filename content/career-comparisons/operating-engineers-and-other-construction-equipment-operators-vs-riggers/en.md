# Construction Equipment Operators vs. Riggers: The Machine and Load Handoff

## What each role delivers

Construction equipment operators handle assigned machine work. Riggers select and check rigging and prepare load handoffs. A worker assigned to signal has separate duties to verify.

## How the work meets

On a fictional installation project, the equipment crew logs completed ground work. The rigger sees a mismatch between a load tag and its work order and reports it to the lift lead. The equipment handoff proceeds as a separate record, while the load item remains open.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training as typical for both. For signal persons under federal OSHA construction Subpart CC, the employer must verify qualifications before signaling through an accepted evaluator option. For the activities listed in §1926.1425(c) when employees are in the fall zone, the materials must be rigged by a qualified rigger. Verify which duty you would be assigned and how the employer records each qualification. [OSHA §1926.1425(c)](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.1425) [OSHA §1926.1428](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.1428) [BLS rows 47-2073 and 49-9096](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Operators focus on one machine and its work area. Riggers coordinate load information across the crew. Ask who assigns the signal person and where qualification and load records are kept.

## Moving between roles

Moving into rigging, carry over equipment handoffs and learn rigging records and assigned roles under supervision. Moving into operating, add training and evaluation for your target machine.

## Try it in 30 minutes

On paper, ground work is complete, the delivery sheet says L7, the load tag says L9, and the signal-person record is unconfirmed. Spend 30 minutes on two handoffs and an owner list. Keep the exercise to documents and assignments.
