# Remote Sensing Scientists and Technologists vs. Surveying and Mapping Technicians: What Can Field Points Validate?

## What each role delivers

Remote sensing scientists and technologists analyze images and assess results. Surveying and mapping technicians record locations and field observations and may maintain point records and map layers.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Remote Sensing Scientists and Technologists | Surveying and Mapping Technicians |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Image analysis and use limitations | Survey coordinates and field notes |
| Basis | Class rules, reference samples and dates | Point IDs, coordinate system and site conditions |

[O*NET 19-2099.01](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-2099.01#Tasks) · [O*NET 17-3031.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-3031.00#Tasks)

O*NET uses the Physical Scientists, All Other wage series for 19-2099.01. A separate figure for this specialty cannot be derived from the broader series. [O*NET wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/19-2099.01)

## How the work meets

A fictional trail project checks a classification of impervious surfaces. The surveying and mapping technician provides point IDs, coordinates, dates and surface descriptions. The remote sensing scientist compares the dates with the imagery used in the classification and logs matches, conflicts and unresolved cases point by point. The eastern observations support checks at those points. Assessing area-wide accuracy requires additional samples covering the relevant classes and geography. [USGS classification accuracy sampling example](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/accuracy-assessment-nlcd-2006-land-cover-and-impervious-surface)

## How to enter

A bachelor’s degree accounted for 60% of U.S. O*NET education responses for remote sensing scientists and technologists. BLS lists high school completion or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for surveying and mapping technicians. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [O*NET education data 19-2099.01](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-2099.01#Education)

## Work conditions

Analysis can iterate with imagery and reference samples. Technical work can alternate among field records, data entry and map review. Ask who updates the point register when a surface changes.

## Moving between roles

Moving into remote sensing, use field and coordinate skills and add classification and validation sampling. Moving into surveying and mapping, use spatial data skills and add instrument practice and field documentation.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records include four eastern points with coordinates and same-week photos, preconstruction imagery in the west and a classification covering both sides. Spend ten minutes each on the point handoff, the date and class fields for a point-by-point check and the sample locations and classes still needed for area-wide validation.
