# Electric Motor Repairer vs. Watch and Clock Repairer: A Drive That Runs and a Clock That Stops

## What each role delivers

Motor repairers isolate faults in motors, tools and related equipment and verify the work. Watch and clock repairers diagnose the condition of a timepiece and check that it works and keeps time after service.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electric Motor, Power Tool, and Related Repairers | Watch and Clock Repairers |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence | Drive symptoms and unit inspection | Timepiece symptoms, movement condition and observed rate |
| Output | Repair record and verified drive or unit | Repaired timepiece and a check of its operation |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $56,210 | $67,230 |

## How the work meets

A fictional shop receives an electric display clock that stops intermittently. The clock repairer logs when it stopped and sends its serviceable drive unit and the symptom record to a motor repairer. The motor repairer reports what was repaired and the drive's test result. After reassembly, the clock repairer checks the completed clock's operation and time indication against the service agreement before deciding whether to return it.

## How to enter

BLS lists a high school diploma or equivalent for both. It lists less than five years of related experience and moderate-term on-the-job training for motor and power tool repair, and no related experience and long-term on-the-job training for watch and clock repair. Ask how employers train for the actual units or movements they service. [BLS Table 1.2](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Motor work may vary with unit size, faults and parts availability. Timepiece repair may require long observation periods and careful small-parts handling. Ask who follows a repair ticket when work moves between benches.

## Moving between roles

Both roles use inspection records and functional checks. Moving toward timepieces, learn movements and rate verification. Moving toward motor repair, learn drive diagnosis and the authorized electrical checks for the equipment.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use mock tickets: the drive passes, the clock runs for ten minutes and the customer reported a stop after an hour. Spend ten minutes listing what is verified, ten writing the handoff and ten specifying what evidence is missing before release. Work from the records only.
