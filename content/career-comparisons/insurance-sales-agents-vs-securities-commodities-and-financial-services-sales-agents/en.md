# Insurance Sales Agents vs. Securities, Commodities, and Financial Services Sales Agents: Two Questions, Two Scopes

## What each role delivers

Insurance sales agents explain coverage and policies. Securities, commodities, and financial services sales agents may discuss products and handle transactions within assigned authority. This example concerns a securities account inquiry.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Insurance Sales Agents | Securities, Commodities, and Financial Services Sales Agents |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Coverage needs and policy explanation | Securities inquiry and documented client instructions |
| Basis | Insured exposure, limits and authority | Client instructions, product features and registration scope |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $62,280 | $78,660 |

## How the work meets

A fictional client asks about opening a brokerage account during an insurance consultation. The insurance agent records the coverage request and, with consent, refers the account question to authorized securities staff. That person clarifies the activity and explains the account process. Both teams log their replies and open documents separately.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent for insurance sales agents and a bachelor’s degree for securities sales agents, with moderate-term on-the-job training for both. State insurance licenses are required where agents work. For the FINRA general securities representative role, check SIE and Series 7 and firm sponsorship for the representative exam. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [BLS insurance licensing](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/sales/insurance-sales-agents.htm) [FINRA Series 7](https://www.finra.org/registration-exams-ce/qualification-exams/series7)

## Work conditions

Insurance work may follow consultations, applications and renewals. Securities work may follow market activity and client contact. Ask what each team records after a referral.

## Moving between roles

Moving into securities adds product knowledge, firm procedures and applicable registration. Moving into insurance adds coverage, policy terms and required licensing to client communication.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock request: the client asks about home coverage and opening a brokerage account. Spend ten minutes each on a case note, identifying who handles each question and confirming the scope of each reply.
