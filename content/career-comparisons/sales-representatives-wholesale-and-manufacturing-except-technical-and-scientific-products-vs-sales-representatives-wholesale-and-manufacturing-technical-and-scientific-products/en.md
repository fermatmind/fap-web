# Nontechnical vs. Technical and Scientific Wholesale Sales Representatives: Two Product Lines on One Quote

## What each role delivers

Both roles quote goods and terms to business customers. Technical and scientific sales work can also require matching the product to an application.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Except Technical and Scientific Products | Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Technical and Scientific Products |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Standard goods quote and delivery conditions | Technical requirements check and quote |
| Basis | Size, quantity, pack and delivery | Application, compatibility and verification limits |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $72,080 | $104,920 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 41-4012 and 41-4011](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional lab buys labels and a measuring instrument from one supplier. The nontechnical representative checks label size, pack quantity and delivery. The technical representative records range, site conditions and acceptance requirements and checks them with product specialists. Both share confirmed terms and unresolved questions with purchasing.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent for nontechnical representatives and a bachelor’s degree for technical and scientific representatives. Both have moderate-term on-the-job training as typical. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Both can follow quote and delivery dates. Technical sales may also involve site assessments and demos. Ask who validates technical claims.

## Moving between roles

Moving into technical sales adds product principles and application testing to commercial skills. Moving into nontechnical sales adds standard assortments and volume supply to needs assessment.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock order: 1,200 labels in packs of 500 and one instrument with a specified range but unverified site conditions. Spend ten minutes each on a standard quote, technical checks and unresolved items.
