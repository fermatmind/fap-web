# Electrical and Electronics Drafters vs. Electronics Engineers: Revising a Connector Drawing

## What each role delivers

Electrical and electronics drafters turn engineering inputs into traceable drawings. Electronics engineers design electronic systems and evaluate technical requirements.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electrical and Electronics Drafters | Electronics Engineers, Except Computer |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Drawing package and revision notes | Electronic design and verification |
| Basis | Engineering inputs and drawing standards | Performance requirements, components and test evidence |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $76,870 | $130,220 |

## How the work meets

A fictional sensor board gets a new connector. The engineer confirms placement and compatibility requirements. The drafter revises the board and assembly drawings, records the revision and flags one unconfirmed orientation for engineering review before release.

## How to enter

BLS lists an associate degree as typical for electrical and electronics drafters and a bachelor’s degree for electronics engineers. For drafting, show a revision package. For engineering, show component choices and validation evidence. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Drafters track drawing revisions and reviews. Engineers coordinate design decisions and evaluation. Ask who maintains drawing versions and who approves changes.

## Moving between roles

Moving into engineering, bring drawing fluency and add circuit analysis and validation. Moving into drafting, bring design knowledge and learn drawing conventions and document control.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional connector change and two sketches, with orientation missing on the assembly sketch. Spend ten minutes each listing affected sheets, writing questions for engineering and preparing a release checklist with owners.
