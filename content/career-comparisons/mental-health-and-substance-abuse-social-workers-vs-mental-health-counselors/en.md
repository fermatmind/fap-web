# Mental Health Social Worker vs. Mental Health Counselor

## What each professional follows up on

Mental Health and Substance Abuse Social Workers can coordinate care, connect clients with services and help them reach appointments. Clinical social workers may also provide therapy. Mental Health Counselors use sessions and progress notes to track a client's concerns, goals and coping strategies.

## Compare the next steps

| Decision | Mental health and substance abuse social worker | Mental health counselor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find the barrier | Check whether the client can get to the service | Explore what makes it hard for the client to attend |
| Act this week | Verify an open appointment and feasible transportation | Agree on what the client wants to try before returning |
| Follow up | Confirm the appointment and check whether the connection happened | Review the client's experience and update the counseling plan |

## After two missed follow-ups

A client discharged from a program has missed two outpatient visits. The bus stops running before the appointment ends, and the client has also felt too worried to walk into reception. A social worker can check clinic hours, available slots and ways to get home. A counselor can ask what happens as the client approaches reception and agree on what to follow up on. With permission, the two can coordinate around the appointment and the barriers the client wants shared.

## U.S. education and licensure

For social work, identify whether the opening is a clinical role. BLS describes a master's in social work, supervised clinical experience and state licensure for clinical social work. For mental health counselors it describes a master's degree and internship as typical and state licensure with supervised clinical work and an exam. Check the state board for each track, including which supervised tasks and placements count.

## A week of referrals versus booked sessions

Ask a social work employer how much time goes to coordination calls, outreach and waiting for other agencies to confirm services. Ask a counseling employer about appointment blocks and protected time for notes and supervision. For both roles, verify who is assigned to follow up after missed visits.

## Experience you can carry across

From social work, bring interviewing skills and examples of coordinated care, then check counseling coursework and clinical training requirements. From counseling, bring progress documentation and follow-up skills, then develop experience checking service eligibility and tracking referrals through completion. Review degree and licensure requirements for clinical social work separately.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use a fictional outpatient clinic. The Wednesday bus ends early. A ride is available Friday but the clinic has not confirmed a slot. The client reports once turning back at reception. In ten minutes, write a referral tracker with one owner and a time to verify each open question. In ten minutes, draft two counseling questions and a follow-up item. Use the last ten minutes to mark which details could be shared with permission.
