# Mental Health and Substance Abuse Social Workers vs Psychiatric Technicians

## Choose between helping someone connect to services and supporting care on the unit

Mental health and substance abuse social workers assess service needs, connect people to resources and follow up. Licensed clinical social workers may also provide clinical services within scope. Psychiatric technicians provide assigned direct care, observe changes and report them to the supervising team.

## Compare the record each worker owns

| At work | Social worker | Psychiatric technician |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work product | Referral follow-up or psychosocial service record | Shift observation and care handoff |
| Partners | Community programs, patient and care team | Unit supervisor and direct-care team |
| Facility or setting | Clinic, hospital, agency or community program | Behavioral health unit or residential setting |

## Follow two open items before discharge

In a fictional California unit, the discharge team has two open items. A social worker checks whether a community program received the referral and records that the intake appointment is pending. A psychiatric technician documents a change observed during an assigned activity and gives the note to the directing registered nurse. The nurse reviews the observation through the unit's workflow, and the authorized clinical team owns the discharge decision. [Social work referral tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/21-1023.00) · [California technician scope](https://bvnpt.ca.gov/licensees/psychiatric_technician.html)

## Check the actual job and its route into practice

For a California clinical social work role, the LCSW pathway includes an accredited MSW, supervised experience and exams. Register as an Associate Clinical Social Worker before accruing California supervised hours unless the Board's 90-day rule applies. For psychiatric technicians, BLS lists a postsecondary nondegree award as typical. California has a technician license exam with qualifying routes, including a board-approved program. [California LCSW requirements](https://www.bbs.ca.gov/applicants/lcsw.html) · [BLS entry table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) · [California technician requirements](https://bvnpt.ca.gov/applicants/summary_pt.html)

## Compare wages and the rhythm of follow-up

BLS reports 2025 U.S. median annual wages of $60,280 for mental health and substance abuse social workers and $45,130 for psychiatric technicians. Ask about caseload and outreach for social work, and shifts and supervision for technician roles. Use a fictional open-items log to show how you document who owns the next step. Moving toward social work adds resource coordination and the education required for the intended job; moving toward technician work adds unit training and state-specific credentials.
