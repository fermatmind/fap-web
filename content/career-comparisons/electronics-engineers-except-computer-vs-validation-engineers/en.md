# Electronics Engineers vs. Validation Engineers: Does the Retest Close the Case?

## What each role delivers

Electronics engineers design electronic components and evaluate performance. Validation engineers set objectives and criteria and evaluate traceable test evidence.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electronics Engineers, Except Computer | Validation Engineers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Design and technical rationale | Validation plan, results and deviation record |
| Basis | Performance requirements and design revision | Predefined criteria and controlled records |

[O*NET 17-2072.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2072.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 17-2112.02](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2112.02#Tasks)

O*NET uses the Industrial Engineers wage series for Validation Engineers. That series covers a broader occupation and does not establish a validation-specific median. [O*NET wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/17-2112.02)

## How the work meets

After a fictional recorder revision, an electronics engineer checks readings at nominal conditions. The validation engineer reviews the approved plan, finds that low-temperature evidence is still required and records the gap.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for electronics engineering. For validation, check the target industry and show an example of criteria, test coverage and deviation handling. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Electronics work follows design and prototype cycles. Validation work coordinates test schedules, records and deviation review. Ask who approves the plan and closes the record.

## Moving between roles

Moving into validation, build on test experience and add coverage and deviation handling. Moving into electronics, build on evidence management and add circuit design and technical tradeoffs.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use three fictional cards: a revision ID, a pass at nominal conditions and a missing low-temperature result. Spend ten minutes each on the engineering summary, validation coverage and the owner and evidence needed to close the gap.
