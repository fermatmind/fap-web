# Solar Energy Installation Managers vs. Solar Photovoltaic Installers: The Missing Mounting Parts

## What each role delivers

Solar energy installation managers coordinate crews, materials and inspections. Solar photovoltaic installers set up modules and supports to drawings and report actual progress and exceptions.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Solar Energy Installation Managers | Solar Photovoltaic Installers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Crew schedule and inspection plan | Installed components and field record |
| Basis | Materials, work areas and inspection status | Drawing revision, component IDs and installation status |

[O*NET 47-1011.03](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-1011.03#Tasks) · [O*NET 47-2231.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-2231.00#Tasks)

O*NET uses the broader First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers wage category for solar energy installation managers. That series does not provide a separate figure for the role. [O*NET occupational profile](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-1011.03)

## How the work meets

A fictional retail project has east and west work areas. The installer finds one missing set of supports in the west and submits an exception. The manager confirms the east area is ready and coordinates a revised work and inspection window for the west.

## How to enter

O*NET reports high school or equivalent as the most frequent education response for solar installation managers. BLS lists high school or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for photovoltaic installers. Some states require photovoltaic installers to be licensed; ask the state licensing board about the assigned scope. [O*NET education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/47-1011.03#Education) [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [BLS licensing overview](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/solar-photovoltaic-installers.htm)

## Work conditions

Managers coordinate crews, materials and inspections. Installers follow site tasks and conditions, including weather. Ask how exceptions reach the project team.

## Moving between roles

Moving to management, bring site records and add crew and inspection coordination. Moving to installation, bring project process skills and add drawing interpretation and site training.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use mock records: revision B calls for four support sets per area, four arrive in the east and three in the west, and the west inspection is on tomorrow’s schedule. Spend ten minutes each on the installer’s exception, the manager’s coordination and the evidence needed to revise inspection.
