# Craft Artist vs. Jeweler: Checking a Brooch After the Design Is Approved

## What each role delivers

A craft artist develops a physical object through material, form and finish. A jeweler or precious stone and metal worker makes or repairs jewelry and checks it against specifications. The work can overlap in a small studio.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Craft artists | Jewelers and Precious Stone and Metal Workers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Visual sample and color and finish notes | Fabricated jewelry and an inspection record |
| Basis | Material, outline and surface finish | Component dimensions, wearing orientation and specifications |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $46,080 | $52,540 |

## How the work meets

A fictional studio is making a commemorative brooch. The craft artist submits the face sample and marks its orientation. The jeweler checks the space on the back, finds that the original clasp would cover an inscription on the back and sketches two mounting positions. The craft artist verifies that the chosen position preserves the visible orientation. The jeweler makes and inspects the approved assembly.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential as typical for craft artists and a high school diploma or equivalent for jewelers and precious stone and metal workers. It lists long-term on-the-job training for both. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Craft schedules can follow sampling and client feedback. Jewelry making can alternate between fine bench work, repairs and finished inspection. Ask how a specification change is approved and recorded.

## Moving between roles

Craft artists can bring form and finish skills to jewelry and add precision measurement, joining and inspection. Jewelers can bring tool and quality-control skills to craft and add prototype development and presentation.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock drawing: the brooch must keep its visible orientation. The usable width on the back is 24 millimeters. Clasp A needs 23 millimeters with installation clearance; clasp B needs 27. Spend ten minutes each on the sample handoff, choosing a fit candidate and listing orientation and assembly checks before delivery.
