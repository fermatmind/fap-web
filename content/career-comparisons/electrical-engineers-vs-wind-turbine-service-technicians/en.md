# Electrical Engineers vs. Wind Turbine Service Technicians: The Replacement Part Record

## What each role delivers

Electrical engineers design and verify electrical equipment and systems. Wind turbine service technicians diagnose, maintain and repair specific turbines within assigned authority.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electrical Engineers | Wind Turbine Service Technicians |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Electrical component and design review | Turbine service ticket and recheck |
| Basis | Revision, load and interface documents | Asset ID, part history and field status |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $120,630 | $64,120 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 17-2071 and 49-9081](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

At a fictional wind site, the service technician records the new part revision and a passed recheck for W9. The electrical engineer finds only the old revision in the approved configuration and requests a compatibility review.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for electrical engineers. For wind service technicians it lists a postsecondary nondegree award and long-term on-the-job training. Ask which tasks require site-specific training and authorization. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Engineering can follow design and verification reviews. Turbine service can follow field shifts, weather and maintenance windows. Ask who approves return to service and configuration changes.

## Moving between roles

Moving to service, build on system design and obtain turbine-specific training. Moving to engineering, bring service evidence and develop electrical design and verification.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Read mock records only. W9 changed from part revision R1 to R2. The field recheck passed, the approved list shows only R1, and change review is open. Spend ten minutes each on the service note, engineering review and the evidence needed to close both.
