# Clinical Nurse Specialist vs. LPN or LVN

## Specialty nursing and assigned basic care

Clinical nurse specialists provide specialty nursing care and may educate staff and evaluate nursing practices. Licensed practical and licensed vocational nurses provide assigned basic care, chart observations and report changes through their designated clinical chain.

## Compare the work each role records

| Item | Clinical nurse specialist | LPN or LVN |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Review | Specialty care needs and recurring gaps in practice | Assigned care and the patient's reported or observed changes |
| Document | Specialty recommendation, staff guidance or a practice review | Observations and when and to whom they were reported |
| Follow up | Coordinate with nursing and management leads | Escalate through the designated clinical process |

## A handoff form repeatedly misses a time entry

In a fictional inpatient unit, an LVN notices a transfer note omits when a patient raised a concern. The LVN checks the original note and reports the issue to the responsible nurse. A clinical nurse specialist reviewing recurring omissions compares example forms and proposes a field and staff training. The designated leadership reviews any proposed form change; the assigned clinical team continues the patient's follow-up.

## U.S. preparation and licensing

BLS describes a state-approved LPN or LVN program, a licensing exam and a state license. BLS identifies the clinical nurse specialist as an advanced practice registered nurse, with a nursing master's degree and typically at least a year of RN or related experience. States set additional licensing requirements, which may include specialty certification. Verify the applicable board's CNS role and population requirements. [BLS practical nursing entry guidance](https://www.bls.gov/OOH/healthcare/licensed-practical-and-licensed-vocational-nurses.htm#tab-4) · [BLS registered nursing and CNS entry guidance](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/registered-nurses.htm#tab-4)

## Ask how the week is allocated

Ask a CNS employer which time is reserved for specialty consultation, staff education and practice evaluation. For the LPN or LVN role, ask how shifts, assigned care and supervision are organized.

## Moving between roles

LPNs and LVNs can bring observation and documentation skills to an RN education pathway, then pursue applicable RN experience and graduate CNS preparation. A CNS moving into a basic care setting can bring documentation and escalation skills and should verify the specific license and responsibility of the opening.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use a fictional shift transfer note missing one time entry and two earlier examples with the same omission. Spend ten minutes writing an item for the current handoff, ten drafting a practice review question, and ten identifying the patient follow-up owner and the person who can approve a form change.
