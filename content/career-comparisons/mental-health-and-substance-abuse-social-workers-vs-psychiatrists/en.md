# Mental Health and Substance Abuse Social Workers vs Psychiatrists

## Choose between social support work and physician responsibility

Mental health and substance abuse social workers connect people to services and follow up on access barriers. Clinical social workers can also provide assessment and psychotherapy within their scope. Psychiatrists are physicians who assess mental disorders, diagnose and make medical treatment decisions, including prescribing.

## Compare what each role hands off

| At work | Social worker | Psychiatrist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typical notes | Service needs, referral status and follow-up, or clinical social work notes when authorized | Medical assessment, diagnosis and treatment plan |
| Common partners | Counselors, community agencies and medical teams | Primary care, nurses and behavioral health professionals |
| Work setting | Treatment programs, hospitals or community services | Hospital, clinic or consulting role |

[Social work tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/21-1023.00) · [Psychiatrist tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-1223.00)

## Follow a missed visit in a collaborative care team

The American Psychiatric Association describes a model pairing a primary care clinician with a behavioral health care manager and a consulting psychiatrist. In a fictional clinic, a social worker in the care manager role documents a missed visit, asks about a transportation barrier and updates the team. The consulting psychiatrist reviews the case and writes a recommendation for further medical assessment. The primary care clinician decides and documents the next clinical action. The care manager follows up on whether the logistics are resolved. [Collaborative Care roles](https://www.psychiatry.org/patients-families/integrated-behavioral-healthcare/)

## Compare the training and licensing routes

For California clinical social work, the Board of Behavioral Sciences lists an accredited MSW, supervised postdegree experience and required exams on its LCSW route. In-state applicants generally register as an Associate Clinical Social Worker (ASW) before their California supervised experience counts. The Board describes an exception under its 90-day rule. Check whether a posting is clinical or focused on service coordination. A psychiatrist completes medical school and specialty residency training. For the California physician route administered by the Medical Board of California, check approved medical education, postgraduate training and exams. [California LCSW](https://www.bbs.ca.gov/applicants/lcsw.html) · [Clinical social work scope](https://www.bbs.ca.gov/pdf/publications/lawsregs.pdf) · [California physician licensure](https://www.mbc.ca.gov/Licensing/Physicians-and-Surgeons/Apply/Physicians-and-Surgeons-License/) · [BLS entry characteristics](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Put national pay alongside the work week

BLS reports 2025 U.S. median annual wages of $60,280 for mental health and substance abuse social workers and $281,870 for psychiatrists. Ask about caseload and supervision for a social work job and appointment length, call and care setting for a psychiatry job. Bring a fictional referral tracker to a team interview to show how you distinguish a request, its owner and its completion status. A social worker exploring psychiatry can start by observing team case reviews where permitted, then plan for medical school, residency and licensure. A physician moving toward community coordination can build on team handoffs and learn local referral systems and case management. [BLS national wage table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Try a 30-minute handoff on paper

Use two fictional notes: R12 missed a visit and asks for transportation information, and the next medical follow-up is unconfirmed. Spend eight minutes sorting the service and medical questions, 12 minutes drafting a handoff with separate owners and ten minutes checking that a colleague can say what happens next. Leave diagnosis and treatment out of the exercise.

## Ask who owns follow-up in a real team

Ask a social worker what counts as a completed referral. Ask a psychiatrist what information they need before making a clinical decision. Use those answers to compare the recurring work you would want to own.
