# Power Plant Operators vs Pump Operators, Except Wellhead Pumpers

## Take responsibility for generation or for a transfer

Power plant operators operate and monitor generating units and auxiliaries, and track electric output. Pump operators run assigned pumps, regulate and monitor flow within their duties, and log what moved and when. Both may work around pumps. Choose by the equipment you would be responsible for and the record you would hand to the next shift.

## Compare two operating records

| Compare | Power plant operators | Pump operators, except wellhead pumpers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input | Unit status, generation and auxiliary records | Station readings and transfer schedules |
| Setting | Plant control room and equipment areas | Pump station or other transfer facility, where operators run pumps and monitor flow |
| Handoff | Generation status and unresolved events | Equipment status, transfer intervals and exceptions |

## A pump station sends a record to the plant

The Texas environmental permit record for Oklaunion Power Station describes a pump station supplying a makeup water pond. On a fictional site with a similar division of work, the pump operator flags a missing transfer interval. The generation operator checks the corresponding unit log and records what it says about the plant, then shares both items with the shift lead. Each checks the source data from their own assignment. The authorized shift lead coordinates decisions about operation under site rules. [Texas technical summary](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/assets/public/comm_exec/agendas/comm/backup/HR-RFR/2021-0257-IWD-Ltr1.pdf)

## Compare training and check any applicable credentials

BLS identifies high school or equivalent as typical for both roles. It assigns long-term on-the-job training to power plant operators and moderate-term training to pump operators. Some nonnuclear plant operating work may be licensed depending on the jurisdiction and actual duties. Ask the employer which rules apply, which equipment the job covers and what trainees do under supervision. [BLS preparation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) · [BLS power plant profile](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/production/power-plant-operators-distributors-and-dispatchers.htm)

## Compare shifts and travel alongside pay

BLS reports 2025 national median annual wages of $102,040 for power plant operators and $61,770 for pump operators except wellhead pumpers, on an OEWS basis. Ask plant employers about rotating shifts and time in the control room. Ask pump employers about callouts, site travel and time spent on equipment rounds. Compare local offers with similar hours and responsibilities. [BLS wage table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Bring an auxiliary equipment record to the next role

Plant operators can bring experience tracking equipment status to transfer work, then learn the facility’s meter records and handoff practices. Pump operators can bring transfer logs to a plant auxiliary role, then build generation training and control-room coordination experience.
