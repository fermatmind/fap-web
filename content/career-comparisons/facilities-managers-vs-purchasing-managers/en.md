# Facilities Managers vs. Purchasing Managers When Outsourcing Maintenance

## Separate site and supplier decisions

A facilities manager works out what a maintenance visit needs to accomplish without disrupting the building. A purchasing manager uses those requirements to compare vendors and terms. Both may talk to the contractor while owning different parts of the decision.

## Compare the handoffs

| Work product | Facilities Managers | Purchasing Managers |
|---|---|---|
| In this case | Site window, operating limits, and acceptance checks | Comparable bids, contract questions, and a recommendation |

U.S. occupational task sources · [Facilities managers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/11-3013.00#Tasks) · [Purchasing managers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/11-3061.00#Tasks)

## Plan the inspection

A fictional campus wants an outside provider to perform a routine equipment-room inspection. The facilities manager documents permitted access times, services that must stay available, and what the facility needs at handoff. Qualified staff carry out the work. The purchasing manager compares bids for the same scope, asks about rescheduling charges, and sends the tradeoffs to the designated approver.

## How to prepare

BLS describes a bachelor’s degree and related experience as typical for both occupations. It specifically describes several years of related experience for purchasing managers. For facilities, look for building operations or work-order coordination. For purchasing, look for supplier evaluation or contract support. [BLS facilities preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/management/administrative-services-managers.htm#tab-4) · [BLS purchasing preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/business-and-financial/purchasing-managers-buyers-and-purchasing-agents.htm#tab-4)

## Work conditions

Facilities work may be interrupted by site checks and contractor delays. Purchasing work may be paced by bids, review windows, and supplier discussions. Ask who approves schedule changes and who confirms completion.

## Moving between roles

Move toward purchasing by translating a site restriction into a bid criterion and learning how the award is reviewed. Move toward facilities by learning maintenance schedules, site handoffs, and how completion is verified.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional inspection window of 6 to 9 p.m. Friday or all day Saturday. Bid A arrives Friday at 7:30 and needs two hours. Bid B can work Saturday morning but has a cancellation fee. Spend ten minutes documenting site limits, fifteen comparing bids, and five writing the outstanding question for approval.
