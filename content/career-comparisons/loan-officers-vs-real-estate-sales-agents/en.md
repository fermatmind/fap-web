# Loan Officers vs. Real Estate Sales Agents: When Closing Moves Up

## What each role delivers

Loan officers explain loan terms and status and process applications within assigned authority. Real estate sales agents communicate transaction conditions and coordinate closing requirements.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Loan Officers | Real Estate Sales Agents |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Mortgage application status and open conditions | Transaction timeline and document record |
| Basis | Loan documentation, review and authority | Party confirmation, contract and milestones |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $76,690 | $52,830 |

## How the work meets

A fictional transaction may move from next Friday to Wednesday. The agent checks party agreement and amendment status and shares the date with the lender with the buyer’s consent. The officer checks that the appraisal is still outstanding and gives the closing team the open condition and expected update.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree, less than five years of related experience and moderate-term training for loan officers. It lists high school or equivalent and moderate-term training for real estate sales agents, who need state licensure and work with a broker. For residential mortgage origination, check federal registration or state licensing by employer and duties. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [CFPB Regulation G](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1007/103/) [CFPB Regulation H](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1008/103/) [BLS real estate licensing](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/sales/real-estate-brokers-and-sales-agents.htm)

## Work conditions

Loan work can follow document and review deadlines. Real estate work can follow showings and closing dates. Ask who logs changes.

## Moving between roles

Moving into real estate adds contract, negotiation and licensing knowledge to loan familiarity. Moving into loan work adds product, documentation and review skills to transaction coordination.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock file: closing may move to Wednesday, the amendment is unconfirmed and the appraisal is outstanding. Spend ten minutes each on the timeline, loan status and order of confirmations.
