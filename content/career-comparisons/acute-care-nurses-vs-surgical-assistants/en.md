# Acute Care Nurses vs Surgical Assistants

## Bedside nursing and assistance during surgery

Acute care nurses provide advanced nursing care for acute conditions, including preoperative or postoperative care in some roles. Surgical assistants directly assist the surgeon during a procedure within their authorized scope. Both work with the perioperative team and hand off different information. This comparison uses a bedside RN assignment. O*NET also lists acute care nurse practitioner titles under the occupation.

## Outputs, settings, and U.S. entry

| Item | Acute care nurses | Surgical assistants |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Output | Nursing assessment, care record, and handoff | Assigned intraoperative assistance and status updates to the surgical team |
| Setting | Acute hospital units, including some perioperative assignments | Operating rooms |
| Typical U.S. entry | Bedside RN example: approved nursing education, qualifying exam, and RN licensure | Surgical assisting education, commonly at certificate or associate level in the BLS profile; check state rules and employer criteria |

## One transfer with an unsigned summary

At a fictional U.S. hospital, the patient has been transferred back to the unit. Operating room note O-16 says the assistant's assigned work was documented and the operating summary awaits the surgeon's confirmation. Receiving note W-16 says the patient arrived and the nursing assessment was recorded. The surgical assistant alerts the surgeon to the pending summary, which the surgeon reviews and signs. The acute care nurse records receipt, flags the final summary as pending, and records who will check for it during the shift. In this hospital's example workflow, the surgical team and unit update their respective records. In this example, the receiving nurse signs the unit note, and the surgeon signs the operative summary.

## Education and authorization

For acute care nursing, check approved programs, the qualifying exam and state RN licensure. Ask the unit how it assesses readiness for independent assignments. Surgical assisting can start through formal study, and BLS describes another route through surgical technology and additional training. Check the state regulator and the employer for certification, supervision and authorized duties. For an acute care nurse practitioner job, check graduate advanced-practice preparation and state APRN authorization separately.

## Ask what can change the shift

Ask acute care units how they distribute new admissions and returns from surgery when several patients need assessment. Ask surgical teams about scheduled cases, unplanned additions, on-call shifts, and time spent standing in the operating room.

## What transfers and what to learn

Simulation handoffs and approved examples of team communication can demonstrate relevant skills. Nurses moving toward assisting need to verify the operative training and authorization expected for the job. Surgical assistants moving into nursing need the nursing education and licensure pathway and experience with ongoing bedside care.

## Try a 30-minute desk exercise

Use O-16 and W-16, plus fictional update M-16, which says the summary was uploaded while its signature status still reads pending. Spend ten minutes identifying what each record establishes. Spend ten minutes deciding whether to mark the summary signed and naming whom to ask. Spend ten minutes drafting separate updates for the surgical team and receiving unit. Check that your unit note can record arrival and assessment while signature verification remains open.
