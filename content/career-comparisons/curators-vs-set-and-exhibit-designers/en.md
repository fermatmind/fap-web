# Curator vs. Set and Exhibit Designer: Moving a Manuscript Away From a Window

## What each role delivers

A curator researches collections, chooses exhibition objects, develops interpretation and coordinates loan conditions. A set and exhibit designer translates the exhibition into scale drawings, display cases and routes.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Curators | Set and Exhibit Designers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Object selection, label evidence and loan conditions | Scaled plan, case specifications and installation drawings |
| Basis | Theme, provenance, object and loan constraints | Site, circulation, sightlines, dimensions and fabrication budget |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $63,420 | $75,240 |

## How the work meets

A fictional museum borrows a manuscript. The curator confirms that the loan prohibits placing the original near the window and provides the draft label. The designer identifies an interior case on the scale plan and marks the sightlines and entry route after the move. The curator checks how the new location affects the story, and the two hand the confirmed location and conditions to the installation team.

## How to enter

BLS lists a master’s degree as typical for curators and a bachelor’s degree for set and exhibit designers. Ask who confirms display conditions and approves drawings. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Curatorial work can follow research and loan milestones. Design follows drawing approvals, fabrication and installation windows. Ask how a new loan restriction reaches both the label and installation teams.

## Moving between roles

Curators can bring object requirements and narrative into exhibit design and add scale drawing, materials and space planning. Designers can bring circulation knowledge to curation and add collections research, loan conditions and label writing.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock display brief: the window case has the clearest view but cannot hold the original. An interior case is allowed, but visitors would miss the first label in the old layout. A replica may go in the window if identified. Spend ten minutes each on curatorial options, a revised route and a drawing and label handoff.
