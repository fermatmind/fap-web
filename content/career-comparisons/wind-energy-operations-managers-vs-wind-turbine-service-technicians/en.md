# Wind Energy Operations Managers vs. Wind Turbine Service Technicians: Two Outages, One Service Window

## What each role delivers

Wind energy operations managers oversee maintenance, staff and downtime across assets. Wind turbine service technicians carry out assigned maintenance and document the work and recheck.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Wind Energy Operations Managers | Wind Turbine Service Technicians |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Site maintenance schedule and downtime status | Field service and verification record |
| Basis | Downtime, crew and access schedule | Assigned work, condition and recheck |

[O*NET 11-9199.09](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/11-9199.09#Tasks) · [O*NET 49-9081.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/49-9081.00#Tasks)

O*NET uses the Managers, All Other wage series for 11-9199.09. A separate figure for this specialty cannot be derived from the broader series. [O*NET wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/11-9199.09)

## How the work meets

At a fictional site, W2 and W3 are down and both are accessible today. There is one crew qualified for both and one service window. W2 needs a recheck before tomorrow morning’s review. W3 needs an initial inspection and is accessible tomorrow afternoon. The manager follows the site’s deadline rule and assigns W2 first; the technician records the result.

## How to enter

Two-year degrees and bachelor’s degrees were the two most frequent O*NET education responses for wind operations managers. BLS lists a postsecondary nondegree award and long-term on-the-job training for wind turbine service technicians. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [O*NET 11-9199.09 education](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/11-9199.09#Education)

## Work conditions

Operations can span crews and sites. Turbine service can involve outdoor work, heights and confined spaces such as the nacelle. Ask about field training, authorization and how weather affects service windows. [BLS wind technician work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/installation-maintenance-and-repair/wind-turbine-technicians.htm)

## Moving between roles

Moving to service, bring work order and equipment records and get turbine-specific training. Moving to operations, use service knowledge and build staffing and cross-site prioritization skills.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock card: both units are accessible today, with one qualified crew and one slot. W2 needs a recheck before tomorrow morning’s review. W3 needs an initial inspection and can be reached tomorrow afternoon. Site policy prioritizes dated commitments and requires signed verification to mark a unit ready. Spend ten minutes each on priority and rationale, service handoff and missing records.
