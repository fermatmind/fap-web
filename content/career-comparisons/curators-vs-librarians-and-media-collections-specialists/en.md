# Curator vs. Librarian and Media Collections Specialist: Checking a Date for an Exhibit Label

## What each role delivers

A curator researches collections, develops exhibition themes and selects objects. A librarian or media collections specialist clarifies reference requests, locates materials and explains how to access and verify sources. Both roles can work with collections records and the public.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Curators | Librarians and Media Collections Specialists |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Object selection, label evidence and display decision | Source records, access instructions and reference response |
| Basis | Exhibition theme, object, provenance and display conditions | Patron question, catalog fields, original material and access |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $63,420 | $68,270 |

## How the work meets

A fictional local history exhibition will display a factory photograph. The curator asks the library to check its date. The librarian finds a catalog entry and a nondigitized negative register, notes the conflicting years and explains how to consult the register. The curator checks whether the register refers to the photograph, keeps the unresolved date in internal notes and drafts a label using confirmed details.

## How to enter

BLS lists a master’s degree as typical for both occupations. Ask whether the position calls for particular subject expertise, catalog systems or exhibition experience. Public school librarians and library media specialists typically also need teacher certification, with requirements varying by state. Some states require certification for public librarians as well. Check with the state department of education for school roles and the state licensing board for public library roles. [BLS librarian credentials](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/education-training-and-library/librarians.htm#tab-4) [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Curatorial work follows research, loans and opening dates. Library reference work may balance searches, patron service and internal requests. Ask how to schedule original-material checks and record the results.

## Moving between roles

Curators moving into library work can bring subject research and add search systems and reference interviews. Librarians moving into curation can bring source verification and add object selection, label writing and exhibition coordination.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock sources: the catalog says 1936, the negative register says 1938, the original can be checked tomorrow and the label draft is due today. Spend ten minutes each on the reference response, a label draft that handles the unresolved date, and a list of records to update after verification.
