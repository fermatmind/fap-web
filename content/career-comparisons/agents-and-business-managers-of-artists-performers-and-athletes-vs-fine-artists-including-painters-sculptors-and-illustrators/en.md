# Artist Agent or Business Manager vs. Fine Artist: Adding a New Use to a Commission

## What each role delivers

Agents and business managers pursue opportunities and negotiate within the client’s authority. Fine artists develop concepts and deliver drafts and finished assets against the agreed commission.

## Compare the work

|---|---|---|

| Basis | Existing terms, client authority, intended uses and budget | Medium, composition, dimensions and revision time |

## How the work meets

A fictional publisher commissions cover art and approves a sketch. The publisher now wants the same image for a vertical print display. The illustrator checks whether the crop cuts off a figure and estimates time to recompose the image. The agent checks the agreed uses and fee, confirms what the illustrator will accept and submits the revised scope and delivery date to the publisher.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for both. It lists less than five years of related experience for agents and business managers and long-term on-the-job training for fine artists. In California, a person or corporation, including a manager, needs a Labor Commissioner license if their business obtains, offers or promises to obtain, or attempts to obtain work or engagements for artists covered by the statute. Obtaining, offering to obtain, or promising to obtain recording contracts alone does not trigger licensing under this chapter. For an illustration commission like the one above, check whether the agency’s actual activities fall within the definition. [California Division of Labor Standards Enforcement](https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/Talent_Agency_License.html) [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Agent work follows negotiation, quotes and changes. Artists plan around feedback, fabrication or rendering, and delivery dates. Ask who confirms the scope of a rushed revision.

## Moving between roles

Agents can bring briefing and project coordination to art and add medium skills and a portfolio. Artists can use delivery knowledge in representation and learn delegated authority, contract terms and pricing.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock brief: the agreement covers only an e-book cover. A vertical poster requires recomposition and two additional days, and the printer’s deadline is two days away. Spend ten minutes each on the artist’s estimate, the agent’s use and approval questions, and an interim reply to the publisher.
