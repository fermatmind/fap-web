# Pest control workers vs Pesticide handlers, sprayers, and applicators, vegetation

## What each worker hands off

A structural pest worker investigates signs of pests in and around a building and leaves the site contact with a record and follow-up plan. A vegetation applicator works against a defined plant or turf problem and records the site and work performed. The job address may be the same. The work order, authorized activity and person who can change it may differ.

## Tasks, settings and preparation

| | Pest control workers | Vegetation pesticide applicators |
|---|---|---|
| Primary record | Inspection and service notes by building area | Work and application record by site or planting area |
| Example setting | Apartment building or commercial property | Managed turf, ornamental plantings or crops |
| Entry focus | Employer training and structural credential when required | Training and applicable credential by site and work type |

[BLS pest control workers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/building-and-grounds-cleaning/pest-control-workers.htm) · [O*NET vegetation applicators](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/37-3012.00)

## Two requests at an apartment property

An apartment manager reports rodents near a utility entrance and weeds in a planted strip. In this California example, the registered structural company sends a Branch 2 field representative to inspect and report the building findings. The gardening crew records the planted strip on its separate work order and asks its qualified supervisor to confirm whether the assignment fits the business’s authorized scope. A tenant’s request to add another building area goes to the structural company. A request to add a different planted area goes to the gardening supervisor before the order changes.

## How to check California requirements

For structural pest control, a Board Applicator license covers applying on behalf of a registered company in Branches 2 and 3; ask which duties need a Field Representative credential, including the inspection and quoting work in this example. BLS lists a high school diploma and on-the-job training as typical nationally. For the separate California business that uses pesticides for hire incidentally to maintenance gardening, check the Maintenance Gardener Pest Control Business License and its qualified supervisor. DPR lists a Qualified Applicator Certificate with Subcategory Q or Category B, or a Qualified Applicator License with Category B. The business must register with the agricultural commissioner in each county where it intends to work before operating there. Subcategory Q covers qualifying incidental work at eligible sites and excludes California restricted materials. A Category B credential does not broaden the sites allowed by the business license. Check the licensing and examination route for your own duties and state before enrolling. [Structural Pest Control Board](https://pestboard.ca.gov/howdoi/lic.shtml) · [DPR business requirements](https://www.cdpr.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/mgardener.pdf) · [DPR certificate and category guide](https://www.cdpr.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/qac.pdf)

## What the shift feels like

Ask the structural company how many visits are inside occupied properties and who handles follow-up calls. Ask the vegetation crew how often jobs move because of weather or public access, who updates the site list and who reviews the completed record.

## Moving between the two roles

A structural pest worker can bring site notes and follow-up records to a grounds crew, then learn plant identification and verify the applicable vegetation credential and supervision. A vegetation worker can bring site mapping, documentation and escalation habits to structural service, then learn building inspection routines and qualify for the tasks the company expects.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use a fictional property map and four job cards. B1 reports signs inside a utility room on the building order; B2 adds a basement outside that order. G1 matches the approved planting strip on the grounds order; G2 adds a courtyard outside it. Under the mock rules, send B1 and G1 to their listed teams. Hold B2 until a structural field representative confirms and records the revised inspection scope. Hold G2 until the gardening supervisor confirms and records a revised eligible area. Spend ten minutes marking the map, ten drafting the two handoffs and ten checking the owner and clearance record for each exception.
