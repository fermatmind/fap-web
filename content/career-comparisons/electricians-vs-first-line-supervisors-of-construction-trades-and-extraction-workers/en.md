# Electricians vs. First-Line Construction Supervisors: Before the Wall Closes

## What each role delivers

Electricians install, maintain or repair electrical equipment and wiring within their assignment. First-line construction supervisors coordinate people and work areas, track reviews and schedule the next crew.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electricians | First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Assigned electrical work and discrepancy record | Crew sequence and release tracking |
| Change | Raise and document a drawing discrepancy | Coordinate the next trades using the approved update |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $63,190 | $79,920 |

National occupation medians can help compare typical pay. Local pay and starting offers vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 47-2111 and 47-1011](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

In a fictional shop renovation, the electrician sees that E7 is shown in different places on the work order and drawing and sends a marked-up request to the electrical lead. The supervisor can reschedule the wall crew to an available area while the affected area awaits the designated review.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and an apprenticeship as typical for electricians. For first-line supervisors of construction and extraction workers, BLS lists high school or equivalent and five or more years of related work experience as typical. [BLS rows 47-1011 and 47-2111](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) In Texas, for example, an electrical apprentice license covers work under the general supervision of a master electrician, journeyman electrician or residential wireman. Verify which rules apply to your location and role. [Texas apprentice license](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/electricians/apply/individuals/apprentice-electrician.htm)

## Work conditions

Electricians track locations, revisions and inspection windows. Supervisors handle shifts in location, labor and sequencing. Ask how the employer records professional sign-offs and alerts downstream crews.

## Moving between roles

To move into supervision, build on work reports, issue follow-up and handoffs; add scheduling across trades. To move into electrical work, check local requirements and complete training and supervision for the assigned electrical work.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use fictional work orders: A has a signed electrical check, B has two E7 locations, and C has no handoff. Spend ten minutes drafting the discrepancy, ten planning the next crew, and ten listing whose confirmation is needed for each area. Use paperwork only.
