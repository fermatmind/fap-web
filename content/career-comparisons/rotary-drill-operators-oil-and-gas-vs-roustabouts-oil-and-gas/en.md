# Oil and Gas Rotary Drill Operator vs. Roustabout

## What each person contributes to the crew

A rotary drill operator works with drill controls, records drilling activity and directs assigned crew work. A roustabout supports oilfield work such as moving pipe, helping assemble equipment and maintaining the work area.

## Compare where each record goes

| Item | Rotary drill operator | Roustabout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assignment | Carry out and log assigned drilling work | Help prepare the equipment and materials requested |
| Record | Drilling activity and an open issue for the incoming crew | A material or work-area discrepancy for the shift lead |
| Follow-up | Identify which planned job depends on confirmation | Tell the lead what was checked and what is still unclear |

## One tag is unreadable at delivery

A fictional crew receives pipe for a planned job. The packing slip lists six items for one area, but a tag on one piece is unreadable. A roustabout assigned to help with delivery can report the discrepancy and what is visible. The drill operator can identify the scheduled task that depends on the material and flag it in the handoff. The site's designated lead verifies material availability under the site procedure.

## U.S. education and related experience

BLS assigns both occupations no formal educational credential and moderate-term on-the-job training. Its detailed table lists related experience of less than five years for rotary drill operators and none for roustabouts. These are typical assignments, so verify the particular employer's training and equipment authorization. [BLS education and training table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/education-and-training-by-occupation.htm)

## Ask about the shift and the worksite

For a roustabout job, ask how a shift is divided among material handling, equipment support and site upkeep. For drill work, ask about the rotation, log responsibilities and time with the incoming crew. Find out how open issues get assigned across shifts.

## Moving between assignments

Roustabouts can bring equipment familiarity and experience reporting discrepancies, then build the employer's required drill operating and recordkeeping skills. Rotary drill operators can bring experience reading work plans and handing off problems, then learn the site's material verification and support assignments.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use a fictional slip listing six items for Area A, five readable tags and one unreadable tag. In ten minutes draft a material discrepancy report. Spend ten minutes updating a shift handoff to identify the task waiting for confirmation. Use the remaining ten minutes to name the recipient and the evidence needed to close the issue. Work from the records only.
