# Data Entry Keyers vs. Office Machine Operators: The Same Forms, Two Deliverables

## What each role delivers

Data entry keyers enter and verify information from source documents. Office machine operators use equipment to prepare and check document orders.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Data Entry Keyers | Office Machine Operators, Except Computer |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Verified fields | Finished document order |
| Basis | Source forms and field rules | Quantity, sequence and sample copy |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $41,340 | $40,960 |

## How the work meets

A fictional training office records attendance and prepares packets for three rooms. The keyer flags two forms with the same course number and different dates. The operator uses the confirmed master and checks counts and page order by room.

## How to enter

BLS lists a high school diploma or equivalent and short-term on-the-job training for both. For keying, ask how discrepancies are handled. For machine operation, ask which equipment and finishing steps the job covers. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Keyers work through queues and exceptions. Operators may move between equipment and pickup areas. Ask about peak volume, rework and any lifting requirements.

## Moving between roles

Moving to equipment operation, bring batch accuracy and learn setup and output checks. Moving to data entry, bring order verification and add field validation and exception tracking.
