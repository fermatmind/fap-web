# Parking Attendants vs. Traffic Technicians: When a Queue Reaches the Road

## What each role delivers

Parking attendants direct drivers, assist customers and document shift issues. Traffic technicians measure volume, speed or delays and compile records for review.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Parking Attendants | Traffic Technicians |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Vehicle directions and shift notes | Timed counts and traffic observations |
| Basis | Spaces, active queues and facility procedures | Count location, intervals and study method |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $35,150 | $59,090 |

## How the work meets

After a fictional arena event, the garage still has spaces but vehicles queue near the intersection. The attendant directs drivers to open areas under the facility’s procedures and logs the backup. The technician separately counts drivers turning into the garage and going straight, and records how long the queue lasts.

## How to enter

BLS lists no formal educational credential and short-term on-the-job training as typical for parking attendants. It lists a high school diploma or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for traffic technicians. Confirm driving requirements if the attendant moves customer vehicles. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Attendant work may follow shift schedules and event peaks. Traffic surveys may use several intersections or specific observation windows. Ask how staff split field checks and data work.

## Moving between roles

Moving to a traffic technician job, build standard counting and sampling skills on top of observation and customer communication. Moving into parking, adapt traffic awareness to facility routing, payments and handoffs.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock records show 40 vacant spaces and a queue in two consecutive five-minute intervals, with no record of where drivers went. Spend ten minutes each on a shift note, a two-column turning count and the next observation that could locate the delay.
