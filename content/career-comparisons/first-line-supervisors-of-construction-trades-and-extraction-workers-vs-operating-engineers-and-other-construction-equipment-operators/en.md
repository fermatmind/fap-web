# First-Line Construction Supervisors vs. Equipment Operators: Who Updates the Work Plan?

## What each role delivers

First-line construction supervisors coordinate when crews use each work area. Operating engineers and other construction equipment operators carry out assigned machine work and record conditions for the handoff.

## Compare the work

| Output | Crew sequence and access assignments | Machine work and issue log |
| Checks | Shared work areas and dependencies | Machine status and assigned work |

## How the work meets

On a fictional earthmoving job, survey crews are using the north area. The south-area machine log contains an unresolved issue. The operator reports the completed work and outstanding machine item. The supervisor updates the north-area schedule and asks the designated equipment contact to resolve the south-area item.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training as typical for operating engineers and other construction equipment operators. For first-line supervisors of construction and extraction workers, BLS lists high school or equivalent and five or more years of related work experience as typical. [BLS rows 47-1011 and 47-2073](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) Check how training and evaluation work for the assigned machine.

## Work conditions

Supervisors divide attention across crews and work zones. Operators stay closer to one machine and its crew signals. Ask how assignments change and who follows a machine issue from report to resolution.

## Moving between roles

To move into supervision, use equipment issue and handoff records to build responsibility for scheduling multiple crews. To move into operating, use your coordination experience and complete training for the equipment under supervision.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use a fictional north-area schedule with a survey crew and a south-area log with one machine item still under review. Spend ten minutes writing the machine note, ten revising crew assignments, and ten naming who confirms each remaining item. Use paper only.
