# Correspondence clerks vs. data entry keyers

## One request, two work products

Correspondence clerks investigate what the writer needs and prepare a reply. Data entry keyers verify information from source records and put it into the right fields. The same document can start both jobs.

## Compare the deliverables

| Work | Correspondence clerk | Data entry keyer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read first | The request, case history, and relevant policy | Source documents, field definitions, and validation rules |
| Deliver | A response and a record of follow-up | A verified record and a list of exceptions |
| Work setting | Desk work with email and handoffs to other teams | Batch work at a computer. Check how much time is spent reconciling records. |
| Typical U.S. entry and training | High school diploma or equivalent, short-term on-the-job training | High school diploma or equivalent, short-term on-the-job training |
| 2025 U.S. median annual wage | $46,800 | $41,340 |

The wage figures cover U.S. nonfarm wage and salary employment. They exclude self-employment.

## When the invoice and the letter disagree

At a fictional retailer, a customer disputes $180, while the attached invoice shows $108. The keyer records the two amounts in separate fields, cites each source, and flags the case. The correspondence clerk drafts an acknowledgment with a follow-up date and routes the discrepancy to the billing supervisor. The supervisor determines the correct amount. The keyer updates the approved amount and the clerk uses that decision in the reply.

## Getting started in the U.S.

O*NET lists both occupations in Job Zone 1–2, which indicates very little to some preparation. Check the employer’s requirements for each opening. For correspondence, practice turning a case file into a short acknowledgment and follow-up note. For data entry, practice using field definitions and reconciling entries against the original. [O*NET correspondence preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/43-4021.00#JobZone) · [O*NET data entry preparation](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/43-9021.00#JobZone)

## Ask about deadlines and review

On a correspondence team, ask how deadlines are tracked and who reviews drafts that promise a remedy. On a data entry team, ask whether work arrives in batches, how accuracy is sampled, and when you can correct an error or must escalate it.

## Moving between the roles

For a move into data entry, bring a redacted case tracker and attachment index, then build skills in field mapping and batch verification. To move into correspondence, bring an exception log with source references, then practice explaining business rules and tracking a reply across teams.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use a mock customer letter and invoice with the amounts above. The form allows both amounts, and only the billing supervisor can set the approved amount. Spend ten minutes filling in the record, ten writing a customer acknowledgment, and ten preparing a short referral to the supervisor. Show where each number came from and when the customer will hear back.
