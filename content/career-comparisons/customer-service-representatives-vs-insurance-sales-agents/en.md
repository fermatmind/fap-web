# Customer Service Representatives vs. Insurance Sales Agents: An Address Change Raises a Coverage Question

## What each role delivers

Customer service representatives log requests and follow up. Insurance sales agents identify coverage needs, explain policy terms and handle sales within their authority.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Customer Service Representatives | Insurance Sales Agents |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Service ticket and status update | Coverage needs and policy explanation |
| Basis | Current policy status and service authority | Customer needs, policy terms and sales authority |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $44,770 | $62,280 |

National medians; local and starting pay vary. [BLS Table 1.2, rows 43-4051 and 41-3021](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

A fictional customer updates a mailing address and asks whether personal property at the new home is covered. Service staff log both requests and route the coverage question to the authorized agent. The agent reviews the existing terms and residence address and flags what the insurer must confirm. Service staff follow up with verified status.

## How to enter

BLS lists a high school diploma or equivalent for both, with short-term on-the-job training for customer service representatives and moderate-term training for insurance sales agents. Agents need a license in the states where they work. If a service job includes sales duties, verify the activities with the employer and state regulator. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) [BLS state licensing](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/sales/insurance-sales-agents.htm)

## Work conditions

Service work may follow case queues and response deadlines. Sales may follow consultations and renewals. Ask which questions are referred and who records the insurer’s confirmation.

## Moving between roles

Moving into sales adds policy knowledge and applicable licensing to interviewing and follow-up skills. Moving into service adds queue management and escalation to product knowledge.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock file: the mailing address is updated, the policy shows the old insured location and the customer asks about the effective date. Spend ten minutes each on a service update, questions for the agent and items requiring insurer confirmation.
