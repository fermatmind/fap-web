# Art Therapist vs. Marriage and Family Therapist

## Art-based treatment and relationship-focused therapy

An art therapist can use creative activities within treatment to help clients express and reflect on experiences. A marriage and family therapist considers how relationships and patterns of interaction affect the client's concerns. Both roles may work with individuals.

## Compare the deliverables

| Work | Art therapist | Marriage and family therapist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First check | How the client uses and interprets creative expression | How people respond to one another and what they want to change |
| Deliverable | Treatment notes on the creative process and progress | Relationship-focused goals and a follow-up plan |

O*NET links Art Therapists to the broader Therapists, All Other wage series. It does not isolate pay for art therapists. [O*NET wage coverage](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-1129.01#WagesEmployment)

[O*NET art therapy tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-1129.01#Tasks) · [BLS marriage and family therapy duties](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/marriage-and-family-therapists.htm#tab-2)

## Two records about the same family conversation

In a fictional clinic, a client draws a scene about going quiet during a family conversation. The art therapist records how the client describes the experience. The family therapist asks how relatives respond when the client goes quiet. With the client’s consent and under applicable confidentiality rules, the clinicians share observations relevant to the goals they agreed to review.

## U.S. preparation and state credentials

Look for graduate training and supervised experience aligned with the duties you want. BLS describes a master’s degree and licensure for marriage and family therapists. New York, for example, has separate requirements for licensed creative arts therapists and marriage and family therapists. Check the education, experience, and examination pathway for the title and state where you plan to work. [New York creative arts therapy](https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/creative-arts-therapists/license-requirements) · [New York marriage and family therapy](https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/marriage-and-family-therapists/license-requirements) · [BLS family therapy preparation](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/marriage-and-family-therapists.htm#tab-4)

## Compare workplaces and schedules

Art therapy can involve direct contact, preparing materials, and documenting sessions. BLS lists settings such as mental health centers and private practice for marriage and family therapy. Ask about individual and group sessions, evening appointments, and time for supervision and documentation. [O*NET art therapy work context](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-1129.01#WorkContext) · [BLS family therapy work environment](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/marriage-and-family-therapists.htm#tab-3)

## Moving between the roles

An art therapist can bring observation and documentation skills into family therapy training, then develop supervised experience with relationship-focused cases. A family therapist can bring goal setting and interviewing skills into art therapy training and learn to plan and assess art-based sessions.

## A 30-minute paper exercise

Use two fictional accounts. The client says they went quiet when a relative interrupted them. The relative says they thought the conversation was already over. Spend ten minutes documenting the accounts separately, ten drafting a question about the client’s experience, and ten drafting a question about the family interaction. Work from the fictional notes.
