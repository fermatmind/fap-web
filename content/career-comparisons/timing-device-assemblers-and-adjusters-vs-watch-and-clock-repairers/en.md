# Timing Device Assembler vs. Watch and Clock Repairer: A Pilot Build and a Returned Watch

## What each role delivers

A timing device assembler and adjuster assembles mechanisms and checks fit and operation against the work order. A watch and clock repairer diagnoses the condition of a used item, performs the work agreed with the customer and checks function afterward. The roles overlap in disassembly, adjustment and testing.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Timing Device Assemblers and Adjusters | Watch and Clock Repairers |
|---|---|---|
| Starting point | Work order, component set and fit or motion requirements | Customer complaint, used timepiece and service history |
| Output | Assembled mechanism and inspection results | Repair record and verification on the returned item |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $62,620 | $67,230 |

## How the work meets

At a fictional watch company, a pilot unit and a customer's returned watch both stop intermittently. The assembler passes the pilot unit's lot, build stage and test conditions to the repairer. The repairer checks the returned movement and its service history, then reports any comparable observations. The assembler records the disposition of the pilot unit. The repairer documents whether the customer's symptom has been resolved.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent for both and moderate-term on-the-job training for timing device assemblers versus long-term training for watch and clock repairers. Ask which movement families trainees handle and who signs off on their first independent jobs. [BLS Table 1.2](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Assembly may involve repeat work on a lot. Repair may involve more variation in the condition and history of each item. Ask how a failed inspection is recorded and when the next bench gets the ticket.

## Moving between roles

Assembly experience in fit, test conditions and inspection records can carry into repair. Build skill in reproducing customer complaints and assessing worn parts. In the other direction, add lot traceability and repeatable inspection to your repair experience.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use mock tickets showing two devices that stop in the same position but only one traceable parts lot. Take ten minutes to list the known facts, ten to draft the two-way handoff and ten to record separate dispositions. Identify what the shared symptom alone cannot establish. Use paper records only.
