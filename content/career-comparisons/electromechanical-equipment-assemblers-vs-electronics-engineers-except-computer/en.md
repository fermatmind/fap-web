# Electromechanical Equipment Assemblers vs. Electronics Engineers: The Part That Doesn’t Fit

## What each role delivers

Electromechanical equipment assemblers locate, connect and inspect parts against drawings. Electronics engineers assess electronic design and compatibility and help resolve specification issues.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Electromechanical Equipment Assemblers | Electronics Engineers, Except Computer |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Assembled unit and exception record | Electronic design and specification review |
| Basis | Drawing revision, assembly sequence and inspection | Circuit requirements and change verification |

[O*NET 51-2023.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/51-2023.00#Tasks) · [O*NET 17-2072.00](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2072.00#Tasks)

The published assembly wage series covers a broader electrical, electronic and electromechanical assembly category. It does not establish a separate median for this role. [O*NET wage mapping](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/51-2023.00)

## How the work meets

On a fictional instrument build, a cable routed as drawn conflicts with a bracket. The assembler records the part ID, drawing revision and observation. The electronics engineer and mechanical counterpart review interface constraints and route a proposed change for verification.

## How to enter

BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for electronics engineers. For assembly jobs, check the specific drawing, inspection and training expectations. Bring an example of a clear exception record. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Assemblers commonly work at a station with defined sequences and handoffs. Electronics engineers may follow prototypes, design reviews and technical exceptions. Ask who approves a deviation and who releases the next build.

## Moving between roles

Moving into engineering, bring device familiarity and add circuit theory and validation. Moving into assembly, bring drawing skills and learn the station’s sequence, inspections and handoffs.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Drawing B routes cable C2 above bracket K1. The trial build records contact between C2 and K1, and the parts list leaves the K1 revision blank. Spend ten minutes each writing an exception ticket, identifying two questions for engineering and naming the record needed before work resumes. Use paper only.
