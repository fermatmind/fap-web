# First-Line Construction Supervisors vs. Structural Iron and Steel Workers: A Drawing Mismatch

## What each role delivers

First-line construction supervisors coordinate crew assignments, sequence and inspection windows. Structural iron and steel workers read the erection drawings, install members within the assigned scope and check their alignment.

## Compare the work

| Work item | First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers | Structural Iron and Steel Workers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Area schedule and inspection handoff | Erected steel and exception record |
| Checks | Crew, deliveries and review windows | Member IDs, drawings and alignment |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $79,920 | $62,780 |

## How the work meets

At a fictional plant project, the delivery drawing for member S4 is outdated. The ironworker records the member ID and drawing discrepancy for the technical lead. The supervisor keeps that area pending and reschedules the crew that was due to follow.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school or equivalent for both roles, with an apprenticeship typical for structural iron and steel workers and five or more years of related experience typical for first-line supervisors. [BLS rows 47-1011 and 47-2221](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Ironworkers spend more time on member-specific work. Supervisors adjust delivery windows, crew plans and checks. Ask how revisions reach the field and who updates an area’s status.

## Moving between roles

To go into supervision, build from member verification to handoffs and crew scheduling. To move into structural steel work, bring plan-reading experience and seek supervised training in member placement and connection checks.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Use three fictional cards: S3 matches its drawing, S4 arrived with an old revision, and another crew is booked in the shared area. In 30 minutes write a member query, a revised area schedule and a list of confirmation owners. Use paperwork only.
