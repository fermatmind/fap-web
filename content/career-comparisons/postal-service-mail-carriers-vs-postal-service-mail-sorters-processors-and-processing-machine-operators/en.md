# Postal Service Mail Carriers vs Postal Service Mail Sorters, Processors, and Processing Machine Operators

## Dispatching batches and delivering a route

Mail sorters and processing workers route mail and containers to designated destinations. They may use barcode sorters and sort odd-sized mail by hand. Mail carriers prepare mail for an assigned route and record outcomes as they deliver. Each checks where the mail belongs. The processing role works across batches, and the carrier works across stops.

## A container reaches the wrong delivery unit

In a fictional postal network, a carrier preparing a South Unit route spots a container marked North Unit. The carrier reports the arrival discrepancy to the delivery lead and continues preparing mail confirmed for the route. A processor checks the dispatch list against the container and receipt records. The designated leads in this example verify its destination and arrange the next transfer under the facility's procedure. The processor logs the transfer and the delivery unit records who took custody.

## Ask how the pace changes

For delivery, ask how much of a shift is outdoors, whether you cover more than one route, and who adjusts assignments during severe weather. For processing, ask about night work, station rotation, time standing, and equipment training. Ask what happens when dispatch deadlines and a backlog coincide.

## Show that you can account for a handoff

Moving from delivery into processing, bring a fictional misroute log, then learn dispatch records, container-level checks, and equipment training for the assigned station. Moving toward delivery, bring a fictional dispatch log that shows how you tracked custody. Learn route sequencing, customer-facing delivery and how attempted deliveries are recorded. Review the assessment for the new title. [USPS exam chart](https://about.usps.com/careers/how-to-apply/exams.htm)

## Try a 30-minute tabletop dispatch

Use fictional records. Container C12 is listed for North Unit but was received at South Unit. Item D05 is confirmed for your South Unit route. Spend eight minutes distinguishing the container destination from the route, twelve drafting either a carrier's preparation record or a processor's transfer request, and ten checking the result. D05 is ready for route preparation. C12 stays in the exception record until the destination is confirmed and the next custodian is named.
