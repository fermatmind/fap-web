# Customer Service Representative vs. Restaurant Host: Who Confirms a Table After a Booking Change?

## What each role delivers

Customer service representatives log orders, requests and changes. Restaurant hosts greet and seat guests using reservation records, party size and table availability.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Customer Service Representatives | Restaurant Host |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Updated booking, response and handoff | Waitlist position, table availability and seating update |
| Basis | Original booking, request and service authority | Table capacity, turnover, sections and arrivals |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $44,770 | $31,200 |

National employee wage medians; pay varies by employer and location. [BLS Table 1.2 rows 43-4051 and 35-9031](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## How the work meets

At a fictional restaurant, a party of four asks to become six. The representative logs the request and tells the guest the restaurant needs to confirm seating. The host checks when a six-person table may open and whether the floor team can combine smaller tables. The representative updates the booking after the host replies.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent for customer service representatives and no formal educational credential for restaurant hosts as typical entry education. It lists short-term on-the-job training for both. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Customer service follows calls and online requests. Hosts also work around meal periods, table turnover and lines. Ask who gives waiting guests a revised estimate.

## Moving between roles

Representatives can bring intake and records to hosting and learn table layouts and floor coordination. Hosts can bring live communication to customer service and learn booking systems, case tracking and callbacks.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock booking: a party grows from four to six. One four-person table is ready, a six-person table may open in 20 minutes and combining tables is unconfirmed. Spend ten minutes each on the booking note, seating checks and a guest update.
