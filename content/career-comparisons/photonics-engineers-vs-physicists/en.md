# Photonics Engineers vs Physicists

## Build to a requirement or test an explanation

Photonics engineers design and verify optical components and systems. Physicists use theory, experiments, and computation to explain observations. Think about which deliverable you want to own: evidence that the prototype meets its requirement or evidence that distinguishes physical explanations.

## Compare the work products

| Work | Photonics engineers | Physicists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting point | Performance requirement and design constraints | Research question and competing explanations |
| Common delivery | Prototype, verification results, and revision proposal | Model, experimental evidence, and uncertainty analysis |
| Review question | What should change before the next version? | What does the evidence support, and what remains open? |

[O*NET engineering tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.07) · [O*NET physics tasks](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-2012.00)

## A clock needs both a controlled system and an explanation

NIST researchers reported using a radiation shield in an ytterbium optical clock and checking how the blackbody radiation shift depended on temperature. Imagine two assignments on a similar study. The photonics engineer would hand the physicist a run table with temperatures, frequency readings, and the readout stability check for each run. The physicist would compare the shifts with a model using runs that passed the readout check, document uncertainty, and send the engineer a list of runs that need repeating. These are illustrative assignments. [NIST optical clock study](https://www.nist.gov/publications/atomic-clock-1x10-18-room-temperature-blackbody-stark-uncertainty)

## Compare U.S. preparation by setting

Ask engineering employers which design tools and lab experience they expect, and check research postings for their degree and specialty requirements. ## Ask how development and research time are spent

Prototype work can alternate between design reviews and time on an optical test bench. Experimental physics work may use dedicated facilities and involve periods of analysis and reporting between measurement runs. Ask a photonics team about prototype cycles, handoff deadlines and access to test facilities. Ask a physics team how work is split among experiments, analysis and review. BLS publishes a physicist wage estimate. The O*NET engineering figure covers Engineers, All Other, so it cannot establish a specialty-specific pay difference. Compare offers with similar location, experience, and contract length. [BLS physics work environment and pay](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/Life-Physical-and-Social-Science/Physicists-and-astronomers.htm) · [O*NET engineering wage coverage](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/17-2199.07)

## Build a sample for the other role

Moving toward physics? Reuse your measurement skills and add a hypothesis, a control, and a description of what the result cannot establish. Moving toward engineering? Reuse your experimental analysis and add a requirement trace, a versioned test record, and a recommendation tied to acceptance criteria.

## Try a 30-minute clock-team handoff

Use fictional data. An optical readout shows 2 units of variation against a limit of 3. Separately, the observed frequency shift is 4 units and the model predicts 3; uncertainty has not been estimated. Spend 12 minutes recording the readout result against its limit. Spend 13 minutes writing a research note with the difference, missing uncertainty and a proposed next check. Use five minutes to verify the engineering pass is only for the readout, and the model conclusion stays open.
