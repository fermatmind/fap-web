# Concierge vs. Meeting, Convention, and Event Planners: A Last-Minute Room Change

## What each role delivers

A concierge guides guests and coordinates individual requests. Meeting, convention, and event planners translate an event’s requirements into a venue, equipment and supplier plan.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Concierge | Meeting, Convention, and Event Planners |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Guest directions and service confirmation | Approved room setup, vendor instructions and change record |
| Basis | Guest location, arrival time and access needs | Headcount, room specifications, equipment, cost and approval |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $38,950 | $61,160 |

## How the work meets

The reserved room for a fictional forum is taken out of service. The planner checks alternate rooms with venue staff, including capacity, projection and recording equipment, and updates vendors after the event lead approves the change. The concierge uses the revised location to verify access routes, directs late arrivals and reports recurring access problems to the planner.

## How to enter

BLS lists high school completion or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training as typical for concierges. It lists a bachelor’s degree as typical for meeting, convention, and event planners. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Concierge traffic may rise around arrivals and session breaks. Planners may work across both advance coordination and live operations. Ask which channel carries an approved room change.

## Moving between roles

Concierge experience with attendee requests helps in event planning; add budgeting, crew coordination and venue specifications. A planner moving into concierge work can build on site coordination by learning one-to-one request confirmation and follow-up.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock brief: the forum has 60 attendees. Room A seats 70 but recording equipment is unconfirmed. Room B has the equipment and a limit of 50. Spend ten minutes each on room choices and open checks, the approval and vendor update sequence, and what the concierge can tell attendees before a move is approved.
