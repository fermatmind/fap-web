# Real Estate Sales Agents vs Title Examiners, Abstractors, and Searchers

## Coordinate a deal or trace the property record

Sales agents track what buyers and sellers need to decide and when. Title examiners, abstractors, and searchers look for records that explain ownership, liens, or restrictions. Think about whether you would rather organize a showing and decision timeline or trace a document back to its source.

## Compare the work products

| Work | Real estate sales agents | Title examiners, abstractors, and searchers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input | Client requirements, listing and transaction status | Legal description, recorded instruments and search scope |
| Delivery | Showing schedule, offer updates, and pending documents | Source references, title summary and review questions |
| Final call | The client decides whether to offer or accept terms | The authorized reviewer decides how to address title issues |

[O*NET sales work](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/41-9022.00) · [O*NET title work](https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/23-2093.00)

## A Texas resale has conflicting lot numbers

The Texas Department of Insurance explains that a title commitment sets conditions for issuing insurance. In a fictional Texas resale, a listing worksheet shows Lot 14 and the deed copy supplied for review shows Lot 41. The sales agent alerts the broker and client and tracks when a verified description is needed. The searcher records the sources, checks the relevant property records, and routes the conflict to the authorized title reviewer. The agent updates the transaction tracker after the review response. [Texas title insurance FAQ](https://www.tdi.texas.gov/title/titlefaqs.html)

## Check the Texas license and role scope

BLS lists high school or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training as typical for both occupations nationally. Texas's standard sales agent route includes 180 qualifying classroom hours and an exam, then broker sponsorship to activate the license before brokerage practice. On the title side, ask which tasks are search and examination, which entail signing insurance forms or handling escrow, and what credentials apply. Texas separately regulates escrow officer duties and appointments. [BLS occupation rows](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm) · [TREC sales agent route](https://www.trec.texas.gov/become-licensed/sales-agent) · [TDI escrow officer rules, L-2](https://tdi.texas.gov/title/titlem6a.html)

## Ask about client hours and review deadlines

BLS reports that real estate sales work may include evenings or weekends and commonly involves commissions. Ask about availability and commission terms. Title work involves comparing records and identifying discrepancies. Ask how many files are in the review queue, which record systems you use, and who reviews ambiguous results. U.S. May 2025 median annual employee wages were $52,830 for sales agents and $58,650 for title examiners, abstractors, and searchers. OEWS excludes self-employed earnings. ## Try a 30-minute discrepancy handoff

Use a fictional worksheet dated July 5 showing Lot 14 and a deed copy dated July 3 showing Lot 41. Spend 12 minutes writing the agent's note with a deadline for the broker to follow up. Spend 13 minutes building a two-row source log with the conflicting values and an assigned reviewer. Use five minutes to check that neither record presents either value as verified.
