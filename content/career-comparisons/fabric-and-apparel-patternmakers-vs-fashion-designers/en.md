# Fabric and Apparel Patternmakers vs. Fashion Designers: A Jacket Pulls When the Arm Lifts

## What each role delivers

Fabric and apparel patternmakers turn specifications into pieces and revise them after fittings. Fashion designers define appearance and material requirements and review sample garments.

## Compare the work

| Work item | Fabric and Apparel Patternmakers | Fashion Designers |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Pattern pieces, marks and sizing records | Design requirements and sample decisions |
| Basis | Piece alignment, sizing and fitting notes | Silhouette, fabric and sample appearance |
| U.S. median annual wage (2025) | $62,750 | $80,960 |

## How the work meets

A fictional jacket fits when the model stands still but pulls under the arm when she reaches. The designer specifies that the shoulder silhouette must stay. The patternmaker compares the fitting notes with the current pattern, proposes a trial change to the sleeve and armhole, records the revision and hands the sample back for review.

## How to enter

BLS lists a high school diploma or equivalent and moderate-term on-the-job training for fabric and apparel patternmakers and a bachelor’s degree as typical for fashion designers. Ask how the company assigns first patterns, grading and sample review. [BLS occupation table](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)

## Work conditions

Pattern work can follow sample iterations and delivery dates. Design can involve fabric review, fittings and collection reviews. Ask who updates the specification after each fitting.

## Moving between roles

Patternmakers can bring garment fit and construction to design and add concept development and material selection. Designers can bring sample judgment to pattern work and build drafting, matching and grading skills.

## Try it in 30 minutes

Mock fitting: the jacket fits at rest, pulls when the arm lifts and must retain the shoulder silhouette. Spend ten minutes each on a fitting note, pattern and design checks, and a retest and sign-off plan.
