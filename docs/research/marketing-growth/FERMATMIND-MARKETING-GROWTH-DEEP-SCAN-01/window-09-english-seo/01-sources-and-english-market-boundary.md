# Sources and English-Market Boundary

## Authority order

1. Backend/CMS public APIs define current public content facts.
2. `en-content-parity-control-master.v2.json` is the only sitewide V2 English parity control authority.
3. GSC exports measure search performance; they do not establish user language, content authority, or publication eligibility.
4. SERP samples describe observed result types, not a stable rank guarantee.
5. Historical 1046 Career inventory is demand research only.

## English segmentation

An English page is identified by its verified `/en/` canonical, not the visitor country. US, UK, and OTHER are mutually exclusive country groups. GLOBAL is a separate total view and must never be added as a fourth country group.
