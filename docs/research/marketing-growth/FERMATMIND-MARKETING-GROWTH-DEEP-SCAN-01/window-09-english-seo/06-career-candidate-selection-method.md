# Career Candidate Selection Method

## Gate before ranking becomes a batch

C03 recoverability, C06 production/publication truth, C07 locale/governance closure, formal publication eligibility, an EN asset, reviewer PASS, backend projection, and no governance/locale leakage must all pass.

## Research formula used now

1. Start only from historical demand-tier A rows.
2. Prefer rows already marked `dataset_publish_track=candidate` in the historical audit.
3. Rank by retained global impressions, then position.
4. Keep all authority, release, locale, reviewer, source, and country fields explicit.
5. Mark every result `CANDIDATE_ONLY`.

The formula cannot confirm a URL, publication batch, claim, or search eligibility. HTTP 200, file presence, GSC impressions, and historical 1046 membership are insufficient.
