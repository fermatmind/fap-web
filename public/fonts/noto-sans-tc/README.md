# Global History Taiwan portrait font

`global-history-quiz-700.ttf` is a local weight-700 subset of [Noto Sans TC](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/3be1884c48c3e45b52ecc725676a08f87776373e/ofl/notosanstc), distributed under the included OFL.

The original variable font SHA-256 is `864727d210d54f2537bbe23b3a839436c3992af72de9322af5270897246bd44f`. FontTools 4.68.0 instantiated weight 700 and kept the Taiwan roster name, region and field characters, both `zh-TW` card labels and printable ASCII. All 451 selected characters were checked in the saved cmap. Only the Taiwan PNG portrait renderer uses this file.

`legacy-story-700.ttf` uses the same original and license. Its saved cmap covers 1,324 characters from the 99 Taiwan story titles, descriptions and public portrait summaries, the card labels and printable ASCII. The public Taiwan story share card is its only consumer.

`changan-history-quiz-700.ttf` uses the same original and license. Its saved cmap covers the 90 distinct characters used by the Taiwan Chang’an card labels, the six names and epithets, and the site address.

`archetype-questionnaire-700.ttf` uses the same source and OFL license. Its saved cmap covers the 2032 selected characters in the public Taiwan archetype and Likert cards, the two local fantasy card templates and printable ASCII.

The archetype subset also includes U+51EA, U+7DD1 and U+9ED2 from Noto Sans JP at weight 700 under the same OFL. These outlines preserve the published spellings 凪, 四緑木星 and 二黒土星. The upstream Google Fonts revision is 66a36c8c94b1a5d992ee4e7f392fccfe4945767c.

`practice-questionnaire-700.ttf` uses the same source and OFL license. Its saved cmap covers all 901 selected characters in the public Taiwan practice cards and printable ASCII.
